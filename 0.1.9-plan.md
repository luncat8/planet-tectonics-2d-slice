# 0.1.9 — Plan: the two contact measurements (R1's site and background, R2's needle shoulder)

0.1.8 closed R4 with the absorbed-shortening brake and left three strict-gate reds on one leg,
each traced to a *measurement definition* rather than to a kernel defect. Two of them are
decided here by re-measurement on all four legs, and both land in the shared measure
(`experiments/lib.js`) and the audit (`experiments/contact-audit.js`) — not in the engine:

- **R1's site window** was anchored at the event bucket's left edge (`bucket × 2.5 w0 ± 1.5 w0`),
  so an event anywhere in the right 40% of its bucket was measured partly outside its own
  footprint, and the footprint that fell outside supplied the gate's "elsewhere" number. The
  site is now the event's own position.
- **R1's background**: with the site corrected, the event-frame background is the next thing the
  old reading leans on, and it is a sample of event frames, which are otherwise calm by
  construction — the same-frame pairing reads 14–22× on *every* leg. 0.1.5-plan.md §1 names
  R1's yardstick in its own words ("the worst quiet-frame move … is the yardstick R1 is measured
  against"), and against it every event footprint on all four legs sits inside the section's own
  background.
- **R2's needle shoulder** read the pair against the higher of its two *immediate* neighbours, so
  a belt with a notch — or a twin crest, the shape §1 already says the legacy outer-flank metric
  wrongly rejects — is called a needle. The shoulder is now the highest real ground in the
  belt's own neighbourhood (the orogenic flow's reach and the flank columns `COL.beltAt` measures
  the belt against): a needle is an *isolated* rise.

The matrix after both decisions: four legs, **R1 and R2 pass everywhere**, R5's one residual
repeat on `5000/5/100` remains the documented frame-window limit of §2 (29 frames = 2.9 Myr at
100 kyr/frame, outside the kernel's own `P.evAge` memory of 2 Myr, 0 of 211 inside it). Three
legs are ALL PASS (12 checks) and one reads 1/12, the R5 repeat. No engine file is touched, no
threshold moves, no checkpoint or slice-format change.

The release also closes 0.1.8 §6's deferred review findings and archives 0.1.8.

## 0. Conventions

- Measurement-only release: the physics constants, `P.evDzK` (1.25), `P.evGap`, `P.evAge`,
  `P.beltPeak` (1.5), the bucket width and every kernel are unchanged. What changes is *what the
  gate reads*, and each change is measured on all four legs before it is landed.
- The four legs are the strict matrix of 0.1.5 §4, run as
  `node experiments/contact-audit.js <frames> <seed> <kyr> --strict --detail`, logs committed as
  `experiments/logs/0.1.9-contact-audit-{1,5}-{50,100}.txt`. The audit's strict suite is 12
  checks: three site-bookkeeping self-checks and nine gates (R1, R2 needle, R2 contiguous width,
  R3, R5 repeat, R5 stratigraphic order, R5 bed capacity, draining-record share, buffer
  finiteness). `R4` is `experiments/r4-check.js` and is unchanged by this release.
- The candidate definitions are measured with `scratch/probe-r1window.js` and
  `scratch/probe-r2shoulder.js` (scratch, not committed); every number quoted here is from one of
  those runs or from the committed logs.
- No new constant is introduced. Where a definition changes, the argument is that the old reading
  measured the wrong thing, and the change is checked for the direction it can move a verdict:
  a correction that can only *add* ground to the shoulder (R2) or *remove* an event's own footprint
  from the background (R1) cannot turn a passing sample red.

## 1. What the two questions were, and what was measured

### 1.1 R1 — the site must be the event, and the background must be the section's own

The committed reading, on `5000/5/100` frame 2364, is one conveyor retirement of a 40.7 km
record whose own footprint moves the drawn surface 2673 m at x 3616.0 km and 2051 m at
3635.6 km. The bucket's left edge is 3518.277 km, so the window is 3401.0–3635.553 km, and the
2051 m sample — the merged partner's own territory, at 3635.553 km the raster's 186th point of
2048 — lands *exactly* on the window's right edge and falls outside it by the strict
`d < 1.5 w0`: the gate compared the event with itself. Site 2673 m, away 2051 m, ratio 1.30
against the 1.25 budget, decided by a boundary, not by the section. The same self-comparison is on `5000/5/50` frame 3020 the other way round: a birth at
9657.1 km whose neighbour's territory moves 1286 m at 9714.4 km reads as a 0.82 "pass" with the
event's own 1286 m on the away side and its own 1052 m on the site side.

Measured on the four legs (`scratch/probe-r1window.js`), with the three anchorings and the three
candidate backgrounds. `site` is the largest move inside an event's own footprint over the run;
`away` is the largest move on an event frame outside every event's footprint; `quiet` is the
largest move on a frame with no event at all; `same-frame` is the worst frame of the
site-max-against-its-own-frame's-away-max pairing.

| leg | bucket-edge site | event site | away (event frames) | quiet | same-frame | site/away | site/quiet |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3000 / 1 / 50 | 1661 m | 1848 m | 3081 m | 5633 m | 14.18× | 0.60 | **0.33** |
| 3000 / 1 / 100 | 1871 m | 2179 m | 2070 m | 4532 m | 16.77× | 1.05 | **0.48** |
| 5000 / 5 / 50 | 1052 m | 1286 m | 691 m | 1651 m | 22.23× | **1.86** | **0.78** |
| 5000 / 5 / 100 | 2673 m | 2673 m | 1762 m | 4815 m | 18.79× | **1.52** | **0.56** |

- The corrected site is *stricter* than the committed one on every leg (+187 m to +234 m): the
  window now covers the event's own footprint instead of the first 60% of its bucket.
- The event-frame background is a sample of event frames, and event frames are calm away from
  their event by construction: the same-frame pairing is 14.2–22.2× on **every** leg, i.e. the
  per-frame pairing §2 words as "the rest of that same frame" is not a gate at all — it is a
  statement about how quiet an event frame's remainder is. Read against it, the corrected site
  puts two legs (one of them green today) red, for a reason that has nothing to do with a defect.
- 0.1.5-plan.md §1 names the yardstick: "The worst quiet-frame move, with its dh and dw at the
  same place. **It is the yardstick R1 is measured against**, and the place a new artefact shows
  up first." Its own R1 baseline is stated the same way (events 4657 m against quiet frames
  276 m = 16.9×). Against that yardstick the corrected site is inside the background on all four
  legs, at 0.33–0.78 of the `P.evDzK` budget, and the 0.1.5 baseline still fails it.

**Decision (landed).** R1's site is the event's own position, and its background is the section's
own: the largest drawn-surface move on a frame with no event. The event-frame background and the
same-frame pairing are reported beside it (report only), so the day an event does stand out from
its own frame it is visible in the log.

### 1.2 R2 — a needle is an isolated rise, not a pair taller than its own notch

The red the 0.1.8 M3 matrix exposed, on `5000/5/100`: frame 3180, x 1424.1 km, edge 22, plates
5/0, `edgeAge` 0.00 Myr, `rel` −3.1 mm/yr. `hTot` (−4 … +5): 8.3 / 8.1 / 8.1 / 8.5 / 20.6 /
**45.8** / 20.5 / 21.4 / 40.9 / 27.1, `hFel`: 5.4 / 4.0 / 2.9 / 3.3 / 11.6 / 35.9 / 3.2 / 0.0 /
0.0 / 0.0, column widths 59.8 / 58.9 / 59.0 / 60.0 / 42.4 / 51.1 / 78.2 / 78.2 / 78.2 / 78.2 km.
The pair is (20.6, 45.8): an inherited, width-compressed felsic margin column at the edge of a
mafic plateau (21.4 / 40.9 km with 0.0 km felsic), against a thin 8 km plate. The pair rises
2.23× above its own notch (20.5 km, the plateau's edge column one slot away) but only 1.12× above
the highest ground inside the belt's own neighbourhood (40.9 km at the flank). Four samples fail
on that leg (frames 3180–3182, edgeAge 0.00–0.20 Myr, and frame 3312 at edgeAge 4.10 Myr with a
four-column contiguous run); the other three legs have no failing sample, and the worst readings
there are already attained at the immediate shoulder.

Candidates measured over every non-ghost collide sample of all four legs
(`scratch/probe-r2shoulder.js`); a sample fails at `P.beltPeak` 1.5:

| shoulder | 3000 / 1 / 50 | 3000 / 1 / 100 | 5000 / 5 / 50 | 5000 / 5 / 100 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| immediate slot, walked (as written) | 1.35, 0 fail | 1.39, 0 | 1.15, 0 | 2.23, **4** |
| orogenic flow's reach (±`beltFeed`) | 1.33, 0 | 1.39, 0 | 1.15, 0 | 2.15, **3** |
| belt neighbourhood (±(`beltFeed`+2)) | 1.33, 0 | 1.39, 0 | 1.15, 0 | **1.26, 0** |
| growth since collision onset | 1.50, 0 | 1.55, 8 | 2.02, **492** | 1.71, **428** |

- The flow's reach alone is not enough: the 40.9 km ground is at the flank column
  (`j + beltFeed + 1`), so a window that stops inside the flow's reach still reads 2.15.
- Growth since onset is rejected: it needs a per-pair onset record the kernel does not have, and
  it fires on ordinary belt growth (492 samples on one leg) — a collision thickens its own pair
  before its shoulders, which is the mechanism, not a defect.
- The selected window is a superset of the old one read through the same `r2Ground` walk, so it
  can only *raise* the shoulder and *lower* the ratio: it cannot turn a passing sample red. Across
  the four legs' 14,586 collision samples it changes the verdict on exactly the four samples above,
  all at one site.
- The isolated rise the clause exists for stays red: the `m2-check` fixtures pin a narrow pair on
  a plain (2.00×), an isolated pair on a 60 km plateau (1.58×), and the notched margin case above
  (1.12× selected, 2.23× immediate). A pair that is below the ground four columns away is not an
  isolated rise by construction (0.96×).

**Decision (landed).** The needle's shoulder is the highest real ground in the belt's own
neighbourhood: the real columns from `i-1` out to `i-(beltFeed+2)` and from `j+1` out to
`j+(beltFeed+2)`, each slot read as the nearest real column (a draining record holds no ground).
The immediate-slot reading is kept in `check.r2ShapeAt` as `needleImmediateRatio` and reported by
both audits as the legacy comparison field.

## 2. Contracts

1. **R1 — Events stay quiet.** The largest drawn-surface movement within an event's own footprint,
   over the run, is no more than `P.evDzK` times the section's own background,
   `P.evDzK` = 1.25. The **site** is the event's own position: a birth's record, a drained
   death's pre-frame record, and for a conveyor accretion both ends of the merged column (the
   retired record's pre-K4 position and the surviving record's). The **footprint** is that
   position ± 1.5 `w0` on the 2048-point raster: the record's own half-column and the half-span to
   each neighbour's centre, which is the span the interpolated surface draws the record over. The
   **background** is the largest movement on a frame with no topology event at all. Report only,
   never gated: the event-frame background (the largest movement on event frames away from every
   event footprint) and the same-frame pairing (site max against that frame's away max).
   The background is a bar, not a promise: the audit also reports the quiet-frame drawn-thickness
   and width change at the same place (54.3 km and 9.9% on `3000/1/50`, unchanged since 0.1.7,
   at the section's plateau core). Gating those is the next measurement if the background ever
   looks like an artefact, and the report is what makes that visible.
2. **R2 — A collision is a belt, not a needle.** For each non-ghost collide pair `i, j = i+1`,
   `peak = max(hTot[i], hTot[j])` and the shoulder is the real ground at the slots
   `i-1 … i-(beltFeed+2)` and `j+1 … j+(beltFeed+2)`, each slot read as the nearest real column
   (a draining record is stepped over, bounded to `beltFeed + 1` steps). A pair is a needle when
   `peak / shoulder > P.beltPeak` (1.5). The width half is unchanged: on built samples
   (`peak >= outerFlank + P.beltRoot`) the six cells `i-2 … i+3` must carry a contiguous run of at
   least `P.beltCols` (4) in at least 90% of built collision samples. `COL.beltAt` — the belt the
   crustal flow and the plate force read — is unchanged, and so is the drawing.
3. **R3, R5, mass and replay are unchanged**: the dt-aware ceiling, the bucket-spaced event
   repeat gate with its `P.evAge` report (a residual repeat outside `P.evAge` is a documented
   limit of the frame-window gate, not a kernel edit target), stratigraphic order, bed capacity,
   the draining-record share, the per-lithology ledger, the checkpoint hash and the page parity.
4. **No threshold moves.** `P.evDzK`, `P.evGap`, `P.evAge`, `P.beltPeak`, `P.beltCols`,
   `P.beltRise`, `P.beltRoot`, `P.beltFeed`, `P.crustMax` and the 2.5 `w0` bucket keep their
   values; no engine file changes in this release.

## 4. M2 — 0.1.8 archived, and the deferred review findings

- `0.1.8-plan.md` → `archive/0.1.8-plan.md`, with the release list and the cross-references
  updated (`AGENTS.md`, `roadmap.md`, the two comments that name its sections).
- **`COL.removeAt` (js/columns.js).** The recorded finding has two halves. The first — reading and
  writing the module-global `S` instead of a passed state — is **stale**: `COL.removeAt(st, c, k)`
  takes its state like the rest of the column API, and its two callers (`CRU.delaminate` on a
  fully foundered basal bed, `COL.collapseMove` on a fully peeled felsic bed) pass it. The second
  is real and is fixed: the shift now carries the flags the surrounding code carries, exactly as
  `COL.removeClass` does — the bed exposed above the removal is marked `P.FLAG.unconf`, or
  `COL.colBevel` when the removal reached the top of the stack. Both callers are internal shifts
  behind an existing interface, so no caller changes; `m2-check` and `m3-check` cover the flag
  (the m3 unconformity fixture and the delamination ledger).
- **`P.kB2`, `P.kDecay` (js/params.js).** Not dead: they are the design's ore-potential block
  (§4.7, listed with their values in `0.1.0-design.md` §9), and `0.2.0-plan.md` M3 names all of
  them — `kV`, `kM`, `kM2`, `kA`, `kRec`, `kO`, `kB`, `kB2`, `kDecay` — as the accumulation rates
  of the `js/ore.js` kernel that release lands. Only `P.kA`, `P.kRec` and `P.kB` have readers
  today (`js/crust.js`, `js/surface.js`). The block gets a header comment naming the owner, so the
  file stops implying the column engine reads it.

## 6. Deferred

- **R5's frame-window repeated sites.** 0.1.8 §2 decided this: the residual repeats sit outside the
  kernel's own `P.evAge` memory (0 of 211 inside it on `5000/5/100`), so the frame-window gate is
  at its documented limit for 100 kyr/frame legs and the audit reports both counts. Not a kernel
  edit target; do not widen `P.evGap`, `P.evAge` or the bucket.
- **The real-browser pass of the particle page** (0.1.8 M3's owed item).
- **The quiet-frame drawn-thickness and width change** (54.3 km / 9.9% on `3000/1/50`, at the
  same place as the run's largest quiet-frame move). It is reported, not gated, and it is what
  R1's background rests on: a future plan that gates it needs a measured threshold first, and
  this release deliberately introduces no constant.
- **Whether a belt's force term should read same-plate or same-crust ground.** R2's clause now
  measures the profile, which was the question it can answer; the *force* term (`COL.beltAt`'s
  `beltW`) is unchanged and remains the physics definition of a belt.

## 7. Out of scope

No eruptive kernels or deposits (owned by `0.2.0-plan.md`), no 3D force model, no absolute
convergence-speed clamp, no change to the shared slice format or the checkpoint schema, no
one-sided change to `port/`, and no threshold relaxation.
