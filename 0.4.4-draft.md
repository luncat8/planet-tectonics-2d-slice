# 0.4.4 — Draft: the road to the globe is negotiation, not measurement

Status: the direction that closed 0.4.3, kept here as the reference of what was asked and in
which words. The plans it asks for are `0.4.4-plan.md` (the C-return/C-live agreement) and
`0.9.0-plan.md` (C1 namespacing plus the globe-side adapter). Worklog:
`archive/0.4.4-agreement-worklog.md`.

## What was said

> What's left on the road to the globe is negotiation, not measurement: agree C-return/C-live
> with the globe, then C1 namespacing plus the globe-side adapter for 0.9 — each with its own
> plan written against the globe's then-current commit.

## What it means, restated

1. **The measurement phase is over.** 0.4.3 closed the one local block on the live bridge
   (C-live gate 4, the automatic plate-topology rebuild). The globe's 0.8 line is complete as
   shipped (`0.8.3` §5), its gate table names everything that remains, and nothing left is a
   question this repository can answer alone.
2. **The first negotiation is C-return/C-live.** Both sides hold (2026-10-16, mirrored), and
   both holds name their own re-open trigger: "a scheduled import (C-return) or a plan for
   gate 4 (C-live)". Gate 4 is closed, so the trigger is the schedule. `0.4.4-plan.md` is that
   schedule — the slice-side decision to open the return channel as the globe's `0.8.3` §2.1/§2.2
   already specified it — plus the pair-level gate definitions for C-live 1/2/3/6 and the
   review that gate 7 asks for. It proposes; it does not implement. No shared byte moves until
   the globe records a mirrored decision.
3. **The second negotiation is C1 plus the adapter.** `0.9.0-plan.md` covers the namespacing
   pass (`0.4.1-plan.md` §8.6) and the globe-side adapter request. Written against the same
   globe commit, because the adapter is their plan and their release number to choose
   (their 0.9.0 is already taken — climate integration — and its grid audit has started).
4. **"Written against the globe's then-current commit"** — both plans pin the revision they
   were written from (`3c960e7d924a6ca61b9609bd3c865988ce522ddd`, the globe's `main` tip on
   2026-10-18, unchanged since its 0.8.3 audit), and each must be re-pinned against the tip
   current when its implementation starts.

## What this draft does not decide

- anything the globe must accept: every protocol choice in `0.4.4-plan.md` is marked proposed,
  and the globe's answer is a decision record in its own repository, not a review here;
- the adapter's shape on the globe's side: `0.9.0-plan.md` specifies what this side promises
  (the host contract) and what this side requests; the adapter itself is their plan;
- whether C4 stays comparison-only: that is gate 7's review, scheduled by `0.4.4-plan.md`,
  held by neither repository alone.
