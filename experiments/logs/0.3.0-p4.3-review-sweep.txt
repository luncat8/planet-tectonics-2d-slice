DECISION: runoff/stream power ships: E = kStream*sqrt(A)*slope, capped at eroMax,
  each column settling at the end of its own flow path (js/pt/erode.js). The hillslope
  rule loses D3 and D4 by construction -- every transfer is one hop, so material creeps
  along the profile instead of following the drainage the profile defines -- and D5 by
  measurement: at this model's gradients a diffusive law circulates material between
  neighbours and only its divergence changes the crust, so its net redistribution is a
  small fraction of the stream-power rule's.
