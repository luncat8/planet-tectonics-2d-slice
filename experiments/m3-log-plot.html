Source: experiments/logs/0.3.2-M3-* from commit dc465f4, reduced by experiments/m3-log-plot.js into logs/m3-plot-data.json. The page needs an http origin for fetch (this is a test task): from the repo root run python3 -m http.server 8000 and open http://localhost:8000/experiments/m3-log-plot.html.

1 · inventory — 44 files, 11 trajectories

One row per cell (a fingerprint). Segments are its files, sized by bytes: kept (the richest json of the cell — watch data where the cell was watched), byte-identical twin (the harness writes one file; the worklog simply re-ran each command under a second name), re-run (same trajectory, a different purpose: the serial clock read, the assert, the composition record, the pre-fix observer). No harness reads any of these files — they are evidence, and the .txt halves are evidence of nothing.

2 · frame cost per cell, against the 20 ms gate

msFrame of the serial timed re-run where the worklog made one, else the batch run. The five 600 Myr seed-5 cells all sit under the gate (the serial reads the worklog quotes: 15.78 / 15.00 / 14.21 / 13.97 / 14.46 ms); the seed-2 mobile-melt cells at 100 and 500 kyr do not — 28.1 and 139.7 ms/frame, the coarse clock is what costs.

3 · the cold tail — the global mantle minimum over time

Each break is a new all-run minimum of T (the --watch observer). The plain baseline bottoms at −0.489 by frame 7827; the sub-grid row's minimum arrives late — frame 10927 of 12000 — but shallower at −0.419. The water-stripping probes go far colder still (−1.069 at dpart 0.01, −1.137 with the budget on top), which is why they are probes and not shipping changes: their temperature gate is red. The seed-2 500 kyr cell finds its minimum in the first 5 Myr; the 50 kyr cell not until frame 5534.

4 · per-depth-band temperature extrema

The band table of one --watch cell: minimum T per phase per depth band. The melt phase never gets cold (its job is the opposite), the deposit phase only exists near the surface, air only in the top band.

5 · the mobile-melt face — parcels above T = 1.5

The excursion observer's samples for the seed-2 cells: every frame a melt parcel is above the 1.5 bound, dots; the excursions themselves, bars at their entry temperature. All three crossings happen while the parcel rises (210→81, 186→87, 314→299 km deep) with an entry gap near −1.4 — the transit carries the excess, not the gather. The seed-5 600 Myr cells never cross.

6 · G5 transfer events — where melt re-routed

Every G5 candidate event of one cell, by position (km across the 16 000 km map) and time, coloured by the routing margin field − solidus — the parcel's head above the solidus it must beat. Water depletion (--dpart) collapses the count 8 559 → 75 and shifts the whole population to hotter margins; the mass budget (--cap) leaves the count near the control's but delivers only 50 of its 6 604 candidates.

7 · the routing margin population

Histogram of field − solidus over all G5 candidates of the sub-grid cells (wet margins; every candidate clears the solidus it is routed on). The composition re-run asked the dry question separately: strip the water outright and 4 509 of 8 559 candidates (52.7%) still sit above the fully dry solidus — drying alone closes less than half of the row.
