# 0.2.0 — Plan: eruptives, deposits, save/load (the rest of 0.1.0)

The remainder of the 0.1.0 feature set, re-planned as the 0.2.x series. Physics source:
`0.1.0-design.md` §1.5 (toy eruptive scale), §4.7 (ores), §5 (eruptive box, write-back),
§6 (ledger, save/load) and the vendored reference design §7.3–§8. Feature milestones are
0.2.0-M1..M4; the acceptance pass closes the series. `0.1.0-plan.md` (archived) held the
same content as M5/M6 and stays the historical record.

Ordering is not arbitrary: 0.1.5 lands first, and both of its results are inputs here.
Eruptions write layers back into a column's stack, and 0.1.5 is what makes a stack
ordered and continuous; deposits are placed by depth inside a stack and by potential
fields (`oArc`, `oOro`, `oBas`, `oVms`) that 0.1.5 redefines for the belt and the
trench slivers.

## 0. Conventions

Unchanged from `0.1.0-plan.md` §0: one unit system, headless kernels, fixed-capacity
state in `state.js`, tunables in `params.js`, tabs, no allocation in the frame loop,
every phase ends with a page that draws something new and with its harness green.

The second clock is the only seconds quantity: `SIM.tErupt` (s) advances by the
`Lava & eruptions` slider, `SIM.dG` (Myr) by the `Plates & plumes` slider. Kernels that
serve the eruptive clock take seconds; everything else stays in Myr. Convert once, at
the kernel boundary (`magma.js`), never inside a rate constant.

**The surface budget of 0.1.5 R1 applies to the tectonic clock only.** The eruptive
clock is *supposed* to move the surface fast (that is what makes an eruption watchable);
what it must respect is the ledger, the `layerCap` discipline and `COL.insertVol`
ordering, and it may only ever write into the vent's own column (plus its one-hop
routing target).

## 1. Milestones

### 0.2.0-M0 — The toy box, headless (2 d)

`js/erupt.js`: a per-vent `ventBoxW x ventBoxH` (48 x 32) cellular pile.

- Cells hold height, lithology and temperature in the fixed arrays
  (`toyH`, `toyLi`, `toyT`, `state.js` already allocates them at `maxVents`
  boxes). One box is `P.ventBoxW` wide; the box's world width is the vent's cone
  footprint, chosen so a stratocone reads 10–30 px at the default window and 17 px
  wide at `overview` (design §1.4/§1.5).
- Per step: material arrives from the vent's chamber as a flux, is placed at the
  conduit, slumps when the local slope exceeds the angle of repose (`P.repose`, slough
  to the lower neighbours), freezes below `P.Tsol` into `tephra` or `lava` by style.
- Particles: preallocated ballistic + buoyant sets per vent (`prX/prY/prVX/prVY/prT/prL`),
  advanced on the eruptive clock, retired on landing, never allocated.
- `erupt.js` is DOM-free and takes `(state, dtSec, vent)`; the box is a pure function of
  its state, so the bench can step it without a page.

Done: `experiments/erupt-bench.js` steps >= 60 toy steps/s with 16 vents; a prescribed
flux builds a cone whose width and height match the design §1.5 numbers within 10%;
the pile is mass-exact (the integral of the box equals what the chamber supplied minus
what froze into layers); sloughing only ever moves material downhill.

### 0.2.0-M1 — Vent lifecycle and the second clock (2 d)

`js/magma.js` on the eruptive clock (K7 slot, empty since M0):

- A vent is born where a chamber is full (`Vch` 5 km3), dies after `tauVent` (2 Myr) of
  an empty chamber, and never exceeds `maxVents` (16): the chamber's own cap
  (`chamberCap`) and the vent cap are independent, and a full vent list must idle the
  magma rather than drop it (`vedIdle`-style accounting, ledgered).
- Eruption style from gas fraction and chamber pressure: effusive below `gasBlast`,
  explosive above it, with the design §5.2 thresholds; the schedule emits mass per
  frame, which is what the toy box consumes.
- Vent placement is by supply: arc columns (`trenchDist` 1..3 and `colRecycle > 0`) get
  arc-style volcanic fronts, LIP/plume columns get shield/fissure styles, and a vent
  keeps its column until it dies or the column is consumed. With 0.1.5 in place the
  arc columns are the trench slivers and the belt flanks, which is where they belong.

Done: a prescribed full chamber starts and stops an eruption with the mass ledger
exact; an empty chamber idles the toy; a full chamber under a fast tectonic clock
builds sills instead of vents (the existing `MAG.spill` path) and the two clocks stop
fighting (measured: no frame where a vent gains mass it was not given).

### 0.2.0-M2 — Write-back and the visible edifice (2 d)

- On freeze (effusive) or on landing (explosive), the toy box hands mass to the stack
  as a `lava` or `tephra` layer on the vent's column through `COL.insertVol`, and
  records the edifice in the vent record (style, volume, last-eruption time).
- `js/render.js`: the cone is drawn as a fill clipped to the profile, on top of the
  column's own stack, from the box itself (not a second copy of it); eruption FX
  (the plume, the ballistic particles) draw from the particle buffers in the overlay.
- The column's `z`, `hTot` and Moho may only move by the mass that was actually
  transferred; the probe reads the new `tephra`/`lava` beds like any other bed.

Done: one effusive and one explosive run each build a 10–30 px edifice at the default
window with conserved mass; the probe shows the `tephra`/`lava` beds with their ages;
`raster-bench.js` stays inside the 6 ms body budget with 16 active vents.

### 0.2.0-M3 — Deposits and extraction (2–3 d)

`js/ore.js` (design §4.7): depth-resolved deposits on top of the existing potentials.

- A deposit is `(column, layer slot, class, grade)` — `state.js` already has
  `depCol/depLay/depCls/depGr` with `depCap` 4096, and `COL.removeTop`, `COL.removeAt`,
  `COL.compact` and `COL.moveDeposits` already keep the slot valid.
- Potentials: `oVms` on buried oceanic horizons, `oArc` under arc columns, `oOro`
  inside the 0.1.5 belt, `oBas` in wet sediment, `oPla` riding the mobile load.
  Accumulate (`kV`, `kM`, `kM2`, `kA`, `kB`, `kB2`, decay `kDecay`) and instantiate a
  deposit when the potential crosses the class threshold.
- Overlay symbols + a ranked list by (class, tonnage) behind a key; extraction removes
  mass from the deposit's bed and books it as `ledCons`.

Done: > 60% of `oArc` deposit mass sits under edifice/arc columns; placer downslope of
its source; VMS on buried seafloor horizons; a deposit that erodes away disappears
instead of pointing at a wrong bed.

### 0.2.0-M4 — Save/load and diagnostics (2 d)

- `state.js` JSON save/load over every typed array and scalar, `ui.js` buttons and a
  seed entry field; a saved state must resume bit-identically (`S.hash()` before and
  after 1000 frames).
- `sim.js` diagnostic HUD: invariants every 2 Hz (sorted, widths, plate counts, per-lith
  ledger including `ledDelam`, min gap >= `gFloor * w0`), plus fps, sim ms and the toy
  step cost.

Done: save -> reload -> 1000 frames reproduces the live run bitwise, at two different
zoom levels and with an active eruption at save time.

### 0.2.0-M5 — Acceptance and tuning (2 d)

## 2. Acceptance (whole series)

1. Eruptions: one effusive and one explosive run each build a 10–30 px edifice at the
   default window with conserved mass; write-back layers read back through the probe;
   empty chamber idles the toy, over-full chamber builds sills (ledger).
2. Two clocks: the tectonic clock at 10 kyr/frame next to the eruptive clock at 30 min/
   frame produces a visible eruption without the crust moving under it; the plates
   clock at 100 kyr/frame does not teleport an eruption.
3. Deposits: > 60% of `oArc` mass under edifice/arc columns; placer downslope of its
   source in the probe; VMS on buried seafloor horizons.
4. Save/load: bit-identical resume; the diagnostic HUD invariants hold at 2 Hz for a
   500 Myr run.
5. Regression: every 0.1.5 harness stays green (`contact-audit --strict`, `m2-check`,
   `m3-check`, `ledger-check`, `raster-bench`, `smoke`, `scale-check`, `view-check`,
   `section-check`), and the body raster stays inside 6 ms.
6. Determinism: same seed + same sliders => bitwise-identical 1000-frame hash.
7. Stability: 500 Myr at 10 kyr/frame and at 100 kyr/frame with finite state and per
   lithology ledger drift < 0.1% (excluding recorded sources).
