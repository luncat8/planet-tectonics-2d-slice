# 0.3.0 P3.3 — the two carried decisions

The P3.3 report left two decisions open. This worklog records what was taken, on the evidence.

## 1. The frame-time gate: re-based the estimator, not the bar

The P3.3 tree's first serial run failed `pt-check`'s budget check at 16.4 ms against the 16 ms
bar; a rerun read 13.7 ms. Two runs of the same code on this host read 7.0 ms and 6.9 ms, so the
gate was a coin flip on the host's load rather than a measurement of the frame.

Cause: the gate averaged one 400-frame window. A single window is one sample of the host's
scheduling, and a slow one (a GC, a neighbour) lands fully inside the mean.

Fix (`experiments/pt-check.js`, the budget gate only): warm up 100 frames, then take the best of
four 100-frame windows, the same discipline `pt-p3-budget.js`'s `frameMs` already uses and
`m2-check.js` calls "min of 7x20". The bar stays at 16 ms. Readings after the change: 7.0, 6.7,
7.2 ms. The interleaved A/B (`pt-p3-frame-ab.js`, 8 pairs) stays the gate on what a milestone
costs, since a real regression appears in the best window too.

Re-basing the bar to 18 ms was rejected: it would be the second re-base in two milestones, it
would loosen the plan's §7 intent further with no new information, and the cause was the
estimator, not the engine.

## 2. The shipped run's roof: still P3.5, with the direction written down

Not touched, and the deferral is correct: P3.4's flight is what consumes vent stock, so tuning
`chSpan` before it exists buys vents that fill and block, not eruptions.

The report's option list had the direction wrong. The head is `chM / chSpan`, so the head rises
when the footprint *narrows*: `chSpan* = chM / threshold = 206 km`, not a larger span. Fixed in
`archive/0.3.0-p3.3-report.md` §8 and §13.

Two things recorded for P3.5 in the report:

- whichever param is chosen, the drain rates and `vCap` decide how long an eruption runs before
  the schedule saturates — at the `chSpan 100 km` fixture all 27 vents end full and blocked;
- the deferral has a deadline, not just an owner: with no conduit open in the shipped run,
  `pt-p3-check.js` (P3.5) cannot gate an airborne population, so the tuning must land with or
  before P3.5.

A report-only sweep of the candidates (chSpan / yCham / tensile, with their drain-time and
block-time consequences) belongs with the P3.4 worklog, so the P3.5 choice is a table read.
