## M2 review and the continuity cleanup that M3 exposed

Before adding the crust, we reviewed M2's liquid and `js/sp/sp.js` and found two issues,
one of which became critical as soon as M3's mass transactions touched cell mass `M.m[k]`:

1. **Exact continuity + barotropic gauge cleanup at the end of `project(dt)`.** In M2, every
   cell had a fixed time-invariant mass `m0[k]`, so the ~0.9%–4.9% divergence residual left
   by 20 red-black Gauss-Seidel projection sweeps only compressed `H` slightly over a 50 kyr
   substep. In M3, H5 transactions (`accreteFromCell`, `launchClast`, `unload`) allow shallow
   cells (`j = 0, 1`) to carry variable liquid mass `m[k] != m0[k]`. Because flux-form
   `advectTo` updates specific enthalpy via `H'[k] = (m[k] H[k] - dt div(F_H)) / m[k]`, a
   non-zero velocity divergence `div(m_0 u)` in a mass-depleted cell (`m[k] = 0.55 m0[k]`)
   amplifies `H[k]` by `1 - dt div(m_0 u) / m[k]` every substep — blowing up at upwelling
   stagnation points under `clock = 500`. Closing the barotropic column flux on `u`
   (`u[j,i] -= (colU[i] - uBar) / depth`) and integrating `v` downward from `v[0,i] = 0` by
   exact finite-volume continuity at the end of `project(dt)` is a single `O(nx · ny)` pass
   that reduces discrete cell divergence `d.div` from `4.9e-2` to **`1.7e-14 .. 3.5e-14`**
   (machine roundoff) while preserving the RBGS rotational flow pattern (`peakV = 51.96 km/Myr`,
   3 upwellings on S1).
2. **`SP.reset` and `SP.applyPreset`.** `SP.reset` had omitted `this.opts.commit()`, and
   `SP.applyPreset` had only set `clock` without applying `preset.options`, `camera`, or
   `tool`. Both now match `HY`.

## The M3 crust, the two books, and the scenario measurements

1. **Two-book conservation (`hy-ledger.js`).** At `reset(seed)`, the `wrap × depth` box
   (`4.640000e7 km²`) is partitioned between `1,536` crust parcels (`3` rows across `256`
   columns in `j = 0, 1`, holding ~65% of rows `0..1`) and `6,144` mantle cells. Every H5
   and H6 transfer debits one book and credits the other with the identical floating-point
   quantity (`dm`, `dm · H`, `dm · cF`, `dm · cW`):
   - Over a 300 Myr integrated leg (`6,000` frames at `50 kyr/frame`), the 80,583
     transactions (`424` whole-parcel subduction assimilations, `108` partial-melt sheds,
     `79,920` basal freeze accretions, `68` vent emissions, `68` clast landings, `63` deposit
     welds) close total mass to `max |dM|/M0 = 1.61e-16` and total enthalpy against wall
     flux to `max |dH - wallNet|/wallGross = 5.41e-12`, with `0` capacity deferrals and a
     steady population of `1,846` parcels.
2. **S2 melting and the skin composition (`hy-crust.js`).** In `js/pc/params.js`, volatile
   solidus depression has weight `meltWW = 20` and felsic depression has `meltWF = 0.2`.
   Calibrating the initial crust skin to `cF = 0.15, cW = 0.01` gives a `0.23` solidus
   depression: cold surface skin sits at `T ~ 0.02..0.15` well below its surface solidus
   (`~0.55`, above `TLock = 0.35`), while subducting parcels entering the `100–180 km`
   (`j = 4, 5`) window heat rapidly against the mantle (`tau_sub = 0.5 Myr`), cross their
   local solidus (`~0.60`), shed partial melt (`shedMeltParcel`), and assimilate into the
   mantle (`absorbParcel`) below `180 km`. On S2 (`100 Myr`), peak melt in the `40–220 km`
   window is `0.5233`, max melt outside that window is `0.00e+0`, and the relative
   equilibrium manifold gap across all cells and parcels is `0.00e+0`.
3. **Bonds, plates, and two-pass `bondWalk` (`hy-crust.js`).** Evaluating pair strain rate
   inside the same loop that mutates parcel velocities with elastic spring forces feeds
   spring corrective velocities into subsequent pair `load` evaluations, shattering bonds.
   Splitting `bondWalk` into a read-only kinematic strain pass (accumulating `pLoad` and
   `pCnt` from pure flow + buoyancy velocities) followed by `PCBOND.damage`/`settle` and an
   elastic spring force pass produces `2,346` active bonds across `7` coherent plates (`max
   plate = 735` parcels) under `links: true` vs `0` bonds and `1,540` unbonded welded parcels
   under `links: false`.
4. **S3 eruption, wall-clock flight, and relief (`hy-erupt.js`).** Overpressured shallow
   melt columns (`y <= 220 km`) beneath thinned or damaged roof skin (`roofCap <= 0.62`,
   recharge cooldown `4 Myr`) schedule vent episodes (`68` episodes over 300 Myr on S3),
   emitting `PH.air` clasts that fly under `gFlight` with `flightDtMax = 0.05 s` (including
   when geologically paused, landing a 60 Hz wall-clock volley in `72` frames / `1.20 s`),
   land as `PH.dep`, cool against `wallTop` (`heat residual/stock0 = 1.15e-16`), and weld
   into `PH.crust`. The S3 chain `airMass - depositMass - weldMass` closes to `0.00e+0` at
   300 Myr (`max |chainGap| = 2.27e-13` across all 61 samples), with column relief `zh`
   spanning `-4.06 km` (trenches) to `+61.40 km` (volcanic edifices and thickened crust).
5. **S4 unpressurize and the `solidusOf: 'depth'` control (`hy-crust.js`, `hy-ui.js`).**
   Excavating the surface crust patch (`HY.unload(x, halfW)`, automatically triggered at
   `t = 0.05 Myr` in S4 and armed on canvas click when `HY.tool === 'unload'`) drops the
   underlying column's overburden load and lowers its solidus by `0.0222`, generating an
   immediate decompression melt pulse (`1,580 km²` at `0.05 Myr`, decaying to `875 km²` at
   `20 Myr` as surrounding shallow mass re-equilibrates). Running the identical S4 leg with
   `solidusOf: 'depth'` produces a `0.00e+0` pulse peak.
6. **Load-back stability fixture (`hy-crust.js`) and frame budget (`hy-bench.js`).**
   `HY.loadBack` (coupling column overburden anomalies back into the shallow horizontal flow)
   was tested at `0` (one-way default), `0.05`, `0.15`, and `0.30` over 200 frames: all four
   legs remain bounded (`tMin = 0.014, tMax = 0.999`, `peakV = 53.7..56.5 km/Myr`, discrete
   divergence `~3e-14`, mass closure `<= 1.6e-16`). At the `256x24` default rung, the
   integrated M3 step takes **2.98 ms/frame** (`1.27 ms` flow, `0.30 ms` transport, `1.15 ms`
   crust + transactions + vents); at `512x48`, it takes **9.60 ms/frame**.
