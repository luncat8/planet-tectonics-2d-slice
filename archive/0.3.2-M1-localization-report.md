# 0.3.2 M1 — the latent-book failure localized; the bounded redistribution measured and rejected

**Decision:** the extended-cell P3.1 latent identity failure is fully localized on the
unchanged banked cell: it is the unbooked equilibrium snap of airborne material that
`FLIGHT.launch` creates off-manifold, and nothing else — the attributed events close
against the drift to 2.0e-8 of 167.41 km²·H. The node-wise conservative bounded
redistribution (`spread`) was then measured against the same bank and is **rejected**:
it preserves the wall integral exactly as designed, but local absorption capacity is the
binding constraint — roughly half of all bounded placements were infeasible, and the
probe's own giveback leaves the cold tail worse than the baseline's. No production code
changed, no constant frozen, 0.3.1 condition 5 intact, nothing selected.

## Part 1 — the latent identity, localized

The metric is `resid = hLedger − ledger − meltLat·(invLTot − invL0)`. The trace evaluates
it after every kernel boundary of every frame (a naive latent sum in the inventory's own
order; ~1e-8 noise floor against steps of 1e-3..1e2), attributes each boundary's delta,
decomposes the equilibrium pass per phase, and records every vent launch individually.

The chain telescopes exactly (drift −167.412985247 against chain sum −167.412985247), the
residual starts at bitwise zero on the reset planet, and the drift decomposes as
**launch snaps −167.412985 + in-flight snaps 0.000000, gap 2.0e-8**. Every booking path
the engine owns — conduction, the phase pass, arc dehydration, flight cooling, erosion
transport, intrusion, recycling, landing — closes at 600 Myr scale to under 4e-7
cumulative. The identity fails in exactly one place.

### The mechanism, event by event

`FLIGHT.launch` gives an air parcel its temperature from the equilibrium reading of the
batch's specific enthalpy (`byH` at the surface) but its melt fraction from the vent
stock's *aggregate* (`vL/vM`), which was collected from intrusions at chamber depth and
composition. When that stock is mushy, the pair `(T, melt)` is off-manifold. Air is the
one phase no ledger books — `PHASE.equil` maps the *whole pool* back onto the manifold
every substep but books the sensible change of grid-coupled phases only — so the next
equilibrium pass snaps the clast's melt fraction to `xiEq` and steps `invLTot` by
`m·(xiEq − xiStock)` with nothing against it: `Δresid = −L·m·(xiEq − xiStock)`.

Measured on the banked cell:

- **203,592 launches** (970,100.6 km² through the vents); **34,826 left off-manifold**,
  of which **8,916 significantly** (the rest are round-off-level, xiStock
  0.9999999999999999); snapped mass 175,806.5 km² (44,009.5 km² significant).
- Every significant snap goes the same way: **xiEq = 1** against **xiStock
  0.901..0.999** — the surface solidus (0.77 less composition terms) sits far below the
  chamber-depth solidus, so the batch's enthalpy always reads fully molten at the vent.
  The steps are all negative: predicted sum **−167.419050**.
- Measured first snaps: **−167.412985**. The 6.065e-3 difference is exactly the five
  final-frame launches (frame 12,000) whose equilibrium re-read never followed — the
  prediction and the measurement agree event-for-event everywhere else.
- Onset: **frame 6919 (345.95 Myr), vent 11**, xiStock 0.993036, a 15.7 km² batch, step
  −0.02735 — after 6,918 frames and ~19,700 launches at bitwise-zero drift. Largest
  single events: frames 10942–10946, vent 3, 39.7–43.9 km² batches at xiStock 0.9415,
  steps −0.58..−0.64 each. 39 vents contribute; the drift then grows every frame to the
  banked worst at 11992 and 167.41299 km²·H at the end (9.6020e-3 relative).
- In-flight re-reads (a clast's own depth moving its solidus between launch and landing)
  contributed **exactly 0** on this cell: above the surface the solidus is flat, and no
  clast spent a re-read below sea level.

### Why the owners stayed green

`pt-p4-check.js` gates this identity at the end of its seed-1 300 Myr leg, and that leg is
not eruption-free: it ran **75,979 launches** (287,998.5 km² to air, fingerprint
6271680363413605 reproducing the banked census) with an end residual of **4.35e-9 km²·H,
1.09e-13 relative** — every one of its batches left the vent on-manifold inside that
horizon. The failure needs a *mushy chamber stock reaching a vent*: the seed-5 cell first
produces one at 346 Myr, past both owners' horizons. This is the second 0.3.x defect
whose only visible instance lives beyond the owner cells (the first was the deposit route
itself, silent at 300 Myr and live at 600); the extended cell is what found it, and the
M2 clock/seed matrix is what will have to hold it fixed.

### What a fix must respect

This is a booking asymmetry, not round-off and not the adjoint gather: decompression
melting on the way to the surface is physically real — the batch's enthalpy genuinely
reads fully molten at the vent — but no book follows the air phase, while `invLTot` spans
the whole pool plus reservoirs. The candidate families (to be specified and measured, not
shipped from this report): make the launch on-manifold and stock-consistent in one
operation; scope the identity's latent term to the grid-coupled set plus reservoirs, with
the airborne transient measured as an explicit, bounded exclusion; or book the air snap
where it happens. Whichever is chosen: the wall ledger stays untouched (M0's rule), the
gate becomes per-frame on the extended cell rather than end-of-leg on the short ones, and
any parcel-field change re-banks every state-dependent fingerprint (plan M2).

## Part 2 — node-wise conservative bounded redistribution (`spread`), rejected

The probe the plan's M1 names: inside the adjoint gather, bound each parcel's predicted
equilibrium temperature at the gate's own bounds (1.5 upper, −0.5 lower); the withheld
extensive increment goes to the same nearest-node bucket's members in proportion to the
headroom each still has under the same bound; per-node totals are preserved, so intake and
wall see the baseline's own delivery. Where the bucket cannot absorb, the remainder
returns to the capped parcels that raised it — never more than each one's own withholding —
and the event is counted and integrated. The bound is read at pre-advection depth; the
frame-end gate reads after advection.

| ms/frame (this host) | 10.92 | 11.51 | pass (< 20) |

| Out-of-bound samples (dep) | 2,856 | **3,118** | **fail** |
| Capped deliveries / absorptions | — | 4,451 / 3,222 | |
| Infeasible givebacks / energy returned | — | **3,118 / 4,268.96 km²·H** | |
| Latent identity (mechanism orthogonal) | 9.665e-3 | 2.843e-3 | fail (both) |
| Held-back census | 30,906 / 68,140.1 km²·Myr | 39,578 / 69,179.8 km²·Myr | report |

| Exact baseline-event matches | 3 | **0** | no survival evidence |

The conservation design worked: the wall integral is not merely preserved but sits at the
baseline's own scale (the small difference is trajectory, not delivery), the mass and
enthalpy books close identically (0 and 5.3e-12), and the hot tail shrank (2.267 → 2.046).
What failed is **feasibility**: 3,118 of the bounded placements found no local capacity at
all — the out-of-bound sample count equals the giveback count exactly, i.e. every
frame-end violation of this trajectory is an infeasible giveback whose parcel took its
full uncapped delivery back. A nearest-node bucket holds about four parcels; a light
parcel's amplified increment (gains to 11.9 on this cell) routinely exceeds what three
neighbours can absorb inside the bounds, and the probe's own success at the hot tail makes
the cold side worse — with the hot deposits held down, the same node's cold deliveries
find no headroom either, and one deposit was flung to T −2.441 where the baseline's
coldest is −0.791.

The probe also moved the trajectory like every other candidate: the single routed transfer
is a whole-parcel event at T 1.4837 — the cap bound itself produced the fully molten
reading — with zero exact matches to the three protected events. As always, no count
selects anything; this is reported, not tuned against.

### The first cut's pathology, kept as evidence

The first implementation divided the giveback by the bucket's *net* withheld excess. A
mixed-sign bucket near cancellation would then have amplified one parcel's giveback
without bound; that cut ran to completion and produced a deposit at T −2.44
(`0.3.2-tail-spread15-v1.json`). The second cut places the hot and cold sides separately
and caps every giveback at the parcel's own withholding. The two cuts ran **bitwise
identical** on this cell (same fingerprint): no mixed-sign bucket ever occurred, so the
−2.44 was the honest output of feasible-set exhaustion, not of the amplification bug — the
fix is kept as implementation hygiene, and the rejection stands on the measured
infeasibility. A rejected probe is an outcome; a silently repaired probe would have been a
misdiagnosis.

### What this says about the family

Bounding the distribution while conserving the integral does not remedy the tail at node
scale, because the quantity the tail violates is *local heat capacity*, not local energy:
the amplified increment is bigger than its neighbourhood can hold inside the bounds. The
remaining directions the plan's M1 leaves open are a wider feasible set (stencil-scale
rather than nearest-node placement, which weakens the per-node conservation unit to a
per-stencil one) or a different family entirely (a relaxation whose correction is booked
against the wall ledger honestly instead of hidden in the intake). Both should be measured
against a tree whose latent identity is green first, so the two defects do not mask each
other.
