# 0.3.0 P3.2 worklog — melt segregation and intrusive storage

Gates: `experiments/pt-p3-melt.js` (the milestone), the re-based suite of P3.1 (§9 table in
`0.3.0-p3.1-report.md`), and `experiments/pt-p3-budget.js`. Logs for this milestone:
`experiments/logs/0.3.0-p3.2-*.txt`.

Review of the P3.1 commit (the "improve if need" part) found two defects and both were fixed
first: the phase-law comment in `params.js` named `meltSup` where `phase.js` evaluates the
derived `meltDeep` (the constant that makes the third anchor bitwise), and `pt-p3-budget`'s
frame gate was still the bare P3.0 absolute bar (`BUDGET = 14` ms) where `pt-check` had been
re-based in P3.1. The budget gate is now the same shape as `pt-check`'s: a host-carried bar
(16 ms for the reserve-filled rung) plus the relative half that pins the filled reserve's
marginal price (< 1.4x an empty pool at the same rung, the sweep's 1.3x plus window noise).
Nothing in the P3.1 code itself needed changing: `byT`/`byH` re-verified as exact inverses,
both ledgers close, the suite green.

## Deviations

- **G5 is one kernel with three rules, not one module per rule.** `js/pt/melt.js` (one
  global, `PTMELT`) owns detach/transit/store and nothing else; the two transactions it
  drives live in `pool.js` next to the other ones (`separate`, `intrude`). The flow
  interpolant `gatherVel` owned is now `grid.js` `G.velAt`, shared by the mantle advection
  and the melt's rise — one interpolant, one owner, the way the phase law owns one curve.
- **The split criterion is one threshold used both ways** (`meltHold`): PH_SOLID source rock
  sheds at `xi >= meltHold`, transit melt freezes back to the mantle below `xi < meltHold`,
  and a wholly molten parcel is one whole-parcel phase change (with its bond state zeroed —
  non-solid carries none). The symmetric threshold is what makes the pair a fixed point; an
  asymmetric pair would ping-pong a parcel between the phases.
- **The intrusive store is inventory.** Chamber mass, enthalpy, melt and composition sum
  into the totals both inventory passes report (`invChM`/`invChL` split out for the HUD), so
  an intrusion is a transfer, not a leak. The P3.1 latent identity survives the whole
  pipeline only because `invLTot` counts the chamber melt: the burst moved half the planet's
  melt stock into chambers over 10 Myr, and an identity that tracked parcels alone would
  have read that as a latent release of `meltLat * 0.5%` of the planet.
- **Refusal accounting is one event.** `admit` counts a capacity refusal itself; `separate`
  briefly counted it a second time (caught by `pt-p3-melt` section 3). `split` never
  double-counted; the three now agree.
- **The P3.0 proportional split stays as it is** and is *not* the segregation split. It is
  the shared-intensive-state primitive whose inverse is `merge` (P3.4's round-trip). The
  equilibrium split (`separate`) is a different transaction: its children land exactly on the
  phase law's solidus/liquidus and are bitwise-quiet under the next equilibrium pass, which
  is precisely why the P3.1 identity (`hLedger - ledger = meltLat * (invLTot - invL0)`)
  survives it. A proportional split's children are off-manifold; the next pass would re-read
  them, book a temperature change, and break the identity. This is the "split B" of the
  P3.1 report's question, chosen for its bookkeeping, not its look.

## Pitfalls spotted

- **A fixture that runs live frames owns the live pipeline.** Every synthetic pool script
  that measured "before, do transactions, after" broke in the same way once G5 left melt
  children in flight: a drawn slot could be melt-phase (an inverse `merge` is refused across
  phases), counters read the run's own splits, and "the pool is at its starting count" no
  longer meant `partBase`. The fix class is deltas and pinned inputs, not stubbing G5 out of
  the fixtures that test the live contracts.
- **`remove` compacts the tail into the hole.** `melt.js`'s transit walk re-visits slot `p`
  after a removal; any fixture driving `intrude`/`remove` by slot must process the tail
  first or reload its indices. `pt-p3-melt` section 6 says so at the top.
- **Timing bars are idle-host numbers.** Capturing the suite with five parallel node
  processes re-redd both budget gates (31-33 ms against a 16 ms bar; serial: 14.2/12.6 ms).
  The budget gate class must be captured serially; the worklog records which runs were not.
- **The field-temperature bar moved with the physics, not the code.** `pt-stability`'s
  `T < 1.10` was a P3.1-world bound: mush parcels sat in `[Ts, Ts + 0.265*gap]`, never at
  the liquidus. P3.2's melt children sit *exactly* at `Tl = Ts + gap`, which reaches 1.13-1.17
  at depth. The bar is now the law's own ceiling (`1 + meltDeep + meltGap`), so a future
  runaway fails it and a legitimate liquidus state does not.
- **A deferred intrusion must not keep rising.** Melt that finds the chamber set full waits
  at `yCham` (checked before the rise step); rising through the intrusion depth to reflect at
  the top wall would have made a bouncing pool of magma at the surface.

## Reusable patterns

- **The equilibrium split's arithmetic** (`pool.js` `separate`): `m_child = m * xi`,
  `m_host = m - m_child`; host at `Ts(y, cF, cW)` with `H = T = Ts`, child at `H = Tl + L`
  then snapped to `PHASE.byH`'s own reading of that H. Measured conservation: `m*H` exact
  (0 rel), `m*T` 2e-16, `m*xi` exact; both children bitwise-quiet under the next equil pass.
- **A kernel that owns motion samples the flow through `G.velAt`** and writes `G.vxAt/G.vyAt`
  — no allocation, one interpolant, and the seam wrap comes free via `keepInside`.
- **A reservoir is a record, not a parcel**: fixed-capacity arrays in `state.js` (`ch*`), a
  matched transfer in `pool.js`, stock in the inventory, position as the mass-weighted mean
  of what it ate. The record set is allocated once (the frame loop still constructs nothing:
  `pt-p3-budget` section C green with chambers live).
- **The twin list must travel with the transaction history** (`pt-wrap`): `ph` and `id` per
  parcel, `nextId`, `tx`, `hLedger`, `invL0`, and the chamber arrays. A save that omits them
  replays the other run's pool.

## What to do next

P3.3 — eruptions and vent routing (G6/G7, `experiments/pt-p3-vent.js`). The chamber records
P3.2 made are the vents' sources: an overpressure rule drains a chamber into a vent schedule,
ballistic flight on wall time (P3.4's clock, but the ejecta at least can be stashed now),
lava on the surface as deposits. The plan's §5 open decisions that remain are the vent
density and the deposit properties; the melt split criterion, the rise law and the chamber
geometry are measured here and only need the P3.5 freeze.

Caveats carried forward: (1) the airborne-clast latent routing question is still open for
P3.4 — G5's freeze-back already gives cold airborne material a home (the mantle), but clasts
that carry latent heat on wall time still sit outside the book; (2) the reserve is *not*
frozen: the melt side is measured (1389-slot burst into a 4711-slot shared reserve, zero
refusals over 100 Myr, 30% peak use) but `resAir`/`resDep` wait for P3.4's populations, so
the freeze stays with P3.5; (3) capture budget-gate logs serially (see above).
