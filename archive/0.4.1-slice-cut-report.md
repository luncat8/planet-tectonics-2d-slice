## 2. The cut, planned

- **What a cut is:** one sample per cell the line crosses, in order, each with the arc the line
  spends inside that cell. No interpolation, no resampling on the globe's side.
- **Two transports:** clipboard (46 KB at L5, 93 KB at L6) and a saved file (189 KB at L7). Both
  are the same bytes through `SlicePack.decode`. `localStorage` and `postMessage` are *not*
  transports on `file://` — they are the live link, and they are browser-dependent.
- **Two run modes:** a closed great circle (arc scale **1.00** for the column engine, plate clock
  allowed) and an open window (clock off — a window needs end boundary conditions the engine does
  not have yet). Both start stopped.
- **The section keeps its own 512 columns** and lays the cut on them with an arc-length box
  filter, measured mass-exact in both directions; the page prints both resolutions
  ("source L5 · 241 km cells → 512 columns · 78 km").
- **What is not inherited:** beds. The globe's columns hold aggregate thicknesses, so the starting
  stack is three layers and everything above them the section makes itself — and says so.

`0.9.0-draft-sync.md` holds the later request; `0.4.1-plan.md` §8 is the path to it, including
the coupling choice (the globe sends plate intervals and velocities, the section slaves its plate
kinematics and keeps its own crust) and the channel ladder
(`postMessage` → `localStorage` → **copy/paste and file, always**).

## 3. Built in this session

- **`port/slice-format.js`** — `pgt-slice-pack` v1 as one shared-identical module: the field
  table (17 fields), the rounding, the checksum, the validation (twelve refusals), the encoder,
  and `latLonAt`, which both sides use to label the cut. In `port/`, not `js/`, because
  `experiments/smoke.js` asserts `columns.html` loads every `js/*.js` exactly once.
- **`experiments/slice-cut.js`** — 57 checks, log in `experiments/logs/0.4.1-slice-cut.txt`: the
  walk at three densities (no cell jumped, arcs sum to the circumference, reads the same from
  either end), the pack and its sizes, the twelve refusals, the fit into `colCap`/`plateCap`, the
  resample, and the path block.

Three bugs it caught, which is what an experiment is for: a lat/lon bucket grid cannot find a
pole cell's neighbours (spatial hash instead), `JSON.stringify` of a typed array is an object
(a replacer), and the first box filter double-counted the arc (a cursor over the spans).

Validation: slice-cut 57, pt-ui 37, smoke 64, view-check 119, pt-check 18, pt-load-check 9 — all
pass; the mirror is clean. No engine number and no page changed.
