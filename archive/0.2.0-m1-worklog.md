## Decisions and why

- **Birth at `Vbirth`, not at `Vch`.** The plan's first draft said "born where a chamber is
  full (`Vch`)"; design §5.2 is explicit that the slot opens when the chamber first reaches
  `Vbirth` and `Vch` is the vent chamber's cap. The plan text now states the design rule.
- **Chamber km3 convert to engine m2 at the boundary (`venKm3M2` = 1e5).** The design's §9
  chamber figures are km3; the engine's one volume unit is section m2 (findings: ribbon and
  chamber volumes in K4 units). A probe of unmodified supply (seed 1) put plume-column
  chambers at 4.1e5 m2 after 100 Myr and a 5.1e5 m2 plateau after 500 Myr, with arc columns
  two to four orders lower (~41 m2 — `kMelt` feeds far less than `kPlumeMelt`). 1e5 puts
  Vbirth/Vch/Vdie inside the measured range: strong plume columns birth and cycle vents in a
  live run (9 slots in 200 Myr), which the strict 1 km3 = 1e9 m2 reading never could. M5
  re-tunes the three figures together with the look; `tDrain`/`kErupt` scale with them.
- **Blast pressure threshold with an effusive fallback.** Reading §5.2's "explosive when
  gas > 0.4 and P > 1.3 P0" as an absolute gate left gas-rich vents stalled at 0.3 Vbirth —
  above `Vdie`, so they could never die. Falling back to effusive below blast pressure keeps
  the design's both thresholds and makes every vent drain to empty; the bench checks the
  fallback drains (not plugs).
- **`venBlast` is the schedule's output.** The box executes the feed character but does not
  own the policy: gas fraction is chamber state, the pressure test lives in magma.js, and the
  box stays a pure function of its state (`explosive(v)` reads `venBlast`).
- **A dead vent may leak no state (design §5.2).** Normal death returns the `venV` dribble to
  the column chamber and only fires while the box is empty — until M2 write-back empties boxes
  each K7, a vent with a pile stays alive, which is the honest stand-in. A slot whose column
  was consumed (columns.js already leaves `venCol = -1` when the margin has its own vent) has
  no home left: its chamber + box mass is booked to `venLost` and `ledCons[maf]`, never zeroed
  silently.
- **`meltIdle` is a throughput, the mass stays in the chamber.** A full vent list must idle
  the magma rather than drop it; the melt sits in `colChamber` (in `S.mass()` already) and the
  frame's arrivals at stalled vent-ready columns are counted for visibility. The deep cap
  still spills to sills via `MAG.spill` — sills instead of a 17th vent.
- **K-slot kernels are invoked unbound.** `SIM.step` calls `this.k[i](S, ...)` with no owner,
  so `MAG.k7` addresses `MAG` (as `CRU.k5` does), not `this` — the first live-run crash taught
  that (TypeError on `this.venTick` only through `SIM.run`, never through direct `MAG.k7`).
- **The toy box mass lives in `S.mass()`.** The ledger identity (`mass + cons + mixOut =
  initial + prod + mixIn`) is measured against `S.mass()`; melt in flight to write-back must be
  counted where it sits (chamber, vent chamber, box) or any long run with a live vent would
  read as a leak. `toyCellM2` is the one jacobian both the schedule and M2 write-back use.
