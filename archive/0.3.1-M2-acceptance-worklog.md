# 0.3.1 M2 — acceptance, and the eligibility decision

M1 landed the route and left one reading open (`0.3.1-M1-remelt-worklog.md` §4): every crossing
the four-term gate took sat 0.1–0.3 of T above the node field it was embedded in, and §5 asked
for that gap to be measured at the moment of routing before deciding whether eligibility should
also test the local field. This worklog records the measurement, the decision it forced, the term
that landed, and the re-acceptance of the whole series on the resulting tree. `0.3.1-plan.md`
remains the authority and now carries the fifth condition (§3) and the accepted outcome (§5).

## 1. The measurement

`experiments/pt-buried-deposit-melt.js` gained the routing-event columns. Its read-only wrapper
already sampled the G5 boundary; it now records **every routing candidate at the moment the
kernel decides**, per source, with four readings that had never been taken together:

- the parcel's own temperature against **its own solidus** (`T - Ts`, how molten it is by its own
  phase law);
- the same temperature against **the node field it sits in** (`T - Tg`, the M0/M1 field column,
  now at the transfer rather than at the closest approach);
- the **adjoint gain** `sum_q W_pq cw_q / sum_q W_pq` over the parcel's own 3×3 stencil, i.e. the
  factor between the node increments' plain interpolation and the specific increment `gatherDT`
  actually handed it (`cw = mu_k / W_k`, the renormalization that makes the intake exact);
- the parcel's **mass share of that stencil** — the light-parcel reading the gain is a function of.

The same four are accumulated over a 1/8-stride sample of the two populations the gate draws
from, so a routed parcel can be read against its own population and not only against the other
source. Candidates the kernel takes are events (a transfer each, at zero refusals); candidates a
term holds back are not consumed, so they are integrated over `dt` as a resident population.

Canonical leg, seed 6, 300 Myr at 50 kyr/frame, all shipped P4 passes on
(`experiments/logs/0.3.1-M2-remelt-melt.txt`, 37 checks):

| population | samples | T − field | own reading − solidus | gain | stencil mass share |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| mantle, routed by G5 | 3221 | **+0.0044** mean (−0.175..0.664) | +0.0163 | 1.013 | 0.177 |
| mantle, all (1/8 stride) | 1.759e7 | −0.0007 mean (−0.576..0.509) | −0.0449 | 1.052 (0.13..12.96) | 0.089 |
| buried deposits, all (1/8 stride) | 39889 | **−0.126** mean (−0.473..0.090) | −0.581 | 1.079 (0.44..9.62) | 0.092 |
| deposits the four-term gate took | 6 | **+0.244** mean (0.222..0.305) | +0.0150 | **1.200** | **0.005** |

94.33% of the mantle source's routed host mass sits within 0..0.05 of its field; 100% of the
deposit gate's sits in 0.2..0.3. Five of its six parcels were slivers of 0.014–0.021 km² of host
mass against a base parcel of ~1.8e3 km², and the sixth (351 km², the one that carried 35.19 of
the 35.20 km² of intake) read the same way: gain 1.200, share 0.0051, field 0.723 against its own
0.967.

Three facts decide the question, and they are not the same fact:

1. The shipped melt law is field-supported. G5's mantle branch routes parcels whose temperature
   *is* the local field's, at +0.004 mean over 3221 transfers. Melting in this engine happens
   where the mantle is hot.
2. The buried-deposit population is **colder** than its field, not hotter: −0.126 mean, with a
   stride-sampled maximum of +0.090 and a mean reading 0.581 below its own solidus. Buried
   sediment behaves like buried sediment.
3. The parcels the four-term gate took are outside their own population's sampled range
   (+0.222 minimum against a population maximum of +0.090), at 1/18th its stencil mass share and
   a gain of 1.12–1.29. They reached `meltHold` by the pairing's concentration — exactly the case
   `0.3.1-plan.md` §2 named and refused to decide without measuring.

## 2. The decision

Eligibility gains a thermal term. The route's live intake was not sediment the interior had
warmed; it was the light tail of the adjoint gather, and a route that turns that tail into
chamber melt is a heat source at parcel scale, which §1's invariant forbids ("the phase transition
is an internal transfer; it is not a heat source").

The form is the field supporting the parcel's own solidus:

```
G.sampleAt(M, S.Tg, x, e) >= PHASE.solidus(y, cF, cW)
```

chosen over a gap bound for three reasons: it adds **no constant** (§1 forbids tuning from a
routing count, and a `delta` would be a new tunable); it is the physical statement — the mantle
around the parcel is warm enough to melt it — rather than a statistical one; and it is a **strict
refinement of the deposit branch**, so P3.2's frozen mantle path is untouched and every cell that
never routed keeps its M1 fingerprint bitwise. `detach` therefore takes the mesh now
(`detach(M, S)`), and the term is evaluated last, after the cheap tests, so it costs a bilinear
sample and a solidus only on parcels that were about to route.

The measured consequence, accepted rather than tuned away: **the live intake is zero on all
seven 300 Myr cells**, and on the 600 Myr cell it is three transfers the field does support
(§4). No parameter switched, no second path added.

## 3. What landed

- `js/pt/melt.js`: the fifth term as `fieldHolds(M, S, p)`, and `detach(M, S)`.
- `experiments/pt-buried-deposit-melt.js`: the routing-event columns above; four new fixtures in
  section A (27 checks in the section) pinning the term from both sides and pinning that the
  mantle branch does not read the field at all; two new live gates per cell — the term is
  **bilateral** (nothing routes below zero field-minus-solidus, nothing is held back above it)
  and the mantle source stays at its own field (mean gap < 0.05, more than ten times the worst
  any cell reads); the held-back population reported per cell and in the matrix verdict.
- Section A's fixtures now own the node field (`S.Tg.fill`), held flat and above every solidus in
  the domain by default so the certificate/depth/threshold fixtures keep testing their own terms,
  and moved only in the field block. Without that, a probe placed by hand would have been judged
  by scatterT's initial condition — a planet it was never part of.
- `experiments/pt-p4-check.js`: the deposit identity message names the remelted term, the
  integrated leg reports `thr.depMelt`/`tx.depMelt` beside `thr.deepDep` (§5's requirement), and
  the census line's fingerprint note now says what moved it (0.3.1's `depQ` and counters) instead
  of quoting P4.3's superseded value.
- `experiments/pt-p3-melt.js`: the `detach(M, S)` signature. Its 64 checks pass unchanged, which
  is the frozen-mantle-path claim measured by its own owner.

## 6. What is left, and why it is not this release's

The term filters the intake; it does not explain the tail behind it. The concentration is a
property of the marker↔grid pairing — `cw = mu_k / W_k` keeps `sum_p m_p a_pk = mu_k` exactly,
which is why `pt-p4-check.js`'s intake gate reads |intake/wall − 1| ≈ 5e-5 while saying nothing
at all about the distribution — and its tail is not bounded by the deposit phase. The mantle
population's own gain reaches 12.96 at its extreme; deposits show it first because erosion and
landing make light parcels and the surface quench gives them a cold start to be amplified from.
The 600 Myr cell has the tail at full range: 30906 held-back samples at gains of 0.399..11.880
and T-field gaps up to +1.328, whose extreme is a buried deposit at **xi 1.0000, T 2.267,
H 2.517, 73.9 km deep, in a field of 0.288** — 1.6127 of H past the threshold and outside the
−0.5..1.5 wall `pt-crust.js` and `pt-p4-check.js` gate on. The hottest parcel in that world is a
deposit at 8.4 km, T 2.268, in a field of 0.006, against a deep interior whose hottest
non-deposit material below 2400 km reached T 1.170. That is 6.814e4 km²·Myr of certified, buried,
threshold-molten mass-time the four-term gate would have converted to mobile melt.

That is `0.3.2-draft.md`: a mass-share floor or gain cap on the gather, a phase-scoped relaxation
toward the field, or a minimum parcel mass below which a deposit is merged rather than carried —
each measured against the intake gates, not against whether it reopens this route. 0.3.1's
condition 5 is what keeps the artifact out of the chambers until then, and §5 of the plan records
that reopening the 300 Myr intake is not a gate adjustment.

## 7. Next step

Take 0.3.2 from the draft (`0.3.2-draft.md`, `roadmap.md`'s open bullet): decide what the
defect's own statement is — a parcel's temperature unbounded by the field it sits in, or a
parcel's mass allowed far below the node measure it claims — then measure the candidate remedies
(a mass-share floor or gain cap on the gather, a phase-scoped relaxation toward the field, a
minimum parcel mass below which a deposit is merged rather than carried) against
`pt-p4-check.js`'s intake/wall gate, the global books and the frame gate, and not against a
routing count. The 600 Myr seed-5 cell is the configuration to measure on, and it is banked on
this tree now (`experiments/logs/0.3.1-M2-remelt-600myr.txt`): the tail sits 1.6127 of H past the
threshold in a single sample, the held-back population runs to a gain of 11.880 and a T-field gap
of +1.328, and the three transfers the field term admits there — 9.730 km² of melt at gains of
0.683..1.878 with positive field support — are the behaviour a remedy has to leave intact. Only
after that, re-read 0.3.1's intake with the remedy in and decide whether condition 5 stays the
filter it is, loosens, or becomes redundant.
