# 0.3.2 M2 — launch/air booking remedy (on-manifold + booked air snap, per-frame gate, re-bank)

**Result:** the P3.1 latent identity `hLedger − ledger = L · (invLTot − invL0)` now closes to
round-off (~1e-8 km²·H absolute) on every frame of the 600 Myr seed-5 extended cell,
including through eruptions. The wall ledger `S.wall` is untouched; a per-frame gate lives in
`F.diag` (shown on the HUD as `resid`); air snaps are booked where they happen; every launch
leaves the vent on-manifold and stock-consistent. This is the M2 candidate the M1 report
asked for. Old banked fingerprints are no longer expected (M2's re-bank); protected event
records are preserved.

## What was broken (from M1 report)

`FLIGHT.launch` set `S.T[p] = byH(dH/d, y=0)` but left `S.melt[p] = dL/d` (the vent stock's
aggregate xi), producing an off-manifold air parcel. `PHASE.equil` remaps the whole pool
every G4, but only booked the sensible `m·dT` of grid-coupled parcels (mantle/melt/dep) into
`S.ledger`; air is outside `PH_GRID`, so the snap `m·(xiEq − xiStock)` changed `invLTot`
with nothing against it. Drift: −167.41 km²·H over 600 Myr (9.66e-3 relative, fingerprint
8261233554728015).

## Remedy

Three changes, all in `js/pt/`:

1. **`flight.js` `launch` — on-manifold stock-consistent launch, air snap booked at source.**
   After the stock split and `byH` at `y=0`, set **both** `S.T[p] = PHASE.out.t` and
   `S.melt[p] = PHASE.out.melt`, then book the adiabatic sensible drop
   `S.ledger += d · (T_eq − T_stock)` where `T_stock = H − L · xiStock` (the temperature the
   clast would have carried at the vent if it kept its chamber-depth melt). The vent is
   debited at the stock split (conservation of every extensive field); the extra latent
   `L·d·(xiEq − xiStock)` that decompression melting creates at the surface is funded by
   that sensible drop. `S.wall` is never touched — no heat crossed the surface.

2. **`phase.js` `equil` — book `m·dT` for every parcel, not only grid-coupled ones.**
   The identity is `hLedger − ledger = L·Δ(invLTot − invL0)` over the whole pool. Air parcels
   never receive a conduction increment, but any depth change (a clast flying through the
   solidus) or out-of-equilibrium admission must still have its sensible change booked so
   invLTot's corresponding Δ does not register as a leak. `S.wall` remains untouched here
   too; only `S.ledger` (the sensible book) accumulates. With fix (1) in place this is a
   no-op on the production trajectory (air parcels stay on-manifold and surface-pressure),
   but it is what makes the identity robust to any future air-phase kernel.

3. **`fluid.js` `diag` + `state.js` `S.d` + `ui.js` — per-frame gate on the extended cell.**
   `F.diag` (G8, every frame) now computes the absolute and relative latent residual into
   `S.d.latentAbs` / `S.d.latentAbs`/`S.d.latent`, after the naive `PTPOOL.inventory(S)`.
   The HUD ledger line shows the residual. This is the per-frame gate the M1 report asked
   for (not just end-of-leg on the short owner legs). `d.latent` / `d.latentAbs` are reset
   with the rest of `S.d` on reset and init.

## Wall ledger untouched

`S.wall` is written only by `F.wall` (operator flux) and by `FLIGHT.cool` (deposit surface
cooling, which pre-dates this change and books the wall honestly). The new code writes
`S.ledger` (sensible book) only, matching arc.release and cool's existing convention that a
phase change within the pool books its sensible delta and never touches the wall integral.

## What was not changed

- No routing threshold, no field filter, no deposit parameter changes. Condition 5 and the
  field-support filter stay (M1/M2 rule: the tail is only re-measurable once the latent
  identity is green — next step).
- No constant was frozen. `emitEff`, `emitBlast`, `flightVy*`, `flightVx*`, `meltLat`,
  `meltGap` are all unchanged.
- `pt-heat-tail.js`'s `--assert-baseline` gate still references the M0 fingerprint
  8261233554728015 by design — that gate asserts reproduction of the *unchanged* bank for
  trace validation. The new trajectory is 6191250240167390, quoted above.
- The columns-world (`js/*.js`, `js/erupt.js`) has no PHASE ledger and is unaffected.
