The solver is the plan's §4.3 deal: an infinite-Prandtl diagnostic velocity from a
red-black Gauss-Seidel **viscous predictor** (`lap u = mob(T) F`, F the row-mean-removed
buoyancy at 0.3's own `RaK`) followed by a red-black **projection** on the same
staggered D/G pair, `projIters` sweeps split between them, neither converged in a step
and both warm-started. In the developed regime the leftover divergence — printed on the
HUD as a budget — settles at 0.9% of peak strain. Transport is flux-form donor-cell (one
amount per face, subtracted and added as the same number, so H/cF/cW totals conserve to
roundoff whatever the divergence budget is), conduction is the same face loop against
the two Dirichlet walls with every wall increment booked, and a rendered frame is k
fluid steps of at most 50 kyr.

## The measurements that moved the plan

M2's gate said "S1 green; the §8 HY row measured and the target confirmed or the ladder
re-planned". The target is confirmed with headroom (1.27 ms integrated of which flow is
1.03; 512x48 reads 5.26 ms), but three preregistered numbers did not survive contact,
and each amendment is in the plan with its measurement:

1. **The mesh rows.** On 0.3's one-sided asinh rows the bottom cell is 570 km thick and
   conducts **102 km²·H/Myr into the box against 7484 out of the top** — a cell planet
   free-cools through its own CMB resistor. 0.3 never notices because markers gather
   sub-cell against the wall and carry their heat through the fat cell; a cell average
   cannot. HY's rows are now tanh-clustered at both walls (~8 km against each boundary
   layer, ~300 km mid-mantle), the asinh map remains the view (the raster resamples by
   depth, the ruler is shared and unchanged), and every operator coefficient is built
   from actual y differences so the stretching is mesh data. With two-sided rows the
   same planet sustains its convection to a quasi-steady 44-53 km/Myr instead of
   decaying through 34 toward a conductive interior. Cell mass is now the exact
   wrap × depth partition (4.640000e7 km², gated to 1e-12).
2. **S1's velocity floor.** With 0.3's transplanted `Ra` and `kappa`, honest Stokes
   convection does not reach the first-written 6 cm/yr floor on this IC — because that
   floor came from `pt-conv`'s calibration column for a **1000 km-deep** box, where the
   same nondimensional flow reads 3.45x faster in cm/yr (0.3's own plan flags this in
   its units note). The anchors at 2900 km, all measured for this milestone: the
   shipped 0.3 planet on the S1 IC/clock/seed reads a **33 km/Myr median** peak over
   100-300 Myr; `pt-conv`'s steady Ra 1e6 reference converts to 67; M0's soup read
   71.66; HY reads 52.58, between the shipped planet and both references. The band is
   now 3-20 cm/yr, containing every honest measurement instead of excluding the line's
   own shipped engine. `pc-scenario.js`'s synthetic pass/fail fixtures still prove the
   gate fires in both directions (M0's contact control at 498 km/Myr still fails high).
3. **MacCormack's ledger.** The plan called the clamped option "still conservative to
   the ledger's tolerance". It is not: the corrector conserves exactly, but the
   donor-stencil clamp removed **43% of gross wall throughput over 100 Myr** — a sink
   the size of the surface flux, invisible until the stock was read against the walls.
   The clamp is now booked on its own ledger line (`clampNet`), MacCormack's book
   closes at 2.4e-12 with the line included, `hy-conv.js` prints the clamp's size as
   the option's price, and the donor default is gated to need no such line.

## Defects the work caught, two of them instructive beyond this milestone

- **A steady predictor must not carry `grad p`.** The first flow solve put the warm
  pressure gradient inside the predictor's source (the SIMPLE-looking choice, so the
  fixed point would be "true" Stokes). It amplified gradient modes by orders of
  magnitude per frame — mobility-weighting the projection did not save it, because the
  correction potential and the momentum pressure do not share units, so `p += dp`
  mixes two quantities. The shipped fractional step (no pressure in the predictor) has
  the Stokes velocity as its fixed point anyway for constant mobility, since the
  Laplacian and the projection commute. Recorded in `findings-pitfalls-skills.md`.
- **An unconverged projection makes dt a stability parameter.** The clock slider at
  500 kyr/frame blew up in four frames from a cold start: ~40% spin-up divergence
  compresses flux-form H by ~(div · dt), the overshoot feeds buoyancy, which feeds
  divergence. `hy-conv.js` section E caught it as written (a boundedness check at the
  slider's end — the option matrix earning its keep early). The fix is 0.3's own
  `dtFluidMax` contract as an outer substep loop; as a side effect sweeps-per-Myr is
  clock-invariant and the 500 kyr clock lands on the 50 kyr trajectory (both read
  peak 77.5 km/Myr at 100 Myr). A 500 kyr frame honestly costs 13 ms: that is ten
  fluid steps, printed as k on the HUD.
- **From the M1 review, fixed here:** `HY.read('enthalpy')` returned the mass (a
  placeholder that the M1 harness never read); the `solidusOf` GUI option never
  reached the phase law, which reads the shared `PCP.solidusOf` word — both engines
  now write their option there at reset and every step, without which S4's depth-mode
  negative control would silently have compared load against load in M3; both
  skeletons seeded a single-mode perturbation (icMode exact copies of one cell — on a
  cell grid nothing breaks that symmetry), now the shared `icDraw` band; and two
  comments stated the cm/yr conversion backwards. One self-inflicted M2 bug worth the
  line: the band draw was first named `PCP.icBand`, clobbering the 0.6 constant it
  reads — NaN from frame 1, caught by the drive before any harness existed for it.

## What is deliberately not here

- No parcels move: the pool is attached and empty, H1's scatter is the identity at
  zero population (the load column is hydrostatic, so `solidusOf: 'load'` and
  `'depth'` coincide on cells until M3's overburden exists), and no melt/freeze
  transaction family runs. Equilibrium melt *exists* on cells (2.4e5 km² at 300 Myr)
  but `features.melt` stays undeclared, so S2 cannot go green on a reading nobody
  routes anywhere.
- No browser smoke and no rAF number: cost here is the integrated headless step
  (flow + transport + equil + diag, no draw). The file:// Chromium pass remains M6's.
- The 512x48 rung is costed and bounded, not behavior-gated.
