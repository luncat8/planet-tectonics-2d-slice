## P4.2 — arc melt from the slab

P4.1's slab pass drives cold detached lithosphere below `P.yCrust` toward `P.yDeep`. P4.2 adds one
new file, `js/pt/arc.js` (`PTARC`), at the head of G5 ahead of `melt.js`: in the `'cool'` initial
condition the shallowest cold surface skin (`M.yN[j] <= P.yWet = 20 km`, rows 1–2 where `T <= 0.22`)
starts with bound water `P.slabW = 0.005`, which depresses the solidus by `0.10` (`meltWW = 20`) and
leaves a cold lid or slab (`T <= P.slabT = 0.45`) completely solid at every depth (`Ts >= 0.68`).
When a detached cold slab segment sinks through `(P.yArcMin, P.yArcMax]` (`250..600 km`), `ARC.run`
transfers `min(P.kArc * dt, P.arcFrac)` of its water into the warm, unsegregated mantle wedge above
it (`P.yCham..P.yArcMin` within `P.arcSpan = 125 km`), depressing the wedge's solidus through the
existing `meltWW` term of the P3.1 law so the P3.2 segregation, P3.3 conduits and P3.4 eruption path
run unchanged.

Primary gate: `experiments/pt-p4-arc.js`, **ALL PASS (26 checks)** — single-call release arithmetic
and five exclusions read off one state by hand, the dipping-slab calibration under a fixed uniform
wedge, the full P3 melt-to-eruption path, and two 300 Myr live legs. Full serial release validation
with `P.arc` shipped on: **18 processes, 693 quoted checks (685 distinct), 0 failures** — logs
`experiments/logs/0.3.0-p4.2-*.txt`.

`wedge` (collects wet slab parcels in `(yArcMin, yArcMax]` into `S.order`, marks the columns within
