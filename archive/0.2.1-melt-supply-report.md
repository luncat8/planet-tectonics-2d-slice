# 0.2.1 — report (melt supply, and a latent floor defect)

The item 0.2.0 M5 measured and deferred is closed. Worklog:
`archive/0.2.1-melt-supply-worklog.md`.

**Landed**

1. **`P.kPlumeMelt` 2e4 → 2e6.** One toy cell is one default-window pixel
   (`P.toyCellM2` 5.80e5 m2 = 5.80 km3), so the design's 10–30 px edifice needs 3e7–2.6e8 m2
   delivered to one column, and the old rate fed the best-fed chamber 2.7e5–1.4e6 m2 per
   150–300 Myr — under one cell, which is why no live vent built anything. 100× is the
   smallest rate whose live shield reaches the band: edifice stock 5.2e7 / 1.54e8 m2, pile
   7.8–10.9 px tall and 21–29 px wide, against `erupt-bench`'s own reference cone of
   10.06 × 27. The next step (4e6) is 40% larger than that reference in both dimensions;
   1000× and above stop growing the edifice at all (stock saturates near 2e8 m2 — the box
   and the drain law bind) while melt climbs to 2.2–52% of all mafic production.
2. **`P.kMelt` unchanged, on a measurement.** At 10 000× — a melt/water yield of 0.63, within
   an order of magnitude of the petrological factor — the arc's edifice stock is still only
   9.7e6–1.1e7 m2, its pile still ≤1.7 px, and its duty cycle has saturated at ~90%, which
   is the M1 birth/death lifecycle disappearing. The reason is in the toy, not the supply:
   `ERUPT.land` books tephra as solid and `writeBack` drains it into the column every K7
   (0.2.0 M2's "the retained solid shape owns no mass"), so an explosive arc builds beds and
   a plume shield builds a cone. It also bounds `erupt-bench`: that harness never calls
   `writeBack`, so its explosive cone is the box alone.
3. **A latent `COL.floor` defect, found by the landing and fixed.** Acceptance §2.7 went red
   (118 frames, an ordinary contact standing at 9.156 km against its 9.382 km floor) — and
   the same red reproduces at the *old* constant on seed 4 (90 frames), so the melt only
   re-routed which trajectory hits it. `COL.floor` is a max-push Jacobi sweep whose exit
   test was exact equality, so it spun on a residual no reader applies (69.9% of 10 954
   calls ran a lifted 1024-pass cap with nothing left to fix) while a genuine squeeze ran
   out of its 16 passes at ~0.87 convergence per pass. It now settles on the `P.floorTol`
   its own diagnostic already applies, records its pass count, and the cap is 64. Worst
   settle 27 passes, worst gap left exactly the allowed 9.2883 km, and the step is faster
   (1.78–2.09 ms/frame against 1.94–2.14).

**Crust budget:** melt was never the constraint the deferral feared — 100× more melt is
still 0.155–0.277% of everything the section produces as mafic rock. Thickest column 80.3
km against `crustMax` 80 km, no chamber spill, K9 clean on every frame of all 32 sweep legs.
