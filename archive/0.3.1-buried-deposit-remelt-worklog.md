# 0.3.1 M0 worklog — buried-deposit remelting measurement

Scope: measure the deposit-phase material that could meet the existing G5 melt threshold below
P3.4's shallow cooling mask, and specify a conservative future route. This milestone does not
change runtime physics. The routing design is `0.3.1-plan.md`; the full harness output is
`experiments/logs/0.3.1-buried-deposit-melt.txt`.

## Measurement

`experiments/pt-buried-deposit-melt.js` wraps `PTMELT.detach()` read-only and samples at the G5
boundary, after G4 phase equilibrium and the slab pass but before mantle segregation. Its
thermal-candidate mask is `PH.dep && y > depositCoolDepth && S.melt >= meltHold`. This is the
G4-updated fraction the existing G5 detach kernel reads, not a second phase-law interpretation.
The live leg is the P4.4 configuration: seed 1, 50 kyr/frame, 300 Myr, all P4 passes
on. It samples all 6,000 G5 calls.

- **No threshold-eligible buried deposit was observed:** peak host mass 0 km², melt-equivalent
  peak 0 km², host and melt mass-time both 0 km²·Myr, 0 exposed frames and 0 parcel-Myr. No
  consecutive exposure episodes were sampled at 0.05 Myr cadence.
- There was substantial buried deposit stock despite that zero: peak 99,692.536 km²; at end
  352,435.205 km² in 763 parcels. The integrated sampled buried-deposit stock was
  5,843,249.299 km²·Myr.
- P4.1 transferred 1,242.199525 km² of deposit mass into deep records (`thr.deepDep`). That is
  a cold-slab recycling transfer, not a count of material that reached meltHold.
- Global mass residual 0; enthalpy residual 2.95e-14 relative; deposit identity residual
  1.98e-9 km² (5.62e-15 relative); 0 refusals. Fingerprint **3426360007996525**, matching
  P4.4's integrated baseline: the sampler did not change the simulated path.

The controlled boundary probe seeds molten deposits at 29, 30 and 31 km. `melt.detach()` leaves
all three in the deposit phase. `flight.cool()` quenches the first two, including exactly 30 km;
the 31 km deposit's H and melt fraction are unchanged and it remains unrouted. This confirms
that the quench is a shallow mask, not a deep-sediment bound.

## Interpretation and next step

The corrected finding is narrower than the original P4.4 wording: “0 parcels re-phased” only
measured the routing exclusion. In this canonical run the new mass-and-duration measurement
also found no thermally eligible buried deposits at the G5 sampling boundary. Neither result
bounds other seeds, slider settings or sub-frame transients. The 30 km cooling rule cannot be
used as a general bound on later deep remelting.

The M1 routing specification in `0.3.1-plan.md` requires a persistent `depQuenched` latch in
addition to depth and melt fraction, so a hot deposit cannot skip its shallow cooling and
welding interval. Partial melt must transfer `m * xi` with a source-specific `thr.depMelt`
debit; whole-parcel conversion, pool refusal and both phase books are specified there. None of
that runtime state or accounting is implemented in M0.
