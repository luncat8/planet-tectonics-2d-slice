# 0.3.0 P3.2 — melt segregation and intrusive storage

Scope of this frozen milestone, per `0.3.0-p3-plan.md` §4 P3.2: the melt a mushy parcel
carries can leave it, rise, and reach shallow storage — every leg a matched transfer, no
sink anywhere. This document freezes what P3.2 does and what its gates measure. The numbers
below come from `experiments/pt-p3-melt.js` (this milestone's harness) and the P3.1 suite
re-based where the new transactions changed the world; each is re-runnable.

Run: `node experiments/pt-p3-melt.js` (52 checks, 29 s). The suite table is §7 of
`0.3.0-p3.1-report.md` with the re-bases named in §6 here; per-milestone logs are
`experiments/logs/0.3.0-p3.2-*.txt`.

## 1. The split: a split moves material and does not melt it

`PTPOOL.separate(S, p)` is the equilibrium melt split. A parcel in the mush
(`0 < xi < 1`) separates into the two pure phases its own law already names:

- the **host** keeps its unmelted mass `m(1 - xi)` at its solidus `Ts(y, cF, cW)`, exactly
  solid (`xi = 0`, `H = T = Ts`), its id and its bond history;
- the **child** takes exactly `m * xi` at the liquidus, exactly molten, `H = Tl + L`, with
  the fresh id the pool hands out and no bond state;
- position, velocity and composition are shared intensives.

This is the only separation that leaves both children on the equilibrium manifold. Measured
(`pt-p3-melt` §1): `m*H` closes exactly (0 relative), `m*T` to 2e-16, `m*xi` exactly; both
  children are bitwise-quiet under the next `PHASE.equil` pass (it reads them back with zero
change and books nothing); `S.ledger` and `S.hLedger` do not move; and the P3.1 identity
`hLedger - ledger = meltLat * (invLTot - invL0)` survives the transaction untouched. A split
moves material and does not melt it.

A full pool defers the split and counts it (`tx.refuse`, one event per attempt): the phase
fraction stays in its source parcel and tries again (`pt-p3-melt` §3).

## 2. The criterion and the transit

`P.meltHold` (0.1) is one threshold used both ways:

- PH_SOLID source rock (mantle and landed deposits) sheds at `xi >= meltHold`; a wholly
  molten parcel is one whole-parcel phase change to melt (and drops its bond state — non-
  solid carries none). Below the threshold the melt is trapped in the grain boundaries and
  stays with its host (`pt-p3-melt` §2).
- mobile melt with `xi < meltHold` freezes back to the mantle and rides the flow map again
  ("non-rising cold material"); at or above it, melt rises on the flow map plus its own
  buoyant speed `P.vMelt` (60 km/Myr) per geological step (`pt-p3-melt` §4). Zero dt reports
  and changes nothing. The periodic seam wraps (`pt-p3-melt` §5).

The symmetric threshold is a fixed point: a parcel frozen at a *higher* threshold than the
shed one would re-shed the moment it became mantle.

## 3. The intrusive store

`PTPOOL.intrude(M, S, p)` transfers a melt parcel reaching `P.yCham` (80 km) into a chamber
record: mass, enthalpy, melt stock and composition move and the parcel slot closes; the
record's position is the mass-weighted mean of what it has eaten. Intrusions within
`P.chJoin` (400 km, periodic) share one record; a new site opens one; the record set is
fixed-capacity (`P.chCap` = 64, allocated once) and a full set defers the transfer — the
melt waits at the chamber depth (`pt-p3-melt` §6). Chamber stock is inventory: both
inventory passes sum it into the mass/enthalpy/melt/composition totals exactly once, so a
pressure scenario at capacity drains into the store by matched transfers and still closes
(`pt-p3-budget` D).

## 4. The live run

100 Myr of the shipped pipeline (`pt-p3-melt` §7):

- the first frame's burst released **1389 splits** (the t=0 mush at `meltHold 0.1`), peak
  pool **24948 of 28263** slots (30% of the shared reserve), **0 refusals**; 1518 splits and
  **1511 intrusions** over the run, **23 chambers** at `chJoin 400 km`, store mass **0.544%**
  of the planet, total melt stock **0.965%** of mass (parcels + store), 7 parcels in flight;
  the first chamber at **t = 3.1 Myr**;
- mass closes to 1e-12 relative and composition with it; no NaN; coverage clean;
- the two gaps, reported separately as §4 asks: the **marker/grid heat gap is 5.85e-3**
  (the quadrature of the deposit) and the **global enthalpy residual is -1.1e-16**
  (what the wall booked against what the parcels and chambers hold) — they measure
  different things and are not conflated;
- the P3.1 latent identity holds across all of it (residual 5e-9 absolute on a 4e7 stock).

## 6. The suite after P3.2

The P3.1 gate table stands (19 gating harnesses; `pt-check` 19, `pt-p3-pool` 70,
`pt-p3-routing` 39, `pt-p3-enthalpy` 65, `pt-p3-budget` 18, `pt-stability` 12, `pt-ui` 43
(+1 store check), `pt-wrap` 19, `pt-crust` 31, `pt-surface` 36, `pt-emerge` 8,
`pt-load-check` 9, `pt-drift` 5, `pt-conv` 5, `pt-eng-conv` 5, `pt-metric` 5, `pt-lid` 3,
`pt-asymmetry-check` 3, `pt-bench` 6), with these re-bases, all of them the harness
catching up to the new world rather than the engine bending to the harness:

- `pt-p3-pool`: transaction counters compared as deltas around the synthetic scripts; the
  split/merge round-trip pins the pair's phase (the inverse merge is same-phase) and its
  starting count after the live run.
- `pt-p3-routing`: the solid-set denominator counts melt; "the phases are still the phases"
  is the base carried in mantle+melt.
- `pt-p3-enthalpy`: the mapping-idempotence isolation stubs G5 (it is a transaction, not
  the mapping); the melt census includes the intrusive store.
- `pt-stability`: the field bound is the law's liquidus ceiling (`1 + meltDeep + meltGap`),
  not the pre-segregation 1.10 (melt children sit at the liquidus — the first states that
  reach it).
- `pt-p3-budget` D: the capacity claim is "reached the capacity and never exceeded it" (the
  store legitimately drains a full pool); the forced 40 refusals are counted at their call
  sites and the run's own segregation defers are additional counted events.
- `pt-wrap`: the twin list carries `ph`, `id`, `nextId`, `tx`, `hLedger`, `invL0` and the
  chamber arrays.
- `pt-p3-budget`'s frame gate is re-based the way P3.1 re-based `pt-check`'s (host-carried
  bar + the relative reserve price), and the phase-law comment in `params.js` now names the
  `meltDeep` the code evaluates.

Budget gates are idle-host numbers: captured serially (pt-check 12.6-15.3 ms/frame, the
reserve-filled rung 14.2 ms, both inside their host bars); captured under parallel load the
same code reads 31-33 ms and the bars redden — record runs accordingly.

transit`, `G.velAt(M, psi, x, e, y)` (writes `G.vxAt/G.vyAt`), `S.chN/chX/chY/chM/chH/chL/

includes them (`pt-wrap` is the worked example).
