# 0.3.2 M3 — melt depletion, the mobile-melt face and the cold side: build and validation record

Date: 2026-10-14. Base: 369ce20 (M2: tail re-measured on the green identity, clock/seed matrix,
nothing selected). Report: `0.3.2-M3-depletion-report.md`. Specification: `0.3.2-plan.md` §M3.

## Changes

- `experiments/pt-heat-tail.js`:
  - **`--dpart D`**: wraps `POOL.separate`'s own source (the probe convention: `toString`,
    exact-string `replaceOnce`, `new Function` — no runtime file is edited) so the split
    partitions water and felsic at the batch-melting ratio `c_melt = c / (D + F (1 − D))`,
    `c_res = c D / (D + F (1 − D))`; the residue's solidus rises with what the melt took
    (`meltWW · ΔcW + meltWF · ΔcF`), nothing is booked, and both children stay on the law's
    manifold. `dpSt` counts the splits and the rise (max, sum).
  - **`--cap PHI`**: wraps `MT.detach` with a probe-local per-material-id shed budget (`PHI` of
    a parcel's own mass over its life, `capOk`/`capTake`); a blocked candidate is counted apart
    from a delivered transfer (`capSt.blocked`, `capWholeBlocked`, `capSpent`, `capParcels`).
  - The rewritten detach takes the melt kernel's private `fieldHolds` as an argument
    (`fHolds`, the harness's own copy of the same test): a `new Function` copy cannot close
    over the file-private helper, and the first `--cap` run died with
    `ReferenceError: fieldHolds is not defined`. Rule confirmed: rewritten kernel source may
    reference only identifiers passed in.
  - The frozen P3.2 census is printed per cell (`census`: `split`, `whole`, `freeze`, `seg`,
    `intrDr`, `intrCr`, `landDr`, `landCr`, `ventDr`, `chambers`, `meltM`, `depN`, `nMelt`,
    `nDep`) in `result.census`.
  - **`--watch`** (observation only): per-phase temperature extrema by depth band
    (0/50/120/250/500/1000/2000/3000 km) with the frame and material id that set each one
    (`bands`), the global mantle minimum and its update path (`cold`, `coldPath`), and the
    mobile-melt excursion table above T = 1.5 (`excursions`: entry reading, hottest frame and
    state, close or leg's end; `exSamples`, `exSampleLog` capped at 2000 samples).
  - An excursion run still open at the last frame is **closed** by the observer (`open: true`)
    instead of dropped: the per-frame expiry only fires on a later frame, so a crossing live
    through the final frame of the leg vanished. Field-visible in the pre-patch bank: the
    seed-2 50 kyr cell recorded `exSamples` 8 and `excursions` 0.
  - G5 candidate events carry the parcel's composition (`cF`, `cW`), so a bank can answer
    "would this residue still route with its water removed?" without a different instrument.
- `experiments/pt-heat-tail.js`, output only (added with the report, after every bank above was
  written): with `HEAT_TAIL_OUT` set the bank file keeps the whole record and stdout prints a
  14-line summary (cell, fingerprint, gates, wall, per-phase T with the outside counts, the
  hottest parcel, candidates/transfers/bleed/shed, the held census, the frozen P3.2 census, the
  depletion counters, the books, and — under `--watch` — the global mantle minimum and the
  excursion count). Without the variable stdout is still the full bank, so an ad-hoc pipe is
  unaffected. This is the fix for the 21 MB of `.txt` twins below: printing the whole bank to
  stdout as well made every `> logs/x.txt` redirect a byte-identical copy of `logs/x.json`.
  `experiments/pt-heat-matrix.js` is unaffected — it spawns the runner with `HEAT_TAIL_OUT` and
  reads the *file*, never stdout.
- `experiments/pt-heat-tail-check.js`: an M3 section, so every number
  `archive/0.3.2-M3-depletion-report.md` quotes is asserted against its own bank instead of being
  transcribed by hand. It reads the 10 kept M3 rows (fingerprint exact, books, wall, refusals,
  field support on every candidate), prints the report's §3 row table with its decision column,
  asserts the two channels' own numbers on both cells, the five serial timings, the dry-solidus
  reading recomputed from the shipped phase law (4,509 of 8,559), the three seed-2 excursions
  (`dT = dH` at `xi = 1` sample by sample, rising, field more than a unit below), the control's
  global cold minimum and its 0.548 margin, the deposit-band ordering and its inversion under the
  relaxation, and the crossing rows' cold paths (every minimum below −0.5 a shallow parcel of
  ≤ 200 km² at least 0.5 below its own field). A pruned or drifted bank now fails loudly.
- `0.3.2-plan.md`: §M3 added (the two depletion probes, the measurement and its gates, the two
  faces read on their own). No other plan text changed.
- **No change to `js/pt/`, parameters, pages or shipping fingerprints.**

## What was validated

- **Neutrality.** Every harness edit is observation-only and was re-checked:
  `node experiments/pt-heat-tail.js baseline 0 20` reproduces the pre-edit fingerprint
  **5534361194548428**, and the 600 Myr bank cell reproduces **6191250240167390** with its 3
  protected transfers and 9.73015809837463 km² under `--assert-m2` (exit 0;
  `logs/0.3.2-M3-baseline600-final.json`).
- **Determinism, and the serial reads are the same worlds.** Every timed re-run reproduces its
  batch fingerprint exactly: control 3546678272699960, +`dpart` 0.01 7597164752167614,
  +`dpart` 0.2 8062761652553677, +`cap` 0.25 1369981785435233. The serial timings therefore
  read the same trajectory the batch banks do, on an otherwise idle host: **15.777 / 15.002 /
  14.206 / 13.974 ms/frame**, all inside the 20 ms gate. The 600 Myr bank cell timed alone in
  M3 reads 12.539 ms against M2's 14.78 (host drift, not a code change).
- **The observer fix is trajectory-neutral.** The pre-patch watch bank of the seed-2 50 kyr cell
  records `exSamples` 8 and `excursions` 0; the re-run after the fix records one excursion
  (id 56868, frames 5993–6000, `open: true`) with the same fingerprint 2285870507950569.
- **The `--cap` comment's fraction was wrong, and is corrected against a measurement.** The
  probe's header said "47% of them still sit above the residue's DRY solidus". Measured on the
  control bank with composition in the event record (`logs/0.3.2-M3-subgrid1dep-comp.json`,
  fingerprint equal to the control's own 3546678272699960): of the 8,559 candidates, **4,509
  (52.7%) are still above the fully dry solidus** (water removed outright, felsic kept — the two
  readings coincide because the events' `cF` is 0 throughout), with the dry margin (field −
  dryTs) min −0.0850, p10 −0.0793, p50 +0.0031, p90 +0.0184, max +0.1635. So drying alone
  closes **less than half** (47.3%) of the row, not leaves 47% routing. The comment now states
  the measured count; the neutrality smokes were re-run after the edit (fingerprint
  5534361194548428 at 20 Myr, and the 600 Myr bank under `--assert-m2`).
- **Pages and `js/pt/` untouched**, so the page-parity and owner-harness series is not re-run
  here; the M2 series stands (15 legs, 617 checks). `experiments/pt-heat-tail-check.js` reads
  saved banks and passes unchanged.

- **The serial timing chain was re-run on this second host**, strictly one process at a time with

  All six reproduce their fingerprints exactly and every recorded key but `msFrame` is identical,

## Harness revision trail

The M3 banks were produced by five successive stand-ins of one file, all observation-only:

1. the M2 harness, plus `--dpart` / `--cap` / the census block / `fHolds`;
2. `--watch` (bands, cold, excursions) with the per-frame expiry still dropping a run open at
   the last frame — the seed-2 50 kyr bank and the three batch watch banks;
3. the same with the post-loop closure and `exSampleLog` — the seed-2 100/500 kyr banks;
4. `cF`/`cW` in the event record — the control re-run (and, in the same chain, the combination
   cell, which is otherwise the code of (3));
5. the stdout summary — **output only, no bank field added or removed**, so revisions 1–4's banks
   stand as they are and revision 5 re-banks nothing. Neutrality re-checked at both 20 Myr and
   the 600 Myr `--assert-m2` cell after the edit.

The report cites the final revision; every cell's fingerprint is stated, so a re-run of any
command above on the final revision must reproduce the bank's fingerprint exactly (and does:
the timed chain and the control re-run both ran on revisions 3 and 4 and reproduced the
revision-1/2 fingerprints bitwise, and the combination cell reproduced revision 4's whole
observation set on a second host).

## Bank hygiene

`experiments/logs/` was 59 MB and 1,100 files at the end of the M3 runs, 52 MB of it 0.3.2; the
M3 banks alone were 26 MB. Two separate wastes, both fixed, and one convention recorded so the
first does not recur:

- **21 MB was 22 `.txt` files byte-identical to a `.json` next to them** (`cmp` on every pair: 22
  of 23 identical, the 23rd being `0.3.2-M2-matrix`, whose `.txt` is a different record and was
  kept). They came from redirecting stdout to a `.txt` while `HEAT_TAIL_OUT` wrote the same JSON to
  a `.json`. All 22 are deleted, and revision 5 makes the redirect produce the 1.4 KB summary
  instead, which is the console record the older `.txt` logs in this directory already are.
- **8 MB was the same world banked more than once.** A row's world is its fingerprint, and four
  M3 worlds had two or three banks each (a batch run, a `--watch` run and a serially timed run of
  one cell; plus the pre-patch seed-2 observer bank). Deleted, one survivor per world, chosen as
  the bank with the most recorded fields and — where the report quotes a frame time — the serial
  one:

  | deleted | kept instead (same fingerprint) |
  |---|---|
  | `0.3.2-M3-subgrid1dep.json`, `…-subgrid1dep-watch.json` | `…-timed-subgrid1dep-watch.json` (serial + watch) and `…-subgrid1dep-comp.json` (the `cF`/`cW` events) |
  | `…-subgrid1dep-dpart01-watch.json` | `…-timed-subgrid1dep-dpart01-watch.json` |
  | `…-subgrid1dep-dpart02.json` | `…-timed-subgrid1dep-dpart02-watch.json` |
  | `…-subgrid1dep-cap25.json` | `…-timed-subgrid1dep-cap25-watch.json` |
  | `…-seed2-300-kyr50-watch.json` (pre-patch observer) | `…-seed2-300-kyr50-exlog.json` (the same world with the excursion closed) |

  Verified before deleting: for every pruned `.json` a survivor with the identical fingerprint
  remains, and nothing in `experiments/*.js` reads a deleted path. The command list above still
  names the outputs as they were produced — it records what was run, not what is kept.
- **Kept deliberately, and why.** The three 3 KB bank-cell banks (`baseline600`, `-final`,
  `-final2`) are one world at three revisions and are the trail's own evidence, so their size does
  not argue for deleting them. `0.3.2-trace-latent.json` (2.4 MB) cannot be regenerated at all —
  `pt-heat-tail-trace.js` reproduces only on the M1 tree — which makes it the least replaceable
  file in the directory. Everything under `0.3.2-tail-*` is read by `pt-heat-tail-check.js`'s M0
  and M2 sections, and everything under `0.3.2-M3-*` that survives is read by its M3 section: a
  bank this project keeps is a bank a checker reads or a report quotes, and the directory is now
  30 MB with that property.
- **Not done: recompressing the surviving banks.** Dropping the pretty-printing saves 20%
  (30.6 → 24.2 MB measured over all 39 `.json` banks) and rewrites every file for it, which costs
  more in reviewable diff and in grep-ability than it returns. If the directory grows again, the
  cheap wins are the ones above, not the formatting.

## Report

`archive/0.3.2-M3-depletion-report.md` — the decision (nothing selected), the two channels on both
cells, the route's own gate and the dry-solidus reading, the frozen census beside every cell, and
the two faces: mobile melt above the bound (one excursion per clock, `dT = dH`, overshoot scaling
with the clock) and the shallow cold side per band (the quench's reaction path, its carrier
parcels, and the inversion the deposit-scope relaxation produces). Its §9 is the next-step
suggestion this worklog ends on: bound the distribution on both sides on top of depletion, port
the adjoint-gain reader into this harness so the cold-side mechanism is measured rather than
inferred, split the per-phase outside count into its two ends, and decide whether the descending
cold anomaly is geology to protect or the same artifact as the hot tail.
