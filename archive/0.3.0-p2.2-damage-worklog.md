# 0.3.0 P2.2 (step 1) — the damage rate stops depending on the sample: worklog

Follows `0.3.0-p2.2a-wrap-worklog.md`. Its next step listed three items; this is item 1,
"density-independent damage (mean over partners) + recalibrate with pt-load-check". The
switch-bar UI work of the same session is a separate commit (`UI: lit toggle buttons ...`).

## 1. The problem, restated and measured

`solid.js pair()` added `kDamage * (load - yield) * dt` to **both** markers of a pair, so a
marker's damage rate was the sum over its partners. The number of partners is a property of
the Lagrangian sample, not of the rock: the same pass that measures loads now measures it.

`experiments/pt-load-check.js` (extended, still `kDamage = 0` on an intact lid), partner
counts p10/p50/p90 and the rank correlation between a marker's damage rate and its partner
count, under each law:

| t/Myr | markers | partners p10/50/90 | per-pair rate p50/p90 | mean rate p50/p90 | corr pair / mean | rescale |
|-------|---------|--------------------|-----------------------|-------------------|------------------|---------|
| 50 | 9468 | 17/25/30 | 0.075/0.144 | 0.0031/0.0056 | 0.45 / 0.12 | 24.6x |
| 100 | 9276 | 16/25/33 | 0.120/0.262 | 0.0047/0.0101 | 0.53 / 0.12 | 24.9x |
| 200 | 8725 | 11/35/51 | 0.535/1.234 | 0.0155/0.0330 | 0.73 / 0.19 | 33.9x |

(rate units: 1/Myr, quoted without the yield subtraction; "rescale" is
mean(per-pair rate)/mean(mean rate) over the population, i.e. the factor that keeps the
total damage delivered to the lid where P2.1 calibrated it.)

## 2. The law that ships (js/pt/solid.js)

- `pair()` is now read-only: it counts the pair (`S.pCnt`) and adds its positive excess to
  the integral (`S.pLoad`), both per marker. The bond decision reads the *previous* frame's
  damage, so it no longer depends on the order the buckets were visited.
- after the walk, one pass over candidates: `dmg += kDamage * (pLoad / pCnt)`. Markers with
  no partner keep their damage.
- `pLoad/pCnt` are two new arrays in `state.js` (Float64Array / Int32Array, allocated once,
  cleared in `reset` and for candidates at the top of `clusters`).
- `kDamage: 3 -> 90`. The per-pair 3 times the measured rescale: 24.6x, 24.9x, 33.9x across
  the three samples, so 90 sits inside the band; the emergence gate below is what pins it.

## 3. Gates

`pt-load-check` (9 checks, was 5): the old gates hold (body below yield at 50/100/200 Myr,
p99 above it, p50 < yield < max), plus the new ones: partners vary by >2x p10..p90, a
per-pair law tracks the count (corr > 0.5), the mean law does not (corr < 0.2, measured
0.12-0.19), and `params.js` kDamage is inside the measured rescale band.

it follows (P2.1's profile of the same config read G4 ~2.7 ms at 300 Myr).

## 5. Not done here

- The pair walk is still uncapped and still O(partners) per marker; capping or thinning it is
  now legitimate (the damage rate no longer depends on the count) but changes cluster
  connectivity, so it belongs with the deep-pool work below.
- **Still open from P2.2a**: (2) cluster kinematics that let a plate drift — fit to the flow
  at the lid's base rather than to the plate's own markers, plus a slab-pull term; (3) a
  depth/pressure limit on welding so the 'rb' CMB cold pool does not weld (it measured
  8257 candidates in 256 nodes, 34 ms/frame in that config).
