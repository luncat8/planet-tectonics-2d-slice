# 0.3.0 P2.2 (step 3) — slab-pull drive, welding depth ceiling, and surface review report

## Summary

1. **Completed P2.2 (`js/pt/params.js`, `js/pt/state.js`, `js/pt/solid.js`)**:
   - **Welding depth limit (`yWeldMax = 300` km)**: markers deeper than 300 km reset `age = 0,
     dmg = 0` so the unheated `'rb'` CMB cold pool never welds into a bottom plate (cutting the
     150 Myr `'rb'` frame cost from `34 ms` to `6.61 ms` and `pt-check.js`'s Ra 1e6 run from
     `18.78 ms/frame` to `9.38 ms/frame`).
   - **Slab-pull horizontal drive (`kSlab = 18`, `vSlabMax = 45`, `ringMax = 0.04`)**:
     accumulates the root depth-asymmetry moment `csS[c] = sum_{ry > 0} m * (rx / |r|) * ry`
     over cluster members deeper than the cluster centroid and adds `pull = clamp(kSlab * csS /
     csM, -vSlabMax, vSlabMax)` to `csVX[c]` for broken plates. Mean plate drift in
     `pt-drift.js` rises **`5.2x`** (`0.31 -> 1.60 cm/yr`), with active slab-bearing plates at
     **`2.03-4.95 cm/yr`** (`5.15-6.28 cm/yr` peak across seeds 1..3 in `pt-band.js` and
     `pt-crust.js`) while an unbroken lid stays at `0.01 cm/yr` and periodic shift equivariance
     holds to `2.29e-9 km` (`pt-wrap.js`).
2. **Reviewed the flat surface and added Stage `0.3.0-P2.3` to `0.3.0-plan.md` (§4.5, §5, §6,
   §8, §11)**:
   - Identified why the top boundary is currently ruler-flat at `y = 0`: (1) the separable FFT +
     Thomas Stokes solver requires a flat coordinate top `eta = 0` (`psi = 0`, `v = 0`) and
     `keepInside` reflects markers at `yMin = 4.14 km`; (2) `render.js` paints `SKY` for all
     `eta < 0`; and (3) horizontal marker spacing (`31-62.5 km`) is too coarse for raw marker
     tops alone to form a smooth `±5-10 km` relief profile.
   - Designed `0.3.0-P2.3` around a periodic zero-mean 1D surface elevation profile `S.zh[i]`
     (`Float64Array(nx)`) on top of the separable `(x, eta)` Stokes domain, combining thermal
     isostasy (`0..150 km`), dynamic trench normal stress (`-v` / `du/dx`), and welded crustal
     thickness / rift-damage necking, coupled to a per-column terrain warp + ocean band in
     `render.js` and a topographic ridge-push term (`-dzh/dx`) in `solid.js`.
