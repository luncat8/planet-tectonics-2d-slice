# 0.3.0 P2.2 (step 3) — slab-pull drive, welding depth ceiling, and P2.3 surface review worklog

Follows `0.3.0-p2.2-drift-worklog.md`, which established that the near-zero plate drift
(`0.31 cm/yr` mean across 100..400 Myr) was not a velocity-sampling bug: plates span across
upwellings from trench to trench because compressive strain rate at narrow downwellings
(`du/dx ≈ -0.065 /Myr` over 150 km) exceeds `yieldRate = 0.06` while tensile strain rate over
broad upwellings (`du/dx ≈ +0.013 /Myr` over 1,500 km) is ~5x below yield. Consequently, the
leftward (`~-1.5 cm/yr`) and rightward (`~+1.5 cm/yr`) branches of the convection cell under a
plate cancel in `csVX`, and a rigid cluster `(csVX, csVY, csW)` has no hinge at the trench to
convert the vertical sinking of its attached cold root into horizontal plate translation.

## 1. Changes landed

- **Welding depth ceiling (`yWeldMax: 300` km in `js/pt/params.js`, `strength` in
  `js/pt/solid.js`)**:
  - In `strength(M, S, dt)`, `if (T >= P.TSoft || S.y[p] > P.yWeldMax) { S.age[p] = 0;
    S.dmg[p] = 0; }`.
  - In the unheated `'rb'` preset (`T_top = 0, T_bot = 1`), cold sinking plumes pool along the
    CMB at `T ≈ 0.25 < TLock = 0.35`. Without `yWeldMax`, 11,252 of 11,958 strong candidates
    sat in rows 14..23 (`y = 364..2357 km`, 7,941 in row 23 at the CMB) by 150 Myr, driving
    the pair walk to `34 ms/frame`. With `yWeldMax = 300`, `'rb'` at 150 Myr drops to `6.61
    ms/frame`, and `pt-check.js`'s 1200-frame Ra 1e6 timing drops from `18.78 ms/frame` to
    `9.38 ms/frame`.
- **Slab-pull horizontal drive (`kSlab: 18`, `vSlabMax: 45`, `ringMax: 0.04` in
  `js/pt/params.js`; `csS`, `csCnt` in `js/pt/state.js`; `kinematics` in `js/pt/solid.js`)**:
  - During the cluster moment pass in `kinematics`, accumulate the root depth-asymmetry moment
    over markers deeper than the cluster's vertical centroid (`ry = S.y[p] - csY[c] > 0`):
    `if (r2 > 1e-9 && ry > 0) csS[c] += m * (rx / Math.sqrt(r2)) * ry;`
  - Summing over `ry > 0` only is load-bearing: at a plate's trench end (`rx > 0`), the
    shallow lid markers sit above the cluster centroid (`ry < 0`) while the attached cold root
    hangs below it (`ry > 0`), so summing over all `ry` cancels half the root's moment against
    the shallow trench roof.
  - For any broken plate (`csR2[c] < P.ringMax * wrap * wrap * csM[c]`, where an unbroken
    planet-wrapping ring has `r^2 / (M * wrap^2) = 1/12 ≈ 0.0833 > 0.04`), add
    `pull = clamp(P.kSlab * (csS[c] / csM[c]), -P.vSlabMax, P.vSlabMax)` to `csVX[c]` before
    computing `S.d.plV` and projecting marker velocities.
  - A flat plate (`ry = 0` for all members) has `csS[c] === 0` to machine precision, so
    `pt-crust.js`'s unit check (`the cluster travels at the mass-weighted mean`) continues to
    pass at `0.00e+0`.
- **Surface review & `0.3.0-P2.3` design in `0.3.0-plan.md` (§4.5, §5, §6, §8, §11)**:
  - Documented the three reasons the surface is currently ruler-flat at `y = 0` (`eta = 0`):
    (1) the separable FFT + Thomas Stokes solver requires a flat coordinate top `eta = 0` with
    `psi = 0` (`v = 0`) and `keepInside` reflects markers at `yMin = 4.14 km`; (2) `render.js`
    hardcodes `SKY` for `eta < 0`; and (3) horizontal marker spacing (`31-62.5 km`) is too
    coarse for raw marker tops alone to form a smooth `±5-10 km` relief profile.
  - Added Milestone **0.3.0-P2.3 — Surface relief & topographic coupling** specifying a
    periodic zero-mean 1D elevation field `S.zh[i]` (`Float64Array(nx)`) driven by upper-column
    thermal isostasy (`0..150 km`), dynamic normal stress (`-v` / `du/dx` at the lid), and
    welded lid thickness / rift-damage necking, coupled to a per-column terrain warp + ocean
    band in `render.js` and a topographic slope (`-dzh/dx`, ridge push) term in `solid.js`.
