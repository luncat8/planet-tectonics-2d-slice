# 0.2.3 — report (the melt constant, landed from the contact legs)

The item 0.2.2 §6 left open is closed as a contact-leg calibration. Worklog:
`archive/0.2.3-melt-contact-worklog.md`.

**Landed: `P.kMelt` 2e-3 → 20.** The design's two visible eruption styles are now both
on the page — 0.2.1's plume shield and, at last, an arc cone.

**The table it came from** (`experiments/melt-contact-sweep.js`, log
`experiments/logs/0.2.3-melt-contact-sweep.txt`): the four strict contact legs at
`kMelt` 2e-3 / 6 / 10 / 20 under 0.2.2's tephra-only rule, plus the live arc cone at
each candidate.

- **Contact legs: all sixteen pass.** 20's worst margin is the base's own — R2
  contiguous width 92.7% against the committed base's 92.5% (90% required), R3 80.4 km
  against 80.1 km against the same 81.8 km ceiling+influx. The legs cannot tell 6 from
  20, so they are the safety gate, not the decision.
- **The cone decides.** 6 → 17.00 × 1.43 px, 10 → 19.00 × 4.03 px (the apron 0.2.1
  measured), 20 → **27.00 × 8.24 px, 113.7 cells²**, 504.1 cells² of tephra placed at
  death and 0.000 live. In the design's 10–30 px band on width against `erupt-bench`'s
  explosive reference of 10.02 × 27.

**Two corrections along the way**

1. **0.2.2's "not landed" measured a rule that is not in the tree.** Its two numbers —
   R2 2438/2724 and R3 81.9 km — reproduce exactly once `writeBack` is suppressed
   outside death, i.e. with lava delayed too, which is what `params.js` recorded as
   "a trial that also delayed lava". The rule that landed writes lava every K7; under it
   the same two legs read 3590/3814 and 80.4 km. (The base leg reproduces the committed
   0.2.1 contact logs byte-for-byte, so this is the rule, not the machine.)
2. **`tephra-pile.js`'s live-write gate was reading the wrong quantity.** It watched
   `venEdV` grow, which the lava writes the rule still allows also do — measured 63.8
   cells² of lava placed live against 0.000 of tephra. It now counts, via a wrapped
   `ERUPT.place`, the tephra cells placed while a vent is alive. Same gate, no threshold
   moved; harness 6 / 6.
