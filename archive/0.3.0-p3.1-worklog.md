# 0.3.0-P3.1 worklog — enthalpy and partial-melt equilibrium

Plan of record: `0.3.0-p3-plan.md` §4 P3.1, with §1.1 (contracts), §1.2 (the heat invariant) and
§3 (kernel order, G4 = equilibrium → melt weakening → crust). The prerequisite is P3.0's pool,
phases and routing (`archive/0.3.0-p3.0-worklog.md`), which has landed. Nothing here touches the
column engine (`js/*.js`, `columns.html`).

P3.1 is the milestone that retires P1's pressure-release overlay and replaces it with a
*per-material* equilibrium state. `H` becomes the conserved quantity; `T` and `melt` are the
reading of `H` through a solidus/liquidus law that depends on depth and composition. Melt stays
attached to its host: no segregation, no reservoirs, no eruption, no flight — those are P3.2-P3.4
and this milestone claims none of them.

## 2. The law

Written against the model's own anchor, the CMB temperature (`T = 1`, where the wall is held):

```
u(y)  = 1 / (1 + y * meltInvLam)          y > 0; u = 1 above the surface
Ts(y) = 1 + meltSup - meltDrop * u * u - meltWF * cF - meltWW * cW
Tl(y) = Ts(y) + meltGap
H     = T + meltLat * xi                  xi = (T - Ts) / meltGap inside the mush
```

`u²` is a rational decay, not the exponential this was first written as: an `exp` costs a
transcendental per parcel per substep, and the rational form puts the same three anchors in the
same place. The derived block in `params.js` owns the two constants that pin them:

```
meltDrop   = 1 + meltSup - meltSurf = 0.25
meltInvLam = (sqrt(meltDrop / meltSup) - 1) / meltOnset = 0.0036221912941896252  (1/276.07 km)
meltDeep   = meltDrop * u(meltOnset)^2 = 0.02 exactly
```

so `Ts(meltOnset) = 1` **bitwise** and the deep curve's margin over the CMB wall is `meltSup`.
The curve, measured through `PTPHASE`:

| y (km) | 0 | 100 | 200 | 300 | 400 | 500 | 700 | 1000 | 2900 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Ts` | .7700 | .8853 | .9359 | .9626 | .9783 | .9884 | 1.0000 | 1.0083 | 1.0181 |
| `Tl` | .9200 | 1.0353 | 1.0859 | 1.1126 | 1.1283 | 1.1384 | 1.1500 | 1.1583 | 1.1681 |
| `xi` at `H = 1` | .5750 | .2868 | .1602 | .0935 | .0542 | .0291 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

`Ts` is monotone in depth (gated), so a parcel melts in exactly one window: above the depth where
its own solidus crosses its own `H`. `xi` at `H = 1` is the fraction of fully-CMB-temperature
material that is molten; it is 0 at and below 700 km, which is where the shipped planet's melt
window ends. Crossing the mush costs `1 + L/gap = 2.6667` units of heat per unit of temperature
(gated at 7e-13 relative against the analytic value), and `L = 0.25` sets how much of a parcel's
`H` can be latent: a fully molten parcel at the solidus holds `0.25` of latent heat.

The two mappings are exact inverses (50 000 samples across depth and composition: worst
`|dH| 4.4e-16`, `|dxi| < 1.3e-15`), which is what makes the reset able to seed `H` from an initial
temperature and the kernel able to read `T` back out of `H` every substep. Both return a
module-level scratch triple, so a fixture that wants two states at once keeps its own copy — the
convention the harness documents.

## 3. The four calibration rounds, and the `t = 0` decision

The law was calibrated by running the shipped planet, not by fitting a literature curve; four
rounds were needed and the first three were abandoned. The figures below are what each round
measured at the time; rounds 2 and 3's numbers are **void as measurements** (the census probe
itself was wrong at the time — it mis-reported depth bands and, in one version, printed `NaN`),
and are recorded here as the reason each round was dropped, not as evidence. Round 4's numbers
were re-measured this milestone with the shipped law and are the live ones.

| round | constants | what it measured | outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | onset 250 km, decay 60 km, drop .25, gap .15, `L` .3, ww 4 | the deep solidus (1.009365) sat above any attainable `H` — the CMB wall is the hottest material in the box and it is held at exactly 1 | 0.000% melt over 240 Myr. Dead end. |
| 2 | onset 500 km, decay 400 km, drop .25, gap .15, `L` .3, ww 4 | melt live (0.42% → 0.823% of the mass over 240 Myr) **but** the cool start's own geotherm crossed the solidus below ~200-300 km: 2916 of 23552 parcels (12.4%) were molten at `t = 0` | dropped: melting in the deep interior rather than in the band the law was meant to open |
| 3 | onset 400 km, drop .25, gap .10, `L` .25, ww 20 | melt effectively unreachable (0.001% → 0.041% of the mass over 160 Myr) | abandoned |
| 4 | onset 700 km, gap .15, `L` .25, ww 20, wf .2, and the decay derived from the anchors rather than tuned | **live and rising**: 1.033% of the mass at `t = 0`, 0.920% at 100 Myr, 1.367% at 300 Myr; 2194-4823 mushy parcels; deepest mushy parcel 696-1099 km; node-mean `xi` 0.154 → 0.239 | shipped, with the `t = 0` melt accepted and documented |

**The `t = 0` melt is a decision, not a surprise.** The shipped `'cool'` initial condition is a
cold skin over a hot interior (`1 - exp(-y/80)`) plus a seeded wave of amplitude `icAmp 0.02`; its
wave peaks straddle the solidus below ~700 km even though the base profile alone does not. So the
shipped planet starts with 1.033% of its mass (4823 parcels, `xi` up to 0.195, mean depth 398 km)
inside the mush. Two ways out were considered and rejected:

- raise the solidus back above the wave peaks (round 3's family) — melt becomes unreachable in a
  live run, and P3.2-P3.4 would have nothing to segregate or erupt in the shipped configuration;
- edit the initial condition down until it is subsolidus — that changes the *planet* to suit the
  calibration, and P1-P2's accepted convection, lid and drift fixtures are all measured against
  this initial condition.

Accepted instead, and gated: `experiments/pt-p3-enthalpy.js` §4(d) asserts that the shipped start
**is** the equilibrium state of its own `H` (0 of 23552 parcels disagree, worst `|dxi| 2.78e-16`,
worst `|dT|` exactly 0 — the reset seeds `H` through `byT`, so the first phase pass is a no-op),
and reports the melt census rather than hiding it. The wording of the shipped law's own comment in
`params.js` says the same thing: the deep mantle is subsolidus *where `H <= Ts`*, and the melt
that exists is the wave's, carried by its host.

Round 4 also fixed two things in the *derived* block that round 2/3 had carried: the surface drop
is `1 + meltSup - meltSurf` and the decay solves `u(meltOnset)^2 = meltDeep / meltDrop` exactly, so
`Ts(700 km) = 1` bitwise and `Ts(depth) = 1 + meltSup` in the limit. The earlier block put the
deep asymptote at `meltSup` *before* subtracting the decay, which made the third anchor
approximate rather than exact.

The x-operator section is the plan's closed-box invariant in the form this world allows: the walls
here are the model's cold surface and hot CMB and `G.diffuse` re-imposes them on every call, so
there is no non-trivial no-flux state in `eta`; the periodic direction has no wall at all, and
*that* is where the operator is proved to be a redistribution — conservation of every row, and a
second moment that falls by exactly the discrete Dirichlet energy. The `eta` half is `§4(a)`: what
crosses the walls is the wall book, to 1.8e-7 relative.

## 5. The wiring

- **G3 → `H`.** `gatherDT` no longer touches `T`: a conduction increment a parcel used to spend on
  raising its temperature can now be spent on melting it. The `flip < 1` relaxation toward the
  grid became `flip * d + (1 - flip) * (byT(grid T).h - H)`, i.e. it relaxes toward the *enthalpy*
  the grid temperature implies — the same blend as before once nothing melts, and the only
  definition that cannot double-count latent heat.
- **G4 = `equil` then `crust`.** `equil` maps every parcel (airborne and landed included, so "`T`
  is the equilibrium reading of `H`" is a whole-pool statement) but books the sensible delta only
  for `PH_GRID` parcels, because only they received — and booked — a conduction increment. Keeping
  the two books over the same set is what makes their gap exactly the latent heat the coupled
  material has taken up. Both halves of `k[4]` no-op at `dt = 0`.
- **Melt weakening** multiplies the existing strength law by `1 - xi`, which is where the plan puts
  it in the G4 order: the fraction read is the one `H` implies this substep.
- **The overlay** (`S.Mg`) is now `scatterT`'s mass-weighted node mean of the parcels' own melt,
  zeroed at the walls and where no marker covers a node — the same treatment `S.mug` gets. The
  raster, the HUD's `max`/`depth` line and the pool's inventory therefore describe one field, not
  two opinions.
- **The books.** `gatherDT` books `S.hLedger` (the intake as enthalpy); `equil` books `S.ledger`
  (the sensible part of the same intake). Their difference is the latent heat the run has stored,
  `meltLat * (S.invLTot - S.invL0)`, and `S.invL0` is captured at reset so the identity survives a
  restart. `experiments/pt-p3-routing.js` §H and `pt-p3-enthalpy.js` §4(a) both gate it.

every one traced to the harness's own assumptions, not to the engine: byH's mushy branch and byT's

contradicts the two formulas' last ulp (14839 of 50000 samples) — the gate is now 1e-12 on the

## 8. What the checks do not prove

- **The curve is a calibration, not a material measurement.** The three anchors (surface solidus
  0.77, deep margin 0.02, onset 700 km) are the model's choices; the fixture checks that the law
  is consistent with them, not that any rock behaves this way.
- **`t = 0` is mushy by decision** (§3). The gate asserts the start is *equilibrium*, not that it
  is solid.
- **The mush's heat capacity is one constant** (`1 + L/gap = 2.6667`). A real melt fraction is a
  temperature-dependent curve, not an affine one.
- **Water is one linear coefficient** (`ww 20` per unit mass): no saturation and no partitioning.
- **Buoyancy reads `T`, not density.** Latent heat makes an isenthalpic parcel *colder* in `T` and
  therefore less buoyant, while real melt is less dense than its host and should rise. The density
  contrast is the plan's §5 open decision and belongs to P3.2.
- **Melt does not move.** P3.1's `xi` rides its host through every pass; the trajectory's deepest
  mush is material carrying its own heat, not a segregation. P3.2 claims the split, P3.3 the
  eruption.
- **The 150 Myr live gate is not a 1000-frame fingerprint** (P3.5 owns that) and the fixture's
  `deepWorst < 1200 km` reads the 150 Myr window; the 300 Myr report reaches 1099 km.
- **The reserve price is still unfrozen** (P3.0's open item). P3.2 splits into it, so it is the
  first thing P3.2 needs.

## 10. Next step

P3.2 — melt segregation and intrusive storage (`experiments/pt-p3-melt.js`): split a melt-bearing
parcel only when the capacity benchmark shows headroom, hand the new parcel exactly the removed
mass, enthalpy and composition, and give it buoyant rise at a geological-time step. Two things to
settle before writing it: the pool reserve price (P3.0's unfrozen candidates `resMelt .05 /
resAir .03 / resDep .12` — re-run `pt-p3-budget.js` on an idle host), and the melt split criterion,
which is the plan's other open decision. The enthalpy identity this milestone gated is the thing
P3.2 must preserve across the split: the host's `m` and `H` must fall by exactly what the new
parcel's rise by, with `S.ledger` unchanged, since a split moves material and does not melt it.
