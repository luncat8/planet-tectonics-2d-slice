# 0.3.2 M3 — melt depletion measured, and the two faces read as their own quantities

**Decision: nothing is selected.** That is what §M3 asks of a measurement step, and no row would
have been eligible if selection were on the table: every probe row fails the two-sided
temperature bound, and none reproduces any of the three protected events or routes their material
ids. Both depletion channels ran alone on the M2 bank cell and on M2's sub-grid row, the sub-grid
row was re-measured on top of depletion and of both channels in processes of their own, and the
two faces the M2 matrix opened were read with instruments of their own (depth-band extrema with
the frame and material id that set each one, the global mantle minimum's own path, and a
per-excursion table for mobile melt above T = 1.5).

What M3 establishes, and why the step was worth running:

- **Water-flux depletion is the piece of the melt law the M2 sub-grid row was missing, and it
  closes the defect that rejected that row.** `--dpart 0.01` takes the row from **8,559 transfers
  / 7,876 bleeding** to **75 / 3**, from 40 re-splitting parcels (busiest 1,919) to 13 (busiest
  21), from a smallest routed host of 5e-324 km² to 1.2e-4, and returns the frozen P3.2 census
  from **13,273 splits to 5,017** against the bank cell's own 5,086 — the relaxation's cost to
  the pipeline was very nearly all bleed, and depletion removes it. Mass, enthalpy, the deposit
  and melt books and the latent identity close on every frame, the wall reads 2.680e-3, refusals
  are zero and the row times 15.002 ms/frame serially.
- **It moves the violation to the cold side of the same bound.** The row that stops the bleed
  puts **814 mantle samples below −0.5**, minimum **−1.069**, and the refractory cap does the
  same by a different route (217 samples, −0.872); both together reach **3,484 samples and
  −1.137..1.519, violating both ends of the bound in one phase**. No probe row holds −0.5..1.5.
- **The cold side is the same mechanism with the other sign, and the control already carries
  it.** The bank cell's own global mantle minimum is a 191 km² parcel at 4.2 km sitting **0.548 of
  T below the field it is in**, 0.011 inside the wall; every crossing row's minima are 1–3
  individual parcels of 36–154 km² at 4–23 km, 0.55–1.26 below their own field. The adjoint gain
  amplifies whatever increment a light parcel's stencil carries, and the increment is not always
  positive.
- **Drying alone closes less than half the bleed row.** Of its 8,559 candidates, **4,509 (52.7%)
  still sit above the fully dry solidus** — so the water budget is not the whole gap, which is
  what the refractory cap is for.
- **The refractory cap stops the bleed by force and pays for it in candidacy.** 50 delivered
  transfers from 6,604 candidates at zero bleed, but 178,181 blocked split candidates, 25,361
  blocked whole conversions, 176,087 mantle re-presentations (37× the control's) and the
  held-back population returns at 30.96 km²·Myr.
- **The mobile-melt face is a per-frame intake, not a transit law.** One excursion per clock: a
  16–21 km² fully molten parcel *rising* through a field 1.4–1.7 of T below itself, taking
  ~0.001 of H per frame with `dT = dH` exactly, overshooting the bound by 0.011 / 0.016 / 0.027
  at 50 / 100 / 500 kyr per frame. The overshoot scales with the clock, which is a per-frame
  gather reading and not a property of the ascent.
- **The deposit-scope relaxation inverts the subducted cold anomaly.** In the control, quenched
  deposits are the coldest material in every depth band, one of them holding −0.2941 → −0.2928 →
  −0.2925 while it sinks from the 250–500 km band to the 1,000–2,000 km band over 36 Myr, 0.0016
  of T in all. Under the relaxation the deep deposit minima are +0.317 and +0.399, *warmer* than
  the same bands' mantle minima. The relaxation restores light parcels toward their field in both
  directions, and the gate list does not currently read the cold one.

default. Neutrality is measured, not assumed: `baseline 0 20` reproduces the pre-edit fingerprint

cell's grid-coupled readings are therefore still the M2 bank's, and `pt-heat-tail-check.js`'s M0
and M2 sections pass unchanged.

### Depletion closes the bleed at its source

The M2 reading was that P3.2's split leaves the residue exactly at its own solidus with its
composition unchanged, so a rate that restores a light residue toward its supporting field
re-melts it every frame. `--dpart 0.01` removes exactly that: the residue's solidus rises with
what the melt took, so the residue is left *subsolidus* by its own rise and has to be re-heated
before it can split again. Measured over the row: 5,017 rises, one per split, maximum **0.4599**
of T, sum 15.4171, **mean 0.0031 per split** — a seventh of the median 0.0217 by which the
bleeding residues sit above their own solidus, and enough, compounding over a leg, to take the
transfer count from 8,559 to **75** and the bleed from 7,876 to **3**.

The frozen P3.2 census is the honest cost reading, and here it is a *credit*: the row's 13,273
splits (2.6× the bank cell's 5,086) fall to **5,017**, `whole` 5 → 2, `nMelt` 103 → 10, `nDep`
1,803 → 1,737, and the standing melt inventory halves (3,788 → 1,843 km²) because the bleed's
8,559 melt children are no longer being made. Depletion does not merely stop the degenerate route;
it returns the pipeline to the census the unrelaxed cell already had.

`D = 0.2` is the same channel weakened, and the row shows why the partition value matters: the
mean rise per split is unchanged in kind but the maximum falls to 0.1562, and the bleed only
partly closes — **872 transfers, 717 of them bleeding**, smallest host 8.1e-83 km². Between
D = 0.01 (water's own measured partition) and D = 0.2 the row goes from 3 bleeding transfers to
717. The mechanism is not marginal in its parameter.

### The refractory cap stops the bleed by force

`--cap 0.25` bounds what the water budget cannot: a parcel may shed at most a quarter of its own
mass over its life. It does stop the bleed outright (**0 of 6,604 candidates below 1e-3 km² of
host**, smallest routed host 2.8e-3) and delivers 50 transfers of 132.012416 km². The price is
candidacy, and the plan asks that it be reported rather than hidden: **178,181 blocked split
candidates and 25,361 blocked whole conversions** over 3,974 budgeted parcels, 126 of which spend
their budget; `mantleRoutes` reads 176,087 against the control's 4,719, because a blocked residue
keeps its melt fraction and presents itself again next frame. One material id (24950) accounts for
1,424 of the row's candidates. The held-back population the relaxation had removed comes back at
30.96 km²·Myr over 52 samples.

Both channels together deliver the smallest row (41 transfers, 48.298934 km², zero bleed, 595
candidates, 51,169 blocked) and the worst bound violation of any M3 cell: 3,484 mantle samples
outside, spanning **−1.137..1.519**, i.e. the mantle breaks both ends of the wall in one row. Its
zero-sum exchange also leaves the largest unreturned remainder, 29.764 km²·H worst substep against
the control's 8.386, booked and never dropped (R / wall 5.97e-5).

## 4. The route's own gate, and what drying alone can close

The plan makes the bleed count the route's own gate and asks that a candidate count never be
reported as a transfer. Both are in the §3 table: `--cap` rows have candidates and transfers that
differ by two orders of magnitude (6,604 → 50, 595 → 41), and only the `--dpart 0.01` row reduces
the transfers themselves (8,559 → 75) rather than blocking them after the fact.

The `--cap` probe's own reason for existing is a measurement on the control row, taken with the
parcel composition added to the event record (`logs/0.3.2-M3-subgrid1dep-comp.json`, fingerprint
equal to the control's 3546678272699960, so the instrument change moved nothing):

| Reading over the control row's 8,559 candidates | min | p10 | p50 | p90 | max |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| field − own (wet) solidus | +0.0081 | +0.0159 | +0.0219 | +0.0766 | +0.2338 |
| field − fully dry solidus | −0.0850 | −0.0793 | +0.0031 | +0.0184 | +0.1635 |
| T − own (wet) solidus | +0.0150 | +0.0159 | +0.0217 | +0.0753 | +0.2343 |

**4,509 of 8,559 candidates (52.7%) still sit above the fully dry solidus** — water removed
outright — and within the 7,876 bleeding candidates the count is 4,002 (50.8%). Percentiles are the
nearest-rank reading (`a[floor(q (n − 1))]` of the sorted set), which is the convention
`pt-heat-tail-check.js` asserts. Every candidate is felsic-free (`cF` = 0 throughout), so the dry
reading and the water-only reading coincide.
Drying therefore closes **47.3%** of the row, not all of it, and the measured median excess over a
parcel's own solidus is **+0.0217** of T, which is the plan's 0.022. That is the size of the gap a
water-flux partition has to cover, and it is why `--dpart 0.01`'s mean rise of 0.0031 per split
has to compound rather than close a residue in one step — and why a refractory budget is a second,
independent channel rather than a redundant one.

Two costs read out of this table that the route counts do not show:

- **On the bank cell, water-flux depletion doubles the standing melt inventory** (3,837 → 7,638

What the table says, and what it rules out:

- **The parcel is fully molten throughout (`xi` = 1) and its mass never changes** (20.63 km² over
  all 28 frames), so `T = H − meltLat` exactly and every sample satisfies `dT = dH` to 1e-12. The
  whole crossing is enthalpy intake; nothing about the ascent's decompression is required to
  explain it.
- **It takes that intake from a field 1.4–1.7 of T below itself**, and the gap grows monotonically
  more negative as it rises (−1.4467 → −1.6898). The gather delivers `sum_q W_pq cw_q inc_q` — the
  nodes' *conduction increment*, renormalized by `cw = mu_k / W_k`, not the nodes' temperature — so
  a light parcel in a cold but locally warming stencil gains. This is the draft's mechanism, in a
  phase that moves.
- **The overshoot scales with the clock** (0.0105 / 0.0156 / 0.0273 above the bound at 50 / 100 /
  500 kyr per frame) while the parcel is 16–21 km² in all three — about 1% of a ~1.8e3 km² base
  parcel. A per-frame intake into a fixed small mass, not a transit law: `H` rises ~0.0010–0.0011
  per frame at 100 kyr, peaks, and then falls as the parcel nears the surface.
- **The parcel is born inside the bound and crosses it later.** Its own dry liquidus at 186–314 km
  is 1.081–1.115, so the entry reading of ~1.501 is 0.386–0.419 of T *above* its own liquidus:
  this is accumulated intake, not a split child born hot. (That was worth checking rather than
  assuming — a born-above-the-bound liquidus would have made the wall a statement about the phase
  law instead of about the gather, and it is not.)

No M2 probe reaches this face by design: a parcel in flight is not relaxed toward its host's
field, and the deposit-scope rows never touch mobile melt. Whether a melt parcel in transit should
exchange with the field it crosses at its own rate is a question about the transit law, and it is
still open.

## 7. Face two: the shallow cold side, per band

## 8. What M3 says

1. **The defect is two-sided, and one signature runs through both sides.** A parcel's temperature
   is not bounded by the field it sits in, in *either* direction, and both departures are carried
   by parcels that are light against a base parcel and far from their own field: the hot tail
   (2.267 against a field of 0.288, in 0.90 km²), the held-back population (gap to +1.328) and the
   cold crossings (0.55–1.26 *below* field, in 36–191 km²) are the same shape with the increment's
   sign varying. That this is the `cw = mu_k / W_k` amplification is the draft's reading of the hot
   face and the natural reading of the cold one, but the gain at a cold minimum is **not measured
   here** — §9's second item is the measurement that would close it. What is measured is that M3's
   band and path instruments find the cold face where M0–M2 only read the hot one, and that the
   control was already 0.011 of T from crossing it.
2. **Depletion is necessary and not sufficient.** `--dpart 0.01` is the only candidate measured in
   three milestones that removes the degenerate route at its source *and* returns the frozen
   census to the unrelaxed cell's own — 8,559 transfers to 75, 7,876 bleeding to 3, 13,273 splits
   to 5,017 — with every book, the wall, the refusals and the frame gate intact. It is rejected on
   the bound, on the cold side, in the mantle, and on the three protected events. A melt law
   without depletion cannot host a restoring exchange; a melt law with depletion still needs the
   distribution bounded.
3. **The bound is not composition-invariant, and the report has to say which bound is meant.** A
   depletion channel changes composition, and `T` is a reading of `H` *through* composition; the
   refractory cap changes composition not at all and still crosses, so the crossings here are the
   light-parcel mechanism rather than a reading artifact. But any future channel that moves `cW`
   moves the wall's own meaning, and the −0.5..1.5 gate will need to state whether it bounds `T`
   or the enthalpy `T` is read from.
4. **Condition 5 stays.** Nothing here reopens the 300 Myr route or argues for moving a constant,
   and no probe row reproduced the three protected transfers — the same outcome M2 had, now with
   the melt law's missing piece supplied.

## 9. Next step

1. **Bound the distribution on both sides at once, on top of depletion.** The candidate that M3
   leaves standing is `--dpart` (a melt-law change with its own census gate) plus a *bounded*
   restoring exchange whose bound is the field itself rather than a constant: a parcel may not
   depart from the field it sits in by more than the increment its own stencil's mass share
   justifies. The cold crossings say the bound has to be two-sided, and the erosion floor says
   where to look first: every crossing minimum is a parcel at or below `P.eroMinM` = 50 km² inside
   `eroBand` = 120 km, i.e. material the surface law has thinned to its own limit.
2. **Read the gain, not just the mass and the margin.** The cold-side and excursion instruments
   record a parcel's mass, depth and `field − T`, which identify the mechanism but do not measure
   it. `experiments/pt-buried-deposit-melt.js` already reads the adjoint pairing's own gain
   (`cw = mu_k / W_k` over the stencil) and the parcel's mass share of that stencil; porting that
   reader into `pt-heat-tail.js`'s excursion samples and cold-minimum path would turn §3's
   inference into a measurement. It is observation-only, and it is the first act of the next step.
3. **Split the per-phase outside count into its two ends.** The combination row reports 3,484
   mantle samples outside a range of −1.137..1.519 and cannot say how many are below −0.5 and how
   many above 1.5; the harness records one count per phase. Two counters would cost nothing.
4. **Decide what the cold anomaly is for.** §7's inversion is a scope question the gate list does
   not currently read: if a descending quenched deposit holding its temperature for 36 Myr is
   geology to preserve, the deposit-scope relaxation needs a cold-side protected reading the way
   the route has its three protected transfers. If it is the same artifact as the hot tail, the
   relaxation is right and the gate should say so explicitly.
5. **The mobile-melt face is a transit-law question, and it is still open.** The measured overshoot
   is small (≤ 0.027 of T) and clock-scaled, so it belongs with the per-frame gather, not with the
   ascent. Whether a melt parcel in transit should exchange with the field it crosses at its own
   rate is the plan's own question 2 and no M3 row answers it.
6. Keep condition 5 and the deposit field filter; no constant moves to reopen the 300 Myr route.
