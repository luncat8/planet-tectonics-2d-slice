# 0.3.2 M10 — shipped drift beyond 300 Myr

## 1. The question and the controlled pair

M8 left a specific side-effect: with erosion off, the isolated P2.2 fixture's last
254-marker plate drifts at 0.01 cm/yr at 400 Myr with the deposit bound on, against
4.24 unbound. Its own-marker flow is 0.51. The integrated acceptance stops at 300 Myr;
it cannot say whether that late slowdown occurs with all shipped passes on.

M10 measures that question on **seed 1**, the fixture's seed, at 50 kyr/frame through
600 Myr. Two fresh serial processes: all shipped passes on, then the same configuration
with only `P.depBound = 0`. No shipping constant or acceptance gate changes.

Commands (prefix `experiments/logs/` on output paths):

```
node experiments/pt-drift.js 600 --shipped --out 0.3.2-M10-drift-s1-bound.json
node experiments/pt-drift.js 600 --shipped --unbound --out 0.3.2-M10-drift-s1-unbound.json
```

Banks and console records: `experiments/logs/0.3.2-M10-drift-s1-{bound,unbound}.{json,txt}`.
`experiments/pt-drift-check.js` re-derives the late aggregates and checks the pair against
its existing heat banks. The default `pt-drift.js` remains the isolated P2.2 acceptance;
`--shipped` is a report-only mode, not an extension of its demonstration-speed promise.

## 2. What was sampled

The existing owner's diagnostic is reused: per cluster with at least `P.plateMin = 64`
markers, magnitude of mass-weighted mean marker velocity after a full frame (`plate`),
then the same cluster's net flow re-gathered at its own markers, below its base, and at
150 and 300 km. A net vector speed is not the mean of individual speed magnitudes;
the 300 km mean individual speed is banked separately. All values below are cm/yr.

Samples are at the first frame whose accumulated time reaches each 100 Myr mark.
Floating accumulation makes the first frame 2001 (100.05 Myr), not 2000; subsequent
samples are 4000/6000/8000/10000/12000. This is six snapshots, **not continuous residence
or a minimum drift over each interval**. Cluster numbers are rebuilt, not persistent
plate identities, and there is no matching of cluster labels across the pair.

thermal placement pinned it.

## 4. Observation neutrality and scope of the answer

The shipped mode saves and restores all marker velocities around the probes; positions
and mapped depths are restored by the existing diagnostic. Its full state hash must be
unchanged across each observation. The stronger end-to-end test also passes:

- bound final fingerprint **5025891284893073**, exactly M8's landed s1 heat bank;
- unbound **5524785744115553**, exactly M6's pre-landing s1 control;
- final deposit melt and bound cap totals agree with those same banks.

Both live runs pass the diagnostic's two sanity lines. The saved-bank checker validates
all six sample frames, finite readings, counts, final replay and the late table's aggregates.
There is no new heat acceptance or timing claim: these are the already banked worlds,
observed for drift, with M8/M6's respective heat limitations still attached to them.

**Answer:** the isolated slowdown does not carry over to the sampled late shipped seed-1
world. It remains a real isolated-fixture result; erosion and the resulting divergent lid
history cannot be omitted from an integrated drift claim. This closes the *missing
measurement on that seed*, not a universal guarantee of mobile plates beyond 300 Myr,
not a repair of the isolated slowdown, and not evidence that changing the bound improves
drift in general. No gate is weakened and no parameter is tuned.

## 5. Next measurement

If a drift remedy is proposed, first instrument the kinematic drives and later contact
projection on a slow plate's **own world and frame** (unbound s1 at 500 Myr is now a
concrete target); read thermal/lid geometry and cluster turnover alongside those terms.
Do not tune `depBound` from a late drift count. A cross-seed late-drift distribution and
continuous slow-episode census remain needed for any general shipped-horizon claim.
The repair-adjacent heat minority, mantle-cold/mobile-melt-hot faces and finite-sweep
contact residue remain separate open owner questions.
