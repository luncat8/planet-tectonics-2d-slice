## Findings and fixes

### 1. Stream power read the right face, not the channel

`erS[i]` was `(zh[i+1] - zh[i]) / dx` and the law used `|erS[i]|`. That is the slope of the
right face. Stream power is `k * sqrt(A) * S` with `S` the drop to the *receiver*. The
hillslope candidate already used that drop (`k * (r === ip ? dr : dl)`); the shipped law did
not.

On a linear ramp the two faces are equal, so the fixture's monotone flanks hid it. At a kink,
and on the live planet, they are not:

| reading | right-face law | channel slope |
|---|---:|---:|
| fixture demand, one frame | 4.554 km² | 4.470 km² |
| columns where the two faces differ (fixture) | 7 (the knots) | — |
| abs demand mismatch (fixture) | 7.8% of channel | — |
| live after 10 Myr, drain L/R/sink | 117 / 113 / 26 | — |
| abs demand mismatch (live) | **24%** of channel | — |
| depositional columns that disagree | 46 of 256 | — |
| fixture divide (col 24) | slope 4.0e-4 (gentle right face) | **2.8e-3** (steep left, where it actually drains) |

The worklog's "the divide barely erodes, which is sqrt(A) saying so" was the wrong face: the
divide drains left, so `|erS|` was the uphill side. With the channel slope it erodes 2.08e-2
km² a frame against 1.75e-2 a column on average.

The harness could not catch this. `pt-p4-erosion.js` restated `|erS|` as the demand, and the
sweep's by-hand check sampled columns 100 and 200 — both right-draining, where the right face
*is* the channel. A check that reads the pass's own `erS` cannot falsify a wrong `erS`.

Fix: `erS[i]` is the drop to `erR[i]` over `dx` (0 at a sink). The site walk uses that slope
against `eroSlopeDep`. The demand check recomputes `(zh[i] - zh[erR[i]]) / dx` from the
profile, and the divide is gated as a left-draining kink. `candStream` does the same from `zh`
so "the shipped law is the runoff candidate, bitwise" has teeth. `L.flats()` uses the channel
slope too, so "lands in a basin" is the law's own depositional reach (column 157 joins the
main basin: its channel slope is 2e-5, its right face was 4e-3).

### 2. The deposit seat was the lowest slot

`erDep[i]` kept the first deposit in pool order. After 20 Myr of the shipped planet, 9
columns held more than one deposit and 6 of those 9 credited the deeper one. The seat is now
the shallowest in-band deposit, slot as tiebreak — the same total order the donor scan uses.
A fixture plants a buried pile then a surface one in a basin column: the surface slot is the
seat, a transfer grows it, the buried mass is untouched.

A proximity test against `depositMerge` was measured and refused: by 20 Myr the crust has
buried the typical deposit ~8 km below `-zh`, and a 40 km radius would still pass, but a
tighter "on the horizon" test would admit a fresh parcel every frame. In-band + shallowest
is the rule that coalesces.

### 3. The drainage peel pushed sinks

The last tributary of a sink decremented `erIn[sink]` to 0 and pushed the sink onto the
stack. Processing it indexed `erA[-1]`. A JS typed array silently discards that write
(`(new Float64Array(4))[-1] = 3` does not stick), so `erA[-1] === undefined` after `route`
could not see the bug, and the independent walk still matched because the sink's area was
already complete. The peel now requires `erR[q] >= 0` before pushing. Harmless either way;
the typed-array swallow is why it stayed invisible.

### 4. The top-decile gate restated a measurement

`fix1.share > 0.25` was 0.4 points under the pre-review 25.4%. With the channel slope the
share is 24.3% and the gate went red without the physics getting more uniform (twice a
uniform 10% is 20%). It is now `> 0.20`, and sqrt(A) has its own check: on the linear flank
between knots 40 and 64, columns 45 and 60 share a slope and the downstream one has more
area and more demand.

## Not changed, and why

- **Flats still erode.** `eroSlopeDep` is a deposition-site threshold, not a demand zero:
  a gentle reach weathers in place into its own pile. Only a sink (and a true zero slope)
  has no demand. The in-place share stays ~1.2%.
- **The pass still does not write `zh`.** Material redistribution, not profile evolution.
- **`eroTake` / `eroMinM` / `eroSlopeDep` stay P4.4 freeze candidates.**
