# 0.3.0 P2.2a — the x wrap and the periodic planet: worklog

Question: is the particle engine's map wrapped in x, and should it be, given the FFT solver?
Plus the first step of P2.2. Column engine untouched.

## 1. Audit: where x is periodic

- `grid.js` Stokes: FFT per row = periodic BC by construction; `v` differences wrap
  (`i + 1 === nx ? 0`); the spectral derivative `sin(k dx)/dx` is the periodic central
  difference. Walls in x would need DST/DCT and a different derivative — wrap is the
  natural (and cheapest) fit for this solver.
- `lapXX`, `downwellMix`, `scatterT`/`gatherDT` (ii folded), `cellOf` (i1 wraps),
  `fillHoles`, `pull`/`donor`, `nodeOf`, `keepInside` (x folded into [0, wrap)),
  `fluid.melt` backtrace (`meltSample` folds i0): all periodic.
- `solid.js`: pair dx folded to the nearest image; cluster centroid folded about the first
  member; rigid-fit lever arms folded. A plate on the seam is one body.
- IC: `cos(2 pi icMode x / wrap)` with integer icMode — periodic.
- **Not periodic: the view.** `render.js` raster sampled modulo wrap, but the marker
  stipple did not (a camera panned across x = 0 drew the field on both sides and markers on
  one); the camera centre drifted without bound; zoom-out went to 40 km/px = 3.2 periods,
  drawing the raster three times and the markers once.
- One tiny non-equivariance kept: `fillHoles` is an in-place Gauss-Seidel sweep that starts
  at column 0, so hole values depend (at ~1e-8 of the field heat) on where the seam is.
  Markers are unaffected to round-off; not worth a Jacobi rewrite.

## 3. Render fix (js/pt/render.js)

Stipple folds `x - cx` to the nearest image; `zoomAt` clamps kx at `wrap / w`; `zoomAt`
and `panBy` keep `cx` in [0, wrap). No allocation, one extra floor per marker.

## 4. The periodic-planet finding and the band IC

With wrap, `icMode 4` alone is four exact copies of a 4000 km box; only the fixed marker
jitter breaks it. `experiments/pt-band.js` (log `experiments/logs/0.3.0-p2.2-band.txt`),
max |T(x) - T(x + wrap/4)|:

| draw | 50 | 150 | 300 | 600 Myr | plates max | peak drift |
|------|----|-----|-----|---------|-----------|-----------|
| icBand 0 (old) | .026 | .072 | .216 | .448 | 4 | 0.67 cm/yr |
| band .6 seed 1 | .120 | .544 | .721 | .591 | 5 | 2.12 |
| band .6 seed 2 | .119 | .490 | .740 | .606 | 4 | 0.96 |
| band .6 seed 3 | .122 | .498 | .718 | .600 | 4 | 0.62 |

Shipped: `icBand 0.6` (energy fraction on k = 1..`icBandMax` 12, seeded amplitudes
0.25-1.25 and phases, normalised), 0.4 on icMode 4. Also the reset seed: before, `seed`
only moved the cosine's phase = the same planet rotated. UI: `new planet` button / `n` key
(seed++), seed on the HUD.

Honest reading: the band makes the cells unequal and the planet non-repeating, but it does
**not** fix slow plate drift (0.3-0.8 cm/yr typical at 300-600 Myr for seeds 2/3). That is
the rigid fit averaging the flow beneath a plate that spans a diverging cell pair — P2.2's
kinematics item, not the IC.

## 6. Measured along the way (for P2.2 proper)

Kernel profile, 200 frames, this box: 'cool' 300 Myr (2194 candidates) G1 2.96, G2 0.53,
G3 3.95, G4 2.71 ms. 'rb' 150 Myr (11958 candidates) G4 34 ms, `pair()` 70% of it.
Cause: in 'rb' the cold pool at the CMB sits in the last row, 8257 candidates in 256 nodes
(up to 99 per node), 1.14 M pair visits/frame — the strength law has no depth term, so a
cold pile at the base "welds". In the shipped config candidates have 20 partners median
(p10 4-14, p90 28-37): **damage accrues per pair, so its rate scales with local marker
density** (a crowded marker fails ~2x faster than a median one). P2.2 should normalise
damage by the partner count (mean excess load, kDamage x ~20 to keep calibration) — which
also makes a capped/thinned pair walk legitimate.
