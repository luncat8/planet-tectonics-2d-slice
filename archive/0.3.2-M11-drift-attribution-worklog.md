# 0.3.2 M11 — kinematic drive attribution and continuous census worklog

## Development

- Followed M10's next step: instrument the kinematic drives and contact projection
  terms on the unbound s1 slow plate at 500 Myr before proposing a drift fix; broaden
  to a seed distribution and continuous slow-episode census before promising late drift
  in general.
- Built `experiments/pt-drift-terms.js`: hooks into `CONTACT.detect` and `CONTACT.brake`
  to measure raw flow advection, slab pull, ridge push, net pre-brake velocity, contact
  boundary details (closing rate, $\Delta T$, regime classification, override identification),
  subduction override hold brake impulse ($\Delta v$), post-brake rigid velocity, and
  marker post-projection drift speed.
- Ran unbound seed 1 through 500 Myr (window $t \in [490, 515]$ Myr): saved to
  `experiments/logs/0.3.2-M11-slow-plate-s1-500myr.json`. Discovered that cluster 3 at
  frame 10000 has strong pre-brake kinematic drive (-3.49 cm/yr from -6.74 cm/yr slab pull)
  and is held stationary by the contact subduction hold brake ($\Delta v = +3.49$ cm/yr)
  because it acts as the overriding plate at two subduction trenches.
- Built `experiments/pt-drift-census.js`: measures continuous frame-by-frame plate
  kinematics, speed distributions, slow-episode durations and physical attribution
  across 600 Myr (12,000 frames) at 50 kyr/frame.
- Ran batch across seeds 1, 3, 5, 7 on both bound (`P.depBound = 0.25`) and unbound
  (`P.depBound = 0`) configurations (8 worlds). Saved banks:
  `experiments/logs/0.3.2-M11-census-{bound,unbound}-s{1,3,5,7}.json`.
