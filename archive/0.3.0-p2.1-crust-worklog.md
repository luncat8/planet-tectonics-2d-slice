# 0.3.0 P2.1 — the crust forms: worklog

Scope of this job (user request): (1) vertical resolution ~2x less and the horizontal map
~2x bigger, target 4-6 convection cells per map; (2) investigate when the crust should be
formed and how it should be visualized — at 2 Gyr the picture still looked like fluid.
Plus the standing P2 goal: the skin — plates emerging from particles as the planet cools,
connection strength proportional to temperature and age (plan §4.2).

Companion report: `archive/0.3.0-p2.1-crust-report.md`. Column engine untouched.

## 1. The map change (mesh 128x48 -> 256x24)

- Periodic width 8000 -> 16000 km (5.5 depths: four to six cells of the ~1.3-depth
  wavelength pt-conv measures at Ra 1e6; the old 2.76-depth box carried one or two).
  Default window follows to 16000 km (12.5 km/px; the lid preset is the 2x detail view).
  `icMode` 1 -> 4 so the cells arrive early at the wider map.
- Mesh 256x24: the SAME 6144 cells as 128x48 at the same 62.5 km x-node spacing (the frame
  budget survives), eta rows twice as tall (8.3 km top, 601 km base) — still five rows
  through the lid. Quality ladder in `ui.js`: [[256,24],[512,48],[1024,96]], mpc [4,2,2].
- **Pitfall found the expensive way: `params.js` defined the mesh twice** — the top-level
  `nx/ny` and a live `mesh:{nx,ny}` at the bottom that `SIM.init`/UI actually read. All
  runs before the fix silently used 128x48. Now one source (`mesh`); top-level nx/ny gone.
- Result (300 Myr, 'cool'): 11.5 ms/frame, Nu 22->40 through the lid cycle, wells 4-8 —
  the requested 4-6 cells are visible; 14 cm/yr transient plumes stay inside §2.3's
  40 cm/yr cap at 50 kyr/f.

## 2. The crust law (js/pt/solid.js, SIM.k[4])

The kinematic core of plan §4.2, reduced for P2.1 (no rest-length bookkeeping yet):

- `strength()`: age accrues at T <= TLock (0.35), resets at T >= TSoft (0.45), holds
  between (the hysteresis is load-bearing: unconditional aging clusters the whole mantle).
  `mu = (0.05 + 0.95*(1 - heat))*(1 - exp(-age/tauWeld))`, `heat = smoothstep(muLo, muHi, T)`.
  Plate strength (mu >= clusterMin 0.25) after ~5.8 Myr of being cold; full weld ~3 tauWeld.
- `clusters()`: union-find over node buckets (wrap-aware, <= 1.3 cell contact), one pass,
  no per-frame allocation. Markers pair via the half-neighbor bucket walk.
- Failure in RATE form: pair load `|(v_flow_rel . r)|/d^2` (from the flow field — the rigid
  fit that follows must not see it), elastic below `yieldRate*mu`, else
  `dmg += kDamage*(load-yield)*dt` clamped to 1; heal `dt/tauHeal` cold, reset on soft;
  `mu_eff = mu*(1-dmg)`.
- `kinematics()`: mass-weighted rigid fit (V, omega) per cluster with a robust second pass
  (outlier markers are faulted, not fitted), then the PBD projection
  `x += w*(vRigid - vFlow)*dt`, `w = smoothstep(clusterMin, 1.4*clusterMin, mu)`.
  Runs after G3 (the crust applies the rigid correction on top of advection).

## 3. The calibration (experiments/pt-load-check.js)

Loads were measured on the flow field of an intact lid (damage off), 256x24 wrap 16000
(1/Myr): 50 Myr p50 .0031/p80 .0051/p90 .0065/p99 .0147/max .22; 100 Myr .0048/.0093/
.0122/.0255; 200 Myr .0142/.0301/.0444/.0887/max 1.06. **Loads grow ~3x with lid age** —
the yield must sit above p80 of the 100-200 Myr body and below p99 of its tail.

Yield sweep at 256x24 (300 Myr): 0.06 -> 4 plates, lid 9%; 0.09 -> 1 plate; 0.12/0.18 ->
1 plate. Plate breakup lives at yield <= 0.06 here. Kept `yieldRate 0.06, kDamage 3,
tauHeal 40` (tauHeal 40 with kDamage 3: a worked seam heals in ~30 Myr — both the rift and
the suture clocks land in tens of Myr). The earlier 0.03/5/60 build eroded the lid to dust
by 600 Myr (cascade); the first eps0-based law shattered it on day one. Percentile gating
is what keeps this stable.

The load rule as shipped is bounded by the tracked cluster pair limit (64/cluster);
`csMax` flags clusters at the cap. P2.2 should replace the fit with accumulated inertia
(the current fit has no density-weighted omega) and gate pair load by mu, not cluster size.

## 4. Lid formation study (experiments/pt-lid.js)

The "when should the crust form" answer, measured on the real pipeline (log:
`experiments/logs/0.3.0-p2.1-lid.txt`):

- HOT start ('hot', new IC: uniform 1): surface cover (surface row's mug >= clusterMin)
  hits 60% at **13 Myr** and 100% at 15 Myr (the P2 gate allows 90 Myr = 30 s at the
  default clock). Lock isotherm z(T=0.35) grows 3 -> 14 -> 25 -> 48 km at 0/15/50/205 Myr;
  the strong band tracks it (row 1-3 = 8-27 km, mug 0.26-0.56; row 4+ < 0.07). lid% 5-20%.
- COOL start ('cool', the demo default): skin welds by ~10 Myr (the weld clock
  ln(4/3)*tauWeld = 5.8 Myr + margin), one plate to ~130 Myr (lid 21%), then the cycle:
  4 plates at 180 Myr, Nu 20->40, cover 82-94% (seams open).
- So: crust forms whenever rock stays below TLock for a few Myr — the visualization must
  show a THIN (25-50 km) band, not a thick shell, and the plate count cycles.

## 6. Fixture work (the suite at 256x24 + crust on)

Final state — ALL GREEN: pt-check 18/18, pt-stability 12/12, pt-melt-check 7/7,
pt-eng-conv 5/5, pt-metric 5/5, pt-conv 5/5, pt-emerge 8/8, pt-asymmetry 3/3,
pt-crust 27/27, pt-load-check 5/5, pt-lid 3/3.

What the fixtures caught and taught (details in findings-pitfalls-skills.md):

- **`SIM.k[4]` bound `SC.crust` unconditionally** at module load: `P.solid = false` was
  inert. Now the stage checks `P.solid`; pt-stability/pt-melt-check/pt-eng-conv pin
  `P.solid = false` (they gate the fluid pipeline; pt-crust has the solid one).
- **Fixtures must pin every parameter they touch**: the demo's `icMode 1 -> 4` leaked into
  pt-melt-check (blob melt* 0.116 -> 0.090, gate 0.10) and pt-eng-conv. Both now pin
  `icMode/icAmp`; pt-crust pins the shipped draw (4/0.02).
- **pt-eng-conv's velocity gate compared a spin-up snapshot to the reference's steady
  state** and was green by planform luck (plan §8 recorded 2714 nd vs 3562 at 2400 frames
  with a 1-well draw; the seeded draw gives 4 wells and reads 957 nd at the same time).
  The fixture now pins the reference's own perturbation and replays the plan's recorded
  snapshot state (Nu 30.6 vs 27.45, u 3153 vs 3562 nd — ALL PASS).
- **Settled-state study (the honest delta, carried as a printed caveat in the fixture)**:
  run to matched nondimensional time, the engine's rb convection wanders around Nu ~15 and
  u_nd ~1500 against the reference's 27.45/3562 — about 2x weak in both — and marker
  density does not close it (mpc 16: Nu 15.8). The transient peak matches/overshoots the
  reference, so the Stokes pair and scales are right; the deficit is the sustained
  boundary-layer transport (marker disorder/numerical diffusion at BL scale). Open item.
- pt-check's Nu=1 gate carries the estimator's first-cell bias (~0.7% at 128x48, ~2.7% at
  256x24 — scales with the top row's height); gate 3e-2. The §7 budget gate now measures
  the SHIPPED config at the default clock (11.2 ms/frame): double-lid states cost ~24 ms
  because the pair walk scales with strong-marker density (see below).
- A fixture's heal gate runs trivially on pristine markers — re-open the seam first;
  `SC.crust` applies only the rigid correction, so unit tests must pre-advect with the flow;
  the T=0.4 age=10 band legitimately reads mu 0.383 (the hysteresis holding strong rock);
  FLIP overshoot at seam slams reaches T ~ -0.4, so the bounded gate is -0.5..1.5.

## 7. Performance note (P2.2)

`SC.crust`'s pair walk is O(candidates x partners) and dominates when the lid is young or
double (both walls lidded, rb-style): measured 1.0 ms at 231 strong ('hot' early), 3.5 ms
at 4243 ('cool'), 14.2 ms at 8480 ('rb') on this box. The shipped single-lid config sits
at 11.2 ms/frame. P2.2 levers: thin the pair sampling (symmetric hash, 4x damage
compensation keeps the law's expectation), or sub-cell pair buckets. Documented, not
hidden; the budget gate pins the shipped config.
