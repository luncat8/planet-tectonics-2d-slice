# 0.1.9 K5 donor floor: evaluation of 35816d1 against cf98e67

## Scope

Commit `35816d1` ("0.1.9 K5 donor floor: a lateral crustal flow may not un-classify its contact")
was made by an earlier agent that continued from an old commit; its parent is main `2f168c9`.
This job compares it with main and with `cf98e67` (head of `arena/8f3437e1`: the K5 freeze
`7e86fcc`, the 0.1.8 M2 brake, and the 0.1.9 landing) and picks what is useful onto `cf98e67`.

Base: the request named `cf98e67` as "main". GitHub main is `2f168c9`. This branch was
fast-forwarded from `2f168c9` to `cf98e67` (no force), so the picks below sit on `cf98e67`.

## Decision

No engine change is picked from 35816d1.

- **Donor clamp** (`CRU.flowAvail`, `P.hFlowMin`): not picked. On `cf98e67` it adds four failed
  strict checks, all on seed 1. It does not remove next-frame reclassification and raises it on
  seed 2 (§4).
- **Narrow variant** of the clamp (only columns in a closing C-C pair inside the ordinary
  floor): not picked. Every strict verdict stays as in the base, and it cuts drain-caused
  reclassification in every run where the base has any, but it costs margin on seed 1 at
  100 kyr/frame. That trade is for the user (§7).
- **`crushHold` / `markCrushHold`**: not picked. Written, never read.
- **The five donor fixtures in `m2-check.js`**: not picked. They test the clamp rule.
- **`roadmap.md` row and bullets**: not picked. They put a kernel change under 0.1.9, which
  `cf98e67` records as "No engine kernel, constant, checkpoint or slice-format change."
- **Needle make-up report in `contact-audit.js`**: not ported now. Its volume ratio needs the
  shoulder column, which `lib.js` `r2ShapeAt` does not expose. On `cf98e67` no R2 needle fails
  at the four legs, so there is nothing yet for it to explain.

Picked, measurement only (nothing under `js/` or the pages changes):

- `experiments/k5-reclass-probe.js`: next-frame reclassification by cause, the required
  separation, and the freed-tail invariant (§4, §5).
- `experiments/k5-donor-floor-table.js`: the strict-leg table, read from logs (§3).
- `experiments/logs/0.1.9-eval-*`: the logs behind every number here.
- A pitfalls entry in `findings-pitfalls-skills.md`.

## 4. Next-frame reclassification: the freeze defers it, and most of it is not drain-caused

The freeze holds a pair's closing C-C kind from K4 to K5. The next transport re-reads it, so a
pair can change kind between frames. `experiments/k5-reclass-probe.js` counts pairs that the
freeze carries as closing C-C, that the live predicate calls ordinary, and whose gap sits inside
the ordinary floor (C-C floor 3.91 km, ordinary floor 9.38 km; `w0` 78.18 km). Each pair is labelled
by cause: a column below `P.hOceanic` (drained by K5, the 35816d1 mechanism), or both columns
above it (the plate velocities changed between the two solves). Slots are matched by index. Both
freeze sites (`finalFloor` and the topology-stable branch) run after K4's own reorder, and transport
floors before it sorts, so nothing reorders columns between a freeze and the next transport.

Source: `experiments/logs/0.1.9-eval-k5-reclass-probe-{base,clamp,narrow}.txt`. Cells are reclass
frames (pairs), drain / velocity, and the largest deficit (ordinary floor minus gap).

Reading:

- The freeze fixes the within-frame case. Next-frame reclassification remains on `cf98e67`, and
  most of it is velocity-driven: the K2 solve changes plate velocities between frames. Neither
  variant tested prevents those flips (the clamp still has 506 on the mixed schedule, the narrow
  variant 59 on seed 1). They come from the model's floor semantics at a closing-to-opening
  change, which the mistaken commit does not touch.
- Drain-caused events are real. The clamp reduces them in four of the five runs where the base
  has any (13 to 8, 11 to 3, 1 to 0, 91 to 12), but raises them on seed 2 (23 to 52). Its
  velocity-caused counts fall on seeds 1 and 2 (39 to 3, 179 to 4). These trajectories diverge
  after the first change, so those counts are not a clean effect of the operator.
- The narrow variant reduces drain-caused events in all five runs where the base has any
  (13 to 4, 11 to 0, 23 to 6, 1 to 0, 91 to 4). On seed 1 at 100 kyr it also raises the velocity
  flips (39 to 59) and costs margin (§7).
- Magnitude does not separate the two sets. The largest shift the floor applies is about the same
  in reclassification frames as in other frames (base seed 5: 4.08 km against 3.55 km), so the
  deficit is the better size measure.

## 5. Freed tail (latent, found through the clamp trajectory)

`COL.k4`'s topology hand-off clears `edge`, `edgeAge` and `edgeShort` only for surviving slots.
When `nCol` falls, the freed slots keep their old `edgeShort`. The m2 invariant "absorbed shortening
sits only on live inter-plate boundaries" also requires zero past `nCol`, and the clamp trajectory
breaks it: seed 1, mixed schedule, 594 frames with a stale tail
(`0.1.9-eval-k5-reclass-probe-clamp-no-tail-fix.txt`).

The stale value is inert. The reclassification counts are identical with and without the fix
(480 frames, 518 pairs, 5.90 km in both), and the fix leaves the clamp's four strict logs
byte-identical. The base shows no stale tail on any of the six schedules
(`0.1.9-eval-k5-reclass-probe-base.txt`). The fix is one loop that zeroes `[count, n + appended)`
after `st.nCol = count`. It is not picked, because nothing on `cf98e67` triggers it. Any K5 change
that shifts the trajectory could, so the fix belongs with that change.

## 7. Open decision and next step

Status: option 2 was tested on eight seeds in `archive/0.1.10-narrow-hold-worklog.md` and
rejected (six runs the base passes fail, the checkpoint spin-up gate fails, no base failure is
fixed). Option 1 is the recommendation there; option 3 remains open.

Whether drain-caused next-frame reclassification is worth a fix is a physics and modelling call.
Options:

1. **Accept it.** At 100 kyr/frame the base has 11 to 23 drain-caused events per 5000 frames on
   seeds 1, 2 and 5, and the largest deficit of any reclassified pair in those runs is 3.5 to
   7.2 km. Each event needs the floor solve to push its pair apart by that deficit within one frame.
2. **Narrow variant** (`markCrushHold` on closing C-C pairs inside the ordinary floor, using the
   frozen kind; `flowAvail` subtracts `P.hFlowMin` only on flagged columns). It cuts drain events
   to 0 to 6 per run. On seed 1 at 100 kyr it moves R1 0.48 to 0.84, R2 needle 1.39 to 1.44 and
   R2 width 95.4% to 93.6%. It needs more seeds and the full suite before any pick.
3. **Hysteresis on the closing kind.** A new threshold rule with its own gates. A planning item.

Next step: choose 1, 2 or 3. For 2 or 3 the work is a 0.1.10 item (0.1.9 is landed), measured on
all four legs and with the probe over more seeds, with R1, R2 and R5 margins as the acceptance
test. The probe and the table in this branch are the instruments for that.
