## Review of P3.3

The P3.3 implementation conserved chamber-to-vent transfers, but its shipped geometry could not
open a conduit: `yCham/chJoin/chSpan = 80/400/400 km` left the largest measured head below its
roof load. That made the live page stop at vent scheduling. The frame assertions also still used
the previous host bar. The requested review therefore made two prerequisite decisions before
adding flight:

- fix the host frame gate at **20 ms** in `pt-check` and `pt-p3-budget`, with no further tuning
  debate in this milestone;
- move storage into a lower-crustal geometry and give a chamber a regional, rather than
  planet-scale, footprint.

The first working geometry was `40/250/200 km` and emitted at 6.35 Myr. The short closure probe
passed, but the full regressions exposed an accumulated shallow-field undershoot (`Tmin -0.624`
at 150 Myr). A footprint sweep measured 220/230/240 km at `Tmin -0.492/-0.418/-0.302` over the
same 150 Myr trial. The final footprint is **230 km**: it keeps a useful eruption population at
20–30 Myr while retaining margin under the existing long-run bounds. The surface exchange target
was also corrected from a trial value of 0.2 to the model's actual normalized surface boundary,
0. It now relaxes from either side, so a numerically overcooled deposit receives, rather than
silently refusing, the matching wall heat. The final 150 Myr enthalpy run reads
`T = -0.089..1.134`; the 300 Myr crust run reads `-0.191..1.135`.

## Implementation

### Conserved material path

`js/pt/flight.js` owns the P3.4 path:

1. `launch` admits a pool slot before touching vent stock. It then moves one proportional share
   of vent `M/H/L/F/W` into one airborne material batch and increments a matched
   `airDr/airCr` pair. A full pool leaves the complete stock in the vent.
2. `advance` integrates exact constant-acceleration arcs on elapsed wall seconds. Horizontal
   position wraps periodically. An omitted headless interval is 1/60 s; a supplied interval is
   capped at 0.05 s, so a resumed tab cannot tunnel through the ground.
3. `land` snaps the batch to the interpolated physical horizon `y = -zh(x)`, never to the
   visually exaggerated horizon. It changes that same slot to `dep`, clears motion and bond/load
   history, starts age at zero, and increments matched `landDr/landCr` counters.
4. A compatible nearby deposit may absorb the landing. The material remains in the pool; there
   is no renderer-only edifice copy.
5. `cool` relaxes shallow deposits toward the fixed surface boundary on geological time and
   books the exchange to `hLedger`, `wall`, and the sensible ledger. Deposits stay deposits while
   cooling, so G5 cannot immediately reintrude the lava it just landed.

The earlier attempt to route a hot deposit through ordinary G5 failed for exactly that reason:
the next segregation pass returned it to a chamber. Merely disabling G5 was not enough either;
passive grid conduction left the batch molten for the demonstration interval. Explicit surface
exchange plus G5's mantle-only source rule is the smallest path that lets the batch freeze and
then use the existing age/strength/cluster law.

### Two clocks

`SIM.step(wallDt)` keeps geological kernels under `SIM.dt`, then emits one batch per active vent
for the rendered geological interval and advances all airborne material by `wallDt`. The browser
passes requestAnimationFrame elapsed time; headless runs use a deterministic 1/60 s default.
`SIM.wall(wallDt)` advances flight and refreshes phase inventory while geology is paused. Cooling,
vent supply, age and welding do not run in that path.

This distinction required three regression clarifications:

- `pt-stability` disables eruptive supply and passes zero wall time because it gates equivalence
  of the fluid substeps, not equivalence of 360 and 3600 presentation frames;
- `pt-wrap` uses zero wall time for its translated geological twin, then compares floating
  throughputs to `1e-9` rather than demanding JSON-string identity at a threshold;
- `pt-surface` pins its historical non-erupting chamber fixture because it gates the independent
  P2.3 surface law; the integrated terrain collision is covered by `pt-p3-flight`.

### Merge semantics and fixed storage

`PTPOOL.compatible` now requires equal phase and configurable felsic/water tolerances. Merge
mass-weights velocity, thermodynamic/composition state, position, age, strength and damage;
pair-load scratch is cleared. It preserves the older material id. A dense-pool edge case was
also fixed: when argument `a` occupied the tail and `b` was removed, compaction moved the
survivor into `b`; `merge` now returns that actual slot instead of the cleared tail index.

The pool's field table is resolved during state setup, so the first live split or flight does not
allocate its JavaScript field array in the hot path. Airborne velocity reuses `vx/vy` in
presentation km/s; those parcels are excluded from every geological routing mask and motion is
zeroed at landing.

### Rendering and diagnostics

Airborne effusive and explosive batches have bright, distinct marks; deposits use a rust mark.
Air and deposits already carry the unexaggerated terrain height, so rendering adds only the
remaining visual relief factor. The physical collision remains independent of the display toggle.
Both mirrored pages load `flight.js`, retain byte identity, and name flight/deposit colours in the
legend. The HUD reports airborne and landed counts, emitted and landed throughput, and the wall
interval cap whenever air exists.

State reset and the complete fingerprint include emission/landing counters and both new transfer
pairs. A headless lid capture at 10 Myr is `archive/p34-lid-10Myr.png`.

## Gate results

`pt-p3-flight` covers the rules before its live run:

- vent-to-air mass, enthalpy, melt, felsic and water inventories all close;
- a full pool refuses before debit;
- the analytic x/y/vy arc, wall clamp, slider independence and paused flight pass;
- one effusive clast lasts 0.842 s with a 3.54 km apex; one explosive clast lasts 1.400 s with
  a 9.74 km apex; continuous supply keeps the fountain visible for 3.47 s;
- a clast lands at `-3 km` on a 3 km test plateau even though the renderer draws 2x relief;
- landing keeps one parcel and one mass, compatible material merges, incompatible composition
  stays separate;
- cooling and restoring heat both close against the wall transaction; the cooled batch remains
  a deposit;
- at `tauWeld = 20 Myr`, a cold deposit has `mu = 0.632` and shares the substrate cluster;
- a fixed geological/wall sequence replays to the same complete-state hash.

The shipped 20 Myr live leg now gives:

- first ejecta at **6.55 Myr**;
- 666 drains/emissions, 575 landings, 570 compatible deposit merges;
- peak 167 airborne parcels and 5 final deposit parcels;
- zero refusals and zero blocked conduits;
- global mass relative residual 0; enthalpy residual `-6.12e-17`;
- exact debit/credit pairs, with latent-identity residual `6.75e-9`.

At 100 Myr, `pt-p3-erupt` observes 5,323 shipped drains/emissions and 5,057 landings, with all
three transfer pairs exact. The active-load budget carries 91 air / 5 deposit parcels before its
timed window and 105 / 5 after it; it measures **14.60 ms/frame** against the fixed 20 ms gate,
with zero refusals. The reserve-filled synthetic rung measures 14.54 ms; `pt-check` measures
13.4 ms for the shipped frame.
