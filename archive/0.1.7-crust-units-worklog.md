## 2. The unit fix

`js/crust.js`: `CRU.belt` and `CRU.delaminate` dropped `excess * 1000`, and the peel
became the backward-Euler shed `over · k·dt/(1+k·dt)` in both laws — exact linear rate at
small `k·dt`, bounded at any `dt`, no oscillation at the 100–200 kyr legs. The old
`over * 500` cap was removed: it was 1000× the intended `over * 0.5`, so it did not bound
the withdrawal at the tested frame rates.
`CRU.delaminate` moved to the **end** of `CRU.k5` so the frame ends at the ceiling
instead of refilling past the peel. `CRU.belt` gained the receiver-headroom clamp the
0.1.6 §4 named (`beltFeedTo`: never fill past `crustMax`, a full receiver only silences
its own side). `js/params.js`: `kBelt 0.12 /Myr of the excess (m)`, `kDelam 45 /Myr`
(τ 22 kyr — "the ceiling is reached within a frame or two" is now true), comments kill
"per km".

Measured per step: r4-check after the plain ×1000 removal (belt builds, root 3.1 →
29.7 km, closing still rises 18.4 → 32.1); peak-gate variant (no effect, rejected);
`vColl ×8` (brake bites early: 5.8 mm/yr, 58% — then relaxes with the belt); kBelt
sweep {0.12, 1, 2, 4, 12, 40} on the audit (width gate peaks at kBelt 1 with 89%, the
needle never passes). The sweep's event-memory result is invalidated by the audit's Set
iterator/time-gap bug (see review follow-up) — another reason this release settles units
but does **not** re-tune the collision.
