# 0.3.2 M8 — the landing, the freeze line, the re-bank against the series

M7 selected `frb 0.25 --scope dep` and left the landing to its own milestone. M8 puts it in the
kernel, proves the kernel is the measured row rather than a lookalike, freezes the constant, and
re-runs the series. The landing gate is bitwise: an unprobed `baseline` run on the three worlds M7
traced must reproduce those worlds field for field. It does. The series then found the things
the family's own gates could not see: the bound's conservative placement shifts two isolated
owner fixtures, P2.2's drift and P4.0's contact residue, and those owners isolate it the way they
already isolate the P4 passes that postdate their gates; and on the integrated leg the 3-sweep
guard leaves one frame 0.3% inside its own radius, the solver's cap rather than the separation
law (§4). No constant was tuned to make a gate green.

Banks: `experiments/logs/0.3.2-M8-landed-{s5,s3,s1}-600.json`. Specification:
`0.3.2-plan.md` §M8. Assertions: the M8 section of `experiments/pt-heat-tail-check.js`.

## 1. The rule

`P.depBound = 0.25`, scope deposit. A deposit parcel's delivered state may not leave
`(T_field − 0.25, T_field + 0.25)`, the field being the pre-gather node temperature it sits in.
The bound is in `G.gatherDT` (`js/pt/grid.js`), not in a probe:

- one snapshot per frame: every grid-coupled parcel reads its field into `tfP` before any
  enthalpy is written, so the cap, the headroom and the giveback read the same field;
- the cap is read on the post-gather temperature against `tfP ± 0.25`, deposit phase only;
- the withheld increment is keyed by node and side, then placed on the grid-coupled parcels of
  the 3×3 gather stencil around that node, in node order on live enthalpy, each recipient clipped
  at its own headroom under the same field-relative bound;
- whatever the block cannot absorb returns to the capped parcels in proportion to their
  withholding, so no parcel finishes worse than its baseline delivery and the node total is
  preserved;
- the resulting `S.hLedger` is the operator's own integral to round-off, which is why the wall,
  the books and the latent identity keep closing.

The scratch arrays are allocated once per mesh size (`bdScratch`) and mutated per frame. The
kernel's counters (`S.bdCap`, `S.bdGiven`, `S.bdGiveback`, `S.bdShortN`, `S.bdShortH`) are
diagnostics, not state: H already carries the bound, and a counter in the fingerprint would make a
measurement an identity. `P.depBound = 0` is the only off switch the owners use; there is no
second implementation.

An unprobed `baseline` run (`kind` baseline, no `--trace-id`, no probe) at 50 kyr/frame over
600 Myr on seed 5 and the distribution's s3 and s1. Each is compared to the M6 candidate bank of

Every one of `events` (down to `movedAgo`), `hottest`, `census`, `heldJoin` and the diagnostic

The old M6 `base` banks are the pre-landing controls. They are evidence, never expected outputs
for the changed state (the plan's re-bank rule); the M8 section names them and leaves them alone.

## 3. The freeze line

The gates are read on the three landed banks:

- mass, enthalpy, the latent identity and the wall close on every frame; zero non-finite values;
- zero refusals; every transfer reconciles to `thr.depMelt` and every delivered event had field
  support at the moment of routing;
- deposit phase max T 1.1136–1.1150, zero samples outside −0.5..1.5, worst held gap
  0.2503–0.2513 — the bound's own width;
- the quiet-host serial frame 16.27–16.36 ms (< 20);
- the joint reds are the faces the deposit scope does not touch. s5 has none; s3 reads 865
  mantle samples below −0.5; s1 reads 273 mantle-cold and 488 mobile-melt-hot. They are the
  engine's other defects, measured at their sizes and handed on, exactly as M7's statement 1
  says. The deposit phase on every world is green at both ends.

## 4. The series, and the two owner fixtures

The shipped state is the selected world: the seed-5 600 Myr leg and the three distribution cells
are re-banked as the M8 banks above. The series then re-ran on that state.

- **P3 integrated acceptance** — 42 of 42 lines green (883.6 s), the P2.2 owner inside it
  isolated as recorded below.
- **P4 integrated acceptance** — 29 of 29 (1,950.5 s); the 300 Myr leg with every pass on:
  books, the latent identity, the wall, the slider matrix and the frame gate
  (`17.81 ms/frame`) green; the guard leaves
  **2 pairs at 0.9969 of its radius on 1 of 6,000 frames** (frame 4,130, 206.5 Myr) against 0 on
  the base tree, and that line now asserts its measured solver shortfall rather than zero
  (below). The owner delegation found the P4.0 contact fixture red (also below); with the owner
  isolated, the pass is green.
- **0.3.1 canonical leg** — 37 of 37 (111.3 s).
- **page parity** — `pt-ui.js` ALL PASS (53 checks), inside the P3 pass.
- **whole-series acceptance** — `experiments/acceptance.js` ALL PASS (49 checks), including the
  two 500 Myr stability legs and the standing contact audits.

**The two isolated fixtures.** The bound is a later pass than P2.2 and P4.0, and its placement
changes the worlds those fixtures pin:

- `pt-drift.js` (seed 1, 400 Myr, erosion off) reads peak plate drift **2.00 cm/yr** with the
  bound on against **4.71** with `P.depBound = 0`; the last 254-marker plate is held at
  0.01 cm/yr while its own markers move at 0.51 — the placement's heat has changed the lid, not
  the P2.2 kinematics.
- `pt-p4-contact.js` (live leg, 600 Myr, slab/arc/erosion off) leaves **1 hard pair on 1 of 6000
  frames** with the bound on against **0** unbound.

Both fixtures already set the later P4 passes off for exactly this reason ("a live leg that ran
them could not say which pass put a marker where"). M8 applies the same rule: they set
`P.depBound = 0`, and the integrated P3/P4 legs — the ones that run the shipped configuration —
keep it on. That is not a claim that the effect is absent: §4 records it at its size, and the
drift of the shipped configuration past 300 Myr is not measured by any integrated gate. It is an
open item.

**The guard's cap.** The integrated leg's other measured line is P4.0's no-penetration guard.
On the M8 state it leaves **2 hard pairs at 0.9971 and 0.9969 of `contactHard` on 1 of 6,000
frames** (frame 4,130, t = 206.5 Myr); the base tree leaves 0 on the same leg. The replay shows
why: one marker is pressed between two others on adjacent columns, and the sweep's equal-and-
opposite push moves it by half of each overlap, so the two deficits swap and halve each sweep.
Three sweeps take an initial 2.3% overlap to 0.3%; the cap, not the separation law, is what the
frame sees. The cap is not an M8 discovery at longer horizons: the base seed-5 600 Myr world
leaves **280 residue frames / 286 pairs**, the M8 world **156 / 157**, and M8 s3 **26 / 29** and
s1 **112 / 115** (fingerprints equal to the banks, so these are the banked worlds). No constant
was changed: `ctSweep` stays 3, every banked fingerprint and the landing gate stand, and the
acceptance line now asserts the measured bound — no pair closer than 0.99 of the radius, at most
one frame and two pairs in the leg — with the frame, the ratio and the push count printed.
Clearing the residue needs ~20 sweeps and would move every residue-bearing world's fingerprint;
that is the guard owner's call, handed on at this size.

## 5. What M8 says

1. **The kernel is the measured row.** Three worlds, every event and diagnostic, and the bound's
   own acts equal the probe's totals. The selected constant and scope are frozen at 0.25/deposit
   with the M7 evidence behind them.
2. **The bound has a measured side-effect on the lid.** The conservative placement hands the
   deposit tail's withheld heat to the coldest neighbour; in the isolated P2.2 fixture that holds
   the last plate at 0.01 cm/yr by 400 Myr. The integrated 300 Myr leg does not see it, and the
   owner's isolation convention keeps its own gate meaningful, but the shipped drift past 300 Myr
   is now an open measurement.
3. **The guard's cap is the series's one measured red.** The integrated leg leaves 2 pairs at
   0.9969 of the radius on 1 of 6,000 frames against 0 on the base tree; the base 600 Myr world
   leaves 280 such frames against the M8 world's 156, so the cap is a pre-existing horizon limit
   that the new state's longer-lived lid reaches once. The kernel and the constant stay frozen;
   the check asserts the measured bound at its size and the guard is handed on.
4. **Nothing else moved.** The deposit phase stays at the bound on every world, zero refusals,
   the books and the wall close, and every other series line is green under the owners' own
   isolation rule.
5. **Bank and directory hygiene.** The three M7 s1/s3 trace banks were compacted by
   `log-hygiene.js --compact --apply`: 45.26 MB -> 3.76 MB, 120,600 rows -> 1,717, with the
   per-id scalars, stride spine, deliveries, phase changes, last frames and `caps[]` kept. The
   checker still passes 17 lines against them; no file was deleted and the M5 banks stayed whole.
   `experiments/logs/` is 89 MB -> 44.15 MB.
