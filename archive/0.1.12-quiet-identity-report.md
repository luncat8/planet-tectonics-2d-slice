# 0.1.12 quiet-frame identity — the censored population, identified

Measurement only. No engine constant, physics rule, checkpoint field or slice-format byte moved;
`experiments/contact-audit.js` gains two report-only flags (`--quiet-identity`,
`--drain-event`) and `experiments/quiet-identity-table.js` reads the saved matrix. The four
strict legs and the whole suite re-accept unchanged (`archive/0.1.12-quiet-identity-worklog.md`).

0.1.9 corrected the quiet-frame matcher, reached zero unmatched observations over 32 worlds and
still had to leave **2,883 observations censored** by its 10% width-change exclusion, so it
selected no threshold (`archive/0.1.9-quiet-distribution-report.md`). Its own next work was to
establish material identity through re-partition before reading those as quiet changes. This is
that measurement.

## 1. What the censored population is

**It is not a quiet re-partition. It is the kernel's sliver retirement — `COL.drain`, intent 3 —
running on a frame the audit calls quiet.**

Under `--quiet-identity` the audit certifies every quiet pair before reading it:

| claim | 32 worlds (seeds 1–16 × 50/100 kyr/frame, 5000 frames) |
| --- | --- |
| quiet pairs / quiet frames | 79,856,680 / 152,233 |
| C1 neighbour continuity held | 79,400,392 (certified) |
| beside a draining record or a sliver | 456,288 (read apart, never folded in) |
| **beside a different record** | **0** |
| the width decomposition's residual | **0 m** over all certified pairs |
| the advection law's residual (`advD = colU·dt`) | **0.0000 m** |
| observations the 10% exclusion censors | 2,883 — 0.1.9's number exactly |
| …of them on a frame that changed its record set | **2,882** |
| …of them on a certified quiet frame | **1** (seed 8 @100 kyr) |
| quiet frames that changed their record set behind the detector | **1,887 of 152,233 (1.24%)** |
| the kernel's own intents on those frames | drain **1,801**, birth 0, consume 0, accrete 0 |
| …that also had the event cadence called / removed a ghost | 249 / 1,790 |

So the matcher is right (no pair sits beside a different record, and the territory book closes
exactly), and the censored population is one mechanism: a trench that has closed both of a
sliver's gaps to the crush floor retires the sliver, its record leaves the set, and its
neighbour takes the territory in one frame. The audit's event definition counts births and
consumes (`dSites`), and `COL.drain` is deliberately absent from it, so those frames are quiet
by definition and their pops land in R1's background.

The single surviving censored pair is a real quiet re-partition: on seed 8 @100 kyr one
certified pair crosses the 10% width bound and contributes 111 m to the raster split, where the
gate reads 1977 m — it never sets a background anywhere in the matrix.

## 3. The mechanism, from the code and from one frame

`COL.intents` (`js/columns.js` l.913) fires intent 3 on a ghost whose two gaps are at or below
`P.gFloor·P.w0` (≈ 9.375 km), and its own comment says retiring it "moves each neighbour's
surface by metres". `COL.drain` empties the marker's stack and `gather` drops the record; the
neighbour's Voronoi width jumps by the retired record's whole territory, and `COL.k4`'s quiet
branch rescales its beds by `oldW/newW` to conserve volume — so the thickness halves and
`SURF.elev` (`zRef + buoy − therm + zDyn`) drops by kilometres in one frame. A birth has
`COL.rampZ`; a retirement has no ramp at all.

Frame 1271, seed 11 @100 kyr (`scratch/probe-frame.js`, gitignored): record 1 is a zero-crust
ghost `x 50.8 w 43.7 h 0.0`; the drain takes 524 records to 523 and neighbour 0 goes
`w 34.2 → 73.2 km`, `h 73.9 → 34.6 km` (volume 2527 → 2533 km², 0.21% — the conservation is
exact) while its drawn elevation drops ~5.9 km and its neighbours sit at −4.9 km. The comment is
three orders of magnitude off.

## 4. What the certified quiet population really moves

With identity restored (`dhId = |hNew − hOld·wOld/wNew|`, exact because volume is conserved on a
quiet frame), the residual material change on certified pairs is real crustal flow, not
territory:

- identity-corrected drawn thickness max **2457..16476 m** (median 8406) certified, against
  435..5517 m (median 1859) beside a draining record;
- the worst rows are felsic: seed 1 @50 kyr, frame 861 x 12732.0 km, width 43.8 → 42.6 km (2.6%),
  thickness 74.7 → 63.2 km, volume 17.5%, fel/maf/sed **13435 m / 0 m / 19 m** — K5's belt feed
  moving felsic crust, with mafic and sediment untouched;
- exceedance over certified pairs: >1 km 19,516 identity-corrected against 23,174 drawn,
  >5 km 329 against 237, >10 km 18 against 12;
- C2's worst *relative* volume change is 17.4..164.5%, but it sits on near-zero-crust columns
  (seed 1 @100 kyr, frame 470 x 12105.8 km: drawn 0.1 → 0.2 km), so the relative reading needs
  the absolute one beside it — which is why the report prints the row's thickness;
- the drawn surface per record splits (32 worlds): territory unchanged 510..3097 m, inside the
  exclusion 578..2506 m, censored 0..111 m, uncertified 436..3012 m, record set changed
  **1594..5897 m — and the gate reads exactly that bucket in every world**. With a retirement
  counted, that bucket falls to 0..1990 m and the gate reads 628..3097 m instead.

## 5. Decision

1. **No threshold is selected.** The 10% width exclusion is not an identity filter: once a
   retirement counts as an event it has 1 observation left to censor in 32 worlds. There is
   nothing to tune, and 0.1.9's "do not gate quiet width/thickness yet" stands with a better
   reason than before.
2. **`--drain-event` is measured, not landed.** `experiments/acceptance.js` runs the four strict
   legs and requires exit 0, so a definition that turns R1 red cannot land on its own — it lands
   with the engine remedy that clears it.
3. **The engine artifact is named for its own plan** (roadmap §3): a retirement hands a whole
   territory back in one frame with no ramp, where a birth gets `COL.rampZ`. Either ramp the
   absorbing record's `zDyn` the way a birth does, or hand the territory back over frames. Until
   then R1's green on all four legs is a documented false green: the background is the event.
4. **The quiet-frame drawn thickness and width change stays reported, not gated**, now with the
   certified reading (2.5..16.1 km, median 7.6, over 32 worlds) and the knowledge that the
   remaining population is K5 felsic flow: up to 11.2 km of identity-corrected thickness per
   frame at 50 kyr/frame (seed 4) and 16.5 km at 100 kyr/frame (seed 3).

0.4.2 Phase C remains held.
