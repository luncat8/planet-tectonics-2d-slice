The local K5 gradient operator is implemented and directly tested, but the R2 candidate is **not accepted**: the production value remains `P.kBeltGradient = 8`, and current strict default audits still fail on three of four legs. No thresholds were relaxed. The full R4 matrix and mass/replay suite were run; R4 remains open. No brake was considered or changed.

## Local operator and fixture

`CRU.collapse` now runs `CRU.beltLocal` after bulk `CRU.belt`. The local term gathers yield-limited face fluxes over the collision catchment using `excess * geometricWidth * kd/(1+2*kd)`, then limits donor felsic inventory and receiver headroom before moving real stack volume with `COL.collapseMove`. Its work arrays are fixed-capacity buffers.

`experiments/m2-check.js::beltGradientFixture` calls only `CRU.beltLocal` (not `CRU.belt` or `CRU.collapse`). It checks the small-dt face-flux formula and shoulder volume shares, mass ledgers, receiver headroom, and a large-dt no-gradient-reversal case. It does not assert coarse-step subdivision invariance for nonlinear large steps. These direct checks pass.

## M2 floor diagnosis

`COL.finalFloor` is called after a topology-changing K4 pass and after a topology-changing `COL.events` callback (after reclassification and trench update). It is **not** called after K5.

The 500-Myr / 100-kyr trajectory fails its ordinary floor even though the final topology passes. Stage minima (K3 / K4 / event callback / final-floor projection / K5) are `0.1200 / 0.1200 / 0.1200 / 0.1200 / 0.0678 w0`; the required ordinary bound is `0.1188 w0`. At frame 3229, pair 415 has a `0.0678 w0` gap. Immediately before K5 it qualifies as closing C-C (`min hFel = 8.47 km`). K5's gravitational-collapse plus bulk-belt stages lower it to `8.04 km`; the new local-gradient transfer then lowers it to `6.41 km`. Arc growth, LIP growth and delamination leave that value unchanged. Since `P.hOceanic = 8 km`, the unchanged `COL.isClosingCC` predicate stops granting the smaller crush floor and the same gap becomes an ordinary-floor violation. The minimum closing C-C gap is `0.0503 w0` (required `>0.0495`). The final topology is `0.2014 w0` ordinary / `0.1083 w0` closing C-C, and the 50-Myr / 10-kyr trajectory passes. The endpoint is not a trajectory pass.

A post-K5 floor call is deliberately not added: an earlier trial worsened minima and caused an R3 failure. The current harness records stage and pair/classification details so any new correction can be justified against the full unchanged gates.

Log: `experiments/logs/0.1.8-local-profile-m2-check.txt` (289 checks, one ordinary-trajectory-floor failure).
