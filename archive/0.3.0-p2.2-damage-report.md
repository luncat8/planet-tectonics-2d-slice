# 0.3.0 P2.2 step 1 — report

**Two deliverables this session: the switch-bar UI (separate commit) and P2.2's first
physics item, the damage rate.**

## 1. Toggle buttons show their state

Every switch in both pages now carries `aria-pressed="true"` when it is enabled and is lit by
one CSS rule (teal fill/border on the dark bar). The attribute is the *only* copy of the
state — `ui.js` / `pt/ui.js` read it back from the renderer's own flag, so a button cannot
disagree with what is drawn. The three camera presets on each page are a radio group: click
or key lights one, and any manual pan, wheel or single-axis slider puts the whole group out
because the camera no longer is a preset. The particle page's quality button now names its
level (`quality 2/3`) and lights above the light mesh; `g` re-ticks the scale-lines box.

**A real bug fell out of gating it.** `particles.html` had no headless harness at all. The
new `experiments/pt-ui.js` (36 checks — DOM parsed from the page, scripts loaded in the
page's own order inside `vm`, every button and key driven) caught, on its first run, that the
quality *button* called `cycleMesh` — which re-applied the level it was already on. So the
button did nothing except rebuild the mesh, and `SIM.mesh()` rebuilds by re-initialising and
**resetting the planet**: a control that silently threw the run away. Fixed (`nextQuality`)
and gated.

`experiments/smoke.js` (the column engine's page) grew the same checks for its mesh button,
presets and scale-lines box, and its DOM stub now reads the attributes `index.html` actually
ships: 64 checks, ALL PASS.

## 2. Damage no longer scales with marker density

`solid.js` charged damage **per pair**, so a marker's failure rate was the sum over its
partners — and the number of partners is a property of the Lagrangian sample, not of the
rock. Measured on an intact lid (extended `experiments/pt-load-check.js`): partner counts
run p10/p50/p90 = 11/35/51 at 200 Myr, and the rank correlation between a marker's damage
rate and its partner count is **0.45-0.73 under the per-pair law**. The shipped law charges
the marker's *mean* excess instead (the pair integral and count are accumulated during the
walk, applied in one pass after it) and the same correlation drops to **0.12-0.19**.

The constant is measured, not fitted: P2.1's per-pair `kDamage 3` times the ratio of the two
laws' aggregate rates on the same run (24.6x at 50 Myr, 24.9x at 100, 33.9x at 200) gives
`kDamage = 90`, and a gate holds it inside that band. Side benefit: the pair walk is now
read-only, so a marker's bond decision no longer depends on the order the buckets were
visited.

**Behaviour did not move** — which is the point of a rescale. pt-crust 27/27 (live 300 Myr:
lid 8%, plates 4, Nu 38.4, vs lid 10%, plates 4, Nu 38.3 before), pt-lid 3/3 (lid at 13 Myr),
pt-band at 300 Myr: plates 4/4/5, drift 0.64/0.66/0.70 cm/yr (was 4/4/4, 0.47/0.96/0.62),
pt-wrap 18/18, pt-check 18, pt-stability 12, pt-melt-check 7, pt-eng-conv 5, pt-asymmetry 3.
G4 costs 1.90 ms at 200 Myr (the extra pass is inside the noise); the frame is 7.36 ms.
