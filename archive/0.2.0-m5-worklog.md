## 3. Two reds the every-frame K9 sweep found

`save-bench.js` samples `SIM.diag()` every 100 frames and the HUD samples it at 2 Hz, so a
one-frame window could always hide. Sampling every frame on five legs (15 000 frames, both
clock modes, both rates) turned up two, and both were real:

**(a) `sorted` red on every frame that consumed a column.** `COL.k4`'s compaction reuses
`S.sortOrder` as its list of *surviving source indices*, so after a death the array is not
a permutation of `0..nCol-1` and the diag's permutation clause read it as an unsorted
world. The columns themselves were monotone; the buffer's contract was simply narrower than
the clause assumed, and the first reader to rely on it (the M4 diag) got it wrong. Fixed at
the source: after `COL.map` is built the pair is left as the identity the records now have,
which is what the next transport starts from anyway (`js/columns.js`).

**(b) `gap` red on the frame a collision ended.** The floor solve holds a closing C-C pair
at `crushFloor`; the classifier then declares the same pair neutral inside the frame (it
started to diverge); the frame ends with a 5.8–8.0 km gap judged against the *ordinary*
floor. The frame's classification snapshot was being taken after the classifier, in `SIM`'s
K4 slot, so it recorded a verdict no floor solve ever used. Fixed by taking the snapshot
where the geometry is settled — at the end of `COL.transport`, after the gather has
renumbered the slots and before the classifier runs — and by splitting the two readings
apart: `COL.closingCC` is the live rule (what K4's conveyor branch still uses, so its
behaviour is unchanged), `COL.isClosingCC` is the snapshot (what judges the settled
geometry). The redundant re-freeze in the K4 slot is gone; `freezeFloorClass` now always
evaluates the live rule, so a freeze can replace the one before it.

Both fixes are **geometry-neutral**, which is checked rather than asserted: a
`contact-audit 5000 5 100 --strict` run before and after is byte-identical apart from its
`ms/frame` line, so no tuned number in this milestone moved because of them. After them,
all five legs are green on every frame.

## 4. Eruptive tuning: measured, and no constant moved

`experiments/vent-tune-sweep.js` sweeps the one knob the design leaves open — the km³ →
section-m² scale `P.venKm3M2`, which carries `Vbirth`/`Vch`/`Vdie` and the drain constant
derived from them, while keeping the design's 5 : 1 : 0.1 ratios — over two live legs:

| scale | births | duty % | max episode (cells²) | edifice (px) | supply (m²) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ×0.25 | 53 / 89 | 37.6 / 37.7 | 0.60 / 2.96 | 1.0 × 0.6 | 2.9e6 / 5.6e6 |
| ×0.5 | 31 / 60 | 25.3 / 27.2 | 1.01 / 2.99 | 3.0 × 0.8 | 2.9e6 / 5.6e6 |
| **×1 (committed)** | **12 / 37** | **11.9 / 20.7** | **0.50 / 2.46** | **1.0 × 0.5** | **2.9e6 / 5.6e6** |
| ×2 | 4 / 18 | 5.3 / 9.6 | 0.81 / 3.33 | 1.0 × 0.7 | 2.9e6 / 5.6e6 |
| ×4 | 1 / 8 | 1.3 / 4.8 | 0.75 / 2.25 | 1.0 × 0.7 | 2.9e6 / 5.6e6 |
| ×8 | 0 / 2 | 0.0 / 1.3 | – | – | 2.9e6 / 5.6e6 |

(legs: seed 1 at 50 kyr/frame for 150 Myr, seed 5 at 100 kyr/frame for 300 Myr; logs
`0.2.0-m5-vent-tune-3000-1-50.txt`, `0.2.0-m5-vent-tune-3000-5-100.txt`; the K9 sweep is
green on every frame of all twelve legs.)

The measurement decides two things:

- **The committed scale stays.** ×1 sits in the middle of the range where the section's own
  supply can open a vent at all: ×8 is above the best-fed column's whole 150 Myr supply
  (6.4e5 m²) and births nothing on one leg, while ×0.25 opens 53–89 vents for the same
  trickle of melt. `Tsol`, `repose` and the two slider ranges are already pinned by
  `erupt-bench.js` at the design's own numbers (H 10.06, width 27, tan 34° = 0.675) and the
  ore constants by `ore-bench.js` (75) and the deposit replay (`deposits.js` 76,
  `isomorphism.js`), so none of them moved either.
- **A design-scale edifice is not reachable from this section's melt, and that is not a
  chamber constant.** One toy cell is 5.8e5 m², so a 10–30 cell cone is ~50–450 cells²
  (3e7–2.6e8 m²) — while the *whole section* produces 2.9e6 m² of melt in 150 Myr and
  5.6e6 m² in 300 Myr, about 5–10 cells. The 10–30 px edifice of §2.1 is a statement about
  the toy box's own capacity, which `edifice-bench.js` proves with a prescribed 150-cell
  episode; making the live section reach it needs ~50× the melt production (`kMelt`,
  `kPlumeMelt`), which are crust-budget constants owned by 0.1.x/0.3.x, not by this plan.
  Recorded here as a deferred item rather than tuned around.

## 5. One harness bug found on the way: `checkpoint.js` D2

Re-acceptance turned `checkpoint.js` red (1 / 52) at the new constant. The cause was in the
harness, not the engine: D2 ("forward simulation after self round-trip") ran on whatever the
previous check had left in the state — the deliberately *quarter-column-shifted* cut — so
the gate was measuring a mis-registered world against a fresh section with a 1e-3 bound on a
6e-2 quantity. Three corrections, all measured:

- the good cut is laid again before the run, so the check measures the round-trip it names;
- the bound is the metric's own spread: two runs starting within the pack's arc quantum of
  each other diverge chaotically, 1.3e-3 to 7.8e-3 apart over nine values of
  `P.kBeltGradient` in both directions, so the bound is 3e-2 (four times the worst);
- the gate that can actually fail is new and non-chaotic: the re-imported world keeps every
  K9 invariant and its 1e-9 ledger for the same 1000 frames. Measured, the shifted cut does
  *not* leave the mass noise behind (within 0.7e-2 of the fresh section), so no bound on
  that metric can witness a wrong world — which is why the invariant gate is there.
  `checkpoint.js` is now 53 / 53.
