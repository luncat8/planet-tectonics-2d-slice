# 0.5.0 M1 — the shared substrate and both page skeletons

**Result: ALL PASS, 302 checks over seven owner harnesses** (`experiments/pc-check.js`
delegates them and quotes the counts). `js/pc/` is complete, `hybrid.html` and `soup.html`
exist and boot, the option registry and both bars agree entry for entry, and S1–S4 run

## The gates, and what each one cost to earn

**`pc-phase.js` (30).** The transplant is gated the strict way — every returned number
compared with `===` over an 60-pair curve lattice, an 880-state `H` lattice and a 960-state
`T` lattice against `js/pt/phase.js`. One finding: the round trip `byT(byH(H))` is **not**
bitwise (byT recomputes the melt fraction from T instead of recovering it), and it is not
bitwise in 0.3 either. The gate is therefore the claim that actually holds and is worth
having: pc's round trip is 0.3's round trip, bitwise, with a one-ulp closure. The load branch
is monotone, joins the surface value continuously, falls back to depth when a caller has no
column, ignores an extra load argument under `'depth'`, and — the one that makes S4 a real
test — is unmoved by an unload under `'depth'`.

**`pc-pool.js` (40).** Every transaction is checked against mass, total enthalpy and
momentum to 1e-9 or better, on a state deliberately wider than either engine needs today
(a warped coordinate, a column index, a deposit certificate) so the descriptor has to permute
fields it does not understand. The three findings are all capacity or slot behaviour rather
than physics: a removal compacts the tail in and the moved parcel keeps its id and every
field; a merge whose survivor sat on the tail must return the slot the survivor actually
occupies (the naive `return a` hands back a cleared index); and a full pool defers with a
counter, so a split leaves its mass in the host and a carry leaves it in the donor.

**`pc-bond.js` (31).** 308 (T, age, melt) states against 0.3's formula recomputed
independently, term for term — all bitwise. Then the state machine: cold rock ages and
anneals, the 0.35/0.45 hysteresis holds between its two thresholds, hot rock resets both
clocks, nothing below `yWeldMax` welds, air carries no bond, and doubling both the load and
the pair count changes nothing because the law charges the *mean* excess.

**`pc-opt.js` (90).** The bar is read from the HTML, not from a script, because the claim is
about what a reader sees before any script runs. 21 controls on the hybrid, 25 on the soup,
each compared with its entry on kind, range/values and default. M0's per-engine defaults are
asserted on the page that should carry them (inertia 0.98 / 0, mobility 0 / 4), so a
copy-paste between the pages fails here rather than in a screenshot. Two defects were found
and fixed by this gate: the two `solidusOf` selects listed their options in the opposite
order from the registry (so the "load" default read as the wrong enum entry), and the S1
preset named `magmaMode`, which is not on the hybrid's registry at all.

**`pc-scenario.js` (42).** The sampler is gated on cadence (41 samples, 100→300 Myr),
frames (6,000 at 50 kyr/frame, 600 at 500), seed, preset and replay identity; then the three
gate states are gated in both directions with a synthetic engine. Two corrections came out of
it: the S1 velocity band is in **cm/yr** in the plan and the reading is km/Myr, so the
comparison divides by the conversion (the first version compared 71.7 against a 60–200
"km/Myr" bound and passed for the wrong reason); and the decay gate is pending, not failed,
when there is no pulse to decay.

**`pc-boot.js` (62).** Both pages load their declared scripts in order into a DOM parsed
from the page, one frame runs, exactly one `requestAnimationFrame` is outstanding, and a
second `PCPAGE.start` is refused. The bar is driven for real: a range input changes the live
option and its readout, a toggle flips the option and `aria-pressed` together, a select
stages a reset-scope change, a scenario button applies the preset — including rewriting the
clock control from the registry — and pause, step, reset, new planet and the 1–4 keys all
work. A staging bug was found here: a reset-scope toggle whose value is staged must show the
staged value in the bar (otherwise the click looks like a no-op) while the engine keeps
reading the committed one until the next planet. 240 frames leave a finite state.

**`pc-look.js` (7).** Both engines render through their **own** `draw` plus `pc/page.js`'s
chrome into a software 2D context and out to PNG: `experiments/logs/0.5.0-M1-soup.png` and
`0.5.0-M1-hybrid.png`. This is image evidence of the shipped code, not of a parallel renderer.

## Review of M0, and what changed because of it

M0's numbers reproduce on this checkout: `node experiments/sp-bench.js --quick` passes 37
checks and the quick 20 Myr leg still reads a finite, closed, contained state. The report's
own arithmetic checks out (71.665 km/Myr = 7.17 cm/yr). Three things were corrected or
carried forward:

1. **`eps0` was documented as a 0.3 constant and is not one.** 0.3 has no `eps0` anywhere in
   `js/`: its elastic bound lived implicitly inside the cluster projection. The plan's §3.3
   said "starting constants are 0.3's measured ones (`… eps0 0.05 …`)". It is now stated as
   what it is — a new, named, order-of-magnitude guess that M3/M5 own the fixture for. This
   matters because a transplant claim is exactly the kind of thing a later milestone trusts.
2. **`eps0` had to exist for M1 to be honest.** `pc/bond.js` exposes `elastic(mu) = eps0 * mu`
   as the elastic regime a pairwise-spring bond will read in M3/M5, rather than leaving the
   number in prose.
3. **M0's registry amendments are now enforced, not just written down.** The three entries M0
   amended (`inertia`, `mobContrast`, `heatMode`) are asserted per engine by `pc-opt.js`, so
   M4 and M5 cannot quietly run the other engine's default.

Nothing in M0's selection was overturned by M1: the soup's default rung is the measured
6,656 parcels on 128x52, the hybrid's mesh is 256x24, and no kernel default moved.

## What is deliberately not here

- No solver in either engine. `SP.features` and `HY.features` are empty objects, so the
  harness cannot print a green scenario line for code that does not exist.
- No crust, no cells-own-liquid/parcels-own-solid transaction families, no load column, no
  vents, no pile top, no camera presets beyond a name, no LUT palette, no HUD graphs.
- No integrated frame budget claim: M1's ms/frame is the draw path of a skeleton.
- No browser smoke. `pc-boot.js` is a VM rehearsal with a DOM and a software 2D context; the
  Chromium pass over `file://` stays M6's, as the plan says.

## Next step

**M2 — HY's liquid.** Build the hybrid's own kernels on top of the M1 shell: scatter from
the parcel pool, the buoyancy gauge, the red-black projection with `projIters`, donor-cell
advection of H/cF/cW, and the wall book. Two things M1 leaves ready for it: `pc/params.js`
already holds the mesh constants and the pressure-source switch, and `pc/phase.js`'s
`equil(S, yLoad)` already takes the per-parcel load column the hybrid will not have until M3
but the soup will need in M4.

The order that keeps the acceptance surface honest is the one the plan names: M2 gets S1
green on HY with no crust at all, and only then does M3 add the skin that S1's lid gate and
S4 actually need.
