1. **The four legs together** — 300 Myr, seed 1, 50 kyr/frame, contact + slab + arc + erosion

   `closing = (ctV_a - ctV_b)/dx > contactYield` over adjacent columns, so re-running the walk
   after the step reproduces the kernel's decisions bitwise and can read what the kernel does
   not record: the |dT| that chose each regime and the cluster sizes it answered. The
   census-vs-`S.d.contact` cross-check (0 mismatches over the leg) is what gives it teeth. No
   engine change was needed, so no fingerprint moved for this.
3. **The slider matrix** — clocks 10/50/100/500 kyr/frame and seeds 2/3/7 at the demonstration

5. **The owners** — `pt-p4-contact`, `pt-p4-slab`, `pt-p4-arc`, `pt-p4-erosion`,

## Finding 1: the deposit book was missing its third debit

The integrated leg failed the deposit-phase identity `invM[dep] = landCr + eroCr - eroDep` by
**1,242.1995 km² of 353,677** (0.35%). `experiments/scratch/p4-dep-drain-probe.js` wrapped
`POOL.recycle` on the same leg: **22 deposit parcels, 1,242.1995254883575 km²** — the whole
gap to 1.98e-9 — recycled into the deep records by P4.1's slab pass. Subducted sediment is
detached cold lithosphere (`mu = 0`, so cluster-free; `T ≈ 0`, so inside the slab population),
and the plan's settled §4 line is that detached cold material sinks whatever its phase. The
transfer was booked (`thr.deepDr`), but nothing named the phase it debited, so the deposit
identity P4.3 wrote was true only while nothing subducted.

Fix (bookkeeping, not physics): `thr.deepDep` — the deposit mass `recycle()` debits — in
`pool.js` (the phase read **before** `remove()`, which compacts the tail into the slot),
`state.js` (declaration, `reset()`, `hash()`), and the identity gains its third term:

```
invM[dep] = thr.landCr + thr.eroCr - thr.eroDep - thr.deepDep
```

`pt-p3-check.js`'s version of the identity updated the same way (its 50 Myr leg has
`deepDep = 0` — nothing reaches `yDeep` in 50 Myr — so its gate stays bitwise). The deposit
phase's other possible leak was checked and does not exist: a wholly molten *deposit* parcel
stays `PH.dep` (`melt.js`'s `detach` re-phases mantle only), and a *melt* parcel can never
enter the slab population because `T ≥ Ts(y) ≥ 0.82` below `yCrust` outruns `slabT 0.45`.

**The fingerprint moved.** `thr.deepDep` is a cumulative counter, and the plan's rule is that
cumulative counters live in `hash()`, so every fingerprint published before P4.4 stops
reproducing from this tree forward. The same-leg cross-check is recorded here: before the
counter, the integrated 300 Myr leg read **2960640650489454** — exactly the P4.3 report's
live leg, two harnesses one planet — and `pt-p4-check.js` prints the post-change number
beside it. All in-repo gates are within-run replays and stay green.

## Finding 2: the P4 owners' live legs had drifted off their own planets

`pt-p4-contact.js` went red inside this milestone's first full run: *"the live leg leaves no
hard overlap behind either — worst frame residue 1 pairs on 1 of 6000 frames"*. The P4.0
review measured residue 0 on 0/6,000 — on a planet without P4.3's erosion. Each P4 owner
isolates the *later* passes so its live numbers stay its own report's (P4.0's review set
`P.slab = false`; P4.2's review added `P.arc = false`), and P4.3 shipped without adding
`P.ero = false` to the earlier owners — sediment welds into the lid, the lid fragment
population changes, and a marginal pair lands one ulp inside the hard radius once in 6,000
frames. The fix is the recorded convention, not a kernel change: `P.ero = false` in
`pt-p4-contact.js` (all three legs: controlled, live, cost), `pt-p4-slab.js` and
`pt-p4-arc.js`, each with the sentence that says why. All three re-ran green (31 / 27 / 26
checks), and the integrated census belongs to `pt-p4-check.js` — which is one of the things
P4.4 exists to own.

## Finding 3: `pt-drift.js` was already red at HEAD — the P4.3 review's re-validation missed it

The first full run's `pt-p3-check` child failed, and the failing check was not P3's: §7's
`pt-drift` child read *"slab pull drives mean plate drift above the cancelled net flow — plate
1.14 vs own 0.44 cm/yr (2.6x)"* against its `mPlate > 3 * mOwn` gate. A stash run of the
pristine HEAD engine (`da2ab84`, the P4.3 review commit) reproduces the identical numbers, so
this milestone's changes did not cause it: **`pt-drift.js` has been red at HEAD since the
P4.3 review** changed where sediment lands (channel slope, shallowest seat) and its
re-validation list re-ran `pt-p4-erosion`, `pt-p4-transport-sweep`, `pt-ui` and `pt-p3-pool`
but not `pt-drift`. Over 400 Myr the re-seated sediment welds into the lids and shifts the
drift statistics of the planet that harness samples.

Fix by the same convention as findings 1-2's isolations, since the surface law is not a
slab-pull fixture's business: `P.ero = false` in `pt-drift.js` with the sentence that says
why. Re-run: **ALL PASS (5 checks)** — mean plate drift 2.53 cm/yr against a mean own-marker
flow of 0.22 (11.5x), on the planet its gate has been measured on since P4.2 (contact and arc
stayed on: the suite was green with them at P4.0-P4.2, and the integrated planet with
everything on is this milestone's harness, which measures the books, not the drift ratio).

The trail: `experiments/logs/0.3.0-p4.4-p3check-debug.txt` is the standalone `pt-p3-check`
run that isolated it — sections 1-6 all green (42-check harness minus the §7 child), §7 green
on 19/31/36/24/53/28/72 with only `pt-drift` red.
