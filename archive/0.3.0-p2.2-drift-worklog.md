# 0.3.0 P2.2 (step 2, measurement) — is the missing drift a sampling error? worklog

Follows `0.3.0-p2.2-damage-worklog.md`, whose next step was to measure *why* plates do not
drift before touching §4.2's kinematics. `experiments/pt-drift.js` (new, report) runs the
shipped config ('cool', band .6, seed 1, 50 kyr/f) to 400 Myr and, per plate, compares the
fitted velocity that moves it with the flow at three depths under its own footprint:

- `vPlate` — the rigid fit's own velocity (the law that ships);
- `vOwn` — the mass-weighted mean of the flow at the plate's own markers (what it fits);
- `vBase` — the same one node row below the plate's own base in each column it occupies;
- `v150`, `v300` — the same at 150 km and 300 km depth (the asthenosphere);
- `v300s` — the mass-weighted mean *speed* at 300 km (local motion, net or not).

Log: `experiments/logs/0.3.0-p2.2-drift.txt`.

## What this settles

1. **The fit is not sampling the wrong field.** A plate already moves at the mass-weighted
   mean of the flow under it, and that mean is within 15% of the same mean taken 300 km
   down. `vOwn` = `vPlate` is by construction (the projection *is* that mean), and `vBase`
   matching it says the field is smooth across the lid's base at this resolution.
2. **The lids are thin** (4-50 km; one transient body reaches 163 km) and the flow they sit
   in moves at ~0.7-0.9 cm/yr locally at 300 km, but its *net* transport under a plate's
   footprint is 0.2-0.7 cm/yr: the flow under a plate is convergent, and the convergence
   cancels in the mean. That cancellation is the reduced drift, not the depth of the sample.
3. So the missing motion is **force**, not sampling: a plate in a symmetric cell has no net
   traction, and no choice of fit depth changes that. Slab pull (a force toward the sinking
   end, proportional to the cold mass attached to the plate) is what must break the
   cancellation — which is also what §10's open question said before the kinematics existed.

## Next step

Implement the slab term in `solid.js kinematics()`: per cluster, an extra force (equivalently
an added velocity along the plate toward the trench) proportional to the mass of the plate's
members that sit below the lid (cold, deep markers attached to it), converted with the same
`dt` as the fit and clamped so a plate cannot outrun the flow. Gate it in `pt-crash`-style
fixtures: pt-crust's "rides the flow's mean" check must keep passing for a plate with no deep
members, and `pt-band`'s drift column is the target (the picture wants 3-10 cm/yr, the run
measures 0.1-0.8). If the drift does not move with a slab term, the alternative is an
explicit ridge-push/edge-force balance; both are a *force* story.
