# 0.3.1 M1 — the buried-deposit remelt route

The M0 matrix (`0.3.1-buried-deposit-matrix-worklog.md`) closed with eligible exposure on two of
eight seeds, so `0.3.1-plan.md` §5's branch 1 applies: implement M1. This worklog records the
implementation, its fixtures, the live acceptance numbers and the one reading the route leaves
open. The plan remains the authority for the specification; what follows is what was built and
measured.

## 1. The route

**Eligibility (the gate).** G5's detach pass now walks two sources instead of one. A deposit is
routed only when all of these hold at the same moment: it is still `P.PH.dep`, it is below the
cooling mask (`y > P.depositCoolDepth`), it carries the shallow-quench certificate, and its
G4-updated `S.melt` is at or above `P.meltHold`. Every test comes before any state change, so a
deferred transaction leaves the certificate and the thermal state exactly where they were.

**The certificate (`depQ`).** A new per-parcel field, 1 only if the parcel has been through
`FLIGHT.cool` at a positive `dt` and left that pass with its own equilibrium melt below
`meltHold` — checked even when the pass has no enthalpy delta to apply, so material already at
the surface wall still certifies. It is never cleared by warming at depth, which is exactly what
the route is for. It starts 0 on admission and on landing, and propagates by conjunction through
every pool operation that can move material: `POOL.merge` ANDs it, a `carry` into a deposit keeps
it only if donor and receiver were both certified, and a fresh parcel split off a certified
deposit keeps it. `POOL.FIELDS` owns it, so compaction cannot strand it, and `S.hash()` includes
it along with the new counters, so every quoted fingerprint moves.

**The transfer.** A partial melt uses the P3.2 equilibrium split, now source-aware:
`POOL.separate(S, p, dep)` books the shed mass to `thr.depMelt` (and `tx.depMelt`) when the
source is a deposit and to `thr.seg` when it is mantle rock — one implementation, two books. The
residue stays at its own solidus, keeps its certificate, and the melt child carries none. A
wholly molten deposit changes phase in place: no spare slot is required, bond state is cleared,
the certificate leaves with the deposit phase, `tx.whole` counts the transaction and
`thr.whole` stays zero. The child then rides the ordinary G5 transit, chamber and vent path.

**The books.** `thr.depMelt`/`tx.depMelt` join `S.tx`/`S.thr`. The two identities the plan
names now read

```
invM[dep] = thr.landCr + thr.eroCr - thr.eroDep - thr.deepDep - thr.depMelt
thr.seg + thr.whole + thr.depMelt - thr.freeze = thr.intrDr + mobile-melt parcel mass
```

and the fixtures that assert them (`pt-p3-melt.js`, `pt-p3-erupt.js`, `pt-p3-check.js`,
`pt-p4-check.js`) were extended with the new term rather than re-derived.

## 2. The fixtures

`experiments/pt-buried-deposit-melt.js` section A is now the routing fixture (23 checks in the
section, 29/28 in the two matrix runs). It pins the gate term by term — an uncertified deposit is
not re-phased however deep and however molten; a certified deposit at exactly the mask boundary
is not routed; one just past it is; below the threshold nothing happens — then both transfer
shapes, then the certificate's arithmetic (zero `dt` certifies nothing; one short pass is not
enough; enough passes certify once the reading is below `meltHold`; the certificate survives
burial and the parcel remelts at depth), then propagation (merge and carry only clear it; a fresh
parcel of a certified deposit keeps it), then capacity (a full pool defers the split once with
the source untouched, while a whole-parcel conversion needs no slot at all).

Load-order and pool-ownership fixtures needed no change beyond the field list:
`pt-p3-pool.js` passes all 72 checks with `depQ` in `FIELDS` and swept by `remove()`.

## 4. The reading the route leaves open

The M1 intake is **35.2 km² in 6 transfers** on seed 6 and **5.08 km² in 7** on seed 8, while
M0's counterfactual exposure on the same two cells was 1.361e4 and 4.557e1 km²·Myr. The
difference is not a bug, but it cannot be read as "the route sheds less than the exposure":

- M0's exposure integral is a *resident population* — a mean simultaneous eligible stock of about
  45 km² on seed 6, sitting above the threshold for most of the run because nothing consumes it.
- With the route on, a parcel is consumed in the same G5 call it crosses, so what the harness
  samples is the crossing itself: closest xi 0.1003 with a margin of −1e-4, i.e. the kernel's
  first sample above the line.
- The two worlds diverge (the fingerprints move as designed), so the intake is the mass the
  deferred population would have accumulated, not the integral's flow. The M1-live deposit
  population's peak temperature drops from 0.997 to 0.967 on seed 6, consistent with the hot
  material leaving.

What the live samples also show is the M0 field-column finding, now on the parcels the route
actually consumes. The canonical leg's crossing is a 351 km² parcel at t = 253.25 Myr, y 700.7 km,
T 0.915 against its own solidus 0.900 — barely mushy — in a node field of 0.610. It is not a
superheated parcel; it is a parcel holding about 10% melt, and therefore ~35 km² of mobile
material, in mantle that is 0.29 of T below its solidus. Seed 8's crossing and every closest
approach in the two detail blocks read the same way, 0.1–0.3 above their fields, and the deepest
band ends the canonical leg with a 316 km² parcel at margin +0.0117, i.e. the next crossing was
already forming.

The gate the plan specifies — certificate, depth, threshold — is doing exactly what it says.
Whether "reaching `meltHold`" should *also* require the local field to support it is not decided
by it, and the finding is not a defect to patch blind: the adjoint pairing (`grid.js`
renormalizes by `mu_k / W_k`) is what keeps the intake equal to the operator's own wall flux, so
any remedy has to be measured against `pt-p4-check.js`'s intake gates.

## 5. Next step

Run the canonical seed-6 leg with the route enabled and read the routed parcels' own temperature
against their local field at the moment of routing (the harness reports the gap at the boundary
already), then decide the eligibility question of §4 on that evidence — either the route is
complete as specified and the field gap is documented as the deposit operator's quadrature
signature, or eligibility gains a thermal term measured against the same intake gates. Either
way the acceptance counts above are the M1 baseline the decision starts from.
