# 0.3.2 M4 — the field-relative bound: the concentration goes, the engine goes with it

**Decision: nothing is selected.** The candidate M3 §9.1 left standing — a bounded exchange whose
bound is the field a parcel sits in rather than a constant — is built and measured here in four
forms on the cell every milestone of this release is read on (seed 5, 50 kyr/frame, 600 Myr,
shipped geometry and passes, pure FLIP). Every form removes the held-back concentration, and
every form empties the engine or saturates the pool. Two instruments M3 asked for are built on
the way (§1) and they are what makes the rejection an argument rather than a count.

Specification: `0.3.2-plan.md` §M4. Worklog: `archive/0.3.2-M4-field-bound-worklog.md`. Raw
banks: `experiments/logs/0.3.2-M4-*.json`, asserted by `experiments/pt-heat-tail-check.js`'s M4
section. No production code, parameter, page or shipping fingerprint changes; condition 5 stays.

## 1. What was built

**`frb K`** — the M1/M2 conservative bounded redistribution with the bound taken against the
field instead of a constant: the delivered state may not leave (`T_field` − K, `T_field` + K),
`T_field` the pre-gather nodal temperature read once per coupled parcel before the delivery loop,
so the bound, the headroom and the giveback all read the same snapshot. Two-sided by
construction; placement unchanged, so per-node totals, the intake, the books and the wall
integral remain the baseline's own delivery to round-off. Two forms on top of it:

- `--share s0 > 0` — the departure a parcel may hold is `K · share / s0`, share being the parcel's
  own fraction of its stencil's marker mass, so a parcel that *is* the field there is left alone
  and a sliver is clamped toward nothing. This is M3 §9.1's own phrasing.
- `--place mass` — a withheld increment is handed out by marker share instead of by headroom.
  M1/M2 give it to the block member with the most room under the bound, and room is largest for
  the *coldest* parcel; §4 measures what that makes the family do.

**Instruments** (observation only — `baseline 0 20` reproduces fingerprint 5534361194548428 on
this tree, so neither moves a trajectory):

- the per-phase outside count is split into its two ends (`outsideAbove`, `outsideBelow`);
- the adjoint gain (`cw = mu/W` over the stencil) and the parcel's mass share of that stencil —
  `pt-buried-deposit-melt.js`'s `routeGain`, ported — are read at every new global mantle minimum,
  at every mobile-melt sample above 1.5, and at the entry and hottest frame of each excursion.

## 3. What the family does do

It removes the concentration. The held-back burial census — the thing 0.3.1's field-support term
was written to filter and 0.3.2 exists to explain — falls from 68,140.14 km²·Myr of host
mass-time to 21.51 at K = 0.05 (a factor of 3,167) and to 177.80 in the share-scaled row, and the
worst `T − field` gap among what remains falls from 1.328 to the bound itself (0.2507, 0.0501,
0.0478): the population the term holds back is no longer sitting far from its own field, it is
sitting at the bound. The wall integral improves on the same rows (6.867e-5 against the control's
1.546e-3), and the books stay exact, so this is not the wall-booked relaxation M2 rejected: the
exchange is the operator's own, to round-off.

## 4. What it costs, and where the heat goes

Every row that removes the concentration also empties the engine or saturates the pool:

- **room placement, flat or selective** — 0 delivered transfers (the control's three are gone),
  5.2–8.6M pool refusals against the control's zero, and the surface pipeline at a standstill
  (landCr 0, ventDr 0, chambers 0 at K = 0.05). The mantle presents itself to the route 1,000× as
  often (5,083 → 4.1–7.2M routings) — under a bound, an unremarkable parcel is above the melt
  threshold almost everywhere it goes.
- **mass placement** — the pipeline runs (1 delivered transfer, 0.5739 km², landCr 253,142) but
  the world is in permanent meltdown: 976 live melt parcels against the control's 38, 16.9M
  refusals, and the concentration only four times smaller (16,304 km²·Myr, gap 0.8296).

The cause is the placement, not the bound. M1/M2 hand a withheld increment to the block member
with the most headroom under the bound, and headroom is largest for the coldest parcel, so the
family is a heat pump from the tail into the cold bulk — and in this engine the cold bulk is what
melts, segregates and erupts. The new instruments say it directly: on the `frb` 0.25 row the
global mantle minimum is set by the bound and not by the mechanism (3,074 new minima, mass 1.0 to
132.4 km², gain 0.791 to 9.710, share 0.001 to 0.127, and `field − T` reaching 0.2523 against a
bound of K = 0.25), while the mantle's routings explode. Loosening the bound gives some of the
pipeline back (refusals 5.2M against 8.6M, meltM 690 against 110) and gives the cold side back
with it (145 samples below −0.5) — the two are one knob.

The share-scaled form is not the selection the plan hoped for. At this configuration (mpc 4) an
ordinary parcel's stencil share is about 0.25, so `--share 0.1` *loosens* the bulk (0.25 → 0.625)
and clamps slivers to about 0.01. It removes the concentration just as well and empties the
pipeline just as thoroughly: the parcels a ±0.25 bound caps are not only the slivers.

## 5. What M4 says

1. **A bound on the delivered distribution cannot be paid for by moving the withheld heat.**
   Wherever it is placed — coldest neighbour, or the material that is the field — it changes what
   melts, and what melts is the pipeline. Four placements and two selectivities, one outcome.
2. **The bound and the concentration are measured; the cost is the engine's thermal heterogeneity,
   which is not separable from the defect by a bound alone.** The same departure from the field
   that carries the tail (gap 1.328) carries legitimately routed deposits (0.020..0.063) and the
   mantle's own cold structure (control minimum 0.548 below its own field).
3. **The instruments hold.** The split counters and the gain/share reader are in the harness and
   asserted; the cold side is now measured where M3 inferred it.

## 6. Next step

1. **Scope the bound to the deposit phase** (`--scope dep` on `frb`, the M0 relaxation's own
   scope). The mantle's control cold side is −0.489 — inside the bound — and the crossings below
   −0.5 have only ever appeared on probe rows, so the mantle may not need bounding at all, and
   leaving it alone is the one thing no row here has tried. It is one flag on machinery that
   already runs.
2. If a deposit-scoped bound also churns the mantle, the conclusion is that the delivery cannot be
   redistributed, and the remedy has to be at the source: M3's `--dpart` channel on the split (the
   melt law's missing piece) rather than a bound on what the gather hands out. The row the plan
   already names — `frb` + `--dpart 0.01` — is that measurement, and it is only worth running once
   (1) has been read.
3. Keep condition 5 and the deposit field filter. No constant moves to reopen the 300 Myr route,
   and no row here is a candidate for selection.

## 7. Continuation — deposit scope, then frb + --dpart

Two further rows on the same cell (seed 5, 50 kyr/frame, 600 Myr), measured after the
rejection in §2–§5. Banks: `experiments/logs/0.3.2-M4-frb025-dep-600.json` and
`experiments/logs/0.3.2-M4-frb025-dpart01-600.json`.

- **`frb 0.25 --scope dep`** (the bound applied to deposits only; the mantle's control cold
  side, −0.489, is already inside ±0.25 of its own field, so the mantle is left alone):
  fingerprint 1808048552219233. Held-back concentration 694.73 km²·Myr (68,140.14 in the
  control, a factor of 98); worst T−field gap 0.2503; bound holds on every phase and every
  frame; **zero refusals**; the surface pipeline runs (landCr 980,363 km², nMelt 28); 2
  delivered transfers shedding 1.2838 km² against the control's 3 / 9.7302 km². Frame time
  21.589 ms (just over the 20 ms gate on this host). The protected-event material ids do not
  survive (0 of 3), so this row needs an explicit correspondence argument before it can be
  considered eligible; it is the first row of the family that removes the concentration without
  emptying the engine or saturating the pool.

- **`frb 0.25` + `--dpart 0.01`** (the field-relative bound on top of the melt law's
  water-flux depletion): fingerprint 1460769074631962. Held-back concentration 25,105.50
  km²·Myr (only a factor of 2.7); 14 delivered transfers shedding 49.73 km², 46,802 refusals;
  bound holds, but the depletion channel re-opens the route the bound was written to close, and
  the row inherits the M3 depletion row's bleed (host masses down to 0.009 km²). Frame time
  23.819 ms. Not eligible: it regenerates the concentration the bound removes.

Conclusion of the continuation: scoping the bound to the deposit phase is the one form of this
family that removes the concentration while keeping the pipeline and zero refusals; paying for it
with `--dpart` instead re-opens the route. Whether the deposit-scoped row's two transfers are
the control's three in different clothing, or new deposits, is the open question for the next
step, and it needs a material correspondence argument (or a trace) rather than a count.
