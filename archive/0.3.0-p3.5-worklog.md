# 0.3.0 P3.5 worklog — integrated acceptance and the tuning freeze

Gate: `experiments/pt-p3-check.js`, the acceptance pass the P3 plan's §4 P3.5 asks for. It
measures the six integrated claims on the real G1-G8 pipeline and freezes the reserve. Serial
validation of the whole release: 15 processes (the 14 P3.4 harnesses re-run unchanged, plus the
new acceptance pass). Logs are `experiments/logs/0.3.0-p3.5-*.txt`; the paste-back summary is
`archive/0.3.0-p3.5-report.md`.

All fourteen exit zero with exactly the counts the P3.4 worklog quotes (558), the shipped frame
measures 12.9 ms against the fixed 20 ms host gate (P3.4's log quotes 13.4), and
`index.html`/`particles.html` are byte-identical.
The code review of the P3.4 path (`flight.js`, `pool.js`'s `compatible`/`merge`/`separate`/
`intrude`, `vent.js`, `melt.js`, `phase.js`, the raster/HUD hooks, and the budget harness) found
five things worth acting on, none of them a conservation defect:

1. **The shipped planet is dry and uniform.** Every parcel and reservoir starts at `cF = cW = 0`
   and no P3 kernel introduces either: they are only moved by the conserved transfers that copy
   them (`flight.launch`, the equilibrium split, intrusions, merges). So `wBlast` never fires and
   every shipped conduit is effusive; `kBlast`, `emitBlast`, `flightVyBlast/VxBlast`, the blast
   mark and the water/felsic solidus depression are reachable only through fixtures, and
   `mergeCompF`/`mergeCompW` cannot bind at all because every composition is identical. This is
   the composition boundary of the P3 plan (composition's source is the exchange work, §1.1 of
   `0.4.0-sync-plan.md`), not a bug — but it means the explosion half of P3.4 is a fixture claim,
   and the acceptance pass now says so out loud and measures the explosive arc by seeding one
   chamber's water.
2. **`resMelt` sat below the model's own initial condition.** The 'cool' start is mushy in a
   250-700 km band, so 1,389 parcels — 5.9% of the base population — are already melt-phase by
   the first frame, against a candidate `resMelt` of 0.05. The line was never enforced (one
   shared cap, P3.0's rule), but a frozen budget below its own starting state is not a budget.
3. **`flightVyBlast = 30` broke §2.4's own band.** The ±10% launch jitter in `flight.launch`
   puts the longest explosive arc at 1.65 s, outside the design's 0.5-1.5 s.
4. The coalescence rule carries the deposit population: 1,730 merges out of 1,737 landings in the
   first 50 Myr, which is why deposits never exceed 7 parcels and why `resDep`'s line is far
   above its peak. `depositMerge` 40 km is what makes that work, not the composition tolerance.
5. **`pt-p3-budget`'s price gate could not decide.** The P3.4 worklog quotes "1.365x an empty
   pool", but that ratio is two absolute millisecond readings taken two minutes apart on a
   2-core box whose window spread the same harness reports at 94-158% — its own INFO line says
   "a reserve price inside this spread is not resolved". Six runs of the *unchanged* tree priced
   the same reserve anywhere in **1.21-1.56x**, against a gate of 1.4; it duly failed inside the
   first acceptance pass, and passes and fails at random. The pool's marginal price is real; the
   way it was read was not. The harness now measures the empty and the filled frame as an
   **interleaved pair** — reconfigure, fill, warm up and time, alternating, eight windows a
   side — and gates the ratio of the medians. That reads **1.19x** (1.196 / 1.203 / 1.185 over
   three runs, a 1.5% spread) where the sweep's own ratio moved 1.39 / 1.43 / 1.56 in the same
   runs: the difference is the host, not the pool. The sweep stays as the price table and both
   numbers are printed beside each other.

Two further review points needed no change: the wall book has no double-counting (the marker
intake equals the operator's own wall flux to `2.65e-12` relative, so the deposit's surface
exchange and the grid's top-cell flux are the same transaction on both sides), and the paused
path (`SIM.wall`) advances only flight, which the P3.4 gate already fixes.

One deliberate P3.4 behaviour was re-examined and kept: a tab suspended for longer than
`flightDtMax` loses presentation time rather than integrating it, so a clast resumes where it was
instead of landing. The plan asks only that such a frame cannot tunnel or duplicate, which the
acceptance pass measures directly (`0` of 33 airborne parcels under the horizon, mass unchanged);
integrating the whole interval instead would make the return frame cost `O(wall dt / 0.05 s)`
parcel passes, and material cannot pile up past `partCap` because a full pool refuses the next
emission into its vent, where it stays counted.

Nothing in the engine itself needed repairing: every conservation question the review raised was
answered by measurement. What the review did change is two frozen constants, a reserve line that
sat below its own starting state, one gate that could not decide, and the three fixture copies of
the reserve that would have drifted from `params.js`.

## The acceptance pass

`experiments/pt-p3-check.js` owns what no milestone harness owns and delegates the rest, exactly
as `acceptance.js` does for 0.2.0 — a delegated owner runs as a child and its own PASS count is
quoted, never re-measured.

| section | what it decides |
|---|---|
| 1. the live run | 50 Myr at 50 kyr/frame: both books, the four leg pairs, the stock identities, the census |
| 2. the cap | a 2% reserve that refuses from its first frame still closes, and a full pool leaves every stock bitwise in place at all four refusing call sites |
| 3. welding | a deposit older than `tauWeld` is plate and shares its cluster with the substrate under it |
| 4. legibility | the clast arc at 10/50/100/500 kyr per frame, the fountain window, and the effusive/explosive styles |
| 5. replay | 1000 frames replayed, a fixed wall sequence replayed, a different wall interval as state, and a resumed tab |
| 6. the matrix | 7 cells (four clocks on seed 1, three more seeds at the demonstration clock): closure, refusals, the per-phase reserve peaks, the host budget |
| 7. the owners | `pt-check`, `pt-crust`, `pt-drift`, `pt-surface`, `pt-wrap`, `pt-ui`, `pt-p3-budget`, `pt-p3-pool` as children |

Section 6 is the freeze evidence; §5's split/merge half is `pt-p3-pool`'s, quoted in section 7
rather than re-implemented.

## The rung benchmark (0.3.0-plan.md §7)

The plan asks P3 to "benchmark all supported rungs with melt, air and deposit populations
enabled before fixing capacity or promising 60 fps". Sections A and B of `pt-p3-budget.js` price
every rung with the reserve *filled* (all three phases present by construction); §G, added here,
runs each rung live for the same 20 Myr as §E and records what the model itself puts in those
phases:

| rung | mpc | n / cap | melt (share of base) | air | dep | first ejecta | ms/frame |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| 256x24 (shipped) | 4 | 23,658 / 28,263 | 1,398 (5.94%) | 167 | 5 | 6.5 Myr | 14.0 |
| 512x48 | 2 | 48,142 / 57,754 | 2,673 (5.55%) | 56 | 1 | 6.8 Myr | 28.0 |
| 1024x96 | 2 | 194,568 / 233,472 | 10,794 (5.55%) | 0 | 0 | none by 20 Myr | 114.0 |

- the melt band is the initial condition's own mushy layer at every rung (5.94 / 5.55 / 5.55% of
  the base, inside the frozen 8% line), so the reserve's worst case does not depend on the mesh;
- no rung refused a transfer or lost a parcel to its cap, and the live air and deposit peaks stay
  far under their lines (0.71 / 0.12 / 0.00% and 0.02 / 0.00 / 0.00%);
- **the finer rungs erupt on a much slower cadence** — 512x48 emits 223 batches between 6.8 and
  18 Myr and then nothing to 60 Myr; 1024x96 has no ejecta in 60 Myr (each probed separately at
  60 Myr). Measured cause: the initial mush emplaces the same ~2e6 km² into 34-39 chamber records
  (mean head 24-25 km against the `chY + tensile*mug` opening line at about 40 km), and after it
  the melt supply is a few hundred km² per Myr, so whether a record's head crosses the line
  depends on the tail of that distribution and on the size of the parcels topping it up. The plan
  fixes no cadence across rungs, so this is recorded, not gated: the shipped page rung is 256x24
  and its cadence is what the matrix and the 50 Myr census measure, while a rung-independent melt
  source belongs to 0.4.0's exchange work, not to a P3 knob.

The finer rungs make no budget claim: the 20 ms host gate is stated at the demonstration clock on
the shipped rung, and §B's INFO line reports the finer rungs' own cost (28.7 / 106.7 ms with the
reserve filled) beside it.

## Gate results

The 50 Myr live leg at the demonstration clock:

- global mass: relative residual **0** at every sampled frame (compensated summation), and an
  independent fixture sum over parcels + chambers + vents agrees with it to `6.8e-14`;
- enthalpy: `|dH - dH_book| / |dH_book|` ≤ `6.6e-12`;
- latent: `hLedger - ledger - meltLat * dL` = `1.08e-12` relative (`7.13e-9` absolute in km²·H);
- coverage: `|intake/wall - 1|` ≤ `2.65e-12`;
- temperature `-0.004..1.135`, no NaN over 1,000 frames;
- 1,852 drains, 1,852 emitted batches, 1,737 landings, 1,730 merges, 1,446 intrusions,
  **0 refusals**, 39 chambers of 64, no blocked conduit;
- `airCr = airborne stock + landDr` to `1.6e-15`, `landDr = deposit stock` to `4.0e-16`;
- the gross legs moved `4.44e5` km² against a `4.17e7` km² inventory — throughput is reported
  beside the stock and never inside it.

At the cap (reserve 2%, inside the initial condition's mushy band): 124,523 refusals over 300
frames, mass residual **0**, enthalpy `6.5e-12`, and with the pool full all four refusing call
sites (`admit`, `split`, `separate`, `launch`) refuse, count themselves, and leave the seven
inventory totals bitwise unchanged.

Welding: at 40 Myr the oldest deposit has `age 24.5 Myr`, `mu 0.707`, `cluster 0`, and the
nearest substrate parcel below it shares that cluster; 1,249 landings coalesced into 6 parcels.

Legibility (`kBlast` fixture at every clock): medians 0.87-0.92 s, 10-90% 0.57-0.98 s, extreme
`0.483..1.000` s — the short tail is a clast that lands uphill on developed relief, not a lost

## Failed trials (kept, so they are not repeated)

- The first acceptance draft counted a capacity refusal by the *return code* of
  `POOL.separate`. A pure-phase parcel returns -1 without a capacity event — correctly, since it
  is not one — so the fixture now presses a mushy parcel and counts the transaction counter
  instead. The engine was right and the harness was wrong; the fixed check also proves the
  inventory is bitwise unchanged, which the return-code version could not.
- The first replay check built both legs up front and then marched them, so the second leg
  marched the state of the first. Replay requires `meter()` immediately before `march()`; the
  engine has no hidden nondeterminism here, as `400`-frame pairs show.
- §G's first draft gated "every rung really carries the three populations live" and failed at
  1024x96 (no ejecta by 20 Myr; 60 Myr was then measured and it is the same). The gate was wrong,
  not the model: the plan fixes no cadence across rungs, and what it asks for is that the phases
  are enabled and the books close, which §B/§C/§G decide at every rung. The census is now the
  row's content and the gates are the reserve's own lines.
- §G's row quoted 796 ejecta where the same leg measures 666. The counter was read after the
  timing window instead of at the end of the 20 Myr leg (the window adds 5 Myr of geology); the
  row now snapshots n, emit and refusals before `frameMs`. A leg whose pool is *filled* to
  capacity also ejects less (318 against 666 in a 20 Myr leg) — that is `separate()` deferring
  splits at the cap, the semantics the capacity gate exists for, not a defect.
- Gating "every clast flies 0.5-1.5 s" failed at 500 kyr/frame on a 0.483 s clast. The cause is
  mechanical, not a bug: a clast that lands on a slope a few km above its vent completes a
  shorter arc than one that lands at its launch height. The check now gates the distribution
  (median and the 90th percentile inside §2.4's band, a floor of 0.35 s, and the extremes
  reported) instead of pretending relief does not exist.
