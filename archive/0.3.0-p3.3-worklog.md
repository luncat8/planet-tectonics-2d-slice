## Deviations

- **One conduit per chamber record.** Vent `k` is chamber `k`'s outlet and exists exactly while
  the chamber does, because P3 never removes a chamber record (`intrude` only joins them). The
  plan did not fix a vent count; the record-level choice avoids a second allocation and a second
  identity to keep matched.
- **Pressures are km of rock head, not MPa.** Density is uniform (P1), so a melt column of
  thickness `h` pushes with `h`, and the lithostatic load at depth `y` is `y`. The tensile
  strength is `P.tensile = 0.3 km` at full strength, about 10 MPa at `ρg ≈ 33 MPa/km`. The
  unit check is in `pt-p3-erupt` §1, against a closed-form threshold, before any bond machinery.
- **The roof strength is the crust's node field, `S.mug`, sampled by `G.sampleAt`.** The
  conduit reads what the crust already computes; it does not own a second strength field.
- **The drain is capped at the overpressure, and the conduit seals there.** `spent = excess *
  chSpan` is the head the conduit has to spend. `d = min(rate * dt, spent, room, stock)`, and
  when `d === spent` the conduit goes `CLOSED`. The remainder stays in the chamber as intrusive
  storage. This is a decision, not a fit: an overpressure that only decays exponentially would
  never reach zero, so a conduit would never seal and the chamber would drain without limit.
- **The style is fixed when the conduit opens.** A new conduit is explosive when the chamber's
  water mass exceeds `wBlast` (0.01) of its mass, and effusive otherwise. A later change in the
  chamber's water does not re-style an eruption already under way.
- **Vent capacity is a fixture-level knob, not a calibration.** `vCap = 4000 km²` is the room a
  vent has until P3.4's flight consumes its stock. Nothing consumes it yet, so the room is the
  only thing that stops an open conduit.
- **Throughput is kept outside stock.** Gross segregation (`thr.seg`), intrusion
  (`thr.intrDr/intrCr`) and eruption (`thr.ventDr/ventCr`) are counters on `S.thr`. The
  inventory reads the stock fields only. `pt-p3-erupt` §7 checks that the drained mass equals
  the vent stock and that the throughput is never added to the inventory.
- **The melt book has four mass terms, not two.** The first draft of the book closed
  `segregated = intruded`, which held in both live runs and was therefore not evidence: neither
  run fires a whole-parcel melt or a freeze-back (`tx.whole = tx.freeze = 0`). The phase changes
  now carry their mass in `thr.whole` and `thr.freeze`, and the gate is
  `seg + whole − freeze = intruded + melt in flight`. A mutant without the freeze term fails the
  fixture gate, which is the only place that term fires.
- **G6 is the kernel `VENT.run` at `SIM.k[6]`.** The slot was empty in P3.2, so the frame order
  G1–G8 is unchanged. Every kernel no-ops at `dt = 0`, and `pt-p3-erupt` §7 gates that.
- **The crystallised-intrusion acceptance is gated on a fixture.** Nothing cools a chamber in
  P3.3, so no live record holds a crystal-only state. `pt-p3-erupt` §10 builds one and checks
  that its mass and heat count once and its melt count zero. The live check waits for a cooling
  rule, which P3.4 or P3.5 owns.

## Pitfalls spotted

- **The fractional drain never sealed.** The first vent step drained `rate * dt` only. Because
  the excess shrinks exponentially, the conduit never reached the threshold and never closed.
  The fix is the `min` with `spent` above. The harness gate that caught it is `pt-p3-erupt` §4
  ("a long step cannot drain past the overpressure: the conduit seals").
- **A check that multiplies by zero is a tautology.** My first draft of §5 ended its intensive
  checks in `* 0` terms (`S.chF / S.chM * 0`), which pass for any state. They are now the
  per-unit-mass ratio of each field, checked on both the chamber and the vent.
- **Read a ratio before the reset that zeroes it.** The explosive-over-effusive check divided by
  a record that `configure` had already reset, giving `Infinity`. The two drains are now captured
  before the reset.
- **A fixture's seed can melt the whole band.** A water fraction of 0.02 depresses the solidus
  by `meltWW * cW = 0.4`, which melts everything. The fixture uses 0.0008 (a 0.016 depression).
  Any seed should state its own depression before it is trusted.
- **The shipped composition never reaches the explosive style.** Water is zero in the shipped
  start, so the only explosive records are the synthetic ones in §3. The seeded §9 run ends
  with no explosive conduit (`explosive conduits 0`). The explosive path is gated, not observed.
- **A frame-time gate can fail on a noisy host while the code is unchanged.** The final serial
  suite read `pt-check`'s frame at 16.4 ms against the 16 ms bar; a rerun read 13.7 ms. An A/B
  of the P3.2 commit against the P3.3 tree (`experiments/pt-p3-frame-ab.js`, 8 interleaved pairs)
  gave the same medians (about 14.0 and 14.2 ms) on that host. The bar was not moved. Both runs
  are logged (`0.3.0-p3.3-pt-check-run1.txt` and `0.3.0-p3.3-pt-check.txt`).
- **A merged report line can mix two runs.** `pt-p3-melt` §8 printed one run's peak beside
  another run's refusals. Every report line now names the run it reports (see the P3.2 review).

## Reusable patterns

- **A transfer is one fraction.** `transfer(S, k, d)` takes `f = d / chM`, debits every extensive
  field by `f` times its value, and credits the vent with the same amounts. The intensives ride
  along unchanged, and the pair is exact to round-off (`pt-p3-erupt` §5 measures 0 relative on
  every field).
- **A drain is the minimum of named caps.** `d = min(rate * dt, spent, room, stock)`: each cap
  is a separate event that a counter can name (seal, block, empty), so the harness can say which
  one bound.
- **Records are inventory once.** `stock(m, h, f, w, l)` is the one place a reservoir's stock
  enters the compensated totals. Chambers and vents both go through it, so a vent cannot be
  counted in one line and again in the other.
- **Independent fixtures before the live run.** §1–§6 are closed-form and check the rule against
  a hand calculation. §8–§9 run the pipeline. A live number is only cited after its rule has a
  fixture that passes without the pipeline.

## What to do next

Decide the frame-time gate (see the pitfall above): re-base the bar as P3.1 did, or run the
frame gates on an idle host and record it. That is the user's call.

P3.4 — flight, landing and welding (`0.3.0-p3-plan.md` §4 P3.4). The vent stock is the
supply that flight consumes: a vent drains into a ballistic clast on wall time, and the mass
transferred to flight uses geological time. Until that exists, every open conduit fills to
`vCap` and blocks (`pt-p3-erupt` §9: 27 blocked, 0 open at the end of the fixture run).

Carry forward: (1) the shipped run opens no conduit (`max open 0`, the largest head is 40.5 km
against a 78.4 km threshold); the roof rule and `yCham`/`chSpan` are a P3.5 decision, not a
fixture's to change; (2) the crystallised-intrusion live check waits for a cooling rule; (3) the
explosive style is gated by a synthetic fixture only until a seeded composition reaches
`wBlast`.
