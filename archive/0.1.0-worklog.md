## UI — axis scale sliders and the two scale rulers (2026-09-26)

Reviewed `021e6ea` first: all six harnesses re-run green (scale 21, view 75, section 18,
raster 32, smoke 41, m2 60), so the review turned into three fixes rather than a rewrite —
the Moho polyline started with a `lineTo` when screen column 0 had no owner (canvas silently
promotes it, but the first segment was wrong whenever the window began off the columns);
`overlayCursor` guarded `my` against `P.ch` but not `mx` against `P.cw`; and `buildGrid`
allocated an array of `{s, t}` objects plus fresh label strings on *every* view change,
which is every frame of a drag.

**What shipped** (draft in `archive/UI-draft.md`).

*Axis scale sliders.* `GEO.zoomX/zoomY` read the two factors back out of `P.view`, and
`setZoomX/setZoomY` set them (vertical about the window centre, each clamped to its own
range). `index.html` puts a vertical range input down the left edge of the canvas
(`writing-mode: vertical-lr; direction: rtl`, the standard vertical-slider recipe — min at
the bottom) and a horizontal one under it, in one CSS grid with the canvas so they line up
with the axes they scale. Both are log over [axis minimum, `zoomMax`]; the vertical one
bottoms out at `GEO.zoomYMin = 0.532`, the whole planet on one screen. Every camera move —
wheel, drag, preset, key — now ends in `UI.afterView()`, which syncs the LUTs, writes both
factors back into the sliders and rebuilds the `scale x … km/px  y … m/px at 0 m` readout,
so a slider can never drift from the camera.

*Scale rulers.* Replaces the old single-step altitude grid, which was picked from the
deepest visible interval and therefore left the detailed surface half with one line per
190 px. Horizontal: a 1-2-5 step (`gridGapX` 110 px), counted from x = 0 inside each lap so
the seam ends a lap short and restarts at `0` instead of drifting off the round numbers.
Vertical, two stages (design §1.4): a greedy over the round `1..9 × 10^d` ladder outward
from 0 m with a `gridGapY` 26 px minimum, then a 1-2-5 subdivision of any band still wider
than three labels. Stage 1 alone is right in the log part of the map and useless in the
±40 km linear core (the Crust preset showed −3…−10 km every 40 px and then a blank 280 px
strip to the window bottom, because a decade ladder has nothing between 10 and 20 km);
stage 2 alone is the old failure. The greedy is anchored at 0 m over the whole world rather
than the window, so the rung set is a function of the zoom only and a pan slides the lines
instead of reshuffling them. Checkbox + key `g`; sea level stays outside the toggle.

**Files.** `js/geom.js`: ladder/`decadeSet`/`pickRungs`/`fillStep`/`gridMags`/`buildVGrid`/
`buildHGrid` replace `buildGrid`, per-axis zoom API, preallocated `vg*`/`hg*` arrays.
`js/params.js`: `gridGapY`, `gridGapX`. `js/render.js`: `showScale`, the two-ruler overlay,
the two review fixes. `js/ui.js`: the two sliders, the toggle, `afterView`, `fmtScale`.

**Found on the way.**
- The label caches are keyed on the value their string was built for, and `0` is a real
  value on the x ruler — a zero-filled `Float64Array` therefore reported "unchanged" and
  the lap-origin label stayed `null`. Sentinel is `NaN`.
- Greedy "first rung that clears the gap" is the wrong rule on its own: at the default
  window it accepts −9 km (the first of decade 3 to clear 26 px) instead of −10 km. Picking
  the *set* per decade first — `1..9`, else `1,2,5`, else `1` — keeps the round values.
- A uniform world step inside a band is tightest on screen at the deep end, so `fillStep`
  grows the step until that one gap clears; the first sub-interval is short instead (the
  band start is rarely a multiple), and that one is dropped by the same min-gap rule.

## M2.2 — K3 transport, K4 contacts and cadence events (2026-09-26)

**Shipped.** `COL.transport` moves each column by its solved plate velocity, wraps it,
then stable-sorts `(x, old index)` and gathers *every* per-column field and the full
96-slot stack through preallocated typed buffers. It carries deposits/vents through the
inverse permutation; a changed neighbour invalidates edge hysteresis. The pre-move width
travels with the record. K4 scans sorted gaps/contacts into intents, consumes before
births, and applies transfers in **volume units** before recalculating widths and stack
thicknesses. Pure translation also rescales stacks by old/new width, so plate-boundary
motion cannot silently change mass. The final topology is sorted; plate counts/anchors,
caches, slope/profile, reciprocal vents and boundary histories are rebuilt. A consumed
edge owner hands its history to the new left neighbour when the ordered plate pair survives.

Opening > `2*rGap*w0` gives one midpoint packet on the left plate (including the seam):
continental donor sets with mean felsic thickness ≥ `hRiftBreakup` each contribute
1/(K+1) of felsic/sediment layer volumes and all ore potentials; otherwise the mantle
sources a new mafic stack. The newborn starts at age 0/damage 0.6. A closing O–C/O–O
contact < `rContact*w0` sinks the subducting stack except half its sediment, which goes
to an overriding-margin prism; C–C transfers the whole thinner-felsic stack. Deposits
follow retained horizons (or lose their owner for a sunk bed), and vents follow their
surviving margin if it is free. At full capacity, skips are atomic; consumed slots can
be recycled by same-frame births. The 1-Myr event hook after K4 can suture a C–C
contact after 20 consecutive Myr of slow convergence (`edgeSlow`, separate from the
boundary's total `edgeAge`) or split a damaged corridor with ≥24 cells per daughter. K3/K4 and
events run at zero geological time without changing tectonic state.

**Pitfalls fixed during validation.** A newborn needs `colW=1` while its temporary
layer thicknesses represent volumes, otherwise cross-lith compaction records zero in the
mix ledger. Clearing only the old `dead[0..n)` range left a stale flag in the first
appended slot after a prior consume, so a later second birth was produced but silently
excluded from the gather; clear the whole fixed buffer. The event hook is invoked as an
unbound function by `SIM.step`, so it must call `COL.plates()` rather than `this.plates()`.
The old M1 raster fixture's unowned 50 m bed is allowed to subduct now; it disables
K3/K4 during its 100k-frame *storage* check, while M2 checks real moving beds and
mass independently.
