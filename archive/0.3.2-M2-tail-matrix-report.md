# 0.3.2 M2 — the tail family re-measured on a green identity; the clock/seed matrix

**Decision:** nothing is selected. The two remaining families M1 named — stencil-scale bounded
placement and a relaxation booked honestly against the wall — were measured on the re-banked
600 Myr seed-5 cell (fingerprint 6191250240167390), now that its latent identity closes. None
holds the plan's conditions together, and each fails on something new:

- **Stencil-scale placement closes the feasibility gap M1 found** (0 infeasible placements against
  M1's 3,118) and holds every phase inside −0.5..1.5, but it does so by **clipping**: the light
  deposits are parked *at* T = 1.500 while still 0.72 of T above their field, the held-back
  concentration *grows* (94,081 against 68,140 km²·Myr), and its one transfer is a capped parcel
  at T 1.497 in a field of 0.935. It bounds the gate's metric and leaves the defect.
- **Sub-grid relaxation toward the field, zero-sum through the adjoint, is the only family that
  removes the concentration at its source.** On deposits it generates no held-back residence at
  all, keeps every phase in bounds, and holds the wall (1.49e-3) and every book — and it
  **degenerates the route**: P3.2's split leaves an **undepleted residue at its solidus**, so a
  rate that restores a light residue to its supporting field re-melts it every frame. 8,559
  transfers come from 40 parcels, 7,876 of them from hosts already below 1e-3 km², one down to
  5e-324 km² (the smallest double). None of the protected events survives. Extended to the
  mantle, the same bleed routes 850,098 times and the pool refuses 876,932 slots.
- **The wall-booked (non-conservative) relaxation** injects −7.5% of the wall flux as its own
  source and fails the P4 gate, besides bleeding the same way and pushing 395 mantle samples
  below −0.5.

The clock/seed matrix (20 cells at 300 Myr plus the 600 Myr cell, gated on every frame) confirms
the latent remedy everywhere and finds the concentration in a third phase: **mobile melt on seed
2 crosses 1.5 at three of four clocks**. The serial series re-acceptance on the shipped tree is
§4. No production code, parameter, routing term or shipping fingerprint changes in this step;
condition 5 stays.

## 1. The re-banked cell, and what the launch remedy did not move

Seed 5, 50 kyr/frame, 600 Myr, shipped geometry and P3/P4 passes, pure FLIP — M0's explicit
configuration, fresh process. Fingerprint **6191250240167390**, the M2 bank. Every grid-coupled
reading of that cell is **bitwise** the M0 bank's: the three protected transfers (frames 9341,
10157, 10660; IDs 42152, 165372, 194306; 9.73015809837463 km² shed), the 30,906-sample /
68,140.14284555816 km²·Myr held census, 5,083 mantle routings, per-phase temperature extrema and
the 2,856 deposit violations, the 1.5455e-3 wall reading, and both books. What moved is the
airborne melt state and with it the latent identity: the residual never exceeds **6.44e-8
km²·H** on any frame, 6.19e-13 of its books (the plan's floor-1 metric peaks at 6.69e-9, frame
3616), against M0's 167.36 km²·H. Serially the re-bank runs 14.78 ms/frame. The launch remedy is therefore a booking change and not a trajectory
change, and the protected events survive it by identity, not by correspondence argument
(`pt-heat-tail-check.js` asserts this).

### Stencil-scale bounded placement — feasible, and still not a remedy

Widening the placement set from the ~4-parcel nearest-node bucket to the 3×3 stencil block
removes M1's binding constraint completely: 4,688 capped deliveries, 101,544 absorptions, **zero**
infeasible givebacks, and no frame-end violation in any phase. The wall reads better than the
baseline's. Read as the plan's gate list it passes T, wall, books and frame together. Read as a
remedy it is not one:

- The hot tail is a **pile at the bound**: the hottest deposit is T 1.500 at 186.6 km, 5.0 km²,
  in a field of 0.776. A parcel 0.72 of T above its field is the defect the draft opened with
  (2.267 against 0.288), moved under a line rather than removed.
- The **held-back concentration grows** 38% (94,080.79 km²·Myr, 43,057 samples, gap to 1.387):
  capped parcels keep their excess melt fraction at depth, so more of them sit certified,
  buried, threshold-molten and unsupported.
- Its single transfer (frame 11491, 0.118 km², whole-parcel) reads T 1.497 against a field of
  0.935 — the cap made it molten, not the field.

The family now has both halves measured: node-scale placement is infeasible (M1), stencil-scale
placement is feasible but only bounds the distribution's extent, not its relation to the field.
Bounding is not restoring.

### Sub-grid relaxation — removes the concentration, exposes the undepleted residue

Rate `1 − exp(−kappa·dt/m)` toward the bilinear field after the gather: a 1.8e3 km² base parcel
keeps 0.9991 per 50 kyr, a 50 km² deposit 0.969, a sliver nothing. No tuned constant.

**Deposit scope, zero-sum** is the closest any probe has come. The held-back population that
condition 5 exists to filter is **not generated** (0 samples against 30,906), every phase stays
inside the bound on every frame (deposits −0.109..1.114, mantle −0.419), the wall reads 1.49e-3,
refusals are zero. The exchange returns through the adjoint to round-off where the scatter's
`w` still describes the cloud (2.5e-14 km²·H worst substep over the 20 Myr smoke); over 600 Myr
the worst substep leaves 8.4 km²·H unreturned, consistent with `G.reseed` relocating a parcel
after the scatter that built `w`; that remainder is booked, never dropped (net −178 km²·H over
the leg, 3.8e-5 of the wall). It ran 19.1 ms/frame concurrently against the baseline's ~15
serially: inside the 20 ms gate only by a margin a serial reading would have to confirm.

What rejects it is the route. P3.2's split leaves the residue at its own solidus with unchanged
composition. In a field above that solidus, a relaxation fast enough to restore a light parcel
warms the residue straight back into the mush, and the next G5 splits it again. The leg's 8,559
transfers are **40 parcels**, the four busiest re-split 1,919, 1,302, 1,236 and 1,199 times;
7,876 transfers leave a host already below 1e-3 km², and one host reaches 5e-324 km². None of
the three protected records or their material IDs appears. Under pure FLIP the residue's
reheating is rate-limited by grid conduction, which is why the shipped route never shows it.
It is a missing piece of the melt law: no depletion, so the residue's solidus does not rise as
its melt leaves, and a restoring exchange is the first mechanism to reach that gap.

The bleed is also **not a stable quantity**. Folding the probe's two inline stencil loops into
one helper (same formula, different floating-point association) moved the leg from the first
bank's 521 transfers / 43.75 km², 3 held samples and 21 mantle samples below −0.5 (minimum
−0.623) to the table's 8,559 / 224.61 km², none held and none outside. Both banks show the same
bleeding residues; what a round-off change moves by an order of magnitude is how often they
re-split, and whether the shallow mantle's cold side (baseline minimum −0.489, 0.011 inside the
bound) is pushed across. The banks in `logs/` are the re-measured ones, from the code as it
stands; the first bank's code is not recoverable. The cold side therefore stays an open
reading, not a finding of this probe: M0's floor, relaxation and merge probes all crossed it
(1,525 / 528 / 42 samples), and so does `relaxw` below, but a zero-sum deposit relaxation does
not demonstrably do so.

**Mantle+deposit scope** fixes the cold side by construction (mantle −0.183..1.006) and pays for
it through the residue at full scale: the mantle's residues re-melt every frame, 850,098 mantle
routings against 5,083, and the pool refuses 876,932 slots (the hottest parcel is a melt child of
1.9e-41 km²). Not a candidate; it also read 24.5 ms/frame concurrently.

**Wall-booked relaxation** (deposits, the net correction booked as an explicit source beside
the wall and *not* folded into `S.wall`): the operator-only ratio is 2.1e-3, but the source is
+3.58e5 km²·H, **−7.48% of the wall integral**, and the uncompensated gate reads 7.69e-2 — the
P4 gate fails by a factor of eight. Booking the correction makes it visible; it does not make
it conservative. It also bleeds (2,777 transfers, 2,600 below 1e-3 km² of host) and puts 395
mantle samples below −0.5.

### Protected events

No probe reproduces any of the three protected records exactly, and none routes any of their
material IDs. The nearest events by position and time are other material: stencil's single
transfer is 5,000–7,000 km away; subgrid-dep's nearest are 22–124 km and 92–1,340 frames off,
two of them bleed slivers (1e-37 and 2e-5 km² of host); subgrid-solid has a 11.3 km² event
1.3 km from the 10157 record but 148 frames earlier and a different parcel, under a saturated
pool; relaxw's are 124–442 km and 150–550 frames off. There is no correspondence argument to
make, and none is needed for a rejection.

### What the family says

The concentration is a FLIP no-restoring-force effect, and the only probe that removes it
restores light parcels toward their field. That direction stands, and on deposits it already
holds the bound, the wall, the books and the held-back census together. What blocks it is not a
constant but a piece of physics the engine lacks: a residue that the field supporting it cannot
re-melt indefinitely — melt depletion of the source, its solidus rising (or its fertility
falling) as melt is extracted. That is a melt-law specification with its own gate on the frozen
P3.2 census, not tuning.

What the matrix adds:

- **The deposit tail is not a seed-5 600 Myr accident** in kind, only in degree: the held-back
  population forms on other seeds and clocks within 300 Myr, without crossing the bound there.
- **Mobile melt carries the same concentration.** In every cell the hottest parcel is a
  4–21 km² melt parcel about one unit of T above its field, 40–300 km down. A split child is born
  at its liquidus (≤ ~1.2) and decompression at fixed H cannot raise T, so the excess is grid
  intake into a light grid-coupled parcel — the mechanism the draft measured in deposits, here
  in a phase that moves. Seed 2 crosses 1.5 at three of four clocks (by ≤ 0.027, 6–28
  parcel-frames). Nothing gated it before: `pt-p4-check`'s whole-cloud T reading is seed 1,
  whose melt peaks at 1.388. The M2 tail probes excluded mobile melt by design (a parcel in
  flight is not relaxed toward its host's field), so none of them would have reached it.
- **The latent remedy holds across clocks and seeds**, not just on the cell it was measured on:
  the identity is at round-off on 6,000 to 30,000 fluid substeps per cell.
- **Timing is not read from the matrix.** Cells ran two at a time and the per-frame mean
  scales with substeps per frame (~16 ms at 10–50 kyr, ~33 ms at 100 kyr, ~160 ms at 500 kyr,
  i.e. ~16 ms per fluid substep throughout); the 50 kyr gate is read serially in §4.

The four reds are reported, not waived: the per-phase bound is a gate, and the shipped tree does
not meet it. They are the open tail (deposits, seed 5, 600 Myr) and its newly found melt face
(seed 2, 300 Myr).
