# 0.1.9 — the two contact measurements: R1's site and background, R2's needle shoulder

## Scope

0.1.8's M3 matrix left `5000/5/100` at 8/11 with each red traced to a *measurement definition*:
R1's site window was anchored at the event bucket's left edge, and R2's needle compared the pair
against its own notch. This job decides both definitions by re-measurement on all four legs,
lands them in the shared measure and the audit, closes 0.1.8 §6's deferred review findings, and
archives the 0.1.8 plan. No kernel, constant, checkpoint or slice-format change.

## The R1 decision, and what it was measured against

The committed window spans the bucket's left edge ± 1.5 `w0` — the left 60% of a 2.5 `w0` bucket —
so an event, or the far end of its merged footprint, that lands in the remaining 40% is outside the
event's own window. On `5000/5/100` frame 2364 the retirement's own merge supplied *both* numbers
the gate read: site 2673 m at x 3616.0 km and "elsewhere" 2051 m at 3635.6 km. The bucket's left
edge is 3518.277 km, so the window's right edge is 3635.553 km — which is exactly the 2048-point
raster's 186th point (186 × 19.5460 km): the sample carrying the merged partner's territory sits on
the boundary and is excluded by the strict `d < 1.5 w0`. The gate compared
the event with itself: ratio 1.30 against the 1.25 budget, decided by the edge of a window.

Re-anchoring the window at the event raises the site maxima on every leg (a stricter reading) and
lowers the event-frame background (the event's own footprint is no longer counted as background):

| leg | bucket-edge site | event site | away on event frames | quiet yardstick | same-frame worst | site/away | site/quiet |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3000 / 1 / 50 | 1661 m | 1848 m | 3081 m | 5633 m | 14.18× | 0.60 | **0.33** |
| 3000 / 1 / 100 | 1871 m | 2179 m | 2070 m | 4532 m | 16.77× | 1.05 | **0.48** |
| 5000 / 5 / 50 | 1052 m | 1286 m | 691 m | 1651 m | 22.23× | **1.86** | **0.78** |
| 5000 / 5 / 100 | 2673 m | 2673 m | 1762 m | 4815 m | 18.79× | **1.52** | **0.56** |

Read against the event-frame background, the corrected site puts two legs red — one of them green
today — and the same-frame pairing is 14–22× on *every* leg, i.e. it is a statement about how calm
an event frame's remainder is, not a gate. 0.1.5-plan.md §1 names R1's yardstick in its own words
("the worst quiet-frame move … is the yardstick R1 is measured against") and states its own
baseline the same way (events 4657 m against quiet frames 276 m = 16.9×, which the restated gate
still rejects). The landing is therefore: event-anchored site, quiet-frame background, both other
readings reported beside it.

The landed gate on the four legs: 0.33 (accretion 1848 m vs 5633 m), 0.48 (birth 2179 m vs
4532 m), 0.78 (birth 1286 m vs 1651 m), 0.56 (accretion 2673 m vs 4815 m). The event-frame
backgrounds are 3081 / 2070 / 691 / 1762 m and the same-frame worsts 14.18 / 16.77 / 22.23 /
18.79 (report only).

## The R2 decision

The measured site (`5000/5/100`, frames 3180–3182 and 3312, x 1424.1–1424.6 km, plates 5/0): an
inherited, width-compressed felsic margin column, 45.8 km over ground that is 8.5 km on the thin
plate, 20.5 km at its own notch, 21.4 km one column on and 40.9 km at the flank. Immediate-slot
ratio 2.23, belt-neighbourhood 1.12. Candidate shoulders over every non-ghost collide sample of
the four legs (worst ratio, failing samples at `P.beltPeak` 1.5):

| shoulder | 3000 / 1 / 50 | 3000 / 1 / 100 | 5000 / 5 / 50 | 5000 / 5 / 100 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| immediate slot, walked | 1.35, 0 | 1.39, 0 | 1.15, 0 | 2.23, 4 |
| orogenic flow's reach (±`beltFeed`) | 1.33, 0 | 1.39, 0 | 1.15, 0 | 2.15, 3 |
| belt neighbourhood (±(`beltFeed`+2)) | 1.33, 0 | 1.39, 0 | 1.15, 0 | 1.26, 0 |
| growth since collision onset | 1.50, 0 | 1.55, 8 | 2.02, 492 | 1.71, 428 |

The flow's reach alone is not enough (the high ground is at the flank column), and the growth
candidate is rejected: it needs per-pair onset state and it fires on ordinary belt growth, which is
the mechanism. Across the four legs' 14,586 collision samples the wider shoulder flips exactly the
four samples above, all at one site; the isolated spike stays red (m2-check's plain 2.00 and
plateau 1.58 fixtures).
