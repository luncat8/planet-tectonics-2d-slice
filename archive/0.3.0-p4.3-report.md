# 0.3.0 P4.3 — weathering, erosion and sedimentation

P4.3 is the first P4 leg that moves material *sideways*: everything before it moved heat and rock
through the interior, and the surface stayed P2.3's kinematic profile. One new file,
`js/pt/erode.js` (`PTERODE`), sits at G7's tail behind `F.surface` and does three things per frame
on the profile the planet already has: it reads the drainage off `zh` (steepest-descent receivers,
a topological peel for the areas, and a deposition site at the end of every flow path), charges
every column a stream-power demand `kStream * sqrt(A) * slope` capped at `eroMax` with `slope`
the drop to that column's receiver, and pays that demand by transferring mass from the column's
shallowest in-band solid parcel into a deposit-phase parcel seated at the site -- P3.4's own
deposit stock. The law was chosen by a comparison harness before anything shipped, and it is a
measurement, not a preference: the conservative hillslope rule loses on basin capture, on
transport reach and on effect at equal work. A review (`archive/0.3.0-p4.3-review-worklog.md`)
then replaced the right-face slope the first landing had stored with the channel slope, and the
lowest-slot seat with the shallowest in-band deposit.

Primary gate: `experiments/pt-p4-erosion.js`, **ALL PASS (33 checks)** in 154 s for 820 Myr of
simulation — the pass's demand table recomputed from `zh` and the receiver (not from `erS`),
the divide gated as a left-draining kink, donor rule and transfer arithmetic read off one state
by hand, a 2,000-frame leg on the frozen relief fixture, the merge into P3.4's landing pile, two
live 300 Myr legs (pass on and pass off), the pass's own attribution on a frozen frame, and a
bitwise replay. Law choice:
`experiments/pt-p4-transport-sweep.js`, **ALL PASS (37 checks)**. Logs
`experiments/logs/0.3.0-p4.3-review-*.txt`; the development record is
`archive/0.3.0-p4.3-worklog.md`, the review `archive/0.3.0-p4.3-review-worklog.md`.

## Shipped live result at 300 Myr (seed 1, 50 kyr/frame)

- **1,432,033 transfers moved 99,231.1 km²** of the 120-km weathering band down the profile's own
  drainage; the world ends with **352,435 km² of sediment in 763 parcels** (none at t = 0)
  against 277,872 km² in 362 parcels with the pass off — the flights' own pile plus the
  surface law's;
- unmet demand 13.8 km² (0.01% of the throughput), 0 refusals, band mass 2.191e6 km² against
  2.196e6 km² with the pass off;
- fingerprints 2960640650489454 with the pass against 1736079014518515 without it (the off
  fingerprint is the pre-review planet);
- the frozen-frame attribution: 1,416,063 transfers / 11,787.9 km² over 6,000 pass-only frames,
  the losing columns above the gaining ones by 0.247 km of mean height, 0.446 ms/frame of the
  pass's own work (3.48% of the frame); two 200-frame live legs replay to the same complete-state
  fingerprint (186923077625313 both times, 47,106 transfers);
- the law on the fixture is concentrated where the drainage accumulates (the top decile of columns
  carries 24.3% of a frame's demand, more than twice uniform) and, on a linear flank, ranks with
  drainage area (columns 45/60 share slope 5.33e-4, A 1313/2250 km², demand 1.81e-2/2.37e-2 km²).
  The divide drains its steep left face (2.08e-2 km² a frame against 1.75e-2 a column) — the
  right-face reading that made it look quiet was the uphill side.
