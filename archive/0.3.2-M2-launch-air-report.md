# 0.3.2 M2 — launch/air booking remedy: accepted

**Decision:** the latent-book asymmetry M1 localized (`FLIGHT.launch` creating off-manifold
air parcels whose equilibrium snap went unbooked) is remedied by the "booked air snaps"
family combined with an on-manifold, stock-consistent launch, plus a per-frame gate on the
extended cell. The wall ledger is untouched; the fix is local to the latent/sensible books;
every frame of the 600 Myr seed-5 cell now closes the P3.1 identity to ~1e-8 km²·H (down
from 167.41 km²·H). All existing owner harnesses stay green; the extended-cell fingerprint
re-banks to 6191250240167390.

Worklog with numbers: `archive/0.3.2-M2-launch-air-worklog.md`.

## The fix (three files in `js/pt/`)

1. **`flight.js` `launch`** — after `PHASE.byH` at surface pressure, set **both** `T` and
   `melt` to the equilibrium output (so the clast leaves the vent on the manifold) and book
   the adiabatic sensible drop `d·(T_eq − T_stock)` against `S.ledger`. The vent's debit
   remains the stock-consistent `f·d` of every extensive field; the extra latent that
   decompression melting produces at the surface is funded by that sensible drop.
   `S.wall` is not touched.
2. **`phase.js` `equil`** — book `m·dT` for every parcel whose temperature changes, not
   only grid-coupled ones. This makes the identity robust to any future air-phase kernel
   (depth change in flight, etc.) and is a no-op on the shipped trajectory because launch
   already leaves air parcels on the surface-pressure manifold.
3. **`fluid.js` `diag` + `state.js` + `ui.js`** — per-frame latent residual gate on every
   frame (G8) after `PTPOOL.inventory`, exposed as `S.d.latent` / `S.d.latentAbs` and shown
   on the HUD ledger line. This replaces the end-of-leg-only gates on the short owner legs
   with the per-frame gate on the extended cell the plan requires.

## Validation highlights

- 600 Myr seed-5 (M1 banked cell, 50 kyr/f): worst abs residual **6.44e-8 km²·H** over
  12,000 frames vs baseline **167.41 km²·H** — nine orders of magnitude, at the Float64
  noise floor of the gather/equil pair.
- P3 owners (`pt-p3-check`, `pt-p3-pool`, `pt-p3-flight`, `pt-p3-erupt`) all green; latent
  residual 1e-12 .. 1e-8 on the live legs.
- P4 integrated 300 Myr seed-1 leg: latent residual **1.09e-13 relative**, mass/enthalpy/
  wall/contact/slab/arc/erosion all green.
- Three protected G5 deposit transfers survive (9.73 km² melt, same host masses as the
  M1 trace's protected events — path preserved, not tuned away).
- `node --check` passes on all `js/pt/*.js` and experiment runners.

## Constraints respected

- **Wall ledger untouched:** `S.wall` is written only by `F.wall` (operator flux) and
  `FLIGHT.cool` (pre-existing surface cooling, booked honestly). The fix adds writes to
  `S.ledger` only.
- **Per-frame gate on the extended cell:** `F.diag` measures the residual every frame and
  reports it on the HUD; experiment harnesses that sample per frame (`pt-heat-tail.js`)
  see the same identity via their existing `POOL.inventoryComp` call.
- **Full re-bank:** fingerprints change (M2 explicitly calls for this); the new seed-5
  600 Myr fingerprint is 6191250240167390, the seed-1 300 Myr fingerprint is
  7959153446654571. Old hashes remain baseline evidence in the archived logs and are not
  treated as expected outputs.
- **No routing/parameter changes:** Condition 5 and the deposit field filter stay, per the
  plan; the hot-tail family (stencil-scale placement and/or wall-booked relaxation) is the
  next measurement once a clean identity is underneath it.
