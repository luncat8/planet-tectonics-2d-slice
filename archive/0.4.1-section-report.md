# 0.4.1 M2 — the reconstruction, reported

The task was: review the previous commit (`e5e9994`, the plan plus M0's format and M1's reader),
improve what was worth improving, and continue the ladder. M2 is the result: a pasted cut is now a
section — a world the column engine can look at, and optionally run.

## What a cut becomes

`SectionPack.load(text)` verifies the bytes, then `SEED.layout(pack, {seed, t, Tm})` lays them on
the section's own 512 columns: an arc-length box filter for the continuous fields, widest-sample
for the categorical ones, three beds per column (mafic → felsic → sediment, aged from the cut), the
runs of `plate` become plates (capped at `plateCap` 32 by merging the narrowest run into its wider
neighbour, counted), `bnd`/`pol` become the seeded boundaries with `edgeRelN` read off the cut's own
`vt`, and `zDyn` takes the difference the model's isostasy would miss by. `|z − zM|` at frame 0 is
therefore an identity, measured at 9.1e-13 m at L5 and at L7, on every column.

The page switches to the engine's own view (one renderer, no second draw path), with the cut's `zM`
as a step line over it and a hatch below the base of the crust. Three switches — `raw cut` (r),
`overlay` (o), `detach and run` — read their state back from the page, and the HUD carries the
section's six lines above the engine's: both resolutions, the arc scale, the out-of-plane fraction,
the assumption record, the ledger, and the spin-up. A window cut (mode L) is laid the same way,
keeps its leftover arc as a booked tail, and cannot be put on the clock: its run switch is disabled
*and* `frame()` pins `dG = 0`, because three other things write that rate.

## What the review changed

- the plan's `alive` row asked for a trench sliver per gap sample; the measurement (50.7 km of
  crust moved into one neighbour, 7.4 km of relief, one record retired — in a single frame) said the
  box filter's zero is the right hole at a section's resolution, so the plan now says that, with the
  number;
- §4.3.4's absolute quiet-start bound ("a few hundred metres on the first frame") is not met by the
  model's own freshly reset planet either (3 377 m), so the gate is a measured comparison: the
  identity at frame 0, nothing re-signed at frame 1, no topology for `P.evAge`, and the first frame
  no worse than the control;
- the bed-age convention had to be picked (the engine stores relative rock age in one place and
  world time in another); the sediment drape now inherits the crust's age and books it;
- `?seed=` had to reach the drawing stream and not only the plumes, or a seeded section carried two
  worlds;
- `experiments/pack-fixture.js` grew kinematics that agree with its own boundaries (a fixture whose
  velocities contradict its `bnd` lets the seeding "fix" the disagreement and call it a quiet start);
- `port/` was left byte-identical, including a duplication a shared helper could have removed —
  three duplicated lines are cheaper than a broken mirror.

## Found on the way, deliberately not fixed

`CRU.belt` and `CRU.delaminate` scale their flow by `1000` on an excess that is already in metres,
where `P.kBelt` and `P.kDelam` are documented as rates *per km* (`0.1.5-plan.md` §M2(b), (d)). The
two flows are a million times what the plan says and the model runs at its own "never half in one
frame" cap on any frame size. It is in code `e5e9994` did not touch, fixing it re-measures every
0.1.x number, so it is recorded in `roadmap.md` §3 with the measurements and left for its own
milestone. The section reports what it does (over-collapse, the spin-up line) instead of smoothing
it away.
