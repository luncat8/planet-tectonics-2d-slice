## 1. Why Candidate A (`zDyn` ramp on the absorbing record) fails

When `COL.drain` deletes a ghost `G` in one frame between an oceanic column `A` and
an overriding continental margin `B`, two geometric steps happen simultaneously:

1. **The territory dump (`bothFloor` and `openEdge`).** In `bothFloor` (`gL, gR <= min
   ≈ 9.38 km`, 160 of 201 drains on the four strict legs), `A` and `B` each gain
   `4.69 km` of width in one frame. In `openEdge` (`edgeRelN > 0`, 41 of 201 drains),
   consumption resets the new boundary, slab pull drops, and `edgeRelN` crosses zero
   2–3 frames (`0.1–0.3 Myr`) after consumption while the downgoing gap is still
   `39..78 km` wide — dumping `19.5..39.1 km` of width onto `B` in one frame, halving
   `B`'s stack thickness and dropping `SURF.elev(B)` by `3.2..5.9 km`.
2. **The 3-point polyline collapse (`z_G != z_chord`).** While `G` is alive,
   `SURF.morph` relaxes `S.z[G]` toward the straight chord `AB` with `P.tauSliver =
   4 Myr`. When `G` is deleted at age `0.1..2.0 Myr` while sitting `9.4 km` from `B`
   (`z_B ≈ +6.5 km`) and `78 km` from `A` (`z_A ≈ −4.6 km`), `S.z[G]` is still at
   `−1.2..−3.3 km` (`5.2..7.2 km` below the chord `AB`). Deleting vertex `G`
   replaces the bent 2-segment polyline `A → G → B` with the single straight segment
   `A → B`.

On `3000/1/50` frame 1519 (`x_A = 14113.3 km, z_A = −4663 m`; `x_G = 14191.4 km,
z_G = −1210 m`; `x_B = 14200.8 km, z_B = +6498 m`), the two adjacent 2048-raster
samples `m = 726` (`14184.5 km`, `prevZ = −1515 m`) and `m = 727` (`14204.1 km`,
`prevZ = +6547 m`) become linear functions of the single post-deletion margin
elevation `z_new`:
$$\Delta z_{726}(z_{\text{new}}) = |653 + 0.814\,z_{\text{new}}|, \qquad \Delta z_{727}(z_{\text{new}}) = |0.954\,z_{\text{new}} - 6195|$$
- No ramp (`z_new = +1915 m`): $\Delta z_{727} = 4368\text{ m}$.
- Candidate A (`z_new = +6498 m`, holding `B` at its pre-retirement elevation via
  `zDyn = +5747 m`): $\Delta z_{727} = 5\text{ m}$, **$\Delta z_{726} = 5942\text{ m}$**
  (`6409 m` at the adjacent sample).
- Minimax over all $z_{\text{new}} \in \mathbb{R}$ ($z_{\text{new}} = +3135\text{ m}$):
  $\max(\Delta z_{726}, \Delta z_{727}) = 3205\text{ m} > 1.25 \times 1458\text{ m}$.

Holding a `40 km`-thick column at `+6.4 km` via `zDyn` for `tauDyn = 10 Myr` also
drives `SURF.k6`'s cubic erosion and `PLT.basal`'s slope term, re-rolling the
trajectory into an R2 contiguous-width failure (`87.6% < 90%` on `3000/1/50`) and an
R3 ceiling breach (`82.4 km > 81.8 km` on `5000/5/50`). Candidate A fails all four
strict legs (`0 / 4`).

## 2. Candidate B (landed) — handing the territory back over frames

Instead of dropping the ghost in one frame, a finished ghost enters the retiring
state (`S.colGhost[i] = 2`), retains its diminishing width `wKeep` in its unused top
layer-age slot `S.layAg[(i + 1)·P.layerCap − 1]` (keeping `S.oldW[i]` intact for
`COL.k4` volume conservation,including across C4 imports, with no new state array or
checkpoint version change), and releases at most
$$\Delta w_{\text{rel}} = \max\!\left(2000,\; \min\!\left(\frac{\alpha(\text{dt})\,w_{im}}{dr / \text{span}},\; \frac{\alpha(\text{dt})\,w_{ip}}{dl / \text{span}}\right)\right), \qquad \alpha(\text{dt}) = \frac{0.0825\,\text{dt}}{0.025 + \text{dt}}$$
per frame (`0.055` at `50 kyr/frame`, `0.066` at `100 kyr/frame`, `< 0.0825 < 0.10`
at every slider rate). `S.widths()` hands `rel = wVor − wKeep` to `im` (`dr / span`)
and `ip` (`dl / span`), preserving `SUM(colW) = P.wrap` identically and matching the
post-deletion Voronoi widths to `0 m` as `wKeep → 0`. In lockstep, `SURF.morph` steps
`(S.z[k], S.hDraw[k])` by `0.25` toward `(zt, ht)` on retiring ghosts, and
`COL.intents` fires `intent[i] = 3` (`COL.drain`) once `S.colW[i] <= step·1.05` and
`|S.z[i] − zt| <= 500 m`.
