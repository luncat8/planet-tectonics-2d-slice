# 0.3.2 M12 — attribution of the repair-adjacent minority, the non-deposit thermal faces, and the contact guard residue across the 600 Myr distribution

## 1. Scope and observation-neutral census

At the close of 0.3.2 M11 (`archive/0.3.2-M11-drift-attribution-report.md` §6 and `roadmap.md`),
three open items remained from the 0.3.2 series:

1. **The repair-adjacent minority of held deposit material (`0.1–33%` within 1 frame in M6):**
   `G.reseed` relocates donor markers out of rings of radius $r = 2, 8, 32$ nodes ($125$–$2,000$ km)
   with specific enthalpy $H$ unchanged between `G.scatterT` and `G.gatherDT`; M6 reported that
   $0.1\%$–$33\%$ of held deposit mass-time sat within 1 frame of a relocation (`15%` on landed `s1`).
2. **The non-deposit thermal faces (`mantle-cold` below `-0.5`, `mobile-melt-hot` above `1.5`):**
   scoped out of `P.depBound = 0.25` in M7 Statement 1 because P3.2 is frozen, and previously
   inspected with `--watch` only on seed 5 (`archive/0.3.2-M3-depletion-report.md` §9) where
   `outsideBelow[0] = 0` and `outsideAbove[1] = 0` at 600 Myr, whereas seeds 1, 3, and 7 carry
   non-zero counts in the M6/M8 banks.
3. **The contact guard's finite-sweep residue (`S.d.resid > 0` after `P.ctSweep = 3`):**
   the 3-sweep cap in `CONTACT.guard` (`js/pt/contact.js`) leaves surviving hard-radius overlaps
   on 1 of 6,000 frames on the 300 Myr integrated leg (`0.9969` of `contactHard`) and on 26–280
   frames on the 600 Myr M6/M8 worlds.

We built an observation-neutral attribution harness (`experiments/pt-faces-guard-census.js`) and
assertion suite (`experiments/pt-faces-guard-check.js`, 960 assertions) and ran all 8 600-Myr
distribution worlds (`seeds 1, 3, 5, 7` $\times$ `depBound` $\in \{0, 0.25\}$ at 50 kyr/frame,
12,000 frames). All 8 runs reproduce their unobserved M6/M8 reference fingerprints bitwise
(`experiments/logs/0.3.2-M12-{base,dep}-s{1,3,5,7}-600.json`).

## 2. Item 1 — The repair-adjacent held minority and `G.reseed` relocation census

### 2.1 The `null >= 0` coercion in M6's `heldJoin`

In `experiments/pt-heat-tail.js`, `movedAge(S, p)` returns `null` when `lastMove[S.id[p]] === 0`
(a parcel that has never been relocated by `G.reseed`), and `hjAdd(age, mass, gap)` buckets each
held sample by testing `age >= 0` and `age <= JOIN_WIN[i]`. In JavaScript, relational comparisons
coerce `null` to `+0`, so `null >= 0` and `null <= 1` both evaluate to `true`. Consequently, every
M6/M7/M8 bank recorded `heldJoin.never.n = 0` and added every never-relocated held sample into
`heldJoin.ever` and into all five cumulative windows `win[0..4]` ($\le 1, 3, 10, 30, 100$ frames):

$$\text{hjM6.n}[i] = \text{hjTrue.n}[i] + \text{hjTrue.never.n}, \qquad \text{hjM6.mass}[i] = \text{hjTrue.mass}[i] + \text{hjTrue.never.mass} \qquad (i = 0..4).$$

The M7 trace banks confirm this at the individual-parcel level: on `dep-s1`
(`0.3.2-M7-dep-s1-trace-600.json`), deposit parcel `id = 25109` ($28.044\text{ km}^2$ host,
admitted at frame 5,193 and delivered at frame 7,552 with `movedAgo: null`) was held for $2,088$
frames ($2,927.79\text{ km}^2\cdot\text{Myr}$), accounting for **$99.92\%$** of `dep-s1`'s
M6-reported $2,930.05\text{ km}^2\cdot\text{Myr}$ ($2,109$ samples) "within 1 frame of a
relocation". Only $21$ samples ($2.26\text{ km}^2\cdot\text{Myr}$, **$0.0117\%$** of `dep-s1`'s
held mass-time) were genuinely within 1 frame of a `G.reseed` move. Likewise, on `base-s5`,
where M6 reported $22,713.57\text{ km}^2\cdot\text{Myr}$ ($33.33\%$) within 1 frame,
**$22,592.10\text{ km}^2\cdot\text{Myr}$ ($33.1553\%$, $14,035$ samples) was never relocated**
and only **$121.47\text{ km}^2\cdot\text{Myr}$ ($0.1783\%$, $32$ samples)** was truly within
1 frame of a move.

**Physical attribution of `mantle-cold`:**
1. **100% shallow conductive boundary-layer localization (`0–50 km`):** Across all 8 worlds, every
   single `mantle-cold` sample ($6,707$ unbound, $2,953$ bound) sits in the topmost depth band
   $y \in [0, 50]\text{ km}$, and each seed's entire count is carried by only **$3$–$5$ distinct
   material IDs** ($0$ on seed 5).
2. **Weathering floor (`P.eroMinM = 50 km²`) + melt-split mass reduction:** Zero samples occur on
   parcels with $m > 200\text{ km}^2$. On the shipped bound worlds (`dep-s1, dep-s3, dep-s7`),
   **$80.1\%$ ($2,366 / 2,953$)** of samples sit exactly on the weathering donor floor
   $m = 50.0000\text{ km}^2$ (`js/pt/erode.js`) after $155$–$5,503$ erosion steps removed
   $82.4$–$241.9\text{ km}^2$ of initial shallow marker mass; **$11.1\%$ ($327 / 2,953$)** sit at
   $m < 50\text{ km}^2$ where $4$–$35$ P3.2 mantle melt splits (`POOL.separate`) further reduced
   their residue mass below $50\text{ km}^2$ (down to $2.05\text{ km}^2$ on `dep-s3` `id = 3051` and
   $20.23\text{ km}^2$ on `dep-s7` `id = 1357`); and **$8.8\%$ ($260 / 2,953$)** sit at
   $50 < m \le 200\text{ km}^2$.
3. **Pure FLIP passenger cooling (`rawDi < 0`), not `depBound` placement:** Under `flip = 1`, a
   $50\text{ km}^2$ mantle residue in the $0$–$50\text{ km}$ surface cooling layer carries a small
   stencil mass share ($s_p \approx 0.008$–$0.156$, mean $0.041$–$0.064$). When warmer rock shares
   the same shallow stencil ($T_{\text{field}} \approx +0.04..+0.10$), `G.diffuse` cools the node
   toward the $T = 0$ surface wall ($\text{inc}[q] < 0$) every frame, and `G.gatherDT` delivers that
   negative increment to both the warm heavy neighbour and the already-cold $50\text{ km}^2$ mantle
   residue. On the worst carrier of every bound world (`id 374` on `dep-s1`, `id 1345` on `dep-s3`,
   `id 2667` on `dep-s7`), the sub-zero enthalpy comes from raw conduction gather
   (`negRawDi = -0.6852, -0.7778, -0.9427`), whereas `depBound` 3$\times$3 stencil placement is
   **net zero or warming** (`negBdGiven = +0.1321, 0.0000, +0.0467`).
4. **Effect of landed `depBound = 0.25`:** By preventing unbound hot deposit outliers ($T$ up to
   $2.31$) from holding shallow stencils artificially hot next to the $T = 0$ wall, `depBound = 0.25`
   already eliminates `base-s1`'s $-3.7470$ spike (lifting `s1` $\min T$ to $-0.5678$) and reduces
   total `mantle-cold` samples across the 4 seeds by **$56.0\%$** ($6,707 \to 2,953$). Because
   $T < -0.5$ is strictly subsolidus ($\xi = 0$, $H = T$), it never triggers phase transitions or
   segregation and closes exactly in the sensible and enthalpy ledgers.

**Physical attribution of `mobile-melt-hot`:**
1. **Two distinct birth classes — and `depBound = 0.25` eliminates the deposit-born class (`base-s3`):**
   - On `base-s3`, all $38$ samples (`maxT = 1.6518`) belong to a single $0.8756\text{ km}^2$

     (`movedAgoAtEnter = 0`) into a warm stencil ($T_{\text{field}} = 0.915 \ge T_s$) and converted

### 4.2 Mechanism of the finite-sweep residue

1. **100% multi-pair sandwich attribution (`381 / 381` bound pairs, `641 / 641` unbound pairs):**
   In `CONTACT.guard` (`js/pt/contact.js`), `sweep()` displaces an overlapping inter-cluster pair
   $(p, q)$ by $\pm \frac{1}{2}(h_{\text{hard}} - d)$ along their line of centres, where
   $h_{\text{hard}} = 0.55\,\Delta x = 34.375\text{ km}$. An isolated pair clears to
   $d = h_{\text{hard}}$ in a single sweep. Across all 96,000 frames of the 8 worlds, **every single
   surviving overlap after `P.ctSweep = 3` sweeps (`1,022 / 1,022` pairs) involves a marker shared
   across $\ge 2$ colliding pairs during the frame's guard pass** (`sandwichPairs === residPairs` on
   all 8 worlds). Because Gauss-Seidel pairwise pushes divide each overlap in half, a marker pressed
   between two neighbours trades half of its deficit back and forth on each sweep.
2. **Shallow wall reflection (`G.keepInside`) on steep trench pairs:**
   In $20.2\%$ ($77 / 381$) of bound residue pairs (including the worst event on every world, where a
   shallow marker at $y_P \approx 4.18$–$4.85\text{ km}$ meets a deeper marker at
   $y_Q \approx 35.0$–$36.5\text{ km}$ across adjacent columns), the upward push component
   $-u_y \cdot \text{push}$ drives the shallow marker above `M.yMin = 3.01 km` ($\eta < \eta_{\min}$),
   and `G.keepInside` reflects its vertical step back downward, slowing the geometric relaxation.
3. **Magnitude and scratch-sweep clearance:**
   - Across all 48,000 frames of the 4 shipped (`depBound = 0.25`) worlds, `S.d.resid > 0` occurs on
     only $0.76\%$ of frames ($366 / 48,000$, down $40.7\%$ from $617$ in the unbound controls), and
     **the worst surviving distance on any frame of any shipped world is $0.975830$ of
     `contactHard`** ($33.544\text{ km}$ vs $34.375\text{ km}$, a residual overlap of $0.831\text{ km}$
     or $2.42\%$ of $h_{\text{hard}}$).
   - Replaying the exact `sweep()` law on a read-only scratch buffer confirms that **$100.0\%$ of all
     983 residue frames across all 8 worlds clear to zero overlap in finite sweeps** ($4 \le s \le 40$
     on shipped worlds, with $71.6\%$ clearing by sweep 10 and $91.5\%$ by sweep 15). Raising
     `P.ctSweep` is unnecessary: the 3-sweep cap already holds hard-radius separation to $\ge 97.58\%$
     across the entire 600 Myr shipped distribution while keeping the guard pass bounded at 3 sweeps.
