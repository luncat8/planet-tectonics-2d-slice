# 0.3.2 M0 — measured remedies, none selected

**Decision:** keep the shipping engine unchanged. None of these four probes satisfies the
combined tail, intake, books and protected-transfer requirements. No constant is frozen,
no `params.js` value is changed, and 0.3.1 condition 5 remains intact.

Specification: `0.3.2-plan.md`. Raw results: `experiments/logs/0.3.2-tail-*.json`.
The experiment runner is `experiments/pt-heat-tail.js`; the bank/replay checker is
`experiments/pt-heat-tail-check.js`.

## Cell and acceptance interpretation

All measurements use **seed 5, 50 kyr/frame, 600 Myr (12,000 frames)**, the same explicit
configuration as `pt-buried-deposit-melt.js`, with all shipped P3/P4 passes enabled. Each
candidate runs in a fresh Node process, serially, without competing simulation jobs.

The baseline reproduces **8261233554728015**, **3 transfers**, **9.730158098 km²** melt,
**30,906 held-back samples**, **68,140.14285 km²·Myr** held-back host exposure and **5,083**
mantle routings. These match the M2 bank, not merely its rounded headline count.

Important distinction from the draft: `pt-p4-check.js` actually asserts **< 0.01** for the
long-leg worst cumulative `|intake/wall - 1|`, and **< 1e-9** on its short matrix. Its
**4.89e-5** at seed 1 / 300 Myr is a historical reading, not the long-leg assertion. The
new 600 Myr baseline itself reads **0.0015455**. This report shows the actual 1% gate and
keeps the draft's 5e-5 reference as a separate diagnostic; it does not silently relax a gate
to fit a candidate. None of the measured rows meets the 5e-5 reference on this longer cell.

All fit the **20 ms mean frame gate on this host**. Timing brackets `SIM.step`, subtracts
read-only G5 sampling time, and excludes the experiment's post-frame compensated inventory
and extrema scans. Candidate arithmetic, including the merge search and bucket rebuilds,
is inside the timer. This is an instrumented kernel-time estimate; observation can still
affect cache/GC behavior. The end-of-batch baseline replay provides a host-drift check,
not a claim of a statistically resolved sub-millisecond overhead. The baseline replay
measured **17.07 ms/frame**, versus 15.82 initially: this 1.25 ms spread is wider than the
observed differences between candidates. All rows pass the absolute gate; their small
relative timing differences cannot be ranked reliably from this batch.

All four probes still violate the **-0.5..1.5** temperature bound, retain positive
held-back concentration exposure, and fail to reproduce any of the three original event
records exactly. The floor, relaxation and local-merge trajectories also develop mantle
cold-bound violations (1,525, 528 and 42 parcel-frame samples respectively). The shipped
baseline has 2,856 deposit violations and none in the other phases. A remedy must address
the two-sided tail, not only the hottest parcel.

### What each probe means

- The **cap** scales the complete gathered increment by `min(1, 2/gain)` using normalized
  stencil gain. It does not cap node `cw`, and does not redistribute withheld energy.
- The **share floor** attenuates the increment by `min(1, share/0.01)`. It manufactures no
  parcel mass and makes no compensating wall entry. Both gather probes apply to every
  grid-coupled phase, so they test the mechanism outside deposits too.
- The **relaxation** moves deposit enthalpy 0.001 toward the equilibrium enthalpy of the
  ordinary bilinear field after each gather. This is not simply the existing `flip` PIC
  term: that term samples T with unnormalized adjoint weights. The correction is booked
  as intake, not as wall flux. It reduces exposure most here, but is neither conservative
  against the wall nor sufficient to bound the tail. No clock-independent law is claimed.
- The **minimum-mass probe** uses the existing conservative pool merge before scatter,
  into an at-least-1 km² compatible deposit in the same nearest-node bucket, with matching
  quench certificate. It completed **888 merges** but had **774,639 stranded attempts**
  (repeated parcel-frame attempts, not distinct parcels). A compatible local recipient is
  often absent. Thus this policy cannot promise a floor, even though its integral wall
  gate passes. It neither deletes slivers nor mixes them into mantle to force success.

These are four specified probes, **not an exhaustive rejection of every parameter or
algorithm in these families**. No value was selected by how much melt it routes.

## Books: a newly exposed baseline failure, not a hidden pass

Every row has **zero measured relative mass residual**, and worst relative enthalpy-to-
recorded-intake residual **<= 8.11e-12**, below 1e-9. Closing that book does not mean the
intake equals the wall: the cap/share/relaxation probes record their modified delivery
honestly, which is precisely why a second, independent wall gate is necessary.

The **P3.1 latent identity does not pass at this extended horizon**, including on the
unmodified baseline. Its every-frame worst relative residual is:

| Baseline | Cap 2 | Share 0.01 | Relax 0.999 | Minimum 1 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 9.6649e-3 | 1.1737e-2 | 2.9095e-3 | 7.5899e-3 | 5.6346e-3 |

The metric is `abs(hLedger - ledger - L*(invLTot - invL0)) /
max(1, abs(hLedger - ledger))`, using compensated inventories. The existing P4 owner checks
this identity **at the end of its seed-1 300 Myr leg**, not every frame of seed 5 for 600 Myr.
Those earlier green checks and this extended-cell red are different claims. The share,
relaxation and merge rows have end residuals of 2.8726e-3, 7.3450e-3 and 5.6346e-3, so this
is not solely an early near-zero denominator artifact. The baseline replay confirms an
end residual of **9.6020e-3**, with the worst at **frame 11992 (599.60 Myr)** and an
absolute discrepancy of **167.35794 km²·H** at that frame. No bookkeeping patch is folded into
this experiment and no cause is claimed without a kernel-boundary trace. This is an
additional acceptance blocker to diagnose before selecting a remedy.
