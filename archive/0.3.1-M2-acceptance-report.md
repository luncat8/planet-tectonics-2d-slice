# 0.3.1 M2 — acceptance, and the field term that filtered the intake

0.3.1 M1 landed the buried-deposit remelt route and left one reading open: every crossing it took
sat 0.1–0.3 of T above the node field it was embedded in. M2 measured that at the moment of
routing, per source, and the measurement decided the question — the parcels were reaching
`meltHold` by the marker↔grid pairing's own concentration, not because the mantle around them was
warm. Eligibility gained a fifth condition, the field supporting the parcel's own solidus, and the
whole series re-ran on the resulting tree.

Primary gates: `experiments/pt-buried-deposit-melt.js` — **ALL PASS (37)** on the canonical seed-6
leg and again on the 600 Myr cell, **(41)** and **(38)** on the two matrices; `pt-p3-check.js`
**ALL PASS (42)** with its eight owners; `pt-p4-check.js` **ALL PASS (29)** with the five P4
owners and P3 inside it; the five P3 owners whose subject is the melt path — **(64)**, **(70)**,
**(68)**, **(39)**, **(50)**; `pt-ui.js` **(53)**; and `experiments/acceptance.js`
**ALL PASS (49)**. No failures anywhere in the series. Logs `experiments/logs/0.3.1-M2-*.txt`; the
build record is `archive/0.3.1-M2-acceptance-worklog.md`; the specification is `0.3.1-plan.md` §3
condition 5 and §5.

## The measurement that decided it

One leg, seed 6, 300 Myr at 50 kyr/frame, everything shipped on. Every routing candidate is read
at the boundary the kernel decides on, with the pairing's own two numbers beside it: the gain
(`cw = mu_k / W_k` over the parcel's stencil — the factor between the node increments' plain
interpolation and the specific increment the parcel actually took) and the parcel's mass share of
that stencil.

| population | samples | T − field | gain | stencil mass share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| mantle, routed by G5 (the shipped law) | 3221 | **+0.0044** mean (94.33% of its mass within 0..0.05) | 1.013 | 0.177 |
| mantle, all (1/8 stride) | 1.759e7 | −0.0007 mean | 1.052 (max 12.96) | 0.089 |
| buried deposits, all (1/8 stride) | 39889 | **−0.126** mean (max +0.090) | 1.079 | 0.092 |
| deposits the four-term gate took | 6 | **+0.244** mean (0.222..0.305) | **1.200** | **0.005** |

The shipped melt law melts where the mantle is hot. Buried sediment is colder than its field. The
six parcels the route took are outside their own population's sampled range, at 1/18th its mass
share — five of them slivers of 0.014–0.021 km² against a base parcel of ~1.8e3 km². That is
quadrature concentration, and turning it into chamber melt is a heat source at parcel scale, which
`0.3.1-plan.md` §1 forbids.

## The term

```
G.sampleAt(M, S.Tg, x, e) >= PHASE.solidus(y, cF, cW)
```

No new constant, evaluated last so it costs a bilinear sample only on parcels about to route, and a
strict refinement of the **deposit branch**: P3.2's mantle path is untouched and visibly so — the
four cells that never routed under M1 keep M1's fingerprints bitwise, `pt-p3-melt.js` passes its 64
checks unchanged, and one fixture sheds a mantle parcel in a node field of zero. `detach` takes the
mesh now (`detach(M, S)`). The scoping is measured, not assumed: the mantle branch's own routed
samples run −0.7714..+0.3625 of field support at a mean of +0.0017 on the 600 Myr cell, so the
same condition there would veto part of the frozen P3.2 law rather than refine it.

There is no switch for it in `params.js`. A switch would add a constant and a second active path
without answering which criterion is physically correct; `0.3.1-plan.md` §5 records that one is
added only with a concrete hypothesis and tests for both branches, and that the
deposit-admission mechanics are revisited separately when data or justification appears.

## What is open, and it is not 0.3.1's

The term keeps the artifact out of the chambers; it does not explain it. The concentration is the
pairing's distributional tail — exact in total (|intake/wall − 1| = 4.89e-5) and unbounded per
parcel — and it is not deposit-only: the mantle population's own gain reaches 12.96. Deposits show
it first because erosion and landing make light parcels and the surface quench gives them a cold
start to be amplified from. The 600 Myr cell measures the tail at its full range: 30906 held-back
samples at gains of 0.399..**11.880** and T-field gaps up to **+1.328**, whose extreme is a buried
deposit at **xi 1.0000, T 2.267, H 2.517, 73.9 km deep in a field of 0.288** — 1.6127 of H past
the threshold and outside the engine's own −0.5..1.5 wall. The hottest parcel in that world is a
deposit at 8.4 km, T 2.268, in a field of 0.006, while the hottest non-deposit material below
2400 km reached T 1.170. Behind the term stands 6.814e4 km²·Myr of certified, buried,
threshold-molten mass-time that the four-term gate would have converted to mobile melt.

That is **0.3.2** (`0.3.2-draft.md`, and `roadmap.md`'s deferred section): a mass-share floor or
gain cap on the gather, a phase-scoped relaxation toward the field, or a minimum parcel mass below
which a deposit is merged rather than carried — each measured against the intake gates, not against
whether it reopens this route. Reopening the 300 Myr intake is not a gate adjustment and no
constant moves to get it back: `0.3.1-plan.md` §5 says so, and the held-back population above is
the number a remedy has to move.
