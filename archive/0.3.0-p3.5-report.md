# 0.3.0 P3.5 — integrated acceptance and the tuning freeze

P3 closes. The conserved melt-to-plate path is now accepted end to end by one harness,
`experiments/pt-p3-check.js`, which decides the six integrated claims of
`0.3.0-p3-plan.md` §4 P3.5 on the real G1-G8 pipeline and freezes the reserve. Nothing in the
engine changed except two frozen constants; the five findings of the review are recorded in
`archive/0.3.0-p3.5-worklog.md`.

Primary gate: `experiments/pt-p3-check.js`, **ALL PASS (42 checks)** — 34 of its own plus the
eight owner harnesses it delegates to, each quoted by its own PASS count. Full serial release
validation: **15 processes, 596 checks** (the 14 milestone harnesses at 562, plus the acceptance
pass's own 34). One row is a second, single-harness run of the same tree: `pt-p3-budget.js` gained
§G (the live census at every supported rung, `0.3.0-plan.md` §7) after the sweep, so it was re-run
on its own; the other thirteen logs are the sweep's. Logs are
`experiments/logs/0.3.0-p3.5-*.txt`; the pre-change verification of the P3.4 claims is
`experiments/logs/0.3.0-p3.4-review/`.

- 39 chambers of 64, 1,852 conduit drains and 1,852 airborne batches;
- 1,737 landings, 1,730 compatible deposit merges, 1,446 intrusions, **0 refusals**;
- peak 24,950 of 28,263 parcels; deposits never exceed 7 parcels;

- temperature `−0.004..1.135`, no NaN over 1,000 frames.

The slider matrix (7 cells, 15 Myr each; the table is in the harness output) closes both books in
every cell and never refuses. A heavier clock buys fluid substeps with frame time, not a different
trajectory: 14.0–14.2 ms/frame at 10–50 kyr/frame, 26.4 at 100, 129.7 at 500 — the 20 ms host gate
is stated at the demonstration clock.

## Interfaces

New harness: `experiments/pt-p3-check.js` (`--fast` skips the eight delegated owners). Its
section 7 is the plan's item 6 — the owners run as children and their counts are quoted, never
re-measured.

Changed: `js/pt/params.js` (`resMelt` 0.05 → 0.08, `resDep` 0.12 → 0.09, `flightVyBlast` 30 → 27);
`experiments/pt-p3-budget.js` now reads the shipped reserve from `params.js` instead of carrying
a copy, gates the reserve's price as an **interleaved empty/filled pair**: `1.19x`, where the old
two-minute-apart sweep ratio wandered 1.33–1.56x on the same tree, and runs every supported rung
live for 20 Myr with the model's own populations (§G) to decide the plan's §7 rung obligation
(`pt-p3-pool.js` and `pt-p3-routing.js` likewise dropped their copies of the reserve).

No new state, no new page code, no change to the physics kernels.
