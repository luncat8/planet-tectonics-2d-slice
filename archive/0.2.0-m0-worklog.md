## What landed

- `js/erupt.js` (`COLERUPT`, DOM-free): `step(dtSec, v)` cuts the frame into toy ticks
  (`toyTickSec` 10 s, at most `toyMaxTicks` 256 per call). A tick is feed → cool → flight →
  two slump passes.
- Effusive feed (`venGas <= gasBlast`): molten mass into the conduit column; it cools
  (`tauCool` 1800 s) and freezes column-wide below `Tsol`.
- Explosive feed (`venGas > gasBlast`): `toyPackets` ballistic packets per tick. Each packet
  is analytic (launch x, height, velocity, age), lands on the column under its arc (clamped
  to the box edge) as cold tephra. A full packet pool sends the rest to the molten column.
- Slump: each adjacent pair steeper than tan(repose) sheds half its excess downhill. Transfers
  are computed from the pass's start heights and applied together, so a pass is order-free.
- `state.js`: new `venFlux` (the flux the schedule sets, M1 owns it), `venToyIn` (ledger).
  `toyH`, `toyT`, `prL` moved to Float64 so the ledger closes to ~1e-15. New `toyFz`.
- `params.js`: the toy tick, ballistic, cooling and slump constants (the table above, plus
  `toyG` 0.5, `toyVy` 3.5, `toyPackets` 8).
- `checkpoint.js`: VERSION 4 → 5, because the toy arrays are part of the saved state. The
  version check in `experiments/checkpoint.js` now refuses version 4.
- `columns.html`: `js/erupt.js` loaded after `magma.js` (smoke asserts every js file is loaded).

## Decisions and why

- **Lateral ejection `toyVx` = 0.5 cells/s, not 1.** Sweep of the explosive fan at the same
  150 cell²: toyVx 0.2 / 0.3 / 0.4 / 0.5 / 0.7 / 1.0 give height × width of
  10.06×27, 10.05×27, 10.05×27, 10.02×27, 8.15×28, 6.20×33 cells. At 1.0 the fan is out of the
  design's 10–30 band, so 0.5 is the largest value that keeps it inside.
- **Column-level lithology.** The pile has one lithology per toy column (`toyLi`, last
  writer). M2 hands frozen mass to the stack per column, so this is enough for M0; per-layer
  lithology would need a second frozen array and is not needed by the bench.
- **Slumped material arrives molten at the source's temperature, or cold if the source is
  solid.** That makes the frozen part of a column the part that is never removed by slumping
  until the molten part is gone. It is a toy rule, checked for mass, not a flow law.
- **Packets land within a frame.** At the default 1800 s frame the packets (flight ~14 s) are
  airborne across ticks, so they are visible to M2's FX, but a long frame at the top slider
  (14400 s) makes every tick 56 s and most packets land in the tick they were born in. The
  visible ballistic FX is an M2/M5 tuning question, not an M0 one.
- **The packet launch draws from the run's seeded RNG** (`RNG.range`), so a run stays
  bit-reproducible and the checkpoint carries the stream. This couples the toy's draws to the
  column sim's draw order; M1 can give the box its own stream if the coupling matters.
- The 0.2.0 plan's "10–30 px" for a repose cone: a 34° cone is 3 cells wide per cell high, so
  a 30-wide cone is 10 high. The band's two ends meet at that single shape; the bench checks
  both bounds at the repose slope. A steeper style would take a different gate.
