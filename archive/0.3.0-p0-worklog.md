## Numbers the plan quotes

- **Solver**: matches a naive DFT to 2.2e-14, round trip 1.1e-16, per-wavenumber tridiagonal
  matches a dense solve to 7.0e-15 (mode 1) and 3.3e-15 (pinned mode 0). Projection residual
  1.5e-12 at 128x96, 4.3e-13 at 256x96. Solve 0.89 / 3.24 / 3.88 ms at 128 / 256 / 512 x 96;
  with three solid-penalty iterations 2.66 / 9.71 / 11.64 ms.
- **Projection on the engine mesh** (asinh η, 8000 km x 2900 km, 20 km of sky): `D(G p) = L p`
  to 2.4e-16; the buoyancy-driven projection is divergence-free to 7.5e-15 of its source; a
  conduction profile is quiescent to 1.6e-13; hot rises, cold sinks. Buoyancy + projection
  step 1.5 ms at 128x96, 3.0 ms at 256x96 (top row 2.5 km, bottom row 160 km).
- **Convection** (uniform 4:1 box, 128x48, t = 0.5 diffusion times; κ = 1e-6 m²/s, D = 1000 km,
  so 1 nondimensional velocity unit = 3.16e-3 cm/yr):

  | Ra | Nu | max&#124;u&#124; cm/yr | max&#124;v&#124; cm/yr |
  |----|----|--------------|--------------|
  | 1e5 | 125 | 5.6 | 6.7 |
  | 3e5 | 26 | 4.3 | 6.7 |
  | 1e6 | 27 | 11.2 | 19.3 |
  | 3e6 | 35 | 31.4 | 52.9 |

  Ra ≈ 1e6 is the demo regime. With 50 kyr per frame and 6.25 km per pixel, a plume runs at
  1.5 px/frame and crosses the mantle in ~5 s; a plate drifts at 0.24 px/frame (14 px/s).
- **Emergence**: cold old rock's internal strain under a shear flow is 1.0e-6 and the body
  rides the flow's mass-weighted mean exactly (36.21 vs 36.21); hot rock 1.6e-2, young rock
  2.9, both with no cluster. A young seam parts 0.14 cells in 200 steps; after it cools and
  ages the same flow moves it 0.000 cells and the body is one cluster. A hot band splits a
  lid into 12 clusters where a cold band stays whole. Solid pass after caching and single-visit
  bonds: 0.63 µs per particle per step (was 2.2 before).

## Failures found (each is a rule in the plan now)

1. The inverse FFT kernel reused the forward one. Every cosine input passed (8.9e-14); a sine
   input exposed it (2.0e0). Rule: never validate an inverse transform with cosine-only inputs.
2. The Neumann diagonal in the solver had the wrong sign, leaving end rows nearly singular.
3. The Dirichlet (odd-reflection) row in `pt-conv.js` added the eliminated coefficient instead
   of removing it — end rows nearly singular again, NaNs downstream.
4. Wavenumbers were taken per grid index instead of per unit length, making every velocity
   node-invariant and slower than a real convective velocity by ~1/dx².
5. Shape-matching rest offsets measured from particle 0, then from the parent body after a
   split: each put the goal a whole body length away and threw particles 1e5 m in one step.
   Rule: rest offsets belong to the particle's own cluster, and welding freezes the rest shape.
6. Damage accumulating from any strain at all: every loaded bond crept apart and plates
   disintegrated. Rule: bonds are elastic below their own strength.
7. `pt-metric.js` dropped the η face at the bottom boundary, reproducing only the interior of
   the operator (D G = L failed at row 0 only). Rule: a boundary face carries zero flux; it is
   not a missing term.
