## Scale lines

Checkbox (and key `g`). Horizontal: a 1‑2‑5 step at ≥110 px, counted from x = 0 *inside each lap* so the seam ends a lap short and restarts at `0` instead of losing the round numbers.

Vertical is the interesting half. One step can't rule an asinh axis, so it's two stages:

1. a greedy over the round `1..9 × 10^d` ladder outward from 0 m, ≥26 px apart — the log behaviour of the map. Anchored at 0 over the *whole world*, not the window, so the rung set depends on zoom alone and a pan slides the lines instead of reshuffling them;
2. a 1‑2‑5 subdivision of any band still wider than three labels — the linear behaviour inside the ±40 km core. Without it the 10–20 km band is one blank 280 px strip at crust zoom, because a decade ladder has nothing between them.
