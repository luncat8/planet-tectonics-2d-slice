   - `COL.retireStep(st, i, dl, dr, dt)` bounds the per-frame Voronoi release by
     `alpha(dt) = 0.0825·dt / (0.025 + dt)` (`0.055` at `50 kyr/frame`, `0.066` at
     `100 kyr/frame`, linear rate `3.3 /Myr` at small `dt`, `< 0.0825 < 0.10` at every
     slider rate) of each receiving neighbour's width, with a `2 km` floor so retirement
     completes in a finite number of frames and never approaches `CRU.zDyn`'s explicit
     flexure Courant limit (`~1.6 km`).

     `S.layAg[(i + 1)·P.layerCap − 1]` (`S.gather` permutes `layAg` and `Checkpoint`
     already serializes it at `VERSION 9`). Storing `wKeep` in `S.oldW[i]` was caught by
     `experiments/coupling.js` Section L (`1.11e-4` `fel` ledger drift across 400
     post-import frames): a C4 import (`COUP.apply`) writes beds onto all columns
     including ghosts (`colNL = 1`), and `COL.k4` / `COL.drain` need `S.oldW[i]` to remain
     the pre-frame width so `layTh *= oldW / colW` conserves imported bed volume on a
     retiring ghost. Moving `wKeep` to `S.layAg[(i + 1)·P.layerCap − 1]` closed
     `coupling.js`'s 400-frame post-import ledger to `2.50e-14` (`138 / 138 PASS`).
   - `S.widths()` subtracts `rel = wVor − wKeep` from `colW[i]` and adds
     `rel·(dr / span)` to `colW[im]` and `rel·(dl / span)` to `colW[ip]`, keeping
