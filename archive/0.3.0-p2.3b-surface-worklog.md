# 0.3.0 P2.3b — surface shaping worklog

## Review finding

The P2.3 profile treated `hLid - flex(hLid)` as topographic support. That is a high-pass:
small marker-column changes became narrow peaks with negative side lobes. The crust sample also
rounded every marker into one x bin, and there was no erosion or collision uplift. The live
silhouette therefore followed broad convection waves while marker noise added thin teeth.

A full collision solve is not available yet: the solid model has rigid clusters, no inter-plate
contact response, and no buoyant/continental crust species. The follow-up adds a bounded
kinematic shortening proxy rather than claiming to solve continent collision.

## Changes

- Deposit crust-marker mass, strength and damage linearly into adjacent x columns, removing
  nearest-bin aliasing and the one-sided empty-column fallback.
- Replace the high-pass lid term with a small, flexurally filtered lid-load proxy. Filter the
  rift and collision sources as well as the thermal/dynamic sources.
- At each adjacent pair of shallow columns, detect different strong plate clusters moving
  toward each other. Add capped uplift across the boundary; extension, same-cluster motion and
  weak within-plate gradients do not make an orogen.
- Apply conservative nonlinear hillslope diffusion after surface relaxation. Its diffusivity
  increases with slope, so sharp crests transport material into adjacent lows faster than broad
  swells, while periodic flux and the final mean correction conserve the sea-level datum.
- Allocate the shallow plate scratch once with the other state buffers. No frame-loop typed
  arrays or buffers were added.

- independent hand-built target checks cover the thermal, dynamic, lid/rift and collision terms;

  translating single cluster do not uplift;

  column rises by 0.052 km and the profile mean remains within 1e-12 km of zero;
- at 250 Myr the range is -9.03..+3.53 km, hot/cold 5% means are +2.41/-7.67 km, the most
  convergent columns average -8.07 km, the most divergent +2.65 km, and thermal correlation is
  0.95; the live sample has 5 active convergent plate boundaries (max shortening 0.625 /Myr);

  and the surface/render allocation gate observes no typed-array construction over 400 frames.

## Next step

Once P3 supplies compositional crust, replace the kinematic uplift proxy with collision,
underthrusting and isostatic root growth tied to buoyant crust. Keep erosion conservative, but
compare a runoff/stream-power transport law against hillslope diffusion before using it for
sediment deposition.
