M6 §5/§6 leaves condition 5's material branch measured on one world and the selection owing two
statements. M7 runs the traced pairs on the worlds the distribution adds — s3 (the inversion:
eleven transfers against four, the question reversed — what *is* that material?) and s1 (the
152.73 km² host) — and takes the row to the selection. The class reads the same on both worlds,
so the branch holds past seed 5, and the selection is made with the two statements the
measurements cannot make alone. No constant moves; condition 5 and the deposit field filter stay.

## 3. s1: the 152.73 km² host is a 1.39 km² surface deposit that stayed

Id **36321** is admitted at f2844, x11562.5, as **1.389 km² of surface deposit** material (in the
control the same id is a 0.002 km² airborne clast at x5883 that dies in fifty frames). In the
candidate it is buried to 2614 km, held at the routing threshold **1829 of its 1830 routed
frames** by field support, capped 282 times (+13.69 km²·H), and delivered once at f7033 — 57.95
km² shed from a **152.728 km² host**, field-supported, **0.0569 of T above its own solidus** —
settled 3,076 frames from its last relocation (M6 §3). Its widest T − field gap in the candidate
is **0.2499, the bound's own width**. The giant host is one lineage's long residence on the
ordinary route — surface deposit, burial, heating, field-supported routing — not the defect in a
large body.

The s1 outlier is named: id **163877**, a 0.036 km² surface deposit admitted at f8254, buried to
110 km and amplified to **T 2.3128 in a field of 0.3264** — a gap of **1.9945 at f9654**, which is
exactly the world's own worst held gap — and it delivers at f11500 anyway (18.82 km² host, 0.1726
of T above its solidus). Traced in the candidate, that id is fifty-one frames of airborne material
(f8162–f8212) that never routes. The name does not travel — M5 §2/§4's sentence, world-robust —
and the outlier's *class* (a light surface deposit amplified against its field) is the same on
both worlds.

The crossover both ways, on s1: of the candidate's twelve delivered ids, one (25031, init
material) delivers in the control too, six are init deposits that never route there, and five are
short-lived airborne clasts (36321, 161463, 181104, 189011, 194787). Of the control's fourteen
delivered ids, in the candidate one (25031) delivers, nine never route (25118 capped 1,867
times), and four are airborne clasts — including the outlier's own id. Even for the init ids,
whose admission the two runs share exactly (the initial world is common ground), the deliveries
split: 25024 delivers five times in the control and never in the candidate; 25151 never in the
control and five times in the candidate. An id match is available only up to the divergence, and
the divergence is what the bound is.

## 4. The class past seed 5, and the trace's own margin

Every one of the candidate's 36 transfers on the two worlds — surface deposit admission, burial,
heating, held at the threshold by field support, delivered field-supported near its own solidus —
reads M5's protected class. The defect is absent rather than moved on both: deposit phase
1.1138 / 1.1150 against the controls' 1.6072 / 2.3128 (both controls cross the engine's wall at
both ends, both candidates not once), worst held gap the bound's own 0.2504 / 0.2513 against
1.1724 / 1.9945, zero refusals, closed books. **Condition 5's material branch holds beyond seed
5** — on three worlds now (s5, s3, s1), including the two the flux objection named.

One margin belongs to the instrument, quantified at last (M5 §3's caveat): the traced lineages'
own T − field maxima sit at the bound's width (0.2269–0.2519) on sixteen of the twenty-one
delivered lineages. The five wider readings (0.3063–0.9825 on 98814, 148272, 168528, 187345,
161463) are all surface-layer frames — y ≤ 4.4 km or the admission frame itself, against fields
near zero — where landing, intrusion and burial set the parcel's state between gathers. The very
next gather caps it back: 148272's f8751 spike (gap 0.9825 at y 0.6 km) is followed at f8752 by a
cap whose delivery would reach T 0.9625 and is held at 0.2510, withholding 0.5661 km²·H. The
bound limits what a delivery may do; the family's own metrics — the held census and the delivered
events — stay at the bound's width on both worlds.

## 5. The selection

**`frb 0.25 --scope dep` is selected** — the family's remedy for the release's defect, and the
only row eligible to land. M4's gate list, read together: the two-sided bound holds at its own
width on every world; the held-back concentration's hot part is gone (2–98× down, worst gap at
the bound on both candidate worlds); the books, the latent identity and the wall close on every
frame; zero refusals; no degenerate route (zero bleeding hosts in all four cells); condition 5
satisfied by the correspondence argument on three worlds (M5 §3/§6, M7 §2–§4); timing, under
statement 2 below. The decision owes the two statements the measurements cannot make alone.

**Statement 1 — the temperature gate is read scoped to the bound's own phase.** Stated once,
applied to every row. Under that reading the candidate is green on every world (deposit phase
zero samples outside, max T 1.1136–1.1150) and every control is red at both ends (489–3374
samples) — the rows separate exactly along the defect the release is about. Under the joint
reading — the gate as literally written, −0.5..1.5 on every phase and frame — **seven of eight
cells are red and both rows are red on three of four worlds** (s1, s3, s7); the only green cell
in the whole distribution is the quoted world's candidate. The joint reds are two faces the
deposit scope does not touch by design: the **mantle cold side** (273–3268 samples at T to
−3.747 in the s1 control, in both rows on s1/s3/s7) and the **mobile-melt hot side** (38–488
samples at T to 1.652 in the controls, 1.595 in the candidates). One correction to M6 §2, which
named the hot face "air" and called it the flight face: the split counters say phase 1 — mobile
melt, M3's own mobile-melt face — and the air phase never crosses on any world (max T
1.1149–1.1150). Both faces cross in every row, so a joint gate cannot be passed by any row of
this family and cannot discriminate between the rows; they are the engine's other defects. The
selection therefore commits to the scoped reading for eligibility, records the joint reading's
sizes once here, and hands the two faces to the plan that owns them — the cold face's own account
stays outside this family (M6 §6), owed to the plan that measures it.

**Statement 2 — the frame time, quoted from a quiet host.** The gate is mean kernel frame
< 20 ms at 50 kyr, serial, observation excluded. The quiet-host serial readings agree: the M5
host's banks read **11.696 ms** (control) and **15.768 ms** (candidate, traced, gate green), and
the M7 cells — run serially, alone, on this host — read 12.037 / 12.706 (controls) and 16.224 /
16.915 (candidates), 3–9% above the M5 host on both rows. The gate is green on both quiet
readings; the probe's own overhead is 4.07–4.21 ms over the matched control, stable across
worlds and hosts. The M6 distribution's *batch host* — the "~30–35% above the M5 host" reading —
is a third host: it read the same rows 14.90–15.57 and 21.35–21.80, 27–38% above the M5 host's
and over the gate for the candidate. That shift is multiplicative on both rows, so the crossing
there is the host's as much as the row's (M6 §4); timing stays host-specific, and the selection
quotes the row's frame time from the quiet readings: **15.768–16.915 ms, green**.

**What the selection does not do.** No constant moves. Condition 5 stays as the filter it is —
neither loosened nor made redundant. The deposit field filter stays. Landing the probe in the
kernel is not this milestone: its constant and scope need their own measured gate and freeze
line, and the plan's re-bank rule names the set.

- **Condition 5's material branch holds beyond seed 5.** The class reads the same on s3 and s1:

  time quoted from quiet hosts (15.768 ms on the M5 host, 16.224–16.915 on this one) — green.
- **The hot face is mobile melt, not air** (M6 §2's label): it and the mantle cold face cross in
  every row on three of four worlds and belong to the engine's other accounts.
