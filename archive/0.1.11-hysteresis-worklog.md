# 0.1.11 evaluation: closing-kind hysteresis on the K5 floor

## Scope

`archive/0.1.10-narrow-hold-worklog.md` closed the narrow hold and left one open K5 option:
hysteresis on the closing kind (`roadmap.md` §3). The plan is `0.1.11-plan.md`. This job
measures that option on the 0.1.10 tree, over sixteen seeds at 50 and 100 kyr/frame (5000
frames each), with the 0.1.10 instruments (`contact-audit.js --strict`, `k5-reclass-probe.js`).

## Decision

**Not landed. `js/` is unchanged.** The best candidate (V1) removes every velocity-caused
reclassification and ties the base on strict verdicts, but it causes a 126 km crust breach on
one run, a ceiling failure no base run shows in 32 runs. Its bounded form (V1e) does not remove
the velocity flips. The drain-band forms fail gates the base passes. Whether to land V1 anyway
is the user's call (§6).

## Method

- Scratch tree from `git archive HEAD`, with the variants behind `HYS_MODE` and `HYS_BAND`
  (`experiments/logs/0.1.11-hysteresis-variants.patch`). Mode 0 is the base.
- The scratch base reproduces the 32 committed 0.1.10 base logs for seeds 1–8: audits match
  apart from the `ms/frame` timing lines, probes are byte-identical. The 0.1.10 table
  (`k5-hold-table.js`) still prints byte for byte after its parsing moved to `k5-table-lib.js`.
- Logs: `experiments/logs/0.1.11-{base,v1,v1e,v2a,v2b}-{contact-audit,k5-reclass-probe}-<seed>-<kyr>.txt`.
  Table: `node experiments/k5-variant-table.js 1-16` (seeds 1–8 only for the two band
  variants, which were measured before the seed extension).

The variants:

| tag | rule (in `COL.isClosingCC`, transport floor and K4 freeze) |
|---|---|
| base | raw sign converges (`colU[i] > colU[j]`) and both felsic `>= P.hOceanic` |
| v1 | as base, **or** the pair's K3 state is `collide` for the same plate pair (the plan's §3, unbounded hold); felsic gate unchanged |
| v1e | v1, but the hold also needs `relN < P.epsHi` (opening beyond the classifier's open threshold is not held) |
| v2a, v2b | v1, and a held pair's felsic gate drops to `P.hOceanic - 1 km` (v2a) or `- 2 km` (v2b) |

v1e was added after v1's result, as a revision of v1 to test whether the breach in §3 could be
removed; it is judged on its own numbers, not as a pre-registered variant.

## Results

Sixteen runs per row (seeds 1–16 × 50 and 100 kyr). Source: `experiments/logs/0.1.11-*` via
`node experiments/k5-variant-table.js 1-16`. The v2 rows read seeds 1–8 only.

| variant | runs | strict pass | gate failures | R5 repeats | R3 max | drain pairs | velocity pairs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| base | 32 | 17 / 32 | 19 | 25 | 81.7 km | 1917 | 872 |
| v1 | 32 | 17 / 32 | 21 | 19 | **126.1 km** | 1915 | **0** |
| v1e | 32 | 17 / 32 | 21 | 23 | 81.7 km | 1967 | 478 |
| v2a | 16 | 10 / 16 | 9 | 8 | 91.6 km | 891 | 1 |
| v2b | 16 | 10 / 16 | 10 | 6 | 87.1 km | 486 | 1 |

Base, seeds 1–8 only, for the v2 rows: 11/16 pass, drain 1006, velocity 272 (the 0.1.10 numbers).

Runs a variant fails that the base passes:

- **v1:** seed 6, 100 kyr: `R2 the pair is not a local needle` (worst pair 2.23 against 1.5) and
  one R5 repeat.
- **v1e:** seed 6, 100 kyr: the same R2 needle failure.
- **v2a:** seed 4, 50 kyr (R1 and R2 contiguous-run); seed 6, 100 kyr (R5 repeat).
- **v2b:** seed 4, 50 kyr (R2 contiguous-run); seed 6, 100 kyr (R5 repeat).

Other checks on v1 (the candidate):

- **Suite.** The clean v1 tree (`HEAD` with the rule written as in §3, no environment switch)
  passes all thirteen harnesses: `core-log` 33, `coupling` 124, `section-pack` 84,
  `coupling-link` 11, `smoke` 75, `checkpoint` 52 (the spin-up bound holds), `section-seed` 36,
  `m2-check` 324, `view-check` 119, `ledger-check` 19, `m3-check` 22, `deposits` 76,
  `slice-cut` 63. Its seed 1, 100 kyr audit reproduces `experiments/logs/0.1.11-v1-contact-audit-1-100.txt`.
- **The R3 breach** (seed 12, 100 kyr, frame 4263; §7 has the chain): a column that is 0.32 w0
  wide and 60 km thick is raised to 126 km. It is the only R3 value above 82 km in the 32 base
  runs and the 32 v1 runs of seeds 1–16 (the base's highest is 81.7 km).
- **Held-opening pairs** (`experiments/k5-held-trace.js`, `experiments/logs/0.1.11-held-trace-12-100.txt`):
  in v1, the transport floor keeps 715 distinct pairs in the crush floor while they open past
  `P.epsHi`. Eleven of them lie within 120 km and 25 frames of the breach column. They open at
  73 mm/yr falling to 17 mm/yr, with 44–53 km of felsic on each side. The base run logs zero.
  These are co-located with the breach, not shown to cause it (§7).
- **Why v1e does not help.** Bounding the hold at `P.epsHi` removes the breach, but the
  velocity-caused flips it was meant to remove are the same opening pairs, so it leaves 478 of
  them (201 on seeds 1–8, 277 on 9–16). It fails the same R2 run as v1.
- **Drain.** The drain-caused counts do not fall under any variant that keeps the felsic gate
  (v1: 1915 against 1917). Drain bands cut them (v2b: 486) and cost gates (§4 of the plan).

What these results say about the mechanism question:

- In the variants tested, velocity-caused reclassification and the crush hold on opening pairs
  are not separable. The only rule that removed every velocity flip (v1) also held opening
  pairs, and the breach traces to one of them. A bound that stops the hold on opening pairs
  (v1e) leaves the flips.
- R5 repeats fall under v1 (25 to 19 over sixteen runs). That is a real effect on the open R5
  gate. Under v1 the base's R5 failure on seed 1 at 100 kyr clears, while seeds 2, 4, 5 and 7
  at 100 kyr keep one repeat each (the same count as the base).

## 6. Decision for the user

Land v1 as in `0.1.11-plan.md` §3, or keep the base. Both pass the suite and the strict
verdicts tie. v1 buys zero velocity-caused reclassification and 6 fewer R5 repeats, and costs
one 126 km ceiling breach on seed 12 at 100 kyr and one new R2 needle on seed 6 at 100 kyr. I
do not recommend landing it as it stands: a ceiling breach of that size is a physical
regression with a traced mechanism, and the bounded form (v1e) loses the benefit. A
hysteresis that removes the flips without the breach needs a different hold criterion (the
classifier's state alone is not enough in the variants tested), which is a new plan, not a
tuning of this one.

`0.1.11-plan.md` §5 is the acceptance written before the measurement. Its gate 2 reads as a tie
for v1 (17 against 17). The decision above rests on the R3 breach, which §5 does not name, so the
plan now lists it as a fifth gate, added after the measurement.

## 7. The breach, traced (bounded; the cause is not isolated)

`experiments/k5-breach-trace.js` (log `experiments/logs/0.1.11-breach-trace-12-100.txt`) reads
seed 12 at 100 kyr/frame, with the variant applied:

1. The global maximum thickness goes from 67.8 km to 126.1 km at frame 4263. No topology
   operation (consume, accrete, drain, rift) runs in that step.
2. In that step two neighbouring records swap order. The column at x 1175.6 km, which had been
   a 2.70 w0 record of 60 km with 53 km of felsic, takes the place of a 0.36 w0 record of 65 km
   with 8.5 km of felsic. Its width becomes 0.32 w0 and its felsic 121 km.
3. K4 conserves volume over the new width, so the stack is multiplied by `oldW / colW` = 2.70 /
   0.32 ≈ 8.4. That is 60 km to about 506 km, which the audit reports as `k4 512.37`. K5's
   `collapse` (384 km) and `delaminate` (135 km) then bring it to 126 km. The audit's stage
   labels are read this way: `collapse` and `delaminate` are K5 stages.
4. The pair was at a 6.4 km gap for two frames before the swap. Its relative speed was closing
   at 1–29 mm/yr, which is the crush floor's legitimate case in both trees. In the swap frame it
   reverses to +160 mm/yr.

5. **Which of the two holds matters** (`experiments/logs/0.1.11-breach-diagnostic-12-100.txt`,
   one seed). The hold applied only in the K4 freeze gives no rise over 25 km from frame 4163
   to 4264. The hold applied only on the transport floor reproduces the breach at frame 4263
   exactly (67.8 to 126.1 km). So on this seed the breach needs the transport-floor hold, the
   one that the velocity-flip count depends on.

So the breach is a record swap in transport, followed by a volume-conserving rescale that
multiplies a stack by the width ratio, and on seed 12 the swap needs the crush floor on the
transport. The trajectories of the two diagnostic modes differ from the base in earlier frames
too, so the one-seed result is a mechanism claim, not a rate.

## Reproduce

```
# from the repository root: the tables read the committed logs
node experiments/k5-variant-table.js 1-16

# the variants: a scratch copy of HEAD with the patch (not applied on this branch)
mkdir -p /tmp/hys && git archive HEAD | tar -x -C /tmp/hys
patch -p1 -d /tmp/hys < experiments/logs/0.1.11-hysteresis-variants.patch
cd /tmp/hys && HYS_MODE=1 node experiments/contact-audit.js 5000 12 100 --strict --detail
HYS_MODE=1 node experiments/k5-held-trace.js 12 100e3 4264 1175.6 4263
HYS_MODE=1 node experiments/k5-breach-trace.js 12 100e3 4264 1150 1200 4263   # §7 chain
HYS_MODE=4 node experiments/k5-breach-trace.js 12 100e3 4264 1150 1200 4263   # hold in K4 freeze only
HYS_MODE=5 node experiments/k5-breach-trace.js 12 100e3 4264 1150 1200 4263   # hold on transport only
```

Modes 4 and 5 are the one-site diagnostics of §7; they are logged in
`experiments/logs/0.1.11-breach-diagnostic-12-100.txt`.

The patch is a `diff -u` of `js/columns.js` against `HEAD`, with the variant switches read from
`process.env`. It cannot run in the browser-style page harnesses (`smoke.js` loads `columns.js`
into a VM with no `process`), which is why v1's suite was run on the clean rule instead.

## Next step

The user asked for the breach to be investigated before the choice, and §7 is that bounded
investigation. It leaves one trade-off, and it is a structural one. The velocity flips are
counted on the transport floor. The only variant that removed them (v1) holds the crush floor on
that floor. The diagnostic in §7 puts the breach on that same floor, and the hold on the K4
freeze alone does not breach on seed 12. So a rule that removes the velocity flips and keeps the
transport floor ordinary does not exist in this family of variants: the variant that keeps the
freeze hold has the flips too. Two options are left: land v1 with the breach on record, or keep
the base. If the base is kept, no engine change follows and the K5 reclassification item closes
as 0.1.10 closed the narrow hold. Whether the swap needs a 6.4 km gap or only a 16 km relative
advance in one frame is not tested, and is the first question of any new plan.
