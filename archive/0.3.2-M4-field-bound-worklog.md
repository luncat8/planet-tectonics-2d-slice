## Revisions (the harness was edited while the cells ran; this is the order)

1. `frb` added as a mode of the existing spread/stencil generator: `hi`/`lo` constants become
   `hiOff`/`loOff` plus a `fr` flag, the bound, the headroom and the giveback reading one
   per-frame snapshot `tfP[p] = sample(M, Tg, x, e)` taken in the header pass that already builds
   the node buckets.
2. the two instruments (outside split; `gainAt` on the cold path and the excursions).
3. `--share s0`.
4. `--place mass`, which also un-guarded the share pass (the placement weighting needs the share
   whether or not the bound scales by it).
5. the placement accounting put back: edit 4 had replaced `rmd = amt - room` with
   `rmd = amt - placed`, which is the right question for clipped marker-share weights and a
   round-off-level change for the headroom path — two 600 Myr rows had been measured before that
   edit. The headroom path now keeps its own accounting exactly, and the marker-share path counts
   what it actually placed.

## One bug worth recording

`--share` was first declared at the melt-probe block, i.e. **after** the `new Function` that
closes over it, so the probe captured `undefined` and `shr > 0` was never true. Two 600 Myr cells
ran that way and produced two banks of a world the flat row already held (fingerprint
3436672068889011 three times over). The tell was that `--share 1e6` — a bound of essentially zero
for every parcel — changed nothing at all. Fixed by moving the flag above the probe; `--share 1e6`
then caps 11.0M parcel-frames with 1.94M infeasible placements, as it must. The two duplicate
banks were deleted (one bank per world: a world is its fingerprint).
