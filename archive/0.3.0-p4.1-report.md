# 0.3.0 P4.1 — slab sinking and deep recycling

P4.0's contact pass owns the lid band above `P.yCrust`, and its bending band is what puts
material below that line. Below it nothing held a slab: the band stopped moving, the subducted
material stopped being bookable, and the plan's §1.2 line — deep recycling is a transfer into a
persistent deep inventory — stayed unwritten. P4.1 is one new file, `js/pt/slab.js`, at the tail
of G4, plus `recycle()` in `js/pt/pool.js` and the deep records in `js/pt/state.js`.

Primary gate: `experiments/pt-p4-slab.js`, **ALL PASS (27 checks)** — the drive and its three
exclusions read off one state by hand, the transfer's debit/credit arithmetic across a single
call of the pass, the refusal path, a real arrival, the hand-over from P4.0 at the live scale,
and three live legs. Full serial release validation with the pass shipped on: **17 processes,
666 quoted checks (658 distinct), all green** — the suite's census is P4.0's exactly and its
`intake/wall` ledger gate
reads the same 1.024 on a planet that now moves 86,272.6 km² out of the pool — logs
`experiments/logs/0.3.0-p4.1-*.txt`.

## The population is the design

The pass drives `min(kSink · (Trow[j] − T[p]), vSinkMax)` for material that is **below
`P.yCrust`**, **colder than its own mesh row by `slabdT`**, **no warmer than `slabT` in absolute
terms**, and **not riding a plate** (`csCnt[cl] ≥ plateMin`). The first two tests are the plan's;
the third was measured into existence and the fourth is what keeps the pass from pulling a
plate's own root down from under it.

Two implementations were rejected on measurement. Transferring *any* parcel below `yDeep` — a
boundary as a place rather than a point on a slab's trajectory — ate **11.3e6 km², a quarter of
the world's stock**, in 300 Myr, because what is below `yDeep` on a live planet is the hot deep
mantle. And the contrast test alone selects the flow's whole cold half:

| population | driven-parcel-frames | mean descent | transfers | records | pool after 300 Myr |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| contrast only | 10,866,203 | 15.57 km/Myr | 8,159 | 4,035,256 km² (**9.68%** of the world) | 23,552 → **17,367** |
| **contrast and `T ≤ slabT` (shipped)** | **1,060,438** | **29.98 km/Myr** | **439** | **86,273 km² (0.21%)** | 23,552 → **25,017** |
| contrast and `T ≤ 0.35` | 405,929 | 30.00 km/Myr | 48 | 7,978 km² (0.02%) | 23,552 → 25,435 |

The first row is a drain in the floor: the ambient cold boundary circulation crosses the boundary
too, and a transfer is one-way, so the world loses a quarter of its mass and its resolution with
it. `slabT` is **`TSoft` (0.45)** — the engine's own line above which `strength()` stops letting
rock age, i.e. where rock stops being cold lithosphere — and with it the pass drives 0.71% of the
pool per frame at a slab's own speed, books 439 transfers, parks 0.21% of the world's mass, and
the pool grows. The engine's lithosphere memory `S.age` was also tried as the discriminator and
does not work: `strength()` resets it above `yWeldMax`, so at depth every parcel reads 0.

## Interfaces

New file `js/pt/slab.js` — one global, `PTSLAB`, exporting `run` (G4's tail, no-ops at `dt = 0`
or `P.slab` off), `background` (fills `S.Trow`, rows 1..`ny` because `Math.round(ny − 0.5) ===
ny` puts a marker at `eMax` in the bottom row) and `sink` (the drive and the transfer, one walk,
re-visiting a slot after a removal because `pool.js remove()` compacts the tail into it).

New harness `experiments/pt-p4-slab.js` (27 checks; `[liveMyr]` scales the three live legs,
default 300 — below ~100 Myr the lid has not fragmented and below ~170 Myr nothing arrives, so a
short argument goes red on the checks that ask for one). New library
`experiments/pt-p4-lib.js`: the two-plate geometry both P4 owner harnesses drive, which prints
nothing and gates nothing.

Changed: `js/pt/pool.js` (`recycle()`, and both inventories count the deep records once);
`js/pt/state.js` (the deep records, `Trow`, `invDpM`/`invDpL`, `tx.recycle`,
`thr.deepDr`/`deepCr`, `d.slab`/`slabV`/`slabBond`/`deep`, `reset()`, `hash()`);
`js/pt/params.js` (`plateMin 64`, named for the literal `solid.js` already used, and the P4.1
block: `slab`, `slabdT 0.05`, `slabT 0.45`, `kSink 60`, `vSinkMax 30`, `yDeep 2400`,
`deepJoin 500`, `deepCap 32`); `js/pt/solid.js` (reads `P.plateMin`); `js/pt/sim.js` (requires
`slab.js`, G4's tail); `js/pt/ui.js` (the HUD's slab line); `index.html` and `particles.html`
(one script tag each, still byte-identical, which `pt-ui.js` gates with 51 checks).

Every P4.1 constant is a P4.4 freeze candidate measured by the new harness, not a claim.

## Next

P4.2 per `0.3.0-p4-plan.md` §3: arc melt from the slab. The record a transfer leaves carries
water — `dpW`, 458.2481 km² of `cW` in the single-call test — and no melt, which is exactly the
input an arc needs: dehydration melting is a water-fluxed solidus depression and `meltWW = 20`
per unit `cW` is already the engine's own depression law. The measurement to reuse is the record
set's positions (`dpX`/`dpY`, the mass-weighted mean of what each record has eaten), because an
arc is a place: it sits above a slab, not inside one. The carry-over is unchanged: composition
(`cF`/`cW`) still has no source of its own, so the explosive half and `mergeCompF/W` remain P3
carry-overs waiting on the exchange work.
