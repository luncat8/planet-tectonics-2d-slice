# 0.3.0-P1.1 — stable clock and pressure-release melt indicator

Scope: review the first fluid-world commit after the observed jump near 150 Myr and the
request for visually meaningful hot/melt upwellings.

## Findings

The apparent fixed-time event was two numerical issues rather than a geological event.

1. `scatterT` defines a hole by **deposited quadratic-kernel weight**, but `reseed` repaired
   every empty nearest-node bucket. A marker cloud can leave a nearest bucket empty while its
   3x3 deposit still samples that node well. At 135.25 Myr one true transfer hole caused 76
   markers to move; at 171.4 Myr the old `n > nx` rule globally re-dealt the lattice. That
   causes the map-wide discontinuity and later noisy texture.
2. The 500 kyr/f slider sent a whole 0.5 Myr through the marker transport in one update.
   The validated fluid step is 50 kyr, so the large clock skipped across the narrow upper
   rows and could drive the thermal field into noise.

The cold fast downwellings themselves are not a sign-reversed mantle law: a one-phase,
constant-viscosity Rayleigh-Benard mantle correctly has dense cool material descending and
broader hot return flow. What it did *not* show was the separate low-density melt produced
by decompression in hot upwellings. Treating its bright paths as the same scalar as mantle
temperature made the intended distinction invisible.

## Changes

- Added `M.cover`, the one shared deposited-weight threshold. Repair now targets exactly the
  holes `scatterT` reported, while the deposit still describes the same marker positions.
  It runs during G1 before advection, and the full lattice re-deal is only a true local-search
  failure, never a count-of-moves threshold. Reset now also clears its repair diagnostics.
- Added an internal maximum fluid step of 50 kyr. A displayed 500 kyr/f frame runs ten G1–G7
  substeps and accrues wall flux per substep; the HUD reports `fluid Nx` so the work is clear.
- Added a non-conservative, no-feedback `melt*` diagnostic field. It is generated only where
  mantle is both hotter than its row mean and moving upward in the 80–550 km pressure-release
  window, then rises at a separate buoyant segregation speed and fades on extraction/cooling.
  A gold raster overlay makes those shallow hot pathways legible. It is explicitly labelled
  an indicator: P3 still owns conserved melt particles, conduits, vents, and eruptions.

## Validation

- `node experiments/pt-check.js` — **19/19 PASS** after the changes; at Ra 1e6 the 1200-frame
  ledger ratio is 0.995 and the frame is about 8 ms on the reference box.
- `node experiments/pt-stability.js` — **12/12 PASS**. Through 180 Myr the 50 kyr/f and
  500 kyr/f clocks finish with bit-identical marker hash, heat ledger, wall ledger, Nu and
  peak vertical speed. Both see at most one real transfer hole, move at most one marker, and
  never re-deal the lattice.
- `node experiments/pt-melt-check.js` — **6/6 PASS**. Zero Stokes flow produces zero indicator;
  the cool run reaches melt* 0.151 at 102 km by 160 Myr; enabling the diagnostic leaves marker
  motion and the heat ledger bit-identical to the disabled run.
- `node experiments/pt-eng-conv.js`, `node experiments/pt-metric.js`, and
  `node experiments/pt-bench.js` remain green.

## Next step

P2 should replace the provisional free cold skin with an actual bonded lithosphere, so only
plate-boundary failure produces slab-like descent. P3 should promote `melt*` to conserved melt
particles, include pressure/density feedback and conduit failure, and retire the diagnostic
label once vents and surface accumulation are physical.
