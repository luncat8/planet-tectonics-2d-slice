# 0.1.8 — diagnosis of the residual R2/R3/R5 failures

## Scope

Name the mechanism behind every strict-gate failure left open by the 0.1.8 floor-classification
follow-up, on unmodified engine source, and correct their measurement if the measurement — not
the kernel — is what the gate is reading. One mechanism turned out to be a genuine kernel
defect and is fixed here; the rest keep their gates red and are recorded. No threshold, no
`COL.beltAt`, no drawing, no velocity clamp. M2 brake selection resumes after this, on the M1
conveyor.

Changes of the release so far (all outside `js/` except the last):

- `experiments/lib.js` — `r2Ground()` and the walked shoulder in `check.r2ShapeAt()`.
- `experiments/m2-check.js` — two synthetic fixtures pinning the walked shoulder.
- `experiments/contact-audit.js` — the report-only `--detail` instrumentation.
- `0.1.8-plan.md` §1/§2 — the R2 shoulder, R2 onset instance, R3 circular-gap and R5 bucket
  wording.
- `js/columns.js` — `COL.floor` reads the separation gap the short way round the circle
  (6 added lines). This is the only engine edit.

## Method

`node experiments/contact-audit.js <frames> <seed> <kyr> --strict --detail`. The gates are
unchanged; `--detail` wraps `COL.k4` and the five `CRU` stages (`zDyn`, `collapse`,
`arcGrowth`, `lipGrowth`, `delaminate`) and snapshots `hTot` per stage, classifies every R2
sample raw against walked, traces each R3 ceiling breach back through the stage chain to the
pre-K4 column, and lists each R5 repeat as the closest pair of event x-positions inside its
`COL.beltAt` bucket. The run exits on the same gate set as `--strict` alone.

Logs (committed, regenerated under the fix): `experiments/logs/0.1.8-diagnosis-{1,5}-{50,100}.txt`.

## R3 — one crossed pair, one frame, one spike (fixed)

3000 frames, seed 1, 100 kyr/frame, frame 2568, `x ≈ 26051 km`. Two records of different
plates cross the trench inside one frame:

```text
pre-frame   c340  x26042.621 km  w27.87 km  h80.82 km  V2.252e9 m²  plate 4
            c341  x26048.775 km  w42.17 km  h80.00 km  V3.374e9 m²  plate 5
post-K4     one record at x26051.13 with h136.07 km, V3.374e9 (unchanged)
            the other at x26051.35 with h 57.62 km, V2.252e9 (unchanged)
K5          136.07 -> zDyn 136.07 -> collapse 118.38 -> delaminate 86.98 -> 86.78 km
gate        86.78 km against crustMax 80 + 35 km/Myr x 0.1 Myr = 83.5 km
```

The probe that reads the floor's own scan (`scratch/probe-r3f.js`) gives the whole defect in
one line: the pair's raw gap is **−0.225 km** (they crossed), the old code added `P.wrap` to
the negative gap, and the floor therefore saw **40029.9 km** — no breach of the 3.91 km floor,
no action. `COL.k4` then returned false (no intent), so `COL.finalFloor` did not run either;
`COL.transport`'s sort re-handed the territory and the no-topology path conserved the volume
across the width change (`80.00 × 42.17 / 24.79 = 136.07 km`). The volume was never created:
the record's own V is bit-identical before and after. The +0.49e9 m² the earlier 15-column
probe read across K4 is a window-boundary net — the crossed pair occupied different slots
before and after the sort — and the global per-lithology ledger closes to 1e-15.

The fix, in `COL.floor`:

```js
d = st.colX[j] - st.colX[i];
if (d < 0) d += P.wrap;
if (d > P.wrap * 0.5) d = P.wrap - d;   // two records are always nearer the short way round
```

A first form that clamped any negative raw gap (`d < 0 -> d += wrap` followed by a symmetric
`±wrap/2` test) was wrong for sparse rings: it left a neighbouring record on the far side of
the ring, the floor never engaged, and the `m2-check` rift fixture ("a diverging uniform pair
spawns exactly one column") stopped spawning. The circular distance is correct in both cases
and the fixture is what caught it. `COL.intents` keeps its own unwrapped `d` read because the
sort has normalised the order by then.

## R2 needle — the walked shoulder, and one surviving onset transient

The walked shoulder is the measurement correction of the previous job: a draining record holds
no ground, so the shoulder is read at the nearest **real** column (bounded to
`P.beltFeed + 1`). It removed every ghost-slot failure (3000/1/100 worst 2.021 → 1.382) and
cannot turn a passing pair red.

The remaining red is one site on seed 5/50, `x 9811.8 km`, audit frames 2576–2578, three
consecutive samples. `scratch/probe-peek.js` measures the transient frame by frame:

```text
sample   pair left (c131)   peak (c132)   higher shoulder (c133)   ratio
2576     h13.2 fel10.2      h36.6 fel33.3   h18.1 fel 1.7           2.02
2577     h14.8 fel11.8      h33.8 fel30.6   h19.3 fel 2.9           1.75
2578     h15.9 fel12.9      h31.7 fel28.5   h20.3 fel 3.9           1.56
2579     h16.7 fel13.7      h30.1 fel26.9   h21.0 fel 4.6           1.43  passes
```

The pair is a welding felsic margin (plate 1, `rel −3.9 → −5.0 mm/yr`, `edgeAge` 0.05 → 0.25
Myr) against a felsic block on plate 2; the higher shoulder is plate 2's own mafic plateau
(`hFel` 1.7 km, below `P.hOceanic`, so `PLT.oceanic` still calls that column continental by
`hTot`). No topology event fires at the site in these frames. `COL.beltAt` already reports
5 columns and 380 km of belt at the same sample, and the contiguous-width clause passes there.

The disposition question — may a non-felsic / opposing-plate column be the shoulder? — is a
definition question, not a threshold. The alternatives were measured at this site and do not
help: a felsic-only shoulder reads 8.5 km (4.3x, worse) and a shoulder restricted to the
peak's own plate leaves 18.1 km unchanged. The gate therefore stays red; the measured
statement is that it is a three-frame onset transient of a collision that has not yet grown
its belt, not a ghost, a draining record, or a standing needle. Wording is recorded in
`0.1.8-plan.md` §2.

## R2 width — narrow belts, clause unchanged

The clause passes on every leg (90% required) because these are samples whose window averages
just above `outerFlank + P.beltRise`: converging belts building a four-column run, not holes
and not needles. The pre-fix classification of the 3000/1/100 failures had found 442 of 664
reaching four columns only by counting one of the pair's own columns below the rise (which
`COL.beltAt` always counts, per its documented walk), 222 genuinely short, 0 window
truncation and 0 ghost-blocked. Nothing in the clause, the window or `COL.beltAt` was changed;
the leg crosses 90% because the floor fix moves the trajectory, the same way the needle leg
crossed once the walked shoulder landed.

## R5 — consume-scar re-rift, all outside the kernel's memory

The repeats are boundary reversals, and `scratch/probe-r5d.js` names both events of each
(`frame kind column x plate`):

```text
seed 1, 100 kyr   f 812 consume c171 x12590.6 km pl1 subduct  -> f 836 rift x12667.1 km (24 f, 76.5 km)
                  f2500 accrete c237 x16491.7 km pl3 collide  -> f2522 rift x16438.7 km (22 f, 53.0 km)
seed 5, 100 kyr   f1417 consume c126 x 9570.9 km pl1 subduct  -> f1453 rift x 9601.1 km (36 f, 30.2 km)
                  f2339 consume c199 x14946.5 km pl2 subduct  -> f2378 rift x14981.7 km (39 f, 35.2 km)
                  f3195 consume c 31 x 2409.4 km pl0 subduct  -> f3230 rift x 2346.1 km (35 f, 63.3 km)
                  f4659 consume c117 x 8725.3 km pl0 subduct  -> f4686 rift x 8728.5 km (27 f,  3.2 km)
```

Five are a `consume` trench sliver re-opened as a `rift` at the same `COL.beltAt` bucket; one
is a C-C conveyor retirement followed by a `rift`. Inside `P.evAge` (2 Myr = 20 frames at
100 kyr/frame, 40 at 50 kyr) there are **zero** repeats on every leg (0 of 383 and 0 of 390
events at 100 kyr; 0 of 177 and 0 of 187 at 50 kyr). Every repeat is 2.2–3.9 Myr of simulated
time apart: the boundary genuinely reversed after the kernel's anti-repeat memory expired, and
the `P.evGap` frame window (40 frames = 4 Myr at 100 kyr) is what re-detects it. Recorded in
`0.1.8-plan.md` §2 as a documented limit of the frame-window gate.
