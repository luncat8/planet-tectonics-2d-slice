## The bugs the fixtures found

Each of these was invisible in a screenshot and obvious in a number.

1. **The conduction operator dropped the bottom wall's Dirichlet term.** The eta tridiagonal
   carried both walls, but the RHS only carried the top one, so the interior drained at 2.3x
   the true wall flux (a phantom cold base). Isolated check: the operator's identity went from
   a ratio of 2.3445 to 1.00000, residual 3.5e-16.
2. **The marker step was doubled in x.** An editing artifact left `S.x[p] += vx*dt` twice in
   `G.gatherVel`, so the map was 1 + dt du/dx instead of area preserving and the cloud folded
   onto one row and one column within 10 Myr. One line; the fixture's Jacobian check would have
   caught it on its own.
3. **The marker mass was the wrong measure.** `state.js` handed out the *cell* metric
   `dEta·jC·dx` while the conduction operator conserves the *node* metric `dEta·jN·dx`: a
   constant 2.62% offset between the marker heat and the field's, which is exactly the scale of
   every phantom "ledger mismatch" chased before it.
4. **The wall flux diagnostic used the node metric** for a one-sided difference taken across a
   *cell*: a 2.6% bias on both boundary fluxes, which is what put a phantom flux under every
   conduction-only reading.
5. **The walls collected markers.** Clamping a marker at `eMin` is a sink: with v ~ 0 there, the
   marker never leaves. A single convection cell swept 60% of the cloud onto the two wall rows
   and the field had no sample left for most nodes; the stale patches then drove a grid-scale
   instability (T to -0.8, Nu to 300). Reflecting the normal step is the fix, and it is measure
   preserving.
6. **The intake bookkeeping needed the adjoint of the deposit, not the deposit's weights.** With
   plain CIC weights the markers' claim on a node's increment is `W_k`, not `mu_k`; a 4% hole in
   occupancy then read as 360x the wall flux, because the increments' gross structure is ~100x
   their net. Handing back `d_pk * mu_k/W_k` makes `sum_p m_p a_pk = mu_k` exact for every node,
   whatever the cloud has done.
7. **The deposit kernel decides whether the physics survives.** CIC (one cell) left half the
   nodes unsampled under a Ra 1e6 flow and needed patching; the 3x3 quadratic B-spline reaches
   every node within 1.5 cells, which is what made the same run stable and let the ledger close
   at 1.010.

## What was tried and rejected (with the number that rejected it)

- **Re-dealing the marker cloud onto the lattice** (the plan's original `reseedEvery`, every 16
  frames): fixed coverage, held a convecting box at **Nu = 1.00**. Every cadence tried (16, 64,
  106 frames) did the same, and so did the local repair when it ran often enough to matter: the
  re-deal is applied to the *temperature field*, and its resampling is diffusion. The repair now
  runs only when the deposit reports holes, and the re-deal only if the repair cannot cover them.
- **A wider velocity kernel** (quadratic B-spline interpolation of psi): did not stop the
  clumping, and its stencil reads past the wall rows so markers were pushed *into* the walls
  (200k clamp events). The bilinear psi map is the one that is right; the deposit kernel is where
  the width belongs.
- **Position-refreshed marker mass** in the gather (`m = local node measure` every frame): worse
  than carrying the parcel's own mass by 45x in the ledger, and it is not the physics either --
  area is material, and P3's eruptions have to conserve it.
- **Synchronising the markers to the grid** (flip = 0): diverges (T to 5e4) because a node with
  a sliver of weight gives `cw = mu/W` of thousands, and the *state* is then read through that
  amplification. `cw` is only trusted above a sixteenth of the node's measure.

## Numbers for the record

- 24,064 markers (128x48 x 4/node, jittered off the lattice with a fixed seed);
  7.3-8.9 ms/frame at the demo mesh; 26 ms at 256x96 with 97k markers.
- Ledger ratios over 1200 frames: Ra 1e5 **1.000**, Ra 1e6 **1.010**; the residue is the hole
  count (0-8% of nodes).
- `pt-conv.js`'s low-Ra end is unphysical (Nu 125 at Ra 1e5 with 9 upwellings); the engine reads
  Nu 1.00 there. The reference's Ra >= 1e6 end agrees to 2.3%.
- The demo's HUD prints the clock in Myr/Gyr, kyr/frame and Myr/s, Nu, max|u| in cm/yr and
  px/frame, the marker count and node occupancy, the ledger next to the operator's wall flux and
  their ratio, the T range, the heat, and sim/draw ms with fps.
