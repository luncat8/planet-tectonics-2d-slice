## The contact census, and the two §4 decisions

The kernel's site walk is reproducible bitwise at frame end from the table's own snapshots
(`detect()` fills `ctV`/`ctT` before the brake, and the walk is `closing > contactYield` over
adjacent columns), so the harness re-derives every answered pair and reads what the kernel
does not record. The cross-check — census vs `S.d.contact` — agreed on all 6,000 frames.

- **No plate-size threshold on the table.** 25,563 of the 25,664 answered pairs (99.6%)
  involve a cluster under `plateMin` 64 — lid shrapnel from the shipped failure law. The pass
  is what answers them; a threshold would trade "fragment-inflated shortening" for fragments
  that interpenetrate, and the cost of answering them is the guard's 0.21 pushes/frame.
  Frozen as a sentence in the P4.0 block of `params.js`.
- **The brake stays translation-only.** The hold's unbalanced impulse is booked signed
  (`S.contactHold`, −1.044e9 over the leg against a plate-momentum scale of ~3e6 per plate);
  the closing translation-only leaves at a site is the guard's work, and its residue is 0.
- The regime rule's measurement: answered pairs span |dT| 0..0.452 with **4,940 arrests and
  20,724 subductions** around `contactdT` 0.08; answered closings run 0.030..1.969 /Myr, an
  order above the plate interiors' 0.003..0.02 (pt-load-check), which is the yield's freeze
  line.

## Finding 1: the deposit book's third debit

The integrated leg failed the deposit identity P4.3 wrote — `invM[dep] = landCr + eroCr −
eroDep` — by **1,242.1995 km² of 353,677 (0.35%)**. A probe wrapping `POOL.recycle`
(`experiments/scratch/p4-dep-drain-probe.js`) attributed the whole gap: **22 deposit parcels,
1,242.1995254883575 km²**, recycled into the deep records by P4.1's slab pass. Subducted
sediment is detached cold lithosphere (`mu = 0` keeps it cluster-free, its quenched
temperature sits inside the slab population), and the plan's settled §4 line is that detached
cold material sinks whatever its phase — but nothing named the phase the transfer debited.

Fix, bookkeeping only: **`thr.deepDep`** — the deposit mass `recycle()` debits — with the
phase read *before* `remove()` (the removal compacts the tail into the slot). The identity
gains its third term, `invM[dep] = landCr + eroCr − eroDep − deepDep`, bitwise on the leg
(352,435.204934 = 277,495.925386 + 99,231.123524 − 23,049.644451 − 1,242.199525). The other
candidate leak was checked and does not exist: `melt.js`'s `detach` re-phases mantle only, so
a wholly molten deposit stays in the phase, and a melt parcel can never enter the slab
population because `T ≥ Ts ≥ 0.82` below `yCrust` outruns `slabT` 0.45. `pt-p3-check.js`'s
copy of the identity updated the same way (its 50 Myr leg has `deepDep = 0`).

**The fingerprint moved**: `thr.deepDep` is a cumulative counter and cumulative counters live
in `hash()`, so fingerprints published before P4.4 stop reproducing from this tree forward.
The same-leg cross-check is recorded in the worklog: before the counter, this exact leg read
**2960640650489454** — the P4.3 report's own live-leg fingerprint, two harnesses one planet.
All in-repo gates are within-run replays and stayed green. `pt-p4-check.js` prints the
post-change fingerprint beside that number.

## Finding 2: the earlier owners' live legs had drifted off their own planets

This milestone's first full run caught `pt-p4-contact.js` red: *"worst frame residue 1 pairs
on 1 of 6000 frames"*. The P4.0 review measured residue 0 on 0/6,000 — on a planet without
P4.3's erosion. Each P4 owner isolates the *later* passes so its live numbers stay its own
report's (P4.0's review set `P.slab = false`; P4.2's review added `P.arc = false`); P4.3
shipped without adding `P.ero = false`, and sediment welded into the lid changed the fragment
population enough to put one marginal pair one ulp inside the hard radius once in 6,000
frames. Fix by the recorded convention, not in the kernel: `P.ero = false` in
`pt-p4-contact.js` (controlled, live and cost legs), `pt-p4-slab.js` and `pt-p4-arc.js`, each
with the sentence that says why. All three re-ran green (31 / 27 / 26); the integrated census
is `pt-p4-check.js`'s business.

## Finding 3: `pt-drift.js` was already red at HEAD

The first full run's `pt-p3-check` child failed on **its** §7 child: `pt-drift.js` read
"plate 1.14 vs own 0.44 cm/yr (2.6x)" against its `> 3x` gate. A stash run of the pristine
HEAD engine reproduces identical numbers — the break came in with the P4.3 review (the
re-seated sediment welds into the lids and shifts the 400 Myr drift statistics), whose
re-validation list skipped `pt-drift`. Fix by the isolation convention: `P.ero = false` in
`pt-drift.js` — the surface law is not a slab-pull fixture's business, and the integrated
planet is this milestone's harness. Re-run: **ALL PASS (5 checks)**, mean plate drift 2.53
cm/yr against 0.22 of own-marker flow (11.5x), on the planet its gate has been green on since
P4.2. The trail is `experiments/logs/0.3.0-p4.4-p3check-debug.txt`.
