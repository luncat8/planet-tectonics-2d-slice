# 0.2.3 — the melt constant, landed from the contact legs

Owner: `archive/0.2.2-tephra-pile-worklog.md` §6 — "`kMelt` 20 is the remaining half of
two visible eruption styles. It is a contact-leg calibration, not a toy-physics one.
Re-measure R2/R3 at 20 (and maybe 6, 10) with the delayed write-back; land a value only
from that table."

**Landed: `P.kMelt` 2e-3 → 20.** Every strict contact leg is green at it, and the
0.2.2 verdict against 20 is a measurement of a rule that is not in the tree (§2).

## 0. What the table had to carry

One table, two measurements per candidate, both delegated so no gate is implemented
twice:

- **contact** — the four strict contact legs (`contact-audit.js --strict`). This is the
  gate set that decides whether a melt rate may be committed at all: R1's event
  background, R2's needle and contiguous width, R3's crust ceiling, R5's topology and
  stack order.
- **cone** — the live arc cone at the same candidate (`tephra-pile.js`), which is the
  eruptive gain the constant is being raised for. It isolates the arc source
  (`kPlumeMelt` 0) so the cone is not the plume shield 0.2.1 landed.

Both owners gained a `KM` environment override — contact-audit.js's own `KG` pattern —
so a candidate is measured without editing params.js.
`experiments/melt-contact-sweep.js` (new) prints the table and keeps every run's log;
it is report-only, like the other tuning sweeps. Candidates are absolute values, not
multipliers, because the base moved during the measurement and the control row has to
stay the old base.

## 2. The 0.2.2 "not landed" was measured against a different rule

The 0.2.2 report declines 20 on two numbers: R2 2438 of 2724 on `3000/1/100` and R3
81.9 km on `5000/5/50` (log `experiments/logs/0.2.2-acceptance.txt`), and `params.js`
carried the reason as "a trial that also delayed lava". That is literally what it was.
With `writeBack` suppressed outside `finish` — K7 writing neither lithology, everything
landing at death — and `kMelt` 20, this tree reproduces both numbers exactly:

| | 3000/1/100 R2 | 5000/5/50 R3 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| lava delayed too (the 0.2.2 trial) | **2438 / 2724** = 89.5% FAIL | **81.9 km** FAIL |
| the rule in the tree (K7 writes lava, tephra waits) | 3590 / 3814 = 94.1% PASS | 80.4 km PASS |

The rule that landed is the second row: `MAG.k7` still calls
`box.writeBack(v, t, P.LITH.lava)` every frame, and only tephra waits for
`venDeath → finish`. So the 20 that was declined is not the 20 that is in the tree, and
re-measured the objection does not exist.

Two anchors say the difference is the rule and not the machine: the base leg reproduces
the committed `0.2.1-contact-audit-*` logs byte-for-byte (2186/2319, 81.6 km, 173 births
/ 138 deaths), and acceptance §2.6's melt-quiet leg (seed 5, 10 kyr/frame, 30 s/frame)
still hashes to the committed 4131910798.

## 3. The gate that asserted the rule was reading the wrong quantity

`tephra-pile.js` checked "a live arc vent does not write tephra into the stack" by
watching `venEdV` grow. `venEdV` is the edifice's written volume and does not name a
lithology, so it also counts the lava writes the rule still allows. Measured at
`kMelt` 20 over 3000 frames by wrapping `ERUPT.place`: **63.8 cells² of lava placed
live, 0.000 cells² of tephra, 504.1 cells² of tephra at death.** A slot a later birth
reuses also inherits its predecessor's edifice, which is a second way `venEdV` "grows"
without a write. On this tree the check was red at the committed rule.

It now counts what the rule names: `ERUPT.place` is wrapped (the way contact-audit wraps
`COL.k4`) and any tephra cell placed while the vent is alive is a violation; the
live/death split is printed so the log carries both. Same gate, no threshold moved, and
it can only be satisfied by the rule actually holding. Harness 6 / 6.

## 4. What lands, and what it costs

`P.kMelt` 2e-3 → 20 — a melt/water yield of 0.63, 0.2.1 §3's petrological decade.

- **The arc now has topography.** 27.00 × 8.24 px and 113.7 cells² of live pile where
  the base had none, so `erupt-bench`'s explosive style is on the page and not only in
  the toy box. On the two live legs with both sources running
  (`experiments/logs/0.2.3-melt-budget.txt`) the best live edifice is 8.7 × 27.0 px at
  seed 1/50 and 11.6 × 31.0 px at seed 5/100 — the design band, where 0.2.1 left a
  plume shield as the only design-scale edifice.
- **The crust budget barely moves.** Melt is 0.339% / 0.476% of all mafic production
  (0.2.1's landed plume rate was 0.155% / 0.277%), 43.7 / 25.3 km3/Myr for the whole
  section. No chamber spill (sill 0, idle 0, lost 0), thickest column 80.4 / 80.3 km
  against `crustMax` 80 km, K9 clean on every frame of both legs, ledger 1.98e-14 /
  1.87e-14. The 0.2.1 rule — melt under half a percent of the section's mafic
  production — still holds.
- **The duty cycle is the visible cost.** 97.9% / 92.9% of frames have a feeding vent
  (0.2.1 measured 57–69% at the landed plume rate), and the cone harness counts 8543
  alive-vent-frames over 3000 frames. That is the M1 lifecycle 0.2.1 §2 saw saturating
  at 10 000×; its knob is `Vbirth` / `tauVent`, not the melt rate.

8.24 px against erupt-bench's 10.02 is supply (113.7 cells² against 150), the same gap
0.2.2 §6 recorded at 9.12. Further melt buys duty cycle, not shape.

## 7. Open, and next

- **8.24 px against the design's 10–30.** The remaining distance is supply, not the
  write-back rule (113.7 cells² against erupt-bench's 150). Any further `kMelt` — or a
  `toyCellM2` revisit — has to be re-measured on the same four legs and the same budget
  row; the sweep and the budget command are the two commands that do it.
- **Duty cycle 93–98%.** If repose matters before 0.3.0-P3, `Vbirth` / `tauVent` is the
  knob, and it is its own contact-leg table.
- **`venEdV` is still a volume, not a lithology.** `edifice-bench` and `coupling` read it
  where both lithologies are either absent or intended, so nothing there is wrong today;
  a future gate about one lithology should count the lithology.
- **0.2.x stays a patch series.** The particle engine's conserved-melt eruptions are
  still 0.3.0-P3 (`0.3.0-p3-plan.md` P3.0 pool/ledger).
