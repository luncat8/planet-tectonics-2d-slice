  `roadmap.md`): instrument and attribute the three open items remaining at the close of
  0.3.2 M11 across the 8 600-Myr distribution worlds (`seeds 1, 3, 5, 7` $\times$

- Inspected `experiments/pt-heat-tail.js` (`movedAge` and `hjAdd`, lines 574–586) and cross-checked
  against the M6 distribution banks (`0.3.2-M6-{base,dep}-s{1,3,5,7}-600.json`) and M7 trace
  banks (`0.3.2-M7-{base,dep}-s1-trace-600.json`). Discovered that `movedAge(S, p)` returns
  `null` for never-relocated parcels (`lastMove[id] === 0`), and JavaScript's relational
  comparisons `age >= 0` and `age <= JOIN_WIN[i]` in `hjAdd` coerce `null` to `+0` (`true`).
  Consequently, every M6/M7/M8 bank recorded `heldJoin.never.n = 0` and folded all
  never-relocated held deposit mass-time into `heldJoin.ever` and `heldJoin.n[0..4]` (`<= 1f`).
  Verified in `0.3.2-M7-dep-s1-trace-600.json` that parcel `id = 25109` (`28.044` $\text{km}^2$
  host, delivered at frame 7,552 with `movedAgo: null`) was held `2,088` frames (`2,927.79`
  $\text{km}^2\cdot\text{Myr}$), accounting for `99.92%` of `dep-s1`'s M6-reported `2,930.05`
  $\text{km}^2\cdot\text{Myr}$ (`2,109` samples) "within 1 frame of a relocation".
- Updated `experiments/pt-heat-tail.js` to emit `heldJoinTrue` (`age !== null && age >= 0`)
  alongside `heldJoin` so existing M6/M7/M8 bank checks in `experiments/pt-heat-tail-check.js`
  remain compatible while future runs also record the null-separated partition.
- Built `experiments/pt-faces-guard-census.js`: an observation-neutral attribution harness
