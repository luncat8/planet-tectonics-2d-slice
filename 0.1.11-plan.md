# 0.1.11 — closing-kind hysteresis

Status: measured on the 0.1.10 tree, **not landed**. §3 is the candidate measured as v1; the
other variants are in §4. The decision, the numbers and the open choice are in
`archive/0.1.11-hysteresis-worklog.md`.

## 1. Problem

Between two frames a neighbour pair can change its K5 closing kind without any topology event.
A pair is closing C-C when both records are continental, the plates differ, neither is a sliver,
both have felsic at least `P.hOceanic`, and the records converge. K4 freezes that kind per pair
(`COL.freezeFloorClass`) so K5 cannot revoke the floor it solved. The next K3 transport re-reads
the kind live (`COL.isClosingCC`) before it sorts, and a pair whose kind changed then takes the
ordinary floor (9.38 km) instead of the crush floor (3.91 km). Its gap, already at the crush
floor, is pushed apart by up to 7 km in one frame. The reclassification has two causes:

- **Drain:** a K5 stage pushed one record's felsic below `P.hOceanic`. The pair is no longer
  continental-closing by the gate.
- **Velocity:** the raw convergence sign flipped between the two solves.

Over the eight-seed set of 0.1.10 (5000 frames, 50 and 100 kyr/frame) the base has 1006 drain
pairs and 272 velocity pairs. The drain cause was tested and rejected in 0.1.10 (the narrow
hold). This plan addresses the velocity cause.

## 2. Cause, from the code

The boundary state already has hysteresis. `PLT.edgeType` (K3) enters `collide` on a closing
speed beyond `P.epsHi` and keeps it while the speed stays beyond `P.epsLo` (1 mm/yr). The
closing-C-C predicate used by the floor (K4 freeze and the transport floor) does not read that
state: it reads the raw sign of `colU`. Two frames with the same boundary state can therefore
disagree on the floor. This is the same defect as "one owner per concept" (`0.4.0-sync-plan.md`
§1.1): the classifier owns the boundary state, and the floor must read it, not re-derive it.

## 3. Design

**Held pair.** A pair is held as closing C-C at the transport floor when its slot's boundary
state from the last K3 classification is `collide` and the record on its right is the same
plate the state was classified against (`st.edgeRPlate[i] === st.colPlate[j]`). The hold is
the classifier's own state, so it needs no new array and no checkpoint change.

**Rule.** `COL.isClosingCC(st, i, j)` is true when the plates differ, neither record is a
ghost, the held rule holds **or** the raw sign converges, and both records have felsic at
least `P.hOceanic`. The felsic gate is unchanged.

The hold ends when K3 reclassifies the pair (speed below `P.epsLo`, a sign change beyond the
band, or a topology change). It is not a timer.

What is **not** changed: the floor's crush and ordinary gaps, the K3 entry and exit thresholds,
the freeze's placement in K4, the drain (K5) and every topology intent. The field set is the
checkpoint's, so the checkpoint version stays.

## 4. Alternatives (measured, see the worklog)

- **Bounded hold** (v1e): the hold also needs `relN < P.epsHi`. It removes the R3 breach but
  leaves 478 velocity-caused flips, so it does not do what the rule is for.
- **Drain band.** Holding a pair while a record's felsic is below `P.hOceanic` by a band
  (1 km, 2 km). It removes the drain reclassification more fully than the hold alone but
  keeps drained records at the crush floor. Both bands fail gates the base passes (R3 ceiling
  91.6 km and R1/R2 needles). This is the donor-clamp mechanism of `0.1.9` §3 again.
- **Narrow hold** (`archive/0.1.10-narrow-hold-worklog.md`): not adopted.

## 5. Acceptance

The gates are fixed before the candidate is measured on seeds 9–16.

1. **Mechanism.** Velocity-caused reclassified pairs are zero in every run of seeds 1–8 at
   50 and 100 kyr/frame.
2. **Strict verdicts.** Over the 32 runs of seeds 1–16 at both rates, the candidate passes at
   least as many strict legs as the base. A run that the base passes and the candidate fails is
   listed with its failing gate and is a blocker unless the same gate fails on the base under a
   different seed in the same set (a re-rolled trajectory, not a mechanism).
3. **Suite.** Every harness in `AGENTS.md` passes, including `checkpoint.js` (the imported
   spin-up bound) and `m2-check.js` (324 checks), with no fixture edited.
4. **Drain reclassification is reported, not gated.** `archive/0.1.10-narrow-hold-worklog.md`
   showed it does not predict a gate failure.
5. **Ceiling.** No run of seeds 1–16 at either rate exceeds the base's largest R3 value
   (81.7 km) by more than the ceiling's one-frame allowance. Added after the measurement: gate 2
   did not name the R3 ceiling, and v1 breaches it at 126.1 km on seed 12 at 100 kyr/frame.

## 6. Milestones

- **M0 — measurement.** Scratch variants of the rule, the drain band and the base, run with
  `experiments/contact-audit.js` and `experiments/k5-reclass-probe.js` on seeds 1–8 (and 9–16 for
  the candidate). Logs in `experiments/logs/0.1.11-*`.
- **M1 — landing.** If the acceptance gates hold, `COL.isClosingCC` takes the rule in
  `js/columns.js`, with no flag. Not taken: gate 5 fails for v1, so the engine is unchanged.
  The logs and the worklog stay as the record.
- **M2 — tables.** `experiments/k5-variant-table.js` reads every variant's logs and prints the
  totals the worklog quotes. The parsing is shared with `k5-hold-table.js` through
  `experiments/k5-table-lib.js`.

## 7. Risks

| Risk | Warning sign | Counter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The hold keeps a pair closing after its geometry has separated | an R2 or R5 failure on a new seed | the hold ends on the next K3 classification; count holds per run |
| Trajectory re-roll reads as a regression | one new failure with no mechanism | acceptance 2 with the paired base run |
| A drained record stays held | R3 ceiling near the limit | band variants are measured, not landed (§4) |
| A hold on an opening pair puts the crush floor on it | an R3 breach or a column narrower than 0.5 w0 | observed in v1: 11 held-opening pairs within 120 km of the breach at seed 12 (`experiments/k5-held-trace.js`); gate 5 |
