# 0.9.0 — Draft: the integrated globe

Reference record of the later half of the request that opened 0.4.1, kept here so a later session
can see what was asked for and in which words. The path to it is `0.4.1-plan.md` §8; the
prerequisite (a cut that can be copied or saved and loaded here) is `0.4.1-plan.md` as a whole.

## What was asked

> for some later version like 0.9 plan to have integrated globe with working together top view
> simulation and one of cross-section in sync

## What it means, restated

1. **One globe, one section, both alive.** The top-view simulation runs; one cross-section of it
   runs beside it. Not a screenshot, not a bake — two views of one world moving at once.
2. **"Integrated" is a question, and this plan answers it with two documents.** Both projects
   define classic-script globals with the same names (`0.4.0-sync-plan.md` §4.7), so one page
   loading both is a namespacing pass, and that pass is a milestone of its own, not a detail.
   Until it lands, the integration is two documents side by side — most likely the globe's page
   hosting the section in an iframe, since the globe owns the map.
3. **"In sync" has to be defined before it can be built.** `0.4.1-plan.md` §8.1 sets out the
   three couplings and picks the third: the globe sends the plate intervals along the cut with
   their velocities and boundary types, the section slaves its plate kinematics to them and keeps
   its own crust. The section does not drive the globe, and the globe does not ingest the
   section's state.
4. **The channel has to work on `file://`.** `0.4.0-sync-plan.md` §4.7: `postMessage` if the
   handshake answers, `localStorage` if it is shared, **copy/paste and a file always**.

## What has to be true first

- a cut exists and travels (0.4.1 M1–M2);
- the section can seed itself from one (0.4.1 M2);
- the checkpoint and the coupling envelope exist (0.4.0 M5, 0.4.1 M5);
- the shared format is in the manifest on both sides (0.4.0 M0).

## Open questions this draft does not settle

- whose page hosts the pair, and what the iframe handshake has to carry for a paused globe to
  pause the section;
- whether the section's own time may run ahead of the globe's between messages, or must be
  slaved frame by frame;
- what the section is allowed to send back beyond the declared summary (crust and sediment volume
  across the path, trench positions, discovered bodies);
- whether a single page ever replaces the two documents, and therefore whether the namespacing
  pass is worth its cost.
