# 0.3.2 — light-parcel heat concentration: measurement before selection

Reference: `0.3.2-draft.md`. This plan does not authorize a routing-threshold change.

## Defect and protected behavior

The adjoint gather conserves the domain integral, not the distribution of specific enthalpy.
A low marker measure can amplify an increment; small mass alone is neither a sufficient
explanation nor a safe exclusion criterion. The existing 600 Myr seed-5 cell has three
field-supported deposit transfers as well as an unphysical hot tail. Preserve the former;
do not use their count to tune a constant or remove the field-support term.

The same-cell baseline must reproduce fingerprint **8261233554728015**, three transfers
and 9.7302 km² shed. Record individual events (frame, ID, position, host and melt mass,
temperature, field and solidus), not just the aggregate: three unrelated replacement events
are not evidence that the original transfers survived. Changed trajectories require an
explicit material/event correspondence argument before acceptance.

## M0 — bounded candidate experiment

Run fresh processes, seed 5, 50 kyr/frame, 600 Myr, shipped geometry, initial condition,
P3/P4 passes and pure FLIP. Baseline first, then each candidate serially, then baseline again
for replay and host timing drift. No production parameter or kernel changes at this stage.

Candidate definitions (experimental probes, **not frozen constants**):

- **Gain cap 2:** multiply the gathered specific increment by
  `min(1, 2 / gain)`, where gain is `sum(W*cw) / sum(W)` over the interior stencil.
  Apply to all grid-coupled phases. This is a parcel-gain cap, not a cap on each node's `cw`.
- **Mass-share floor 0.01:** multiply that increment by
  `min(1, share / 0.01)`, where share is
  `sum(W * W*m / deposited_node_mass) / sum(W)`. No mass is manufactured.
  This is attenuation below a share floor, not a redistribution or a modified scatter.
- **Deposit relaxation, retention 0.999 per 50 kyr gather:** after the adjoint increment,
  relax deposit H toward `byT(sampleAt(Tg), depth, composition).h` by 0.001.
  Use the ordinary bilinear field, not the unnormalized adjoint PIC gather; its weights
  need not sum to one. This is about a 50 Myr e-folding at this clock, not a proposed
  clock-independent law. Book the correction honestly as intake.
- **Minimum deposit mass 1 km²:** before scatter, merge smaller deposits into an at-least-
  1 km² deposit in the same nearest-node bucket, using the pool's composition compatibility
  and requiring equal quench certificates. Use the conservative pool merge, including
  periodic position and slot compaction. Do not mix phases, delete mass, or search globally
  to force a floor. Count stranded attempts when no eligible local recipient exists;
  this is a local coalescence policy, not a guaranteed lower bound.

These probes test the mechanisms separately. Their failure does not disprove every value
or implementation in a family. No parameter search is driven by routing count.

## Measurements and gates

Use compensated inventories after **every frame**, not HUD samples:

- mass relative residual < 1e-9;
- enthalpy change against recorded intake < 1e-9;
- P3.1 latent identity < 1e-10 relative (denominator floored at 1 near zero);
- worst cumulative `|intake/wall - 1|`, and its end value;
- actual P4 long-leg gate < 0.01; separately report the draft's approximately 5e-5
  historical reference. The latter is a reference target, **not** the assertion in
  `pt-p4-check.js`. That harness also has a < 1e-9 short-matrix identity gate;
- mean kernel frame < 20 ms at 50 kyr, excluding read-only observation and inventories
  added by the experiment, including all candidate work. Repeat baseline to expose host
  drift. Timing remains host-specific, not a universal performance guarantee;
- finite parcel T and -0.5 <= T <= 1.5, with extrema and violating sample counts by phase.
  This is a new candidate gate, not a claim the baseline already passes;
- at G5, count and integrate the certified, buried, threshold-molten deposits rejected
  by field support; record host and melt-equivalent mass-time and maximum T-field gap;
- record all admitted deposit events, actual transfer counter/mass, mantle routing count,
  refusals, and complete-state hash. Report rejected candidates without disguising them
  as failed execution of the measurement harness.

Never balance a capped increment by editing the wall ledger. A closing enthalpy book can
coexist with a failing wall gate if the correction was honestly recorded: both matter.

## M1 — selection or rejection

Select nothing unless the tail, conservation, wall and timing requirements hold together
and the protected transfers have evidence of survival. Keep condition 5 unchanged. If
local caps/relaxation lose the wall integral or conservative coalescence leaves the tail,
measure a node-wise conservative bounded redistribution next; do not ship the least-bad
probe as a fix. Redistribution needs a feasibility treatment where local heat capacity
cannot absorb the node increment under the bound, not an unbooked global correction.

M0 measured all four probes and selected none, and exposed the extended-cell latent
identity failure on the unmodified baseline. M1 therefore runs in two measured steps
before any selection is even considered:

**Localization protocol.** Trace the P3.1 latent residual across every kernel boundary of
the unchanged banked cell (the runner is `experiments/pt-heat-tail-trace.js`), gated on
the banked fingerprint: the trace is read-only observation, and a cell that does not
reproduce the banked latent metric, books, held census and protected event records
exactly is tracing a different world and says nothing. Attribute every permanent
residual step to the boundary that raises it, and decompose the steps of phases no
ledger books into individual material events, predicted at the event and measured at
the next equilibrium pass. A cause is claimed only when the attributed steps close
against the drift they explain.

**Bounded redistribution probe** (experimental, **not a frozen constant**): `spread`.
Inside the adjoint gather, bound each parcel's own predicted equilibrium temperature —
the reading of the delivered enthalpy through the phase law at the parcel's depth and
composition — at the temperature gate's own bounds (upper 1.5, lower bound − 2). The
extensive increment withheld from a capped parcel goes to the members of the same
nearest-node bucket in proportion to the headroom each still has under the same bound.
Per-node totals are preserved, so the intake, the books and the wall integral see the
baseline's own delivery to round-off: the probe bounds the *distribution*, never the
integral. Where the eligible capacity of a bucket cannot absorb the withheld increment,
the remainder returns to the capped parcels that raised it — the node total is still
preserved and the bound stays violated, but by less than the baseline's — and the event
is counted and integrated. Feasibility is measured, never patched with an unbooked
global correction. The bound is read at the parcel's pre-advection depth while the
frame-end gate reads after advection, so a capped parcel can finish a hair outside the
bound; the report states that margin instead of hiding it in a tuned constant.

## M2 — the latent remedy, the tail re-measured, the matrix

**Latent remedy (launch/air booking).** `FLIGHT.launch` debits the vent at the stock split
(`f·d` of every extensive field), then sets the clast's `T` *and* `melt` to the equilibrium
reading of its specific enthalpy at surface pressure, and books the adiabatic sensible drop
`d·(T_eq − T_stock)`, `T_stock = H − L·xiStock`, in `S.ledger` — the decompression melting is
real and is funded by that drop; `S.wall` is not touched because no heat crossed the surface.
`PHASE.equil` books `m·dT` for every parcel whose temperature changes, not only grid-coupled
ones, so any later depth change of an unbooked phase is booked where it happens. `F.diag`
evaluates the residual every frame (`S.d.latent`, `S.d.latentAbs`, HUD). The acceptance of
this remedy is that the extended cell's identity closes on every frame while every
grid-coupled reading of that cell — held census, temperature extrema, wall, books and the
three protected events — reproduces the pre-remedy bank bitwise.

**Tail re-measurement** on the re-banked cell (same protocol as M0; experimental probes,
**not frozen constants**):

- **Stencil-scale bounded placement** (`stencil`): the `spread` probe with the placement set
  widened from the nearest-node bucket to the 3×3 node block of the gather stencil around the
  capped parcel's node; the conservation unit becomes the stencil block. Blocks overlap and are
  placed in node order on the live enthalpy; the infeasible remainder returns only to the
  capped parcels of the node that raised it.
- **Sub-grid relaxation**: after the adjoint gather, each passive host parcel (`PH_SOLID`, or
  deposits only) relaxes toward the ordinary bilinear field — the reading G5's field term uses
  — with retention `exp(−d·kappa·dt/m)`, the parcel area standing for its length squared
  (`d = 1`, the slowest mode of a parcel-sized region). The rate is clock-independent and
  restores the light parcels whose FLIP increments never feed back into the field. Mobile melt
  keeps its own transit. Two bookings:
  - `relaxw` books the net correction `R` as an explicit non-operator source beside the wall
    (`hLedger` only). `R` is never folded into `S.wall`: the binding gate stays
    `|intake/wall − 1|`, with `R/wall` and the operator-only ratio reported beside it.
  - `subgrid` makes the exchange zero-sum: each parcel's extensive correction is scattered
    over the covered nodes of its stencil and taken back from every coupled parcel there
    through the adjoint weights (`sum_p m_p W_pk cw_k = mu_k`), so the reaction is
    `sum_k W_pk E_k / w_k`, never amplified. What the adjoint does not return exactly (a parcel
    reseeded after the scatter that built `w`) is booked, never dropped.

A tail remedy must hold all of: the −0.5..1.5 bound on every phase and frame *without*
clipping parcels at the bound while they stay far from their field; the held-back
concentration no longer generated; the wall, books and timing gates; zero refusals; no
degenerate route behavior (a residue re-split every frame toward vanishing host mass); and
a material correspondence for the three protected events — exact records, or the same
material IDs routing with field support. Condition 5 stays.

**Clock/seed matrix** (`experiments/pt-heat-matrix.js`): seeds 1, 2, 3, 5, 7 at 10, 50, 100
and 500 kyr/frame over 300 Myr, plus seed 5 at 50 kyr over 600 Myr; every cell a fresh
process; the first cell repeated bitwise. Gates per cell, every frame: mass < 1e-9 and
enthalpy-against-intake < 1e-9 relative; end deposit and melt books within 1e-6 km²; the
P3.1 identity below 1e-10 of the larger of the two books it is the difference of
(`|ΔhLedger|`, `|Δledger|`, floored at 1); worst `|intake/wall − 1|` < 0.01; finite `T` and
−0.5 ≤ `T` ≤ 1.5 per phase; zero refusals; every deposit transfer reconciled and
field-supported. The identity's denominator is the books', not `|hLedger − ledger|` floored at
1: near a zero crossing of that difference the latter is an absolute 1e-10 km²·H gate, below
the compensated-inventory round-off of a ~4e7 km²·H pool, while the defect it exists to catch
was 167 km²·H. The floor-1 metric is printed beside it. Frame time is reported, not gated, in
the matrix (cells run concurrently); the frame gate stays with the serial owners.

**Re-bank and series.** Any state change re-banks every state-dependent fingerprint: P3
replay, P4 integrated/matrix, 0.3.1 canonical 300 Myr seed-6 leg, seed/clock matrices and
the seed-5 600 Myr leg. Re-run the P3/P4 owners, melt-path owners, page parity and the column
whole-series acceptance. Old hashes remain baseline evidence, never expected outputs for a
changed state. Only after a tail remedy is accepted reconsider whether the deposit field
filter remains necessary; no constant changes to reopen the 300 Myr route.
