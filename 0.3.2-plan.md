# 0.3.2 — light-parcel heat concentration: measurement before selection

Reference: `0.3.2-draft.md`. This plan does not authorize a routing-threshold change.

## Defect and protected behavior

The adjoint gather conserves the domain integral, not the distribution of specific enthalpy.
A low marker measure can amplify an increment; small mass alone is neither a sufficient
explanation nor a safe exclusion criterion. The existing 600 Myr seed-5 cell has three
field-supported deposit transfers as well as an unphysical hot tail. Preserve the former;
do not use their count to tune a constant or remove the field-support term.

The same-cell baseline must reproduce fingerprint **8261233554728015**, three transfers
and 9.7302 km² shed. Record individual events (frame, ID, position, host and melt mass,
temperature, field and solidus), not just the aggregate: three unrelated replacement events
are not evidence that the original transfers survived. Changed trajectories require an
explicit material/event correspondence argument before acceptance.

## M0 — bounded candidate experiment

Run fresh processes, seed 5, 50 kyr/frame, 600 Myr, shipped geometry, initial condition,
P3/P4 passes and pure FLIP. Baseline first, then each candidate serially, then baseline again
for replay and host timing drift. No production parameter or kernel changes at this stage.

Candidate definitions (experimental probes, **not frozen constants**):

- **Gain cap 2:** multiply the gathered specific increment by
  `min(1, 2 / gain)`, where gain is `sum(W*cw) / sum(W)` over the interior stencil.
  Apply to all grid-coupled phases. This is a parcel-gain cap, not a cap on each node's `cw`.
- **Mass-share floor 0.01:** multiply that increment by
  `min(1, share / 0.01)`, where share is
  `sum(W * W*m / deposited_node_mass) / sum(W)`. No mass is manufactured.
  This is attenuation below a share floor, not a redistribution or a modified scatter.
- **Deposit relaxation, retention 0.999 per 50 kyr gather:** after the adjoint increment,
  relax deposit H toward `byT(sampleAt(Tg), depth, composition).h` by 0.001.
  Use the ordinary bilinear field, not the unnormalized adjoint PIC gather; its weights
  need not sum to one. This is about a 50 Myr e-folding at this clock, not a proposed
  clock-independent law. Book the correction honestly as intake.
- **Minimum deposit mass 1 km²:** before scatter, merge smaller deposits into an at-least-
  1 km² deposit in the same nearest-node bucket, using the pool's composition compatibility
  and requiring equal quench certificates. Use the conservative pool merge, including
  periodic position and slot compaction. Do not mix phases, delete mass, or search globally
  to force a floor. Count stranded attempts when no eligible local recipient exists;
  this is a local coalescence policy, not a guaranteed lower bound.

These probes test the mechanisms separately. Their failure does not disprove every value
or implementation in a family. No parameter search is driven by routing count.

## Measurements and gates

Use compensated inventories after **every frame**, not HUD samples:

- mass relative residual < 1e-9;
- enthalpy change against recorded intake < 1e-9;
- P3.1 latent identity < 1e-10 relative (denominator floored at 1 near zero);
- worst cumulative `|intake/wall - 1|`, and its end value;
- actual P4 long-leg gate < 0.01; separately report the draft's approximately 5e-5
  historical reference. The latter is a reference target, **not** the assertion in
  `pt-p4-check.js`. That harness also has a < 1e-9 short-matrix identity gate;
- mean kernel frame < 20 ms at 50 kyr, excluding read-only observation and inventories
  added by the experiment, including all candidate work. Repeat baseline to expose host
  drift. Timing remains host-specific, not a universal performance guarantee;
- finite parcel T and -0.5 <= T <= 1.5, with extrema and violating sample counts by phase.
  This is a new candidate gate, not a claim the baseline already passes;
- at G5, count and integrate the certified, buried, threshold-molten deposits rejected
  by field support; record host and melt-equivalent mass-time and maximum T-field gap;
- record all admitted deposit events, actual transfer counter/mass, mantle routing count,
  refusals, and complete-state hash. Report rejected candidates without disguising them
  as failed execution of the measurement harness.

Never balance a capped increment by editing the wall ledger. A closing enthalpy book can
coexist with a failing wall gate if the correction was honestly recorded: both matter.

## M1 — selection or rejection

Select nothing unless the tail, conservation, wall and timing requirements hold together
and the protected transfers have evidence of survival. Keep condition 5 unchanged. If
local caps/relaxation lose the wall integral or conservative coalescence leaves the tail,
measure a node-wise conservative bounded redistribution next; do not ship the least-bad
probe as a fix. Redistribution needs a feasibility treatment where local heat capacity
cannot absorb the node increment under the bound, not an unbooked global correction.

M0 measured all four probes and selected none, and exposed the extended-cell latent
identity failure on the unmodified baseline. M1 therefore runs in two measured steps
before any selection is even considered:

**Localization protocol.** Trace the P3.1 latent residual across every kernel boundary of
the unchanged banked cell (the runner is `experiments/pt-heat-tail-trace.js`), gated on
the banked fingerprint: the trace is read-only observation, and a cell that does not
reproduce the banked latent metric, books, held census and protected event records
exactly is tracing a different world and says nothing. Attribute every permanent
residual step to the boundary that raises it, and decompose the steps of phases no
ledger books into individual material events, predicted at the event and measured at
the next equilibrium pass. A cause is claimed only when the attributed steps close
against the drift they explain.

**Bounded redistribution probe** (experimental, **not a frozen constant**): `spread`.
Inside the adjoint gather, bound each parcel's own predicted equilibrium temperature —
the reading of the delivered enthalpy through the phase law at the parcel's depth and
composition — at the temperature gate's own bounds (upper 1.5, lower bound − 2). The
extensive increment withheld from a capped parcel goes to the members of the same
nearest-node bucket in proportion to the headroom each still has under the same bound.
Per-node totals are preserved, so the intake, the books and the wall integral see the
baseline's own delivery to round-off: the probe bounds the *distribution*, never the
integral. Where the eligible capacity of a bucket cannot absorb the withheld increment,
the remainder returns to the capped parcels that raised it — the node total is still
preserved and the bound stays violated, but by less than the baseline's — and the event
is counted and integrated. Feasibility is measured, never patched with an unbooked
global correction. The bound is read at the parcel's pre-advection depth while the
frame-end gate reads after advection, so a capped parcel can finish a hair outside the
bound; the report states that margin instead of hiding it in a tuned constant.

## M2 — the latent remedy, the tail re-measured, the matrix

**Latent remedy (launch/air booking).** `FLIGHT.launch` debits the vent at the stock split
(`f·d` of every extensive field), then sets the clast's `T` *and* `melt` to the equilibrium
reading of its specific enthalpy at surface pressure, and books the adiabatic sensible drop
`d·(T_eq − T_stock)`, `T_stock = H − L·xiStock`, in `S.ledger` — the decompression melting is
real and is funded by that drop; `S.wall` is not touched because no heat crossed the surface.
`PHASE.equil` books `m·dT` for every parcel whose temperature changes, not only grid-coupled
ones, so any later depth change of an unbooked phase is booked where it happens. `F.diag`
evaluates the residual every frame (`S.d.latent`, `S.d.latentAbs`, HUD). The acceptance of
this remedy is that the extended cell's identity closes on every frame while every
grid-coupled reading of that cell — held census, temperature extrema, wall, books and the
three protected events — reproduces the pre-remedy bank bitwise.

**Tail re-measurement** on the re-banked cell (same protocol as M0; experimental probes,
**not frozen constants**):

- **Stencil-scale bounded placement** (`stencil`): the `spread` probe with the placement set
  widened from the nearest-node bucket to the 3×3 node block of the gather stencil around the
  capped parcel's node; the conservation unit becomes the stencil block. Blocks overlap and are
  placed in node order on the live enthalpy; the infeasible remainder returns only to the
  capped parcels of the node that raised it.
- **Sub-grid relaxation**: after the adjoint gather, each passive host parcel (`PH_SOLID`, or
  deposits only) relaxes toward the ordinary bilinear field — the reading G5's field term uses
  — with retention `exp(−d·kappa·dt/m)`, the parcel area standing for its length squared
  (`d = 1`, the slowest mode of a parcel-sized region). The rate is clock-independent and
  restores the light parcels whose FLIP increments never feed back into the field. Mobile melt
  keeps its own transit. Two bookings:
  - `relaxw` books the net correction `R` as an explicit non-operator source beside the wall
    (`hLedger` only). `R` is never folded into `S.wall`: the binding gate stays
    `|intake/wall − 1|`, with `R/wall` and the operator-only ratio reported beside it.
  - `subgrid` makes the exchange zero-sum: each parcel's extensive correction is scattered
    over the covered nodes of its stencil and taken back from every coupled parcel there
    through the adjoint weights (`sum_p m_p W_pk cw_k = mu_k`), so the reaction is
    `sum_k W_pk E_k / w_k`, never amplified. What the adjoint does not return exactly (a parcel
    reseeded after the scatter that built `w`) is booked, never dropped.

A tail remedy must hold all of: the −0.5..1.5 bound on every phase and frame *without*
clipping parcels at the bound while they stay far from their field; the held-back
concentration no longer generated; the wall, books and timing gates; zero refusals; no
degenerate route behavior (a residue re-split every frame toward vanishing host mass); and
a material correspondence for the three protected events — exact records, or the same
material IDs routing with field support. Condition 5 stays.

**Clock/seed matrix** (`experiments/pt-heat-matrix.js`): seeds 1, 2, 3, 5, 7 at 10, 50, 100
and 500 kyr/frame over 300 Myr, plus seed 5 at 50 kyr over 600 Myr; every cell a fresh
process; the first cell repeated bitwise. Gates per cell, every frame: mass < 1e-9 and
enthalpy-against-intake < 1e-9 relative; end deposit and melt books within 1e-6 km²; the
P3.1 identity below 1e-10 of the larger of the two books it is the difference of
(`|ΔhLedger|`, `|Δledger|`, floored at 1); worst `|intake/wall − 1|` < 0.01; finite `T` and
−0.5 ≤ `T` ≤ 1.5 per phase; zero refusals; every deposit transfer reconciled and
field-supported. The identity's denominator is the books', not `|hLedger − ledger|` floored at
1: near a zero crossing of that difference the latter is an absolute 1e-10 km²·H gate, below
the compensated-inventory round-off of a ~4e7 km²·H pool, while the defect it exists to catch
was 167 km²·H. The floor-1 metric is printed beside it. Frame time is reported, not gated, in
the matrix (cells run concurrently); the frame gate stays with the serial owners.

**Re-bank and series.** Any state change re-banks every state-dependent fingerprint: P3
replay, P4 integrated/matrix, 0.3.1 canonical 300 Myr seed-6 leg, seed/clock matrices and
the seed-5 600 Myr leg. Re-run the P3/P4 owners, melt-path owners, page parity and the column
whole-series acceptance. Old hashes remain baseline evidence, never expected outputs for a
changed state. Only after a tail remedy is accepted reconsider whether the deposit field
filter remains necessary; no constant changes to reopen the 300 Myr route.

## M3 — melt depletion, and the two faces read as their own quantities

M2 specified a missing piece of the melt law: P3.2's split leaves the residue exactly at its own
solidus with its composition unchanged, so a residue the field supports re-melts on the next
pass, and the first mechanism that restores a light parcel toward its field reaches that gap.
M3 measures the depletion rule that closes it, and reads the two faces the M2 matrix opened as
quantities of their own, each with its own record rather than one scalar per phase.

**Depletion probes** (experimental, **not frozen constants**; both wrap the melt path's own
kernel text, neither enters the engine state, both off by default):

- **`--dpart D`, water-flux depletion of the split.** The split's melt child takes the parcel's
  water and felsic at the batch-melting partition `c_melt = c / (D + F (1 − D))` and the residue
  keeps `c_res = c D / (D + F (1 − D))`, `F` the split's own melt fraction. The residue's solidus
  therefore rises with what the melt took, `meltWW · ΔcW + meltWF · ΔcF`; the residue keeps its
  enthalpy and is simply subsolidus at the new solidus, and the melt child keeps its own H,
  reading back as superheated melt. Both children stay on the law's manifold, so a split still
  moves material without melting it, and nothing is booked: the composition change is the law's
  own state. `D` = 1 is the no-depletion limit; water's partition is small (`D` = 0.01 measured).
- **`--cap PHI`, refractory mass budget.** A parcel may shed at most `PHI` of its own mass over
  its life (a probe-local map keyed by material id, never engine state): the first split is
  unaffected, later ones stop once the inventory is spent. This bounds what the water budget
  cannot — the M2 row's bleeding residues sit a median 0.022 of T above their own solidus, and
  about half still sit above the *dry* solidus, so drying alone leaves them routing. A blocked
  candidate is counted separately from a delivered transfer, and a spent parcel's later candidacy
  is reported, not hidden.

**Measurement.** Each channel alone on the M2 bank cell and on M2's sub-grid row (deposits only),
then the sub-grid row re-measured on top of depletion in a process of its own (serial timing).
Gates are M2's list unchanged; the route's own gate is the **bleed count** — transfers whose host
was already below 1e-3 km² — and the frozen P3.2 census is printed beside every cell (`split`,
`whole`, `freeze`, `seg`, `intrDr`, `landCr`, `ventDr`, `chambers`, `meltM`, `depN`, `nMelt`,
`nDep`) so a rule's cost to the pipeline is read, not assumed. A candidate count is not a
transfer and a transfer count is not a route: both are reported.

**The two faces, read on their own** (observation only, no state touched):

- **Mobile melt above the bound.** Each excursion run of a mobile parcel above T = 1.5 records the
  entry reading, the frame and state it was hottest in, the field it crossed, and the close or
  the leg's end, with the parcel's own size, depth, melt fraction, enthalpy and composition.
  Measured on seed 2 at 50, 100 and 500 kyr over 300 Myr, the cell the M2 matrix found. A run
  still open at the last frame is closed by the observer rather than dropped.
- **The shallow cold side.** Per-phase extrema by depth band (0/50/120/250/500/1000/2000/3000 km)
  with the frame and material id that set each one, and the path of every new global mantle
  minimum (depth, field, H, melt). The deposit quench's reaction path is read from the deposit
  bands, since the quench is the only rule that drives material to the ambient regardless of the
  depth it ends up at.

Nothing is selected by an M3 measurement: the probes are candidates, and eligibility still needs
M2's gate list together — the two-sided bound, the held-back census, the wall, books, frame time,
zero refusals, no degenerate route, and material evidence for the three protected events.

## M4 — the field-relative bound, and the mechanism measured rather than inferred

M3 §9 leaves one candidate standing and two instruments missing. M4 builds the instruments first,
then measures the candidate on the cell every earlier milestone was read on (seed 5, 50 kyr/frame,
600 Myr, shipped geometry and passes, pure FLIP).

**Instruments** (observation only — they move no trajectory, so `baseline 0 20` still reproduces
fingerprint 5534361194548428 and the M2 bank is untouched):

- the per-phase outside count is **split into its two ends** (`outsideAbove`, `outsideBelow`), so
  a row that violates both ends of −0.5..1.5 says so with two numbers instead of hiding behind
  one;
- the adjoint pairing's own **gain** (`cw = mu_k / W_k` over the parcel's stencil) and the
  parcel's **mass share** of that stencil — `experiments/pt-buried-deposit-melt.js`'s `routeGain`,
  ported into `pt-heat-tail.js` — are read at every new global mantle minimum, at every
  mobile-melt sample above T = 1.5, and at the entry and the hottest frame of every excursion.
  M3 inferred the mechanism on the cold side from a parcel's mass, depth and margin; M4 reads it.

**The candidate (`frb K`).** The M1/M2 conservative bounded redistribution with the bound taken
against the field a parcel sits in instead of a constant: the delivered state may not leave
(`T_field` − K, `T_field` + K), `T_field` being the pre-gather nodal temperature read once per
coupled parcel before the delivery loop, so the bound, the headroom and the giveback all read one
snapshot. It is two-sided by construction, and it bites where M2's constant bound only clipped —
`stencil` 1.5 parked the light deposits *at* T = 1.500 while they were still 0.72 of T above their
own field. Placement, and with it per-node totals, the intake, the books and the wall integral,
remain the baseline's own delivery to round-off: the probe bounds the distribution, never the
integral. Where a block's eligible capacity cannot absorb a withheld increment the remainder
returns to the capped parcels that raised it, as before, and the event is counted and integrated
(`spreadShortN` / `spreadShortH`) — feasibility stays measured, never patched.

**Gates are M2's list unchanged**: the two-sided bound on every phase and frame; the held-back
concentration no longer generated; mass < 1e-9 and enthalpy-against-intake < 1e-9 every frame; the
P3.1 identity below 1e-10 of its books; worst |intake/wall − 1| < 0.01; zero refusals; no
degenerate route (a residue re-split every frame toward vanishing host mass); serial frame time
< 20 ms; and material evidence for the three protected events — the same material ids routing with
field support, or an explicit correspondence argument. Condition 5 stays, and no constant moves to
reopen the 300 Myr route.

**What the measurement has to say beyond pass/fail.** A field-relative bound is a statement about
what a parcel may be *relative to the field it claims*, so M4 reports, per row: how much of the
delivery the probe withholds and where it places it; the gain and mass share of the parcels it
caps, against the same readings for the population they were drawn from; whether the cold anomaly
M3 §7 found in the descending quenched deposits is removed with the hot tail (one mechanism, as
M3 argues) or survives it (geology, needing a protected reading of its own); and the deposit-band
ordering, so the inversion the relaxation produced is either reproduced or not. If `frb` holds the
bound and loses the route, the row that adds `--dpart` — the melt law's missing piece, measured in
M3 — is the next one to read, on the same cell, with the same gates.

## M5 — the material correspondence argument

M4's gate list carries one condition no row of the family has been able to answer with an id match:
material evidence for the three protected events, "the same material ids routing with field support,
or an explicit correspondence argument". The deposit-scoped row is the first that removes the
concentration without emptying the engine or saturating the pool, and it delivers two transfers
where the control delivers three, with none of the control's three material ids among them. A count
cannot say whether those two are the control's three in different clothing — the same deposits
delivered elsewhere or later, which would mean the bound displaced the defect — or new deposits the
ordinary pipeline runs, which would mean the control's three were prevented.

**Why an id match cannot answer it.** Material ids come from `S.nextId`: a split's child is a new
id and a merge survivor takes the smaller of the two, so from the first frame in which a candidate's
delivery differs from the control's, the same material carries different ids in the two runs. An id
match is a valid test only up to the divergence, and the divergence is what is being measured. What
survives it is a parcel's own history inside the run that produced it.

**The instrument (`--trace-id a,b,c`, observation only).** For each named material id, from the
frame it first appears to the frame it last does: its admission (frame, site, depth, mass, phase —
the record that also tests whether one id means one material in the two runs); its burial and
heating history on a 200-frame spine, and in full on every frame that does something to it (at or
above its own solidus − 0.05, molten, capped by the bound, held back by the field support,
delivered, a phase change, the last frame); every act of the bound on it (the equilibrium
temperature the delivery would have reached, the two sides of the bound at that parcel, what was
withheld — counted and integrated whether or not the row is kept); and its routing fate (frames at
the threshold, held by field support, delivered, and at which frame). Its extremes are recorded with
the frames that set them. Nothing here writes engine state, so a traced run keeps the fingerprint of
its own untraced cell. `hottest` gains the material id, because the outlier this release is about
was banked as a temperature, a frame and a place — enough to report it, not enough to trace it.

**The measurement.** Two runs of the cell every milestone was read on (seed 5, 50 kyr/frame,
600 Myr): the control, tracing the three ids it delivered and the two the candidate delivers, and
`frb 0.25 --scope dep`, tracing the same five and the control's hottest parcel. Read side by side
they answer, per protected transfer: whether the candidate run contains that material at all; if it
does, what the bound did to it — capped how often, withholding how much, and its T against its own
field and its own solidus as a result; if it does not, whether that site is quiet in the candidate
or delivered something else; and for the candidate's own two transfers, where their material came
from, and whether the control holds the same material at the same site without delivering it.

**Gates are M4's list unchanged**, and the correspondence argument is condition 5's own second
branch: it names the material, not the count. Condition 5 stays, no constant moves to reopen the
300 Myr route, and no row of the family becomes eligible on a count.

## M6 — the bound against the world, and the repair join

M5 left two questions open and both are measurements, not remedies. Neither changes the gate
list: condition 5 stays, the deposit field filter stays, and no constant moves.

**The distribution (the eligibility question).** One world per row cannot separate a candidate's
cost from its world's own variance: the control's 9.7302 km² deposit→melt flux is dominated by
one 7.4359 km² event, so the ratio against the candidate's 1.2838 km² is n = 1. Run the control
and `frb 0.25 --scope dep` on seeds 1, 3 and 7 (the other seeds of the matrix set, same 50
kyr/frame, 600 Myr) and read `depTx`, `depMelt`, the delivered host masses, `heldMassTime`, the
deposit-phase temperature range and `heldGapMax` as distributions across worlds. The decision
rule is the question itself: if the candidate's deposit→melt flux is a small fraction of the
control's on every seed, the thinning is the bound's doing and the row stays rejected on a
measured cost; if the ratio varies with the world, the 600 Myr cell was reading one large event
and the row's standing is open again. Cell pairs run serially so the frame-time gate is read
from an unpolluted host; every cell closes the same books, wall, latent identity and
per-phase temperature bound every frame.

**The repair join (the mechanism question).** The held-back concentration of the control
(68,140 km²·Myr) is a routing statistic that never asked where the held material had been. The
mesh repair (`G.reseed`'s donor pull, rings 2/8/32 nodes) routinely relocates markers with their
heat intact, so a parcel dropped into a cold neighbourhood is hot against a cold field, and the
field support holds it — which would make part of the concentration coverage repair rather than
melt routing, and a bound on deliveries the wrong instrument for it. The join instrument
(observation only) records, per material id, the frame the repair last relocated it, and at
every held routing sample the age of that relocation. The bank carries the cumulative windows
1, 3, 10, 30 and 100 frames (50 kyr each at this clock): held sample count, held host
mass-time and the worst T−field gap inside each window, beside the never-relocated and
ever-relocated totals that partition the held census exactly. Each delivered event and the
hottest record carry the same age, so the transfer side and the outlier are read with the same
question. The seed-5 pair (the world every milestone was read on, fingerprint 6191250240167390
control and 1808048552219233 candidate) runs again with the join: observation only, so each
must keep its fingerprint, and the join then answers the question on the very numbers M5 quoted.

**Gates are M4's list unchanged.** The join windows must partition the held census (ever +
never = the held totals, monotone cumulative windows), and the instrument is neutral: a joined
run reproduces its unjoined world bitwise on every diagnostic. The reading is a share, not a
threshold — the report states how much of the held mass-time sits on recently moved material
and lets that decide whether the concentration is routing or coverage. Nothing is selected on
this milestone's numbers alone; condition 5's material branch remains the eligibility test.
