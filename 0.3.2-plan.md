# 0.3.2 — light-parcel heat concentration: measurement before selection

Reference: `0.3.2-draft.md`. This plan does not authorize a routing-threshold change.

## Defect and protected behavior

The adjoint gather conserves the domain integral, not the distribution of specific enthalpy.
A low marker measure can amplify an increment; small mass alone is neither a sufficient
explanation nor a safe exclusion criterion. The existing 600 Myr seed-5 cell has three
field-supported deposit transfers as well as an unphysical hot tail. Preserve the former;
do not use their count to tune a constant or remove the field-support term.

The same-cell baseline must reproduce fingerprint **8261233554728015**, three transfers
and 9.7302 km² shed. Record individual events (frame, ID, position, host and melt mass,
temperature, field and solidus), not just the aggregate: three unrelated replacement events
are not evidence that the original transfers survived. Changed trajectories require an
explicit material/event correspondence argument before acceptance.

## M0 — bounded candidate experiment

Run fresh processes, seed 5, 50 kyr/frame, 600 Myr, shipped geometry, initial condition,
P3/P4 passes and pure FLIP. Baseline first, then each candidate serially, then baseline again
for replay and host timing drift. No production parameter or kernel changes at this stage.

Candidate definitions (experimental probes, **not frozen constants**):

- **Gain cap 2:** multiply the gathered specific increment by
  `min(1, 2 / gain)`, where gain is `sum(W*cw) / sum(W)` over the interior stencil.
  Apply to all grid-coupled phases. This is a parcel-gain cap, not a cap on each node's `cw`.
- **Mass-share floor 0.01:** multiply that increment by
  `min(1, share / 0.01)`, where share is
  `sum(W * W*m / deposited_node_mass) / sum(W)`. No mass is manufactured.
  This is attenuation below a share floor, not a redistribution or a modified scatter.
- **Deposit relaxation, retention 0.999 per 50 kyr gather:** after the adjoint increment,
  relax deposit H toward `byT(sampleAt(Tg), depth, composition).h` by 0.001.
  Use the ordinary bilinear field, not the unnormalized adjoint PIC gather; its weights
  need not sum to one. This is about a 50 Myr e-folding at this clock, not a proposed
  clock-independent law. Book the correction honestly as intake.
- **Minimum deposit mass 1 km²:** before scatter, merge smaller deposits into an at-least-
  1 km² deposit in the same nearest-node bucket, using the pool's composition compatibility
  and requiring equal quench certificates. Use the conservative pool merge, including
  periodic position and slot compaction. Do not mix phases, delete mass, or search globally
  to force a floor. Count stranded attempts when no eligible local recipient exists;
  this is a local coalescence policy, not a guaranteed lower bound.

These probes test the mechanisms separately. Their failure does not disprove every value
or implementation in a family. No parameter search is driven by routing count.

## Measurements and gates

Use compensated inventories after **every frame**, not HUD samples:

- mass relative residual < 1e-9;
- enthalpy change against recorded intake < 1e-9;
- P3.1 latent identity < 1e-10 relative (denominator floored at 1 near zero);
- worst cumulative `|intake/wall - 1|`, and its end value;
- actual P4 long-leg gate < 0.01; separately report the draft's approximately 5e-5
  historical reference. The latter is a reference target, **not** the assertion in
  `pt-p4-check.js`. That harness also has a < 1e-9 short-matrix identity gate;
- mean kernel frame < 20 ms at 50 kyr, excluding read-only observation and inventories
  added by the experiment, including all candidate work. Repeat baseline to expose host
  drift. Timing remains host-specific, not a universal performance guarantee;
- finite parcel T and -0.5 <= T <= 1.5, with extrema and violating sample counts by phase.
  This is a new candidate gate, not a claim the baseline already passes;
- at G5, count and integrate the certified, buried, threshold-molten deposits rejected
  by field support; record host and melt-equivalent mass-time and maximum T-field gap;
- record all admitted deposit events, actual transfer counter/mass, mantle routing count,
  refusals, and complete-state hash. Report rejected candidates without disguising them
  as failed execution of the measurement harness.

Never balance a capped increment by editing the wall ledger. A closing enthalpy book can
coexist with a failing wall gate if the correction was honestly recorded: both matter.

## M1 — selection or rejection

Select nothing unless the tail, conservation, wall and timing requirements hold together
and the protected transfers have evidence of survival. Keep condition 5 unchanged. If
local caps/relaxation lose the wall integral or conservative coalescence leaves the tail,
measure a node-wise conservative bounded redistribution next; do not ship the least-bad
probe as a fix. Redistribution needs a feasibility treatment where local heat capacity
cannot absorb the node increment under the bound, not an unbooked global correction.

## M2 — only after an accepted remedy

Give any new constant a physically stated purpose, measured independent gate and freeze
line. Establish per-frame, per-phase temperature assertions in the owning acceptance
harnesses, including the 600 Myr cell and a clock/seed matrix. Re-bank all state-dependent
fingerprints: P3 replay, P4 integrated/matrix, 0.3.1 canonical 300 Myr seed-6 leg, seed/clock
matrices and seed-5 600 Myr leg. Re-run P3/P4 owners, melt-path owners, page parity and the
column whole-series acceptance. Old hashes remain baseline evidence, never expected
outputs for a changed state. Only then reconsider whether the deposit field filter remains
necessary; no constant changes to reopen the 300 Myr route.
