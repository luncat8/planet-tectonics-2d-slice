## 1. Problem

0.1.12 certified the quiet-frame population (`--quiet-identity`: 79,400,392 of
79,856,680 pairs holding neighbour continuity, 0 beside a different record,
Voronoi width decomposition and advection law closing to 0 m) and found that
2,882 of 0.1.9's 2,883 censored observations sit on one of the 1,887 frames
(1.24%) where `COL.intents` fired intent 3 (`COL.drain`, the sliver retirement)
behind the audit's birth/consume event detector. In all 32 worlds R1's
quiet-frame yardstick was set by such a frame (`1594..5897 m`, median `4188`).
With a retirement counted as the event it is (`--drain-event`), the four strict
legs went to `1 / 13 FAILURES` (`R1` ratios `2.17 / 2.43 / 5.08 / 2.39` against
`1.25`), and R1 read `1.29..5.89` across the 32 worlds with the retirement the
loudest site on 29.

Because `experiments/acceptance.js` gates the four strict legs at exit 0, the
corrected event definition must land with the engine remedy that removes the
retirement pop.

## 2. Cause, from the code and geometry

`COL.consume` (`js/columns.js`) turns a subducted column into a zero-crust ghost
(`colGhost = 1`) on the overriding plate (`colPlate[loser] = colPlate[winner]`)
at the separation floor `min = P.gFloor·P.w0` (≈ 9.38 km) from the overriding
margin, while the gap on its downgoing side starts near `w0` (≈ 78.2 km).
`COL.intents` retires the ghost (`intent = 3`) in two regimes:

- **Both gaps at the floor** (`gL <= min && (d <= min·1.0001 || colGhost[j])`):
  the ghost's Voronoi span is `9.38 km` (`4.69 km` per neighbour), or wider in
  a ghost chain, and if convergence closed the gap in less than `P.tauSliver`
  (4 Myr) while the margin thickened, `S.z[ghost]` still sits up to `5.2 km`
  below the straight chord between its neighbours (`3000/1/100` frame 847:
  `z = −3345 m` against chord `+1862 m`, raster jump `3719 m`).
- **Opening trench gap** (`edgeRelN > 0`): after a consumption resets the new
  boundary's state, slab pull drops and `edgeRelN` can cross zero 2–3 frames
  (`0.1–0.3 Myr`) later while the downgoing gap is still `39..78 km` wide.
  Deleting the ghost in one frame dumps `0.5·g_other = 19.5..39.1 km` of
  territory onto the overriding margin (`43.8 → 82.9 km`), halving its stack
  thickness (`layTh /= colW`) and dropping `SURF.elev` by `3.2..5.9 km` in one
  frame (`3000/1/50` frame 1624: `3162 m`; `3000/1/100` frame 1971: `5554 m`;
  `5000/5/50` frame 4970: `5078 m`; `5000/5/100` frame 1164: `3899 m`).

Why a single-frame deletion cannot be cured by ramping `zDyn` alone: when a
ghost vertex `G` at `9.4 km` from a continental margin `B` (`z_B ≈ +6.5 km`)
and `78 km` from an oceanic column `A` (`z_A ≈ −4.6 km`) is deleted while
`z_G ≈ −1.2..−2.7 km` has not yet morphed onto the chord `AB`, the 2-segment
bent polyline `A → G → B` collapses in one frame to a single straight segment
`A → B`. At the two adjacent raster samples bracketing `G` and `B` (`19.55 km`
apart, `x_G` at `81–89%` of `AB`), both post-deletion elevations are linear
functions of the single surviving margin elevation `z_new`:
- leaving `zDyn` untouched (`z_new ≈ +1.9 km`) drops the sample at `B` by
  `4.4..5.5 km`;
- ramping `zDyn` to hold `z_new = z_B` (`+6.5 km`) snaps the chord at `G`
  upward by `+5.9..+6.4 km`;
- even the minimax optimum over all real `z_new` moves the raster by at least
  `3.2..3.8 km` (`> 1.25 · quietDz`), while holding a `40 km`-thick column at
  `+6.5 km` for `tauDyn = 10 Myr` drives `z³` erosion (`SURF.k6`) and oceanic
  slope (`PLT.basal`).

## 3. Design — the two candidates and the landed rule

### 3.1 Candidate A (`--retire-ramp`) — `zDyn` ramp on the absorbing record

On `COL.drain(st, c)`, record the pre-K4 `SURF.elev` of the non-ghost
neighbours `im` and `ip`; in `COL.rampZ(st)` (after `st.widths()` and
`layTh /= st.colW`), add `preElev − SURF.elev(k)` to `st.zDyn[k]` on the
surviving slots `k = COL.map[im], COL.map[ip]`. Kept in
`experiments/contact-audit.js` under `--retire-ramp` for direct reproduction.

### 3.2 Candidate B (landed) — handing the territory back over frames

When a ghost `i` satisfies the retirement condition in `COL.transport` or
`COL.intents`, it enters the retiring state (`S.colGhost[i] = 2`) instead of
being dropped while still holding `9.4..43.8 km` of Voronoi width or a
multi-kilometre chord kink:

1. **Bounded width step (`COL.retireStep`).** On each frame `dt` (Myr), a
   retiring ghost releases at most
   $$\Delta w_{\text{rel}} = \max\!\left(2000,\; \min\!\left(\frac{\alpha(\text{dt})\,w_{im}}{dr / \text{span}},\; \frac{\alpha(\text{dt})\,w_{ip}}{dl / \text{span}}\right)\right), \qquad \alpha(\text{dt}) = \frac{0.0825\,\text{dt}}{0.025 + \text{dt}}$$
   of its Voronoi width `wVor = 0.5·(dl + dr)`. The rate law is linear in `dt`
   at small steps (`3.3 /Myr`), evaluates to `0.055` at `50 kyr/frame` and
   `0.066` at `100 kyr/frame`, and is bounded by `0.0825 < 0.10` at every
   slider rate so neither receiving neighbour's width ever changes by `10%` in
   a frame.
2. **Idempotent Voronoi partition (`S.widths`).** The ghost's retained width
   `wKeep` is stored in its unused top layer-age slot
   `S.layAg[(i + 1)·P.layerCap − 1]` (leaving `S.oldW[i]` intact for `COL.k4`
   mass conservation even if a C4 import placed a bed on the ghost, and
   requiring no new array in `S` or `Checkpoint.VERSION` bump). For any
   `colGhost[i] === 2` with `0 < wKeep < wVor`, `S.widths` transfers
   `rel = wVor − wKeep`:
   ```js
   this.colW[i] -= rel;
   this.colW[im] += rel * (dr / span);
   this.colW[ip] += rel * (dl / span);
   ```
   Because `dr/span + dl/span = 1` and `wVor·(dr/span) = 0.5·dr`,
   `wVor·(dl/span) = 0.5·dl`, `SUM(colW) = P.wrap` holds identically on every
   call and the limit `wKeep → 0` matches the post-deletion Voronoi widths of
   `im` and `ip` to 0 m.
3. **Profile convergence (`SURF.morph`) and drain (`COL.intents`).** While
   `colGhost[k] === 2`, `SURF.morph` applies an additional `0.25` step toward
   the chord `(zt, ht)` each pass so the drawn profile converges in lockstep
   with the width hand-back (~6–8 frames). `COL.intents` fires `intent[i] = 3`
   (`COL.drain`) once `S.colW[i] <= step·1.05` and `|S.z[i] − zt| <= 500 m`.
4. **Event definition.** `COL.drain` is recorded unconditionally in
   `experiments/contact-audit.js` (`SITE_R` / `rSites`, `siteKind = 'sliver
   retirement'`), with no flag required.
