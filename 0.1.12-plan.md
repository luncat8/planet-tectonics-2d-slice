# 0.1.12 — quiet-frame material identity

Status: **Measured, nothing landed in the engine.** Two report-only flags in
`experiments/contact-audit.js` (`--quiet-identity`, `--drain-event`), one table tool
(`experiments/quiet-identity-table.js`), one 32-world matrix. No constant, physics rule,
checkpoint field or slice-format byte moved; the four strict legs with the flags off are
byte-identical with the 0.1.9 baseline apart from the timing line. Worklog:
`archive/0.1.12-quiet-identity-worklog.md`. Report:
`archive/0.1.12-quiet-identity-report.md`. 0.4.2 Phase C remains held.

## 1. Problem

0.1.9 fixed the quiet-frame matcher (cyclic alignment, the same distance bound on both candidate
sides, unusable and already-claimed records skipped, one-to-one pairing) and reached zero
unmatched observations over 32 worlds. Its 10% width-change exclusion still censored **2,883**
observations, so it selected no threshold and handed the next release two obligations: establish
material identity through re-partition before reading those observations as quiet changes, and
select no threshold from censored data.

Without identity the quiet population cannot be read at all. A record's width is its Voronoi
territory, so a record that gains territory thins by conservation, and the drawn surface
(`SURF.elev = zRef + buoy − therm + zDyn`) moves by kilometres with no crust moving anywhere.
What the audit reports as a quiet-frame thickness change is then a mixture of territory and
material, and R1's background — the largest drawn move on a quiet frame — is read off that
mixture.

## 2. Cause, from the code

Per frame: K2 velocities → `COL.transport` (`oldW = colW`; advection by `colU·dt`; `COL.floor`;
sort + `gather`; `st.widths()`) → `COL.k4` (the quiet branch rescales beds, `layTh *= oldW/newW`)
→ `onEvent`/`COL.finalFloor`, **only on topology frames**. Three facts follow:

- **Volume is exactly conserved on a quiet frame.** K4's rescale is the only thickness edit, so
  `hNew·wNew = hOld·wOld` up to the flow laws. The identity-corrected change is therefore
  `dhId = |hNew − hOld·wOld/wNew|` — no model of the flow needed.
- **Territory is a neighbour difference.** `S.widths()` (`js/state.js` l.298) is
  `colW[i] = 0.5·(dl + dr)` wrapped, so with a record's two neighbours unchanged its width change
  is exactly half the difference of their displacements, and each displacement splits into
  advection (`colU·dt`) and floor push.
- **A sliver retirement is a topology change the audit does not count.** `COL.intents`
  (`js/columns.js` l.913) sets intent 3 on a ghost whose two gaps are at or below
  `P.gFloor·P.w0` (≈ 9.375 km); `COL.drain` empties the marker's stack and `gather` drops the
  record, so a neighbour takes the whole territory in one frame. The audit's `dSites` counts
  births and consumes only, so those frames are quiet by definition. `COL.intents`' own comment
  claims the retirement "moves each neighbour's surface by metres"; a birth gets `COL.rampZ`, a
  retirement gets no ramp.

## 3. Design — the instrument

`--quiet-identity` certifies a quiet pair before reading it, and reads certified and uncertified
populations apart. Nothing in it writes to the engine; the wrappers are installed only under the
flag, and every buffer is preallocated.

1. **Frame integrity.** A quiet frame is *certified* when it took exactly one `COL.floor` solve
   over the same record count (`floorN === preN === S.nCol`). Otherwise its pairs are counted
   apart (`badFrames`, `badPairs`, `badCen`, own drawn/identity maxima) and never enter a
   certified book. The kernel's own intents are read from `COL.intent` in pre-K4 slot order,
   with `SIM.onEvent` call counts and the ghost delta beside them, and the first bad frame is
   described in full.
2. **C1 neighbour continuity.** The pair's own two neighbours (cyclically) are still the matched
   record's two neighbours in `MATCH`. Held → certified. Not held but flanked by a ghost, a
   pre-frame ghost or a sliver (`colW < 0.5·w0`) → read apart as uncertified. Not held and not
   flanked → a defect, asserted to be zero by the table.
3. **The territory decomposition.** With C1 held,
   `|wNew − (wOld + 0.5·((advD[bp] + pushD[bp]) − (advD[bm] + pushD[bm])))|` must be zero, and
   `|advD[i] − floorU[i]·SIM.dG|` must be zero. These check the model against the engine rather
   than assume it.
4. **C2 volume.** `|hNew·wNew − hOld·wOld| / (hOld·wOld)`, reported with the row's drawn
   thickness beside it, because a large relative change on a near-zero-crust column is metres of
   rock.
5. **The identity-corrected reading.** `dhId` with its fel/maf/sed split (each bed corrected by
   the same ratio), so a material change is attributed to the crust type that moved.
6. **The drawn-surface split and the raster-yardstick split.** The same populations, applied to
   the audit's own `z` reading and to R1's yardstick, so a background can be traced to the
   population that set it. The five buckets must recombine to the gate's yardstick.

`--drain-event` is the **candidate event definition**: a `COL.drain` wrapper fills a separate
`rSites`/`SITE_R` list with `siteKind = 'sliver retirement'`. It is deliberately not `dSites`, so
R5 cannot read one consumption twice.

## 4. What it measured

- The matcher is right: **0 pairs beside a different record** in 79,856,680 quiet pairs over
  152,233 quiet frames (32 worlds × 5000 frames); the width decomposition closes to **0 m** and
  the advection law to **0.0000 m** over all 79,400,392 certified pairs.
- **The censored population is the sliver retirement.** 2,882 of 0.1.9's 2,883 censored
  observations sit on one of the 1,887 quiet frames (1.24%) that changed their record set behind
  the detector, and the kernel's own intents on those frames are drain 1,801, birth 0, consume 0,
  accrete 0. One censored pair survives on a certified quiet frame (seed 8 @100 kyr, 111 m of a
  1977 m yardstick).
- **R1's background is the event.** The "record set changed" bucket equals the gate's yardstick in
  every one of the 32 worlds (1594..5897 m, median 4188).
- **With a retirement counted**, the 10% exclusion has 1 observation left to censor in 32 worlds,
  the quiet reading drops to thickness 2.5..16.1 km (median 7.6) and width 1.8..9.6% (median
  7.6), and R1 reads ratio 1.29..5.89 against a budget of 1.25 — over budget on 32 of 32 worlds,
  the retirement being the loudest site on 29. The four strict legs go to `1 / 13 FAILURES`, R1
  the only red.
- **The certified residual is real crustal flow**: identity-corrected thickness 2457..16476 m
  (median 8406), the worst rows felsic (13435 m of felsic with 0 m mafic and 19 m sediment on
  seed 1 @50 kyr frame 861), i.e. K5's belt feed.

## 5. Acceptance

1. **Instrument honesty.** 0 unmatched quiet records; 0 pairs beside a different record; the
   decomposition and the advection law close to 0; the raster split recombines to the gate's
   yardstick; the populations partition; no birth, consume or accretion on a frame the detector
   missed; a ghost change only on a drain frame; the censored population never sets the gate
   background. Asserted by `experiments/quiet-identity-table.js`, which exits non-zero on any of
   them.
2. **No behaviour change without the flag.** The four strict legs (`3000/1/50`, `3000/1/100`,
   `5000/5/50`, `5000/5/100`) stay ALL PASS (13 checks) and their output is identical to
   `experiments/logs/0.1.9-quiet-strict-*.txt` apart from the timing line.
3. **Suite.** Every harness in `AGENTS.md` passes, including `experiments/acceptance.js` and
   `experiments/log-hygiene.js` (one file per content), with no fixture edited.
4. **Nothing lands red.** `experiments/acceptance.js` runs the four strict legs and requires exit
   0, so the candidate definition is measured and reported, not landed: it lands with the engine
   remedy that clears R1, in its own plan.

## 7. Out of scope

- **The retirement ramp.** The engine remedy — ramp the absorbing record's `zDyn` the way
  `COL.rampZ` does for a birth, or hand the territory back over frames — is roadmap §3, its own
  plan, with the gates named there. This release does not touch `js/`.
- **Gating the quiet width or thickness change.** No threshold: the exclusion has nothing left to
  censor under the corrected definition, and the certified residual is material flow that a
  quiet-frame gate would have to allow.
- **`COL.intents`' comment** ("moves each neighbour's surface by metres"): it is three orders of
  magnitude off, and correcting it is part of the remedy's plan, not of a measurement release.
- **0.1.9's other deferred items**: R5's frame-window repeats, `COL.beltAt`'s `beltW`
  same-plate vs same-crust, and the real-browser particle-page pass (owed since 0.1.8, passed
